Please answer the following question: How does AFL compare to other mutation-based fuzzing tools in terms of effectiveness and efficiency?

[0001] assistant:
AFL (American Fuzzy Lop) is widely regarded as one of the most effective and efficient mutation-based fuzzing tools due to its innovative techniques such as coverage-guided fuzzing and the use of a fuzzer that learns from the program's behavior. It often outperforms other tools by finding more bugs and doing so more quickly.
