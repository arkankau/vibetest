The value of the modulus \( n \) in the RSA encryption process is the product of two large prime numbers, \( p \) and \( q \). Its significance lies in the fact that it forms the basis of the public and private keys, ensuring the security of the encryption by making it computationally infeasible to factorize \( n \) back into \( p \) and \( q \).
