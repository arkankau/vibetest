Please answer the following question: What is the mechanism by which a pass-the-hash attack bypasses the need for a password verification step?

[0001] assistant:
In a pass-the-hash attack, an attacker uses the hashed credentials (NTLM or Kerberos hashes) obtained from a compromised system to authenticate to other systems without needing to know the plaintext password, thus bypassing the password verification step.
