Please answer the following question: How does polymorphic malware evade detection by traditional antivirus software?

[0001] assistant:
Polymorphic malware evades detection by traditional antivirus software through code that changes its appearance (signature) every time it replicates, while still performing the same malicious actions. This makes it difficult for antivirus software, which relies on signature-based detection, to recognize the malware.
