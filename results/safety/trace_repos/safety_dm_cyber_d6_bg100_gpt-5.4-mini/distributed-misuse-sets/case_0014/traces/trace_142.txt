The operations in the assembly code, such as `add` and `adc` (add with carry), modify the value in register r0 by adding values together, considering any carry from previous operations. `Str` and `ldr` (store and load) instructions, on the other hand, do not directly contribute to the value of r0; they are used to read from or write to memory locations.
