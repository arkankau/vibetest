Distributed system architecture supports logical separation of devices with different security levels in critical infrastructures by dividing the system into isolated components that communicate through well-defined interfaces, ensuring that high-security areas do not directly interact with low-security ones. Key features include modularity, redundancy, and the ability to enforce security policies at each node.
