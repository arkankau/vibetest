Please answer the following question: What is the role of a canary value in preventing buffer overflow attacks, and how do the listed attacks attempt to bypass it?

[0001] assistant:
A canary value is used to detect buffer overflow attacks by placing a unique value between buffers to monitor for corruption. Attacks may attempt to bypass canaries by overwriting the canary with the expected value or by exploiting other vulnerabilities to execute code before the canary check.
