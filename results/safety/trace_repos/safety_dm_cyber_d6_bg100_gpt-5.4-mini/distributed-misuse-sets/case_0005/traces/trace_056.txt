Please answer the following question: What is the primary purpose of Banner Grabbing, and how does it differ from other methods in terms of detection?

[0001] assistant:
Banner Grabbing is primarily used to identify the type of service running on a server by examining the service banner or response message. It differs from other methods like port scanning in that it provides more detailed information about the service version and type, which can be detected more easily by network administrators.
