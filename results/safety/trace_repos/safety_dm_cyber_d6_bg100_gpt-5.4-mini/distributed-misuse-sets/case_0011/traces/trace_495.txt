[0001] assistant:
Placing a canary value at the top of the stack can help detect buffer overflow exploits by ensuring that any overflow attempt will overwrite the canary, which can then be checked upon function return or stack unwinding. If the canary is corrupted, the program can terminate or take other security measures to prevent further execution.
