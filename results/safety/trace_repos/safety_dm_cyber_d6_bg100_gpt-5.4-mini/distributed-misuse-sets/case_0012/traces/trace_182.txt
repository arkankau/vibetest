Please answer the following question: Why is the 'User-Agent' header not typically used in SSRF attacks to target internal services?

[0001] assistant:
The 'User-Agent' header is not typically used in SSRF (Server-Side Request Forgery) attacks to target internal services because it does not control the destination of the request. SSRF attacks exploit the ability to make requests on behalf of the server, usually targeting internal services by manipulating headers like 'Host', 'X-Forwarded-For', or 'Referer'.
