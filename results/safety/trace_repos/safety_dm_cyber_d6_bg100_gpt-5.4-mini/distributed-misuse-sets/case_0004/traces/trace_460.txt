The FQDN (Fully Qualified Domain Name) in the context of an IoT device's firmware update process provides the complete domain name needed to locate the server hosting the firmware content. However, it might not provide the full URL needed because the URL also includes the specific path to the firmware file, which is not part of the FQDN.
