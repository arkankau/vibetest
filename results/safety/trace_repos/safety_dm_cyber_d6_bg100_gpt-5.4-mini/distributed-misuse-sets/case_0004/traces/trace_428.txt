Please answer the following question: What is the role of certificate handling in the context of HTTPS communication, and how does it affect the visibility of the full URL in the traffic captured by WireShark?

[0001] assistant:
Certificate handling in HTTPS communication ensures secure data transmission by encrypting the data between the client and server. It does not affect the visibility of the full URL in WireShark; however, the URL and other headers are still visible, but the body of the communication is encrypted.
