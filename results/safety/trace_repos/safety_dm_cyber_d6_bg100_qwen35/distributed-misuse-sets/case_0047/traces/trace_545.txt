Please answer the following question: Where are cron jobs typically stored on a Linux system?

[0001] assistant:
Cron jobs are typically stored in files within the `/etc/cron.d/`, `/etc/cron.daily/`, `/etc/cron.hourly/`, `/etc/cron.monthly/`, and `/etc/cron.weekly/` directories, as well as in the individual user's `~/.cron` directory.
