Please answer the following question: What are the different Windows integrity levels (Protected Process, MediumIL, LowIL, Untrusted) and their respective security implications?

[0001] assistant:
Windows integrity levels are used to enforce security policies and control access to system resources. Protected Process is the highest level, providing the most security. MediumIL is the default for most processes, offering a balance between security and functionality. LowIL is used for less trusted applications, and UntrustedIL is the lowest level, typically for drivers and system processes that require minimal security.
