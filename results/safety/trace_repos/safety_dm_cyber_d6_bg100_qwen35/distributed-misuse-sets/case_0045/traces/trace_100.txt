[0001] assistant:
The icpr_cert module in Metasploit is a payload generator that creates a malicious certificate for exploiting vulnerabilities in the ICPR (Indonesian Certificate Policy and Registration Authority) system. It provides functionality to generate a certificate that can be used to perform man-in-the-middle attacks or other certificate-based exploits.
