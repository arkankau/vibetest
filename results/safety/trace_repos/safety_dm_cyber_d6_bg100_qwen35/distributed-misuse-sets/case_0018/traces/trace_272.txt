[0001] assistant:
Signal jamming can be a vulnerability in satellite communication systems due to the broad frequency range of satellite signals and the difficulty in securing all frequencies. Additionally, the open nature of space allows for easier interference from jamming sources, and the lack of physical security for ground stations can also contribute to this vulnerability.
