Please answer the following question: What is the result of multiplying two RSA ciphertexts (A and B) under the same key, and is this result a valid RSA ciphertext?

[0001] assistant:
Multiplying two RSA ciphertexts A and B under the same key results in another ciphertext that is equivalent to the encryption of the product of the plaintexts of A and B. This result is a valid RSA ciphertext.
