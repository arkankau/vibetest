Please answer the following question: What is the purpose of the 'whois' command and how is it typically used?

[0001] assistant:
The 'whois' command is used to query and retrieve information about registered domain names, including details like the domain owner, registration date, expiration date, and name servers. It is typically used by network administrators, domain registrars, and cybersecurity professionals to gather information about domains.
