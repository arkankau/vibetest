Please answer the following question: How does the use of OR operators in the decrypt function affect the decryption process compared to XOR operators?

[0001] assistant:
The use of OR operators in the decrypt function typically does not effectively decrypt data, as it combines bits without allowing for the reversal of the encryption process that XOR does. XOR, on the other hand, allows for reversible encryption, making it suitable for decryption.
