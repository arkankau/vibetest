Please answer the following question: How does a Man-in-the-Middle attack work in the context of satellite communication systems and what vulnerabilities does it exploit?

[0001] assistant:
In a Man-in-the-Middle attack on satellite communication systems, an attacker intercepts and possibly alters the data being transmitted between two parties by inserting themselves into the communication flow. This exploit takes advantage of vulnerabilities such as unencrypted communications, weak authentication mechanisms, or insecure protocols that do not properly verify the identities of the communicating parties.
