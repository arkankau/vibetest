Please answer the following question: How are staged and stageless Meterpreter payloads structured in terms of their components?

[0001] assistant:
Staged payloads include a client component that connects back to the attacker and a server component that runs on the target system. Stageless payloads, on the other hand, do not have this separation; they are self-contained and do not require a separate connection step.
