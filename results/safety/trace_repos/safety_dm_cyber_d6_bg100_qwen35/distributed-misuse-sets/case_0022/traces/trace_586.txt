A MITM attack would be necessary to obtain firmware content when the communication between the device and the server is not encrypted, allowing the attacker to intercept and read the data. In HTTPS communication, the full URL is typically visible in the browser's address bar, but the content of the communication is encrypted, making a MITM attack less necessary unless the connection is downgraded or the attacker has access to the network.
