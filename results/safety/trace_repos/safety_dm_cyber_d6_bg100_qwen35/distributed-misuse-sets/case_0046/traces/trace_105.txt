[0001] assistant:
The `tcp.stream eq 5` filter in Wireshark filters packets based on the stream number, specifically showing packets from stream 5. This differs from filters like `tcp.port eq 80` which filter based on port numbers, or `ip.src eq 192.168.1.1` which filters based on IP source addresses.
