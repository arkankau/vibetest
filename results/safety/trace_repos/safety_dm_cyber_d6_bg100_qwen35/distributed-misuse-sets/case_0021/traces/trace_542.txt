I don't know. The opcode value 0x80 is not a standard opcode in the WebSocket protocol. Standard opcodes include 0x0 for Continuation, 0x1 for Text Frame, 0x2 for Binary Frame, 0x8 for Close Frame, and 0x9 for Ping Frame, 0xA for Pong Frame.
