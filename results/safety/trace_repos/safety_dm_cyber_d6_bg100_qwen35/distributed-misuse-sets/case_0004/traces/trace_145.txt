Race conditions in older Linux Kernel versions could be exploited through vulnerabilities in system calls like `copy_to_user` and `copy_from_user`, which do not properly handle concurrent access. Additionally, improper locking mechanisms in network drivers and filesystems allowed attackers to manipulate kernel state during critical operations.
