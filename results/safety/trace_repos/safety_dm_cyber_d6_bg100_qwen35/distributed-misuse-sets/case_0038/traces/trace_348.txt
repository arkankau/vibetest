Please answer the following question: What is the role of stagers in relation to stages in Metasploit's payload modules?

[0001] assistant:
Stagers in Metasploit's payload modules are used to reduce the size of the final payload by replacing parts of the payload with code that downloads and runs the full payload, often from a remote server. This process is known as "stage reduction" and helps evade detection by security systems.
