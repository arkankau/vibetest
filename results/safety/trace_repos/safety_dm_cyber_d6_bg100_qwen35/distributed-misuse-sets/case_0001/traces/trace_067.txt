Please answer the following question: What are the key differences between ARM's Thumb mode and ARM mode in terms of instruction set and length?

[0001] assistant:
In ARM mode, instructions are 32 bits long and can directly access a wide range of registers and memory. In Thumb mode, instructions are 16 bits long and are typically used for more compact code size, though they can only directly access a subset of registers.
