Nmap is primarily a network scanning tool and not designed for intercepting data transmissions. Its limitations include lack of deep packet inspection capabilities and inability to decrypt data. To mitigate these, use dedicated packet sniffing tools like Wireshark for deep packet inspection and ensure data is encrypted to protect it from interception.
