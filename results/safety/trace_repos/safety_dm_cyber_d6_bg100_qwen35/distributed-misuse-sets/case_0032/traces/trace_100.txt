Please answer the following question: How does a stateful firewall track and maintain state information for network connections?

[0001] assistant:
A stateful firewall tracks and maintains state information by inspecting the initial packets of a connection and then monitoring subsequent packets based on that state. It uses a table to keep track of active connections and their states, allowing it to make decisions based on the context of the entire conversation, not just individual packets.
