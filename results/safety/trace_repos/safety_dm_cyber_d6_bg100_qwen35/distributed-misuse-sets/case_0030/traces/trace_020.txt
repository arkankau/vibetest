The `subs w8, w8, #0x0` instruction subtracts 0x0 from the value in w8, which is a no-op but could be part of a larger sequence to conditionally branch or modify the value. These instructions do not directly determine the function's return value; typically, the return value is stored in a register like x0 or w0 and is loaded or modified elsewhere in the function.
