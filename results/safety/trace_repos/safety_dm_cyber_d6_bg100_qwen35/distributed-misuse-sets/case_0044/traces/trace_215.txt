Please answer the following question: What does the GDB command `x/4x $rsp` do and how does it interpret the data at the address pointed to by $RSP?

[0001] assistant:
The `x/4x $rsp` command in GDB displays 4 words (32 bits each) of data at the address pointed to by the `$rsp` register. It interprets the data as hexadecimal values.
