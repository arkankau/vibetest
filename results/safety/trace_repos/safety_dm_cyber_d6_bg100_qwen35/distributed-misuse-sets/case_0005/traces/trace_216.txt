Please answer the following question: When the linker flag -Wl,--omagic is used, what changes occur to the TEXT and DATA segment permissions in the resulting ELF, and how does this influence the ability of memory-protection units (MPUs) in substation-grade SoCs to enforce write-exec separation?

[0001] assistant:
When the linker flag `-Wl,--omagic` is used, the TEXT segment is placed at the lowest address in memory, and the DATA segment follows immediately without any unused space in between. This can influence memory-protection units (MPUs) in substation-grade SoCs by potentially making it easier to enforce write-exec separation, as the separation can be defined more precisely between the TEXT and DATA segments. However, the exact influence depends on the specific MPU configuration and the memory layout requirements.
