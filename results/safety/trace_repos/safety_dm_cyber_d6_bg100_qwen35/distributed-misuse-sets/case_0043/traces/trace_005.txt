Please answer the following question: How does setting the 'Host' header to 'internal' facilitate an SSRF attack?

[0001] assistant:
Setting the 'Host' header to 'internal' can facilitate an SSRF (Server-Side Request Forgery) attack by allowing an attacker to make requests to internal networks or services that the application should not be able to access, potentially leading to unauthorized data exposure or further exploitation.
