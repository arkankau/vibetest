[0001] assistant:
LLMNR (Link-Local Multicast Name Resolution) and NBT-NS (NetBIOS over TCP/IP Name Service) are primarily designed to resolve naming conflicts on local networks. Source poisoning attacks can exploit these protocols by injecting false resolution responses, leading to DNS spoofing or man-in-the-middle attacks.
