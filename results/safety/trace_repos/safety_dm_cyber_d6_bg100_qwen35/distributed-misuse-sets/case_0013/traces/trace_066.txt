Please answer the following question: What is a signed kernel rootkit and how can it be used to establish persistence in a computer system?

[0001] assistant:
A signed kernel rootkit is a type of malware that is digitally signed to appear legitimate, allowing it to load its components into the kernel of an operating system. It can establish persistence by modifying the kernel or system drivers to ensure its modules are loaded at every system boot, making it difficult to detect and remove.
