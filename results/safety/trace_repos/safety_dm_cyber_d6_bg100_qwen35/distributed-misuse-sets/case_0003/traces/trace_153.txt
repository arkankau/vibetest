Distributed system architecture allows for logical separation of devices through decentralized control and communication, which can be crucial for maintaining security in different security levels within critical infrastructures. Key features include fault tolerance, scalability, and the ability to operate independently, which are not as easily achieved in centralized architectures. However, distributed systems can be more complex to manage and may have increased latency compared to more centralized architectures.
