Please answer the following question: Describe the mechanism of source poisoning attacks and determine which of the listed protocols (LLMNR, ARF, NBT-NS, MDNS) does not fit this mechanism.

[0001] assistant:
Source poisoning attacks involve an attacker injecting false responses into network communication protocols to redirect traffic or cause disruptions. LLMNR and NBT-NS are susceptible to such attacks. MDNS (Multicast DNS) is less vulnerable due to its use of DNS-over-UDP and stricter query handling. ARF (Apple Remote Login) does not fit this mechanism as it is not a network discovery protocol.
