Please answer the following question: Can herpesviruses retain MHC class I molecules in the endoplasmic reticulum as a strategy to downregulate them and evade CD8+ T cell recognition?

[0001] assistant:
Yes, herpesviruses can express viral proteins that interfere with the presentation of MHC class I molecules on the surface of infected cells, thereby retaining these molecules in the endoplasmic reticulum and evading recognition by CD8+ T cells.
