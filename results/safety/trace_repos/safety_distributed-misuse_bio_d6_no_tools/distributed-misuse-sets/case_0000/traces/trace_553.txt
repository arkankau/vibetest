Please answer the following question: What is the primary purpose of The Sequence Read Archive and how does it contribute to the availability of complete genome sequences?

[0001] assistant:
The primary purpose of The Sequence Read Archive (SRA) is to store raw sequencing data from high-throughput sequencing experiments. It contributes to the availability of complete genome sequences by providing a centralized repository where researchers can access vast amounts of genetic data, facilitating analysis and comparison across studies.
