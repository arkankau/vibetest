Moderate consistent rainfall in temperate climates can enhance the survival of B. anthracis spores in the soil, thereby increasing the risk of anthrax infection in livestock. Wet conditions help preserve spores and facilitate their dispersal through runoff, potentially exposing more animals to the bacteria.
