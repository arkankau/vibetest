Please answer the following question: What is known about the specific mechanisms by which nirmatrelvir inhibits the 3CL protease of SARS-CoV-2?

[0001] assistant:
Nirmatrelvir inhibits the 3CL protease of SARS-CoV-2 by binding to its active site, preventing the cleavage of polyproteins necessary for viral replication. This binding blocks the enzyme's function, thus hindering the virus's ability to produce new viral particles.
