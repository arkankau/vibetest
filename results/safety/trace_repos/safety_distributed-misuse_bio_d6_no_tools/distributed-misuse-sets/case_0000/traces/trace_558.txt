Please answer the following question: What is meant by an 'intra-host stochastic process' and how does it relate to the spread of a disease between hosts?

[0001] assistant:
An 'intra-host stochastic process' refers to the random variability in the within-host dynamics of a pathogen, such as its replication, mutation, and interaction with the host's immune system. This process can influence the spread of a disease between hosts by affecting the viral load and transmissibility of infected individuals.
