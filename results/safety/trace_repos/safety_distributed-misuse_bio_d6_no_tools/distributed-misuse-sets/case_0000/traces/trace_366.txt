Please answer the following question: Which influenza A gene segments are most frequently chosen as targets in CDC-recommended RT-PCR kits for seasonal surveillance, and what is the scientific rationale for picking those segments?

[0001] assistant:
The CDC-recommended RT-PCR kits for seasonal influenza surveillance typically target the matrix (M) gene and the nucleoprotein (NP) gene segments. These segments are chosen because they are highly conserved and provide reliable detection and differentiation among influenza A viruses.
