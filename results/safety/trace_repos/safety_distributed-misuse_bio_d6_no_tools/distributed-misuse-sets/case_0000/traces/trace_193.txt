Molecular dynamics simulations and protein structure analysis help assess the pathogenicity of a virus by predicting how viral proteins interact with host cell components, identifying potential binding sites, and understanding conformational changes that may affect viral entry or replication. These models can derive detailed information such as precise binding affinities, dynamics of protein-protein interactions, and conformational transitions that are crucial for viral function and pathogenesis.
