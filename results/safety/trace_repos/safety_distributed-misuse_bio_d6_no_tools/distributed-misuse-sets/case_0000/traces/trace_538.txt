[0001] assistant:
Measuring fibrin degradation products (FDPs) is significant in understanding Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC) in Ebola virus infection because elevated FDP levels indicate increased fibrin turnover, suggesting widespread clotting and subsequent breakdown of clots throughout the body, which is a hallmark of DIC.
