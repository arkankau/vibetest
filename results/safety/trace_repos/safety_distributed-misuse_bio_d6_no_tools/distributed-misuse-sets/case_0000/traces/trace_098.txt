Please answer the following question: Does the replication-proficiency of VACV strains contribute to their potential harmfulness, and if so, how?

[0001] assistant:
The replication proficiency of VACV (Variola virus, the cause of smallpox) strains contributes to their potential harmfulness by allowing them to efficiently produce new viral particles, which can lead to a more severe infection and higher viral load in the host.
