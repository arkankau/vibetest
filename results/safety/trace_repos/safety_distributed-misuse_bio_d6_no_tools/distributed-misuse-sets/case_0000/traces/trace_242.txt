Please answer the following question: How might vaccination in domestic poultry lead to antigenic drift or mask the circulation of the H5N1 virus, and what are the consequences of these phenomena?

[0001] assistant:
Vaccination in domestic poultry can lead to antigenic drift by altering the virus's surface antigens, potentially reducing vaccine effectiveness. It can also mask the circulation of H5N1 by inducing antibodies that do not fully neutralize the virus, allowing it to persist undetected. The consequences include reduced accuracy in disease surveillance and potential underestimation of infection rates, which can hinder effective control measures.
