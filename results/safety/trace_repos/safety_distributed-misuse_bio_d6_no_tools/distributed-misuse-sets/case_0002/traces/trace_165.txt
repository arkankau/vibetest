Please answer the following question: What is the role of the viral nucleoprotein (NP) in the process of host cell entry during influenza A virus replication?

[0001] assistant:
The viral nucleoprotein (NP) does not directly participate in host cell entry; its primary role is to package the viral RNA into the helical nucleocapsid and play a crucial part in viral genome replication and transcription within the infected cell.
