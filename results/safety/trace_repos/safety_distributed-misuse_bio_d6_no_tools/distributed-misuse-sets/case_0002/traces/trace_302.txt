Please answer the following question: What is the role of the HA protein in the immune response to influenza and how is it affected by the removal of polybasic amino acids at the cleavage site?

[0001] assistant:
The HA protein is critical for the entry of influenza virus into host cells. The removal of polybasic amino acids at the cleavage site reduces viral replication and transmission by preventing the efficient cleavage of HA, thus hindering viral entry and spread.
