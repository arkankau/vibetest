Please answer the following question: Compare the efficiency of golden gate assembly, extension overlap PCR, gibson assembly, and circular polymerase extension reaction in terms of assembling complex genomes from DNA fragments.

[0001] assistant:
Golden Gate assembly is efficient for assembling up to 10 fragments but has limitations with larger genomes due to restriction site availability. Extension overlap PCR can handle larger fragments but is limited by the length of the PCR product, typically up to 1-2 kb. Gibson assembly is highly versatile for larger genomes and can handle up to several hundred base pairs, making it more flexible. Circular polymerase extension reaction (CPCR) is less commonly used but can efficiently assemble large DNA fragments by ligating multiple PCR products.
