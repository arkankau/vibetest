Please answer the following question: What is the role of the membrane protein in the SARS-CoV-2 virus and how does it interact with the host immune system?

[0001] assistant:
The spike (S) protein of SARS-CoV-2 is a membrane protein that facilitates viral entry into host cells by binding to angiotensin-converting enzyme 2 (ACE2). This interaction triggers cellular fusion, allowing the virus to infect the host. The virus also uses other membrane proteins to evade and manipulate the host immune response, including the production of immunomodulatory proteins that can suppress the immune system.
