Please answer the following question: Can phage display libraries and directed evolution be used to synthesize and assemble large viral genomes, and if so, how?

[0001] assistant:
Phage display libraries and directed evolution are not typically used to synthesize and assemble large viral genomes. These techniques are more commonly employed for protein engineering and selection of specific protein variants. For synthesizing and assembling large viral genomes, methods such as overlapping PCR, Gibson assembly, or other molecular cloning techniques are more directly applicable.
