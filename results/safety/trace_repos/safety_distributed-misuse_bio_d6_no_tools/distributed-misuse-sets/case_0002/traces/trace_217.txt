Please answer the following question: What are the different reading frames in which a protein-coding sequence can be read, and how do they affect the amino acid sequence produced?

[0001] assistant:
A protein-coding sequence can be read in six different reading frames, three starting from each of the forward (5' to 3') and reverse (3' to 5') strands. Reading frames that start with different nucleotides will produce different amino acid sequences, as each reading frame codes for a unique set of codons and thus a unique sequence of amino acids.
