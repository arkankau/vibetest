Please answer the following question: How would comparing the efficacy of vaccines in animal models previously exposed to other coronaviruses help in assessing the risk of ADE in SARS-CoV-2 vaccine development?

[0001] assistant:
Comparing the efficacy of vaccines in animal models previously exposed to other coronaviruses could help assess the risk of Antibody-Dependent Enhancement (ADE) by observing whether pre-existing antibodies react differently to the SARS-CoV-2 vaccine, potentially leading to enhanced viral infection. This would provide insights into how the immune response in previously exposed animals might translate to humans.
