Please answer the following question: How do synonymous mutations assist in the generation of recombinant viruses without altering the protein function, which is beneficial for reverse genetics studies?

[0001] assistant:
Synonymous mutations, which change nucleotides in the coding sequence without altering the amino acid sequence, can be used to introduce genetic diversity without affecting protein function. This allows for the generation of recombinant viruses with altered genetic backgrounds, facilitating reverse genetics studies by enabling the identification of functional roles of specific genes.
