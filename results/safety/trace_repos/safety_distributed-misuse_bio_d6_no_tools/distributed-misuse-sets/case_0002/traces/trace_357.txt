Please answer the following question: Are bacterial toxins primarily enzymes that engage in biochemical reactions with host cellular components, while toxic effectors simply bind to these components?

[0001] assistant:
Bacterial toxins are indeed primarily enzymes that engage in biochemical reactions with host cellular components, while toxic effectors often simply bind to these components to disrupt normal functions. However, some toxins can have both enzymatic and binding functions.
