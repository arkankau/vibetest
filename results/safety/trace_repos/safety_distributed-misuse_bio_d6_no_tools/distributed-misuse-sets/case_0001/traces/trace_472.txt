[0001] assistant:
CHO cells, Vero cells, and BHK-21 cells have limitations in adenovirus vector production due to lower transfection efficiencies, slower growth rates, and less well-characterized viral recombination risks compared to HEK293 cells. HEK293 cells, being derived from human embryonic kidney tissue, offer higher transfection efficiencies, faster growth, and more established protocols, making them more favorable for adenovirus vector production in vaccine development.
