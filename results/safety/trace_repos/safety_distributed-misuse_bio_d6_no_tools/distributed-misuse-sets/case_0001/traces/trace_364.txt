Please answer the following question: How can phage display libraries be used to evolve bacteriophages with improved specificity and affinity for drug-resistant pathogens?

[0001] assistant:
Phage display libraries can be used to evolve bacteriophages with improved specificity and affinity for drug-resistant pathogens by selecting phages that bind more effectively to target pathogens through iterative rounds of panning and amplification. This process allows for the identification and enrichment of phages with enhanced binding properties, which can then be further optimized for therapeutic applications.
