Please answer the following question: How does the growth of Bacillus cereus strains with 5-bromo-4-chloro-3-indoxyl-myoinositol-1-phosphate help in identifying Bacillus anthracis?

[0001] assistant:
Growth of Bacillus cereus strains with 5-bromo-4-chloro-3-indoxyl-myoinositol-1-phosphate does not help in identifying Bacillus anthracis; in fact, B. anthracis does not produce a color change in this medium, while B. cereus and other Bacillus species may. Thus, this test is not useful for distinguishing B. anthracis from B. cereus.
