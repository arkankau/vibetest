Please answer the following question: How do AAV vectors facilitate the insertion of a gene into the herpesvirus genome, and what are their limitations?

[0001] assistant:
AAV vectors facilitate gene insertion into the herpesvirus genome by transducing target cells without integrating into the host genome, thus reducing the risk of insertional mutagenesis. However, their limitations include limited capacity for transgene insertion (typically up to 4-5 kb), potential immune responses, and the inability to transduce non-dividing cells effectively.
