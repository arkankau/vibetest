Please answer the following question: What is the function of the viral immediate-early protein ICP47 in the context of herpesvirus infection?

[0001] assistant:
ICP47 ( Immediate-early protein 47) in herpesvirus infection functions to inhibit the host's immune response by blocking the presentation of viral peptides on the cell surface, thereby preventing the activation of cytotoxic T cells.
