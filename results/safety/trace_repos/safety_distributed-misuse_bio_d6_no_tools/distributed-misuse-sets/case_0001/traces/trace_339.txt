Please answer the following question: For an enveloped virus that must retain infectivity after months at room temperature in a sealed vial with desiccant, does slow shelf freeze-drying provide better maintenance of membrane integrity than rapid freeze-drying?

[0001] assistant:
Slow shelf freeze-drying generally provides better maintenance of membrane integrity for enveloped viruses compared to rapid freeze-drying, as it allows for more controlled dehydration and reduces the risk of membrane damage.
