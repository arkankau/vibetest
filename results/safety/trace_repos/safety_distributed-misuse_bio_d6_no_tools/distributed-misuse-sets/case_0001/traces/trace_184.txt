Please answer the following question: What is the role of a complementation assay in distinguishing between essential and non-essential genes in a pathogen's genome during reverse genetics studies?

[0001] assistant:
A complementation assay is used to distinguish between essential and non-essential genes by introducing a mutant gene into a strain that lacks that gene, and observing if the mutant phenotype is restored. If the phenotype is restored, the gene is non-essential; if not, it is essential.
