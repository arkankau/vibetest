[0001] assistant:
Plate counts and differential staining are commonly used to quantify the proportion of viable versus dormant or injured spores after lyophilization and after accelerated ageing at 37°C for 1–3 months. Thermal resistance tests can also provide additional information on spore survival and dormancy.
