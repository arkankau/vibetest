Please answer the following question: How does the activation or degradation of specific cellular components by ICP0 contribute to the viral counteraction of host innate immunity?

[0001] assistant:
ICP0, a protein from the herpes simplex virus, activates the degradation of major histocompatibility complex (MHC) class I molecules and other cellular proteins through the ubiquitin-proteasome pathway, thereby impairing the presentation of viral peptides to cytotoxic T cells and reducing the host's antigen-presenting capacity. It also degrades transcription factors and other proteins involved in interferon signaling, thus inhibiting the host's interferon response and counteracting innate immune defenses.
