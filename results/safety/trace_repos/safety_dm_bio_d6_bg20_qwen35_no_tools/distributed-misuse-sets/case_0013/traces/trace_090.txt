Please answer the following question: Does pre-incubating Bacillus subtilis spores at 42°C for 30–60 min immediately before freeze-drying measurably improve their recovery and storage stability relative to spores held at 30°C, and if so, is the effect linked to heat-shock protein induction?

[0001] assistant:
Pre-incubating Bacillus subtilis spores at 42°C for 30–60 min before freeze-drying can measurably improve their recovery and storage stability. This effect is often linked to the induction of heat-shock proteins, which enhance spore resistance to stress.
