Please answer the following question: When preparing a freeze-dried Bacillus subtilis probiotic, how does adding 10% skim milk to the cell suspension before lyophilization affect post-drying survival and shelf-life compared with using no cryoprotectant or with using 5% trehalose?

[0001] assistant:
Adding 10% skim milk to the cell suspension before lyophilization can improve post-drying survival and extend the shelf-life of freeze-dried Bacillus subtilis probiotics compared to using no cryoprotectant or 5% trehalose. Milk provides additional nutrients and a protective environment that can enhance the survival of the probiotics during and after freeze-drying.
