Zoonotic transmission plays a crucial role in the process of a virus reaching pandemic potential by allowing a virus to jump from animals to humans, potentially adapting to human hosts and spreading efficiently among them. This initial jump is often the critical step that enables a virus to establish itself in a human population and then spread globally.
