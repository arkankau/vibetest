Site-directed mutagenesis kits are generally effective for reverting mutations, but their success can vary depending on the specific kit and the complexity of the mutations. For reverting 40-50 dispersed synonymous mutations in a 6 kb plasmid, the kits can be effective, but there is a risk of introducing unwanted changes elsewhere in the plasmid.
