[0001] assistant:
Insect cell expression systems are generally more expensive to set up and maintain compared to microbial systems due to the need for more complex culture conditions and specialized equipment. However, they offer advantages like post-translational modifications that can be crucial for the function and stability of animal venom toxins, potentially making them more suitable despite higher costs.
