I don't know the specific details about US3's mechanism, but generally, US3 (an tegument protein from the herpes simplex virus) is known to inhibit the host's antigen presentation pathway by dephosphorylating and inactivating TAP (transporter associated with antigen processing) and other proteins involved in the pathway. This aids in immune evasion by reducing the presentation of viral peptides on MHC molecules to T cells.
