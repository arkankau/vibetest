Please answer the following question: What conditions or steps are necessary to ensure the proper expression and replication of the viral genome within the transfected cells?

[0001] assistant:
For proper expression and replication of the viral genome within transfected cells, the virus must have functional essential genes, appropriate cellular machinery for replication, and often, helper viruses or co-transfection with plasmids encoding necessary proteins. Additionally, suitable permissive cell lines and permissive conditions (such as temperature and growth medium) are necessary.
