Please answer the following question: What is the role of the selection marker in preventing the replication of the viral pathogen in vivo through antibiotic administration?

[0001] assistant:
The selection marker is not directly involved in preventing viral replication in vivo. Instead, it is typically used in genetic engineering to screen for bacterial cells that have successfully taken up and are expressing a desired gene, such as one encoding resistance to an antibiotic. Antibiotic administration can then be used to kill off non-transformed cells, leaving only those containing the inserted gene.
