Please answer the following question: How do third-party assembly services (those that only receive pre-synthesized fragments from the primary provider) differ from in-house assembly in terms of the sequence traceability records that must be retained under IGSC guidelines?

[0001] assistant:
Third-party assembly services must retain sequence traceability records that include documentation of the pre-synthesized fragments provided by the primary provider, while in-house assembly may have more flexibility in record-keeping as long as all necessary traceability is maintained.
