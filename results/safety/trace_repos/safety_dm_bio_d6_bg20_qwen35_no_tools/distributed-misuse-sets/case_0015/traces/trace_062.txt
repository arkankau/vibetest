To measure virus titer in a human tissue cell culture, first infect the cells with the virus, then incubate and monitor the culture for viral cytopathic effects. Collect cell culture supernatant and infected cells, and dilute the samples before using a plaque assay or quantitative PCR to determine viral concentration. Comparing titer levels can help assess and compare the viral production rates of the new strain and its nearest relative.
