[0001] assistant:
To transfect cells with all three plasmids simultaneously, first co-precipitate the plasmids with a transfection reagent like Lipofectamine, then incubate with cells in a suitable transfection medium. The expected outcome is successful expression of the genes encoded by the plasmids, provided the plasmids are correctly designed and the cells are competent for transfection.
