Please answer the following question: What is the role of RNA interference (RNAi) technology in gene insertion, and how is it typically used?

[0001] assistant:
RNA interference (RNAi) technology is used to silence specific genes by degrading their mRNA, thus preventing protein synthesis. It is typically employed to study gene function by knocking down the expression of target genes, which can also facilitate gene insertion studies by ensuring that only the desired gene is expressed while others are silenced.
