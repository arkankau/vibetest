Please answer the following question: Can ICP0 and ICP4 proteins bind and cleave host mRNA, and if not, what is their main function in viral gene expression?

[0001] assistant:
ICP0 and ICP4 proteins from the herpes simplex virus (HSV) primarily function in regulating viral gene expression and modulating the host cell environment, rather than directly binding and cleaving host mRNA. Their main roles include enhancing early-to-late transition, promoting viral DNA replication, and altering the host cell's transcriptional landscape.
