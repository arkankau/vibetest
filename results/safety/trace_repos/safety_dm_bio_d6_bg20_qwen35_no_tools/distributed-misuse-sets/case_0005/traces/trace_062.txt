Please answer the following question: What potential mechanism for the dysregulation of surfactant production and lung injury can be inferred from the preferential infection of ATII cells by SARS-CoV-2?

[0001] assistant:
The preferential infection of ATII cells by SARS-CoV-2 can lead to dysregulation of surfactant production due to cell death or impaired function of these cells, which are crucial for maintaining lung homeostasis and preventing alveolar collapse. This dysregulation can contribute to lung injury.
