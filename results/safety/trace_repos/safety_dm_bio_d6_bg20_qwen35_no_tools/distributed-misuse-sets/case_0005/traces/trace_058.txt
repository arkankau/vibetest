Please answer the following question: How does an infectious clone serve as a template for the synthesis of viral RNA in vitro in the context of reverse genetics?

[0001] assistant:
An infectious clone serves as a template for synthesizing viral RNA in vitro by containing the complete viral genome, including all necessary regulatory elements. When transcribed using reverse transcriptase, this template produces RNA that can infect cells and replicate, mimicking natural viral infection.
