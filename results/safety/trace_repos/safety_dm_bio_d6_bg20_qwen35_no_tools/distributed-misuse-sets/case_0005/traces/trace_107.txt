Please answer the following question: How does the vertebrate telomerase reverse transcriptase domain perform RNA-templated DNA synthesis, and what primer-template sequence requirements must be met for it to extend a 3′ OH in vivo?

[0001] assistant:
The vertebrate telomerase reverse transcriptase domain performs RNA-templated DNA synthesis by using its intrinsic reverse transcriptase activity to copy a telomeric repeat sequence from an RNA template (the telomerase RNA component) onto the 3' end of a telomere. For this process to extend a 3' OH in vivo, the RNA template must base-pair with the existing telomeric DNA sequence to provide a primer-template hybrid, and the 3' overhang of the DNA must be accessible for extension.
