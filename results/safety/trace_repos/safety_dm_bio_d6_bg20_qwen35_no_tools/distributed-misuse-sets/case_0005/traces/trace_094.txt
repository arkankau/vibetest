Please answer the following question: Can the HSV protein ICP4 facilitate viral entry into the host cell and, if so, what specific mechanisms are involved?

[0001] assistant:
HSV protein ICP4 does not directly facilitate viral entry into the host cell. ICP4 is primarily involved in regulating the transcription of late viral genes, promoting viral DNA replication, and enhancing the synthesis of viral proteins, but it is not a receptor or a protein that aids in the initial attachment or entry process.
