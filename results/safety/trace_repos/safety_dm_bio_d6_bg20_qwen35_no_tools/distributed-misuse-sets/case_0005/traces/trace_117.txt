Please answer the following question: What are the environmental conditions in CAFOs that may affect the prevalence or evolution of influenza viruses in Galliform species?

[0001] assistant:
CAFOs (Concentrated Animal Feeding Operations) for Galliform species can lead to high viral prevalence due to overcrowding, poor ventilation, and frequent stress among birds, which facilitate viral transmission and evolution. The close proximity and stress also promote genetic reassortment and mutation, increasing the risk of more virulent strains.
