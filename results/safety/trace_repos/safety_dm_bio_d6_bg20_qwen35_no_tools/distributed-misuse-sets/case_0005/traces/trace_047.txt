Please answer the following question: What is the role of ADP in cellular metabolism, and how does it interact with diphtheria toxin protein?

[0001] assistant:
ADP (adenosine diphosphate) serves as an energy carrier in cellular metabolism, facilitating energy transfer between reactions. Diphtheria toxin inactivates ADP-ribosyltransferase, leading to the ADP-ribosylation of elongation factor-2, which inhibits protein synthesis.
