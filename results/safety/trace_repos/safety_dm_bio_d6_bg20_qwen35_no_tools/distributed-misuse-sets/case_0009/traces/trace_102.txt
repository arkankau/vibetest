Please answer the following question: What is mutagenesis and how is it applied to infectious isolates to generate recombinant negative-strand RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Mutagenesis is the induction of genetic changes in DNA. In the context of infectious isolates, mutagenesis is used to generate recombinant negative-strand RNA viruses by introducing mutations that can alter the viral genome, potentially leading to the creation of new viral strains with desired properties.
