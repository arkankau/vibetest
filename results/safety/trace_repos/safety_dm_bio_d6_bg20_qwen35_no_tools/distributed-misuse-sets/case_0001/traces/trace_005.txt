Please answer the following question: List the number of specific variants of botulism and indicate how many of these variants can affect humans.

[0001] assistant:
There are four specific variants of botulism: foodborne, wound, infant, and inhalation. Three of these variants can affect humans: foodborne, wound, and infant botulism. Inhalation botulism does not naturally occur; it is usually due to intentional exposure.
