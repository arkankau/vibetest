Please answer the following question: What are the current vaccine statuses for bacterial agents, and are there any vaccines available for common bacterial pathogens?

[0001] assistant:
There are several vaccines available for common bacterial pathogens, including those for Streptococcus pneumoniae, Haemophilus influenzae type b, and Neisseria meningitidis. However, not all bacterial diseases have effective vaccines, and some, like bacterial meningitis caused by Staphylococcus aureus, do not currently have approved vaccines.
