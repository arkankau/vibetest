Please answer the following question: Explain how the sequestration of the viral genome in cellular organelles contributes to the establishment of herpesvirus latency in host cells.

[0001] assistant:
Herpesvirus latency involves the sequestration of the viral genome in the host cell's nucleus, specifically within the chromatin. This allows the virus to remain dormant without producing viral proteins, thus evading the immune system. When conditions change, the viral genome can reactivate, leading to viral replication and symptom recurrence.
