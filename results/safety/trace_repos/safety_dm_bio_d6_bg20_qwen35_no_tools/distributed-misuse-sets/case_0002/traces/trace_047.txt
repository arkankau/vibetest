Please answer the following question: Describe the role of Thymidine kinase in the life cycle of Herpes Simplex Virus Type 1 (HSV-1).

[0001] assistant:
Thymidine kinase is essential for HSV-1 as it converts deoxythymidine triphosphate into deoxythymidine monophosphate, a necessary step in DNA synthesis during viral replication.
