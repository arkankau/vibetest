Please answer the following question: Why is the long lifespan of pigs considered a potential factor in their role as 'mixing vessels' for IAVs?

[0001] assistant:
Pigs have a long lifespan and can co-infect with multiple swine, avian, and human influenza A viruses (IAVs), allowing for the potential for these viruses to reassort and generate new strains, making them a significant mixing vessel.
