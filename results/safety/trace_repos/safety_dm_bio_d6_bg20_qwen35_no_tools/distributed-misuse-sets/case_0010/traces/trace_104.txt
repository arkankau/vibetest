Please answer the following question: How does the utilization of 5'-methylated cap structures on mRNA influence the recognition and processing by RNA surveillance mechanisms?

[0001] assistant:
5'-methylated cap structures on mRNA protect the mRNA from degradation by RNA surveillance mechanisms, such as decapping enzymes. The methyl group on the cap can serve as a signal for the recognition and processing of mRNA by the cell, ensuring that only properly translated and stable mRNAs are used for protein synthesis.
