Please answer the following question: What are the characteristics of chimeric adenovirus vectors, and how do they differ from other generations?

[0001] assistant:
Chimeric adenovirus vectors are engineered by combining different serotypes of adenoviruses to reduce immune responses and improve safety. They differ from first-generation vectors by using a different adenovirus serotype and often including deletions in the E1 region to enhance transgene expression, while later generations further refine these characteristics to improve immunogenicity and vector stability.
