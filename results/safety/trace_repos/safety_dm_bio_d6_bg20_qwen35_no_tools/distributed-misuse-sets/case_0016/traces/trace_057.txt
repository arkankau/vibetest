Please answer the following question: Which cellular targets can adenoviral vectors efficiently transduce, and how does this relate to the delivery of miRNAs?

[0001] assistant:
Adenoviral vectors can efficiently transduce a wide range of non-dividing and dividing cells, including primary and transformed cell types. This broad tropism makes them suitable for delivering miRNAs, which can be packaged into the viral vector to modulate gene expression in various cell types.
