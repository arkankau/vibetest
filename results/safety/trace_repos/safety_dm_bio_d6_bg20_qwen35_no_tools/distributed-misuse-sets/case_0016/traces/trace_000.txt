Please answer the following question: How does an infectious clone function as a diagnostic tool for detecting the presence of a virus in patient samples in the context of reverse genetics?

An infectious clone functions as a diagnostic tool by allowing the replication of viral genomes, which can then be used to identify specific viral sequences in patient samples through techniques like PCR. If the viral genome is present in a sample, it will amplify, indicating the presence of the virus.
