Please answer the following question: What is the process of synthesizing proteins from mRNA templates using ribosomes, and is it relevant to introducing new genes into the Vaccinia virus genome?

[0001] assistant:
The process of synthesizing proteins from mRNA templates using ribosomes is called translation. This process is indeed relevant to introducing new genes into the Vaccinia virus genome, as it allows the virus to produce the proteins encoded by the new genes.
