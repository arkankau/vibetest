Please answer the following question: What was the primary goal of rescuing viruses from cloned DNA, and which virus is credited with the first successful implementation of this process?

[0001] assistant:
The primary goal of rescuing viruses from cloned DNA was to stabilize and propagate viruses that were difficult to culture, particularly retroviruses. The first successful implementation of this process is credited to Howard Martin Temin and David Baltimore, who worked with Rous sarcoma virus.
