[0001] assistant:
The hlyE gene in E. coli K12 is involved in the biosynthesis of hemolysin E, which is responsible for the hemolytic phenotype. Disruption or modification of hlyE expression, such as increased expression or deletion, is most likely to lead to a hemolytic phenotype due to elevated hemolysin production.
