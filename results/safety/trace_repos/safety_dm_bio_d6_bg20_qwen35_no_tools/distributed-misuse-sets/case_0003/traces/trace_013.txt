Please answer the following question: What is the primary mechanism by which herpesviruses can retain MHC class I molecules in the endoplasmic reticulum and how does this prevent their presentation on the cell surface?

[0001] assistant:
Herpesviruses achieve this through the expression of viral proteins like UL36 and UL41, which inhibit the proper transport of MHC class I molecules from the endoplasmic reticulum to the Golgi apparatus, thus trapping them in the endoplasmic reticulum and preventing their presentation on the cell surface.
