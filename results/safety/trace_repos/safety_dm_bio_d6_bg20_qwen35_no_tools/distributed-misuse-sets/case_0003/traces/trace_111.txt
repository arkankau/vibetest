Please answer the following question: Which promoters or enhancer sequences have been experimentally validated to confer strong, larval-specific expression of transgenes in Musca domestica, and what methods are typically used to confirm that the adults derived from these larvae show little or no transgene product?

[0001] assistant:
For Musca domestica, the larval-specific promoter from the heat shock protein 70 (hsp70) gene has been experimentally validated to confer strong, larval-specific expression of transgenes. Adult flies derived from these larvae typically show little or no transgene product, as the hsp70 promoter is specifically active during the larval stage. Confirmation of this is usually achieved through qPCR, Western blotting, and immunostaining to detect the transgene product in larvae and adults.
