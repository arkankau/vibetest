[0001] assistant:
The biological role of the rotavirus VP7 protein is to mediate attachment to the host cell and contribute to the virus's capsid structure. The corresponding gene segment is not typically part of the standard panel used for quantitative RT-PCR identification in clinical labs, as the VP7 gene is usually not the primary target for routine diagnostics.
