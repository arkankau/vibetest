Please answer the following question: How does the HA2 subunit contribute to the assembly and budding of influenza A viruses from infected cells?

[0001] assistant:
The HA2 subunit contributes to the assembly and budding of influenza A viruses by forming the viral envelope and interacting with the matrix protein M1, which helps in bringing the viral RNA into the budding vesicle. Additionally, HA2 facilitates the fusion of the viral envelope with the cell membrane, ensuring the release of new virions.
