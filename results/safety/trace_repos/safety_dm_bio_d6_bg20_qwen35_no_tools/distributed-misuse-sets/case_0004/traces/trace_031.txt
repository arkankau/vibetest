Please answer the following question: What are the known consequences of K27-/K29-linked ubiquitination on protein stability and degradation pathways?

[0001] assistant:
K27- and K29-linked ubiquitination typically destabilize proteins by targeting them for proteasomal degradation, leading to decreased protein stability. This form of ubiquitination often signifies a more rapid and specific degradation pathway compared to linear ubiquitination.
