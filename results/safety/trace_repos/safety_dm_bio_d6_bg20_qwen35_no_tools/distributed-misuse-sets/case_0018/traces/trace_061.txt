Please answer the following question: How does the charge of aspartic acid (D) compare to that of glutamic acid (E) at different pH levels?

[0001] assistant:
At low pH, both aspartic acid (D) and glutamic acid (E) are fully protonated and carry a positive charge. At high pH, both are deprotonated and carry a negative charge. The charge of aspartic acid (D) and glutamic acid (E) is more negative at physiological pH (around 7.4) due to their additional acidic side chain.
