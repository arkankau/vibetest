Please answer the following question: In what way can in vitro selection of SARS-CoV-2 with neutralizing antibodies help identify resistant viral mutants, and what are the advantages and disadvantages of this method?

[0001] assistant:
In vitro selection of SARS-CoV-2 with neutralizing antibodies can identify viral mutants that have developed resistance by selecting for those that can still evade the antibodies. Advantages include rapid identification of resistant mutants and the ability to screen large numbers of viral populations. Disadvantages include potential limitations in capturing all types of resistance mechanisms and the need for specific neutralizing antibodies.
