Please answer the following question: What is the role of CD4+ T helper cells in supporting the immune response to Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) and how do they contribute to controlling lytic replication?

[0001] assistant:
CD4+ T helper cells support the immune response to EBV by providing cytokine signals that help activate B cells to produce antibodies against EBV antigens. They also directly control lytic replication by secreting cytokines that induce infected cells to undergo apoptosis, thereby limiting viral spread.
