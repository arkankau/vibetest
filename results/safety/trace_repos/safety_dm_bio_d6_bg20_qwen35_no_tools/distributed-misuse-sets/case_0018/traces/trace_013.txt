Socioeconomic status and access to healthcare can impact pathogen transmission by influencing factors such as hygiene, nutrition, and the ability to seek and receive early treatment, which can reduce infectious periods and limit person-to-person spread. Poorer socioeconomic status often correlates with less access to clean water, sanitation, and healthcare, thereby increasing the likelihood of both animal-to-human and human-to-human transmission.
