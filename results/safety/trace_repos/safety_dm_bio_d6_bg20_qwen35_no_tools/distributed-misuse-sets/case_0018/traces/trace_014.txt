Please answer the following question: What is the role of immediate-early (IE) genes in the initial stage of cytomegalovirus (CMV) infection and how do they contribute to the rapid activation of the viral genome?

[0001] assistant:
Immediate-early (IE) genes in cytomegalovirus (CMV) infection are among the first to be expressed upon viral entry. They rapidly activate the viral genome by promoting transcription and facilitating the expression of subsequent viral genes, thereby initiating the viral replication cycle.
