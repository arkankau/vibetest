Please answer the following question: In the house fly Musca domestica, which endogenous promoters are known to activate transcription specifically during the larval stage but remain largely silent in adults, and how could such promoters be used to restrict transgene expression to larvae?

[0001] assistant:
In the house fly Musca domestica, endogenous promoters like the heat shock protein 70 (hsp70) promoter and the cuticle protein 45 (cp45) promoter are known to activate transcription specifically during the larval stage but remain largely silent in adults. These promoters can be used to restrict transgene expression to larvae by driving the expression of the transgene under the control of these stage-specific promoters.
