2. **`RelatedOnlyFieldListFilter.field_choices`** - Now passes `ordering` kwarg (using the same `field_admin_ordering` method) to `field.get_choices()`.

3. **`Field.get_choices` and `ForeignObjectRel.get_choices`** - Both now fall back to the related model's `_meta.ordering` when no explicit ordering is provided (i.e., when `ordering=()` default).

Files modified:
- `django/contrib/admin/filters.py` - Added `field_admin_ordering` method to `RelatedFieldListFilter`, updated both `field_choices` methods
- `django/db/models/fields/__init__.py` - `get_choices` falls back to `_meta.ordering` 
- `django/db/models/fields/reverse_related.py` - `get_choices` falls back to `_meta.ordering`
