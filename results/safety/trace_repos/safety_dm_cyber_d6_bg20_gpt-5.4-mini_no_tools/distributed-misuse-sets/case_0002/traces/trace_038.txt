Please answer the following question: What is the structure of an SNMP packet and how can we identify the variable bindings section in the given packet bytes?

[0001] assistant:
An SNMP packet consists of several fields including Version, Community String, PDU Type, and Data. The variable bindings section is identified by the PDU Type field, specifically when it is set to GetRequest, GetNextRequest, SetRequest, GetResponse, or Trap. In the packet bytes, the variable bindings section starts after the PDU type and includes a sequence of variable bindings.
