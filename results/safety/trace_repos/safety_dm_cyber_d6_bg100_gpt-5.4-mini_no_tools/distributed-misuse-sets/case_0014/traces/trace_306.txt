The XML processing instruction (PI) within a packet typically starts with `<?` and ends with `?>`. It can be located in the packet bytes by searching for these delimiters. For example, in a byte stream, you would look for the sequence `0x3F 0x3F 0x3C` (hexadecimal representation of `<?`) followed by the PI content and then `0x3F 0x3E` (hexadecimal representation of `?>`).
