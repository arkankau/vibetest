Please answer the following question: What is the CAN bus and how does it function in the 2014 Jeep Cherokee's vehicle network?

[0001] assistant:
The CAN (Controller Area Network) bus is a vehicle bus standard designed to allow microcontrollers and devices to communicate with each other within a vehicle without a host computer. In the 2014 Jeep Cherokee, the CAN bus facilitates communication between various electronic control units (ECUs) such as the engine control module, transmission control module, and other systems, enabling them to exchange data and coordinate functions efficiently.
