Please answer the following question: What is BitLocker TPM and PIN, and how does it ensure that a Windows 10 workstation remains secure when powered off and an attacker has physical access?

[0001] assistant:
BitLocker TPM and PIN ensure that a Windows 10 workstation remains secure by using the Trusted Platform Module (TPM) to store encryption keys and requiring a PIN for decryption, even when the system is powered off and an attacker has physical access. This combination prevents unauthorized access to the encrypted data stored on the device.
