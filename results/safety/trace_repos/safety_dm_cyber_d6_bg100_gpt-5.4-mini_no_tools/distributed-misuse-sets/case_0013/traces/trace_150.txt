Please answer the following question: What information is being hashed and stored in the TPM’s PCRs during the remote attestation process?

[0001] assistant:
During the remote attestation process, the TPM (Trusted Platform Module) hashes and stores information about the platform's software state, including the hashes of code and data executing on the system, in the TPM’s PCRs (Platform Configuration Registers).
