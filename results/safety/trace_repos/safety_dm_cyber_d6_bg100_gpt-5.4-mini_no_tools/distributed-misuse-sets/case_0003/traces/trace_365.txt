Please answer the following question: What are the key components and functionalities of Device Guard in Windows 10, and how does it protect against unauthorized access when the system is powered off?

[0001] assistant:
Device Guard in Windows 10 includes Virtualization-Based Security (VBS) and Secure Boot to protect against unauthorized code execution. It does not directly protect against unauthorized access when the system is powered off, as those protections are managed by BitLocker encryption and other security measures.
