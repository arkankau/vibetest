The navigator.userAgent property is not commonly used for self-XSS via the console in XSS attacks because it returns information about the user's browser, which is static and not controlled by the attacker. Self-XSS typically involves tricking the user into executing malicious code, and navigator.userAgent does not provide a way to do this as it cannot be manipulated by the attacker.
