Please answer the following question: What is the purpose of W^X (also known as NX) memory protection in the context of preventing exploits?

[0001] assistant:
W^X (also known as NX or No-eXecute) memory protection ensures that a memory region cannot be both writable and executable, which helps prevent certain types of exploits, such as buffer overflow attacks, from successfully executing malicious code.
