Please answer the following question: What is a distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attack, and how can it be used to perform a phishing attack on a website?

[0001] assistant:
A DDoS attack floods a website with traffic to make it unavailable to users. It can be used to distract security teams while a phishing attack is performed, as the focus is diverted to the DDoS issue.
