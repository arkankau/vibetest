[0001] assistant:
Wiper malware is a type of malicious software designed to erase data from infected systems, often leaving no trace of the files that were deleted. It can cause significant impact on critical infrastructure by destroying essential data, rendering systems inoperable, and disrupting services, all of which can lead to severe operational and financial consequences.
