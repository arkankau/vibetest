Please answer the following question: What is a man-in-the-middle attack and how can a stateful firewall prevent it?

[0001] assistant:
A man-in-the-middle attack involves an attacker secretly intercepting and relaying communications between two parties to steal information or alter the communication. A stateful firewall can prevent this by inspecting the state of network connections and ensuring that traffic conforms to established protocols, thus blocking unauthorized interceptions.
