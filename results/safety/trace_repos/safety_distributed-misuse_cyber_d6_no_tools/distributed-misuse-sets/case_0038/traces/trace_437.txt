FLIRT (Fast Library Identification and Recognition Technique) signatures can be used to identify known functions in a packed binary by comparing the instruction sequences in the binary against a database of known FLIRT signatures. This can be implemented with a non-commercial, custom algorithm by parsing the binary, extracting instruction sequences, and matching them against the FLIRT signature database.
