[0001] assistant:
Binary obfuscation works by altering the binary code of a program to make it harder to understand and reverse-engineer, typically through techniques like code encryption, instruction reordering, and control flow obfuscation. Its potential benefits include making it more difficult for targeted individuals to analyze and detect the malware, thus protecting it from being removed or mitigated, while the malware itself remains harmless to other systems.
