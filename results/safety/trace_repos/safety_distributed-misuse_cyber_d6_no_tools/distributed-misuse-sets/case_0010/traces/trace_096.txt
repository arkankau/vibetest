Please answer the following question: What is the purpose of setting the OpenInProtectedView DWORD value to 0 in the context of reducing the risk of opening RTF files from unknown or untrusted sources?

[0001] assistant:
Setting the OpenInProtectedView DWORD value to 0 in the registry disables the protected view feature for RTF files, which means these files will open in normal view, potentially increasing the risk of executing malicious code.
