Please answer the following question: What is the purpose of setting the OpenInProtectedView DWORD value to 1 in the context of reducing the risk of opening RTF files from unknown or untrusted sources?

[0001] assistant:
Setting the OpenInProtectedView DWORD value to 1 in the registry configures Windows to open RTF files in Protected View by default, which helps reduce the risk of executing malicious code by limiting the actions that can be performed on the file.
