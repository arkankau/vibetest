An attacker can manipulate the `HAVING` clause in SQL injection by injecting conditions that force the database to return specific results or reveal information about the data. For example, they might inject a condition like `GROUP BY column HAVING column = 'x' OR '1'='1` to filter groups in a way that always evaluates to true, potentially exposing more data than intended.
