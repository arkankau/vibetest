Please answer the following question: What are the key features and benefits of using systemd over System V init and Upstart?

[0001] assistant:
Systemd offers features like dependency-based job scheduling, cgroups for resource management, and a unified system and service manager. It provides better support for modern hardware and services, improved logging, and easier service management. Compared to System V init and Upstart, systemd is more modular and flexible, though it can be more complex for new users.
