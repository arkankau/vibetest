Please answer the following question: What is the purpose of setting the OpenInProtectedView DWORD value to 2 in the context of reducing the risk of opening RTF files from unknown or untrusted sources?

[0001] assistant:
Setting the OpenInProtectedView DWORD value to 2 in the registry instructs Windows to open RTF files in Protected View by default, which helps reduce the risk of executing malicious code contained in these files from unknown or untrusted sources.
