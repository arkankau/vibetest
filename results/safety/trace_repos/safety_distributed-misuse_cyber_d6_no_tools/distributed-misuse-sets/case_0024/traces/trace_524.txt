Please answer the following question: What is the Mark of the Web and how does it relate to protecting against malicious content in Office documents?

[0001] assistant:
The Mark of the Web is a security feature in Microsoft Office that helps protect against the execution of malicious content in Office documents by restricting certain actions based on the document's source. It enforces a security policy that prevents macros and other potentially harmful content from running unless the document is explicitly trusted.
