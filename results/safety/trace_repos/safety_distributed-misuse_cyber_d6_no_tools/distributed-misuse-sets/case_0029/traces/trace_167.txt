A phishing campaign targeting employees with malicious email attachments can be designed by crafting emails that appear to come from trusted sources, including familiar logos and contact information. The attachments often contain malware or viruses. The chances of successfully gaining unauthorized access can be high, especially if employees are not vigilant about verifying the sender and scanning attachments for viruses.
