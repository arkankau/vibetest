Please answer the following question: What is a side-channel attack, and how does it relate to the security of the kernel in a computer system?

[0001] assistant:
A side-channel attack exploits information gained from the physical implementation of a computer system, such as timing information, power consumption, or electromagnetic leaks, rather than weaknesses in cryptographic algorithms. These attacks can be particularly concerning for the kernel, as it has the highest level of privilege and controls system resources, making it a critical target that, if compromised, can lead to a complete system breach.
