Please answer the following question: What is the function of the PACIA instruction in relation to Pointer Authentication Codes?

[0001] assistant:
The PACIA (Pointer Authentication Instruction Assist) function is used to generate and verify Pointer Authentication Codes (PACs) to enhance pointer integrity and prevent certain types of attacks, such as return-oriented programming (ROP) and jump-oriented programming (JOP) attacks.
