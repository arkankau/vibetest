[0001] assistant:
In the context of the Metasploit Framework, a Beacon is a background process that allows a Meterpreter session to maintain a low profile and evade detection. It relates to the use of `windows/meterpreter/reverse_http[s]` by establishing a persistent HTTP(S) listener that the payload can connect to, enabling the session to remain active even if the initial connection is interrupted.
