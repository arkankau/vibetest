Please answer the following question: How do synchronization primitives help in eliminating the race window of vulnerability for concurrent processes/threads?

[0001] assistant:
Synchronization primitives, such as semaphores, mutexes, and monitors, help eliminate the race window by ensuring that only one process or thread can access a shared resource at a time. This is achieved through mechanisms like locking and unlocking, which prevent multiple threads from entering critical sections simultaneously, thus avoiding race conditions.
