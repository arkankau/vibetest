Cobalt Strike recommends handling in-memory artifacts with care to maintain operational security (OPSEC). Specifically, it advises against leaving traces of in-memory operations, such as temporary files or network connections, which could be detected by monitoring tools. This is crucial to avoid detection and ensure the stealth of the operation.
