Please answer the following question: Under what conditions can the DNS Beacon's data channel change automatically based on network conditions in Cobalt Strike?

[0001] assistant:
The DNS Beacon's data channel in Cobalt Strike can change automatically based on network conditions when the "Adaptive Beaconing" feature is enabled. This feature allows the beacon to switch to a different data channel (such as HTTPS or DNS) if the current one is blocked or monitored.
