RSA ciphertexts are valid under the same key because they are encrypted using the recipient's public key, which corresponds to their private key for decryption. The given options adhere to these properties by ensuring that the ciphertext can be decrypted using the corresponding private key, maintaining the integrity and confidentiality of the message.
