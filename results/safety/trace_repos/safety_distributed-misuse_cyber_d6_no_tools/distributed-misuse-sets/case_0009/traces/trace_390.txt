Please answer the following question: What is pretexting and how does it differ from other forms of social engineering?

[0001] assistant:
Pretexting is a social engineering technique where an attacker creates a fictional scenario or identity to trick individuals into divulging confidential information. It differs from other forms of social engineering in that it relies on crafting a specific, believable scenario to gain the victim's trust, whereas other methods might involve general manipulation or phishing attacks.
