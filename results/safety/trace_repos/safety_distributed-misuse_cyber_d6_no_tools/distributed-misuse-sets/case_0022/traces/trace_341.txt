Please answer the following question: Is Shamir Secret Sharing (SSS) more complicated to implement compared to Multi-Party Computation (MPC), and why?

[0001] assistant:
Shamir Secret Sharing (SSS) is generally considered less complex to implement than Multi-Party Computation (MPC) because SSS focuses on threshold secret sharing without the need for secure function evaluation among multiple parties. MPC involves more intricate protocols to compute functions on secret inputs without revealing those inputs, making it more complex.
