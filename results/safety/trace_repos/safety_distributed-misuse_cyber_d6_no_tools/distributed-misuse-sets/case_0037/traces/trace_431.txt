Please answer the following question: What are the flags in the packet and what do they represent in the context of a TCP packet?

[0001] assistant:
In a TCP packet, the flags represent various control bits: the SYN flag is used for initiating a connection, the ACK flag indicates an acknowledgment has been received, the FIN flag signals the end of data transmission in a half-open connection, the RST flag resets the connection, and the PUSH flag requests that data be delivered to the application immediately.
