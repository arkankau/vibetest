Please answer the following question: What are the typical scenarios in which the `pass-the-hash` attack is used for credential exploitation?

[0001] assistant:
The `pass-the-hash` attack is typically used in scenarios where an attacker gains access to a system and can extract the NTLM hash of a user's password, allowing them to authenticate as that user without knowing the actual password. This is often exploited in environments with weak security practices or during lateral movement in a network.
