A Denial-of-Service (DoS) attack floods a system with traffic, overwhelming its resources and preventing legitimate users from accessing it. In the context of a smart home system, a DoS attack can flood the network with excessive requests, potentially disrupting the system's functionality and creating an opportunity for unauthorized access during the disruption.
