[0001] assistant:
Differential privacy can be applied to both aggregate and individual data, providing strong guarantees of privacy even for individual records. In contrast, de-identification methods are generally less effective for individual data and focus more on aggregate data, offering fewer guarantees and being more susceptible to re-identification attacks.
