Please answer the following question: What is a Denial of Service (DoS) attack and how does it differ from an attack that can guess a hashed password?

[0001] assistant:
A Denial of Service (DoS) attack aims to make a machine or network resource unavailable by overwhelming it with a flood of requests, thereby crashing or slowing down the service. Guessing a hashed password involves attempting to crack the hash through brute force or other methods, whereas a DoS attack does not attempt to access the system but rather to disable it by consuming its resources.
