Please answer the following question: How does differential privacy handle the trade-off between privacy and utility, and how does this differ from de-identification methods?

[0001] assistant:
Differential privacy adds noise to the data to protect individual privacy, balancing utility and privacy by ensuring that the output of a query is similar whether or not any individual's data is included. De-identification methods, in contrast, aim to remove or anonymize personally identifiable information, but may not guarantee privacy as effectively, as re-identification attacks are possible.
