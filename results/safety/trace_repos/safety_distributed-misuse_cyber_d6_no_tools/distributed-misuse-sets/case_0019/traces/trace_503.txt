Please answer the following question: Outline the effects of the Poodle vulnerability on the security of SSL/TLS protocols and the measures taken to mitigate its impact.

[0001] assistant:
The Poodle vulnerability exploited the use of 1024-bit export-grade encryption in SSL 3.0, allowing attackers to perform a man-in-the-middle attack. To mitigate this, SSL 3.0 was deprecated, and TLS 1.2 and later versions were recommended.
