[0001] assistant:
OS Fingerprinting is primarily used for identifying the operating system of a remote host and does not inherently target individuals or obfuscate malware. Binary Obfuscation can make malware harder to analyze but does not specifically target individuals. Environmental Keying involves the malware altering its behavior based on the target environment, which can be used to target specific systems. Network Fingerprinting is used to identify network equipment and does not target individuals or obfuscate malware.
