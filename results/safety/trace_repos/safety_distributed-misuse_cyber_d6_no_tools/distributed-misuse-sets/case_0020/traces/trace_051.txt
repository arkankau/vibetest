Please answer the following question: What is steganography, and how can it be employed to hide malicious content within email attachments for an email spoofing attack?

[0001] assistant:
Steganography is the practice of hiding secret data within an ordinary, non-secret file or message to avoid detection. In the context of email spoofing, malicious content can be hidden within seemingly harmless files attached to emails, making it difficult for recipients to identify the threat.
