[0001] assistant:
DNS Beaconing in Cobalt Strike is a technique used for maintaining a low-profile communication channel between a compromised host and the attacker. It works by periodically sending DNS requests from the compromised host to a command and control server, which can be used to confirm the presence of the malware and receive further instructions.
