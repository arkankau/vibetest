Please answer the following question: What is the `pass-the-hash` attack and how does it differ from other credential-based attacks?

[0001] assistant:
The `pass-the-hash` attack involves stealing a user's hashed credentials and using them to authenticate as that user to a different system or service without needing to know the plaintext password. It differs from other credential-based attacks because it does not require the attacker to crack or obtain the actual password; instead, it leverages the existing hashed credentials directly.
