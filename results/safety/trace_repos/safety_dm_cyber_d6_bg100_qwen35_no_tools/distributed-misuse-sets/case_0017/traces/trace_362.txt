[0001] assistant:
The x86 assembly instruction `÷` (division) operates on the EAX register. Specifically, it divides the 32-bit value in EAX by the value in the `CL` register (which is effectively `CX` if the instruction is `DIV CX`). The quotient is stored in EAX, and the remainder is stored in the `EDX` register.
