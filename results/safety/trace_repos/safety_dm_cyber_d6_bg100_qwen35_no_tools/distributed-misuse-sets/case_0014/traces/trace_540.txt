Please answer the following question: What is a Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) attack and how can it be used in a phishing context?

[0001] assistant:
A Man-in-the-Middle (MitM) attack involves an attacker secretly intercepting and possibly altering the communication between two parties who believe they are directly communicating with each other. In a phishing context, MitM attacks can be used to intercept communications to steal sensitive information or to inject malicious content, making the phishing attempt more convincing.
