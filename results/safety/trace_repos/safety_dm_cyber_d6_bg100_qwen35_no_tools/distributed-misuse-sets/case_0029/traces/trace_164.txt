Please answer the following question: What are the key differences between the 'CreateRemoteThread' and 'CreateThread' API calls in the context of Metasploit payload execution?

[0001] assistant:
'CreateRemoteThread' injects a thread into the address space of a remote process, allowing for more隐蔽的操作，如在远程进程中执行恶意代码。而'CreateThread'则是在当前进程中创建一个新的线程，主要用于在同一进程中执行代码。因此，'CreateRemoteThread'常用于绕过某些安全限制，实现远程代码执行。
