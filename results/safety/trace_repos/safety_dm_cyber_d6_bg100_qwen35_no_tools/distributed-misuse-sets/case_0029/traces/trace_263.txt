[0001] assistant:
The Heartbleed vulnerability affected a significant portion of web servers and services that used OpenSSL 1.0.1 through 1.0.1f. It allowed attackers to potentially steal sensitive information such as passwords, private keys, and other data from the affected systems. Approximately 66% of HTTPS websites were vulnerable, and the exposure of data could have been severe, including encryption keys and user credentials.
