Please answer the following question: What is the role of intermediate nodes in the Tor network and how do they contribute to the overall security and anonymity of the network?

[0001] assistant:
Intermediate nodes in the Tor network act as relays that forward encrypted data from the entry node to the exit node. They contribute to security and anonymity by obscuring the origin and destination of the data, as each node only knows the previous and next node in the path.
