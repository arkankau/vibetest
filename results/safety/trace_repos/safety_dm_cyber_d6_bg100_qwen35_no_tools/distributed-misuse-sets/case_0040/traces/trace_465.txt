Real-time operating systems (RTOS) often employ specific mitigations such as bounds checking, null pointer validation, and memory safety features like ASLR and DEP. These help reduce the exploitability of NULL-pointer dereference bugs by preventing unauthorized access to memory and making it harder to predict or control the system's behavior.
