Please answer the following question: Is the TCB defined as the portion of the system that you have reason to trust, based on it being audited or formally verified?

[0001] assistant:
Yes, the TCB (Trusted Computing Base) is defined as the portion of a computer system that is responsible for enforcing the security policy. It includes all the hardware, software, and firmware components that you have reason to trust, typically based on audit or formal verification.
