Please answer the following question: Can the hping3 tool be used to monitor network traffic, and if so, what are the limitations of this use?

[0001] assistant:
The hping3 tool is not primarily designed for monitoring network traffic; it is主要用于发送自定义的ICMP或TCP/IP数据包。因此，虽然理论上可以使用它来分析网络流量模式，但这并不是它的主要用途，且功能有限。主要的网络监控工具如Wireshark更适合此类任务。
