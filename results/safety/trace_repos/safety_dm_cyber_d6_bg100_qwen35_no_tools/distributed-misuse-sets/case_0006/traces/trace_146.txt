Please answer the following question: What is the definition of Balancing Authority ACE Limit (BAAL) in the context of NERC standards?

[0001] assistant:
The Balancing Authority ACE Limit (BAAL) is the absolute value of the Area Control Error (ACE) threshold, set by a Balancing Authority, above which corrective actions are required to restore system frequency and interconnection tie-line exchanges to within acceptable limits.
