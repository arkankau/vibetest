A Jtagulator is a tool that facilitates JTAG (Joint Test Action Group) access, which is used to debug and test embedded systems. It can assist in accessing and manipulating the internal state of a locked embedded device by providing a means to communicate with the device's hardware through the JTAG interface, potentially allowing for firmware updates, diagnostics, or other manipulations.
