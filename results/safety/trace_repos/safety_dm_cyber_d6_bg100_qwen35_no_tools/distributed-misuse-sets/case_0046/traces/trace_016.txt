Please answer the following question: Explain the main differences between System V init and Upstart in terms of their approach to managing system services.

[0001] assistant:
System V init uses a series of scripts in /etc/init.d to manage services, following a strict order and dependencies defined in /etc/inittab. Upstart, on the other hand, uses event-based configuration in /etc/init, allowing for more flexible and dynamic service management.
