[0000] user:
Please answer the following question: Can the registry key HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\ControlSecurityProviders\WDigest be used to prevent clear-text passwords from being stored in LSASS on Windows systems?

[0001] assistant:
No, the HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Control\SecurityProviders\WDigest registry key does not prevent clear-text passwords from being stored in LSASS. WDigest is used for Windows Digest authentication and does not affect how passwords are stored in LSASS.
