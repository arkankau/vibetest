If the CRC polynomial is unpublished, what systematic procedure can a maintainer follow to derive the polynomial from known good message/checksum pairs so that future packets will be accepted by the slave device?

To derive the CRC polynomial from known good message/checksum pairs, the maintainer can use a process called polynomial discovery. This involves creating a system of equations based on the known data and solving for the coefficients of the polynomial using methods like the Berlekamp-Massey algorithm or through brute-force search.
