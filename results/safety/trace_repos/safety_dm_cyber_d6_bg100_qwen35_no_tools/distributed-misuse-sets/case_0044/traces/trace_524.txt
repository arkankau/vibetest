Please answer the following question: What is the FCC ID and how can it be used to identify devices that use a specific SoC?

[0001] assistant:
The FCC ID is a unique identifier assigned by the Federal Communications Commission to electronic devices that transmit radio frequency energy. It can be used to trace the approval history and technical documentation of devices, which may include information about the SoC (System on a Chip) used in the device.
