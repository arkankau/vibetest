[0001] assistant:
The technique involves preventing access to servers by flooding them with data to overwhelm their resources, which is known as a DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service) attack. While DDoS attacks are not specific to ransomware, they can be used in conjunction with ransomware attacks to extort victims.
