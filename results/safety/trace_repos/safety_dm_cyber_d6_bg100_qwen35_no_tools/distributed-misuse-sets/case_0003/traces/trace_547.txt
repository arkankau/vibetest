[0001] assistant:
In differential privacy, the privacy parameter epsilon (ε) controls the amount of noise added to the data, determining the trade-off between privacy protection and utility of the released data. A smaller ε value means more noise is added, providing stronger privacy guarantees but potentially reducing the utility of the data.
