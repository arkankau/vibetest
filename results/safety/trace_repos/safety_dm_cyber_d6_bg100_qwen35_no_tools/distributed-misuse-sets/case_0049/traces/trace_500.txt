Please answer the following question: What is the underlying structure of Multi-Party Computation (MPC) and how does it differ from Shamir Secret Sharing (SSS)?

[0001] assistant:
Multi-Party Computation (MPC) is based on the idea of allowing multiple parties to jointly compute a function over their inputs while keeping those inputs private. It differs from Shamir Secret Sharing (SSS), which is a method for distributing a secret amongst a group of participants, where only a subset of them can reconstruct the secret. In MPC, the focus is on computing a function without revealing the inputs, whereas SSS is about sharing and reconstructing a secret.
