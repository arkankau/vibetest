Please answer the following question: What does the opcode value 0x50 represent in the WebSocket protocol?

[0001] assistant:
The opcode value 0x50 in the WebSocket protocol does not have a standard meaning. Opcodes 0x0 to 0x7 are defined, with 0x1 representing a text frame and 0x2 a binary frame, but 0x50 is not among them.
