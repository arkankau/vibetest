Please answer the following question: Describe the process of MAC address cloning and its role in concealing the identity of a device during a cyber attack.

[0001] assistant:
MAC address cloning involves a device temporarily adopting the MAC address of another device on the same network, allowing it to masquerade as that device. This can be used to conceal the true identity of an attacker's device during a cyber attack, as network traffic appears to come from a trusted or less suspicious source.
