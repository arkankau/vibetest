Please answer the following question: Does IDA Pro require manual generation of FLIRT signatures, and if so, what are the advantages and disadvantages of this process compared to Binary Ninja?

[0001] assistant:
IDA Pro requires manual generation of FLIRT signatures, which can be time-consuming and error-prone. The advantage is that it gives users precise control over the signatures. The disadvantage is the manual effort required, which can be a significant drawback for large binaries. Binary Ninja, on the other hand, automatically generates signatures, reducing the manual effort but may sometimes generate less accurate results.
