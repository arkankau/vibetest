Please answer the following question: When anomaly-based intrusion detection systems inspect Modbus/TCP traffic, which individual header fields are most commonly ignored or receive only superficial checks, and why?

[0001] assistant:
When anomaly-based intrusion detection systems inspect Modbus/TCP traffic, they often ignore or perform only superficial checks on the "Transaction Identifier" and "Protocol Identifier" fields. This is because these fields are less likely to vary between legitimate transactions and thus do not provide significant anomaly detection value.
