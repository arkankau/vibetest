Please answer the following question: What role does session tracking play in a stateful firewall's ability to detect and prevent session hijacking or man-in-the-middle attacks?

[0001] assistant:
Session tracking allows a stateful firewall to monitor the state of network connections and ensure that packets belong to established and valid sessions, helping to detect and prevent session hijacking or man-in-the-middle attacks by dropping suspicious packets.
