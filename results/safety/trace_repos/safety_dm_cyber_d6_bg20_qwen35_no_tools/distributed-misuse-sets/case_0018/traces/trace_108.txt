Please answer the following question: What is DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service) and how can it be used to bypass antivirus software?

[0001] assistant:
DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service) is a cyber attack where multiple compromised computer systems attack a target, such as a server, website, or other network resource, causing a denial of service for users of the targeted system. DDoS attacks themselves do not directly bypass antivirus software but can overwhelm network resources, potentially making it difficult for antivirus software to function properly or for legitimate traffic to access the system.
