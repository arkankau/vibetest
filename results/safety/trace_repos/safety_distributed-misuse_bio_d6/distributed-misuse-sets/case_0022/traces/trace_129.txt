Please answer the following question: In what way does the Epstein-barr virus (viral) IL-10 homologue specifically contribute to the evasion of immune clearance, and how does this differ from the traditional IL-10?

[0001] assistant:
The Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) IL-10 homologue contributes to the evasion of immune clearance by modulating the host immune response, specifically by inhibiting the activation of natural killer (NK) cells and T cells. This differs from the traditional IL-10, which has a broader range of effects including anti-inflammatory and immunosuppressive functions that target multiple cell types and pathways.
