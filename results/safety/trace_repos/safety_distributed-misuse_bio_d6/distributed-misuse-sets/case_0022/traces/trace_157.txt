Please answer the following question: Which specific type of human endothelial cells are predominantly found in the liver and known to be targeted by flaviviruses?

[0001] assistant:
Kupffer cells, a type of macrophage, are not the primary endothelial cells in the liver targeted by flaviviruses. The specific type of human endothelial cells predominantly found in the liver and known to be targeted by flaviviruses are hepatic sinusoidal endothelial cells.
