Please answer the following question: How does the Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) contribute to the pathogenesis of malignancies, and what is the significance of LMP1 in this context?

[0001] assistant:
EBV contributes to malignancy through chronic infection and the expression of oncogenes like LMP1. LMP1 acts as an active receptor that mimics signals required for cell growth and survival, promoting tumor cell proliferation and evasion of immune surveillance.
