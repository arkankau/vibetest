Please answer the following question: What are the notable virulence factors of the B. anthracis strain Ames?

[0001] assistant:
The notable virulence factors of the B. anthracis strain Ames include the production of anthrax toxin, which consists of edema factor, lethal factor, and protective antigen, as well as the capsule, which provides protection against phagocytosis.
