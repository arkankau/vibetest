Please answer the following question: Describe the process of using psoralen-inactivated viruses for generating fusions of fluorescent proteins to VP16.

[0001] assistant:
Psoralen-inactivated viruses are first treated with psoralen and UV light to inactivate them. These inactivated viruses are then used to transfect cells, where they express a fusion protein consisting of VP16 and a fluorescent protein.
