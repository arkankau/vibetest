Please answer the following question: How is Tularemia (F. tularensis) typically transmitted between individuals?

[0001] assistant:
Tularemia (F. tularensis) is typically not directly transmitted person-to-person. It is usually transmitted through bites from infected ticks or deer flies, contact with infected animals or their carcasses, or through respiratory infection from inhaling contaminated dust or aerosols.
