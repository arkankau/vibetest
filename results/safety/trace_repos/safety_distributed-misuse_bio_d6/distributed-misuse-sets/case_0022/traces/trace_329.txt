Please answer the following question: How do NK cells contribute to the immune surveillance against Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) infected cells and what is the mechanism of this surveillance?

[0001] assistant:
NK cells contribute to immune surveillance against EBV-infected cells by recognizing and destroying these cells through the release of cytotoxic granules and producing cytokines. This is mediated by the interaction of NK cell receptors with ligands on the surface of EBV-infected cells, which are often downregulated or altered.
