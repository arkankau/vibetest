Please answer the following question: Is the gene drive targeting an autosomal chromosome that is not passed down to all offspring, and if so, what is the best solution to ensure the genetic modification is passed down to all offspring?

[0001] assistant:
Gene drive targeting an autosomal chromosome is designed to be passed down to a higher proportion of offspring than would be expected by random inheritance. To ensure the genetic modification is passed down to all offspring, a homologous recombination approach or a sex-linked drive system can be used, as these methods can target transmission through both male and female gametes more effectively.
