Please answer the following question: What specific role does the poly(A) tail play as a primer binding site in the reverse transcription process for cDNA constructs?

[0001] assistant:
The poly(A) tail is not typically a primer binding site in reverse transcription; rather, it is often removed and replaced with a different sequence (like a poly(dT) primer) that can bind reverse transcriptase primers for cDNA synthesis.
