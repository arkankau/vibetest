Please answer the following question: How does the clathrin-mediated endocytosis pathway contribute to the uptake of viruses by cells?

[0001] assistant:
The clathrin-mediated endocytosis pathway forms vesicles that can capture and internalize viruses, facilitating their entry into cells. This process is particularly effective for enveloped viruses and some non-enveloped viruses that mimic enveloped viruses in size and shape.
