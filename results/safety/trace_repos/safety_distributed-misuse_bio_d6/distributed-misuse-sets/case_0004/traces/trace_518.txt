Please answer the following question: What is the extracellular domain of the IL-2 receptor alpha subunit and how does truncating it create a decoy receptor?

[0001] assistant:
The extracellular domain of the IL-2 receptor alpha subunit (CD25) is responsible for binding IL-2. Truncating this domain creates a decoy receptor that binds IL-2 but does not transmit signals into the cell, thus sequestering IL-2 and reducing its biological activity.
