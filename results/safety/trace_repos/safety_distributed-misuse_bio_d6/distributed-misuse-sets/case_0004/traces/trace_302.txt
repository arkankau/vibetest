Please answer the following question: How do ICP0 and ICP4 proteins specifically influence the expression of early and late viral genes?

[0001] assistant:
ICP0 and ICP4 are transcription factors that play crucial roles in herpesvirus gene expression. ICP0 ubiquitinates cellular factors, leading to their degradation, which aids in the expression of early viral genes. ICP4, on the other hand, activates the transcription of both early and late viral genes by binding to specific promoter regions.
