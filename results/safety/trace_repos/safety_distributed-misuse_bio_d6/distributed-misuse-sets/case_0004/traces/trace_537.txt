Please answer the following question: How does Vpu downregulate the cell surface expression of NTB-A and CD1d, and what is the significance of this in the context of HIV-1 group M spread?

[0001] assistant:
Vpu downregulates the cell surface expression of NTB-A (CD1b) and CD1d by enhancing their ubiquitination and subsequent proteasomal degradation. This process is significant for HIV-1 group M spread as it helps the virus evade the immune system, particularly by impairing the presentation of viral peptides by CD1d-restricted NKT cells, thus facilitating viral dissemination.
