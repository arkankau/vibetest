Please answer the following question: What is the Flp-FRT recombination system and how does it function in genetic engineering?

[0001] assistant:
The Flp-FRT recombination system is a method used in genetic engineering to manipulate the genome of organisms, particularly yeast and flies. It functions through the use of the Flp recombinase enzyme, which exchanges DNA sequences between homologous FRT (Flp Recombination Target) sites, allowing for precise genetic modifications.
