Please answer the following question: Do inverted terminal repeats (ITRs) facilitate the integration of the viral genome into the host cell's chromosome in the process of developing a reverse genetics system for a DNA virus?

[0001] assistant:
I don't know. Inverted terminal repeats (ITRs) are typically associated with retroviruses, which have RNA genomes. They facilitate integration into the host cell's chromosome by the viral reverse transcriptase. For DNA viruses, the process and genomic structure are different, so ITRs are not directly involved in their integration.
