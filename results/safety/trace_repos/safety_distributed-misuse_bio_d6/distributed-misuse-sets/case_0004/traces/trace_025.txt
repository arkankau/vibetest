Please answer the following question: How are negative-strand RNA viruses transcribed and replicated, and what role does DNA play in this process?

[0001] assistant:
Negative-strand RNA viruses transcribe and replicate their genome using a RNA-dependent RNA polymerase, which synthesizes a complementary positive-strand RNA from the negative-strand RNA template. This positive-strand RNA can then be used as a template to produce viral proteins or to generate more negative-strand RNA, the viral genome. DNA does not directly play a role in these processes; however, some viruses can integrate their RNA into the host genome or convert their RNA into DNA via reverse transcription.
