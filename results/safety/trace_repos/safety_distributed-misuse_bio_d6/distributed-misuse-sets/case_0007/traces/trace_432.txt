Please answer the following question: What are the key differences between introducing a small fragment deletion and a point mutation using CRISPR/Cas9 in terms of the edit's precision and the enzyme's activity alteration?

[0001] assistant:
Introducing a small fragment deletion typically results in a more precise edit with clear boundaries, while a point mutation involves changing a single nucleotide, which can be less precise due to potential off-target effects. The enzyme's activity is generally not altered by the type of edit but rather by the design of the guide RNA.
