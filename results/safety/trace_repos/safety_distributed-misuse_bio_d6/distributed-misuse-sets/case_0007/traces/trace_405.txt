Please answer the following question: What are the challenges in achieving long-term expression of the therapeutic gene when using herpesvirus vectors for gene therapy in the CNS, and why is this difficult?

[0001] assistant:
Achieving long-term expression of therapeutic genes using herpesvirus vectors in the CNS is challenging due to the robust immune response that targets these vectors, limiting their persistence. Additionally, the blood-brain barrier and the restricted tropism of herpesvirus vectors can also hinder their effectiveness and long-term expression.
