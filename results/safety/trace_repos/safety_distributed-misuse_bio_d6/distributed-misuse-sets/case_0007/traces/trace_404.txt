Mutations in the RNA editing site of the P/V gene can lead to the ablation of V protein expression, which is crucial for viral replication. Without proper V protein expression, the virus may be unable to inhibit interferon signaling and evade the host immune response, potentially impairing its ability to replicate and spread.
