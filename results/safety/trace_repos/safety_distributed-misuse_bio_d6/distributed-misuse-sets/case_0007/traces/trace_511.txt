Please answer the following question: How do low seroprevalence domains contribute to the immune recognition of adenoviral vectors?

[0001] assistant:
Low seroprevalence domains in adenoviral vectors can contribute to immune recognition by serving as escape targets. Since these domains are less likely to be pre-exposed to the immune system, they can avoid neutralization by pre-existing antibodies, thus enhancing the vector's immunogenicity.
