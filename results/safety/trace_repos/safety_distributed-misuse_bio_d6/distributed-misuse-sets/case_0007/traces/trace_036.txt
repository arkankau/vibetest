Human activities such as the use of antibiotics in livestock and the consumption of bat products may exert selective pressures that increase antibiotic resistance in bat microbiota. Additionally, habitat destruction and urbanization could change the environment, exposing bats to new selective pressures that favor resistant bacteria.
