CD4+ T-cell loss is a critical aspect of HIV-1 infection, as these cells are essential for coordinating the immune response. The objective of using polyvalent Env vaccines is to prevent this loss by inducing a broad and effective immune response against the diverse HIV-1 envelope proteins.
