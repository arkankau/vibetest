When designing retrotransposon LTRs for bacterial systems, nucleotide motifs that closely resemble native ribosome-binding sites (RBS) can be inserted, such as sequences similar to the Shine-Dalgarno sequence. This mimicry can enhance translational efficiency, potentially increasing downstream gene expression levels by improving ribosome binding and initiation of translation.
