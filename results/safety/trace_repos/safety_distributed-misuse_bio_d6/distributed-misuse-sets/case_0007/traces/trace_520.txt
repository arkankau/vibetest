Please answer the following question: What is the evidence that the EBV latent membrane protein 1 (LMP1) activates the nuclear factor-κB (NF-κB) to support cell survival and proliferation, and how does this contribute to oncogenesis?

[0001] assistant:
EBV latent membrane protein 1 (LMP1) activates NF-κB through signaling pathways that mimic those of the CD40 receptor, leading to enhanced survival and proliferation of B cells. This activation contributes to oncogenesis by promoting cellular transformation and escape from apoptosis, thus supporting the development of EBV-associated cancers.
