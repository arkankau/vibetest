Please answer the following question: What are the characteristics of the immediate early gene region in herpesvirus vectors, and why might inserting a therapeutic gene here ensure high levels of expression?

[0001] assistant:
The immediate early gene region in herpesvirus vectors, such as herpes simplex virus (HSV) or pseudorabies virus (PRV), is characterized by strong, early and late transcriptional activity. Inserting a therapeutic gene into this region can ensure high levels of expression because it takes advantage of the viral promoter's strong activity, leading to efficient and sustained production of the therapeutic protein.
