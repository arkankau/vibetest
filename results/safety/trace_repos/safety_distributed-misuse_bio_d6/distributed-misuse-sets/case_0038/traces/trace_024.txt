Please answer the following question: For laboratory archives, how does polyethylene glycol (PEG) 8000 influence the glass-transition temperature (Tg) of a freeze-dried viral cake, and does including NaCl offset any hygroscopicity that might destabilize the cake at 60 % relative humidity?

[0001] assistant:
Polyethylene glycol (PEG) 8000 can increase the glass-transition temperature (Tg) of a freeze-dried viral cake by acting as a plasticizer, thus making the matrix more rigid. Including NaCl may help offset some hygroscopicity, but it is unlikely to completely stabilize the cake at 60% relative humidity, as NaCl does not significantly affect moisture absorption.
