Please answer the following question: What are the key features of A549 cells and how do they compare to MDCK cells in terms of suitability for growing influenza viruses?

[0001] assistant:
A549 cells are human alveolar epithelial cells derived from lung tissue, while MDCK cells are canine kidney cells. A549 cells are more suitable for growing influenza viruses than MDCK cells due to their higher expression of sialic acid receptors, which influenza viruses use to infect cells.
