Please answer the following question: What is the method of single-cell cloning and how can it be used to select for the virus of interest in viral reverse genetics?

[0001] assistant:
Single-cell cloning involves isolating and culturing individual cells to generate a homogeneous population. In viral reverse genetics, this method can be used to select for cells containing the virus of interest by identifying and expanding clones that express specific viral antigens or exhibit characteristic phenotypes.
