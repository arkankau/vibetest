Please answer the following question: Describe the method by which an in vitro transcription system can be used to produce RNA that can be directly electroporated into susceptible cells to recover live virus in reverse genetics for RNA viruses.

[0001] assistant:
In vitro transcription systems use RNA polymerase to synthesize RNA from DNA templates. This synthesized RNA can then be electroporated into susceptible cells to express viral genes and recover live virus, facilitating reverse genetics studies for RNA viruses.
