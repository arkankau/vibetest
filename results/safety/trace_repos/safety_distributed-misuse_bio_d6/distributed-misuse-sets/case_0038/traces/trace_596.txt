Please answer the following question: How is a Master Donor Virus (MDV) utilized in the process of generating vaccine strains through genetic reassortment in reverse genetics studies?

[0001] assistant:
A Master Donor Virus (MDV) is used in reverse genetics studies to provide a pool of viral genes that can be reassorted with the genes of a virus of interest to generate new vaccine strains. This allows for the creation of viruses with specific genetic combinations tailored for vaccine development.
