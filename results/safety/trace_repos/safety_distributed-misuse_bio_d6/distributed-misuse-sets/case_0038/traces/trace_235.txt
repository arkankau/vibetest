Mutations in the receptor-binding motif (RBM) of the Omicron S-bearing virus enhance its ability to evade the immune response generated by previous SARS-CoV-2 infections by increasing its binding affinity to the ACE2 receptor and reducing antibody recognition, particularly against antibodies developed from infections with earlier variants.
