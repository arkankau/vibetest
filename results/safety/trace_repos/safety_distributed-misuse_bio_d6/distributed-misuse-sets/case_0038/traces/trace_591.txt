Please answer the following question: Does thymidine kinase (TK) have a role in suppressing the host immune response, promoting viral entry, or enhancing the virulence of the herpes simplex virus (HSV)?

[0001] assistant:
Thymidine kinase (TK) is involved in viral DNA synthesis by converting deoxythymidine triphosphate (dTTP) into deoxythymidine monophosphate (dTMP). It does not directly suppress the host immune response or enhance virulence but is crucial for viral replication.
