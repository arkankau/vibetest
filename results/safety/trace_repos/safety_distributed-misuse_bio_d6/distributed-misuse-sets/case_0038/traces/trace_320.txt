Please answer the following question: What is the effect of PBP-2A on the susceptibility of Staphylococcus aureus to methicillin and other β-lactam antibiotics?

[0001] assistant:
PBP-2A confers resistance to methicillin and other β-lactam antibiotics by facilitating their passage through the bacterial cell wall, reducing their effectiveness and making Staphylococcus aureus susceptible to these antibiotics.
