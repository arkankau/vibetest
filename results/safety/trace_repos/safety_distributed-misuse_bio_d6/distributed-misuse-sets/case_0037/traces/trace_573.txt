Please answer the following question: What is the ELISA technique and how does it work for detecting the presence of a specific capsid protein, such as p24, in a lentivirus sample?

[0001] assistant:
The ELISA (Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay) technique is a widely used method for the detection and quantification of antigens or antibodies. For detecting a specific capsid protein like p24 in a lentivirus sample, the ELISA works by immobilizing the target protein on a solid surface. The sample is then incubated, allowing the protein to bind to antibodies labeled with an enzyme. After washing away unbound material, the enzyme substrate is added, resulting in a detectable signal proportional to the amount of protein present.
