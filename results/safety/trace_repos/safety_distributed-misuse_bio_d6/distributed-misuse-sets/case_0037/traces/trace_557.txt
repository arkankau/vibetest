Please answer the following question: Compare the common symptoms of the flu and chickenpox to identify which is more representative of a typical biological agent presentation.

[0001] assistant:
The common symptoms of the flu include fever, cough, sore throat, runny or stuffy nose, body aches, fatigue, and sometimes vomiting and diarrhea. Chickenpox presents with fever, headache, fatigue, and a characteristic itchy rash that develops into fluid-filled blisters. Both are typical of viral infections, but the flu's symptom profile is more common in a wide range of viral illnesses, making it more representative of a typical biological agent presentation.
