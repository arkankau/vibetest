Please answer the following question: Which of the following organisms are known to produce marine biotoxins, specifically paralytic shellfish poisons, and what are the mechanisms involved?

[0001] assistant:
Diatoms of the genus Alexandrium and dinoflagellates of the genus Dinophysis are known to produce marine biotoxins, specifically paralytic shellfish poisons (PSP). These toxins are produced through biosynthetic pathways involving specific genes that synthesize polyketides and amino acids, which are then modified to form the toxic compounds.
