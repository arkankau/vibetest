Please answer the following question: Is naphthaleneacetic acid a strong bioregulator that can be expressed by a single gene addition to a plant through biopharming?

[0001] assistant:
Naphthaleneacetic acid is a synthetic auxin used as a plant growth regulator. It is not expressed by a single gene in plants and cannot be added through biopharming in the same way a gene might be introduced.
