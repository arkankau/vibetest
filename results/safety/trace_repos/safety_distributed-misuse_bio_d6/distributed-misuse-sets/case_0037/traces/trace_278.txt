Please answer the following question: Why is designing primers for RT-PCR particularly challenging when there are no closely related sequenced viruses available for a newly emerged RNA virus?

[0001] assistant:
Designing primers for RT-PCR in the absence of closely related sequenced viruses is particularly challenging because there is limited genetic information to base the primers on, increasing the risk of mismatches and reducing the specificity and efficiency of the PCR reaction.
