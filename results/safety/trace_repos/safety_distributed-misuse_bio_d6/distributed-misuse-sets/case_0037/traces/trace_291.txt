Please answer the following question: Describe the process of EMS mutagenesis and its relevance to reverse genetics for influenza viruses.

[0001] assistant:
EMS mutagenesis involves treating viral genomes with ethyl methanesulfonate to induce mutations, facilitating the identification of functional genes through the generation of a mutant library. This technique is crucial in reverse genetics for influenza viruses by allowing the creation of specific mutants for detailed functional analysis and vaccine development.
