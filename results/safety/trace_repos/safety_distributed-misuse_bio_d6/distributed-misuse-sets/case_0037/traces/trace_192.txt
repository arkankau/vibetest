Please answer the following question: What is White-nose Syndrome (WNS) and how does it affect the health of little brown bats?

[0001] assistant:
White-nose Syndrome (WNS) is a fungal disease that affects hibernating bats, causing a white fungus to grow on their noses. It significantly impacts little brown bats by disrupting their hibernation, leading to dehydration, malnutrition, and ultimately high mortality rates.
