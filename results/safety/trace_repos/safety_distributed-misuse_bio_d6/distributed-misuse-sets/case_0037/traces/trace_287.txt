Please answer the following question: Describe the historical significance and pathogenicity of the B. anthracis strain Sterne.

[0001] assistant:
The B. anthracis strain Sterne is historically significant as it was one of the first virulence-reduced strains used for vaccine development. It is considered low in pathogenicity and safe for use in humans and animals, making it a standard strain for anthrax vaccines and research.
