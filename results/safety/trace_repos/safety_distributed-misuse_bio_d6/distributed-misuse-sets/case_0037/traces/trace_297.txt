Please answer the following question: How does the integration of AAV vectors into the host genome contribute to long-term transgene expression?

[0001] assistant:
The integration of AAV vectors into the host genome contributes to long-term transgene expression by providing a stable genetic modification that is less likely to be affected by transcriptional silencing or immune responses compared to episomal vectors. This integration ensures that the transgene is consistently transcribed and expressed over the long term.
