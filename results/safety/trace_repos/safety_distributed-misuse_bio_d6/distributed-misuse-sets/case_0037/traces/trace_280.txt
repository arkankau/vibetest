Please answer the following question: How can vascular puncture inoculation be performed on corn seeds to introduce maize dwarf mosaic virus?

[0001] assistant:
Vascular puncture inoculation on corn seeds for introducing maize dwarf mosaic virus involves making a small puncture in the seed with a needle, then applying a virus-containing solution to the puncture site. This method directly introduces the virus into the vascular system of the seed.
