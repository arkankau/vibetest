Please answer the following question: How does the increased expression of CD206 and CD163 surface markers on alveolar macrophages relate to the transition from M1 to M2 phenotype and the development of inflammatory injuries and fibrosis in the respiratory tracts during SARS-CoV-2 infection?

[0001] assistant:
Increased expression of CD206 and CD163 on alveolar macrophages is associated with a shift from a pro-inflammatory M1 phenotype to an anti-inflammatory M2 phenotype. This transition is linked to the development of inflammatory injuries and fibrosis in the respiratory tracts during SARS-CoV-2 infection.
