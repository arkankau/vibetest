Please answer the following question: Can the RNA genome of negative-strand RNA viruses be directly translated into functional proteins, and how does this compare to positive-strand RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Negative-strand RNA viruses cannot be directly translated because their RNA is not directly readable by the cellular machinery; they require an additional RNA-dependent RNA polymerase to produce positive-sense RNA for translation. In contrast, positive-strand RNA viruses can be directly translated by the host cell's ribosomes since their RNA is immediately readable.
