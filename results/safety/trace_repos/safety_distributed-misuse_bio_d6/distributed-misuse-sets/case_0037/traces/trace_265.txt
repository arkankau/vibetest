Please answer the following question: Why is it particularly difficult to measure the concentration of OVs in the blood over time, compared to traditional drugs?

[0001] assistant:
It is particularly difficult to measure the concentration of oncolytic viruses (OVs) in the blood over time because OVs are often cleared quickly by the immune system and may not maintain stable levels in the bloodstream, making them challenging to quantify accurately.
