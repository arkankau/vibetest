The genetic sequence of SARS-CoV-2 shares the highest similarity with RaTG13, a bat coronavirus, and GD-Pangolin-CoV, a pangolin coronavirus, but SARS-CoV-2 is more closely related to RaTG13 with about 96% identity at the whole genome level.
