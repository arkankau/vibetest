Please answer the following question: How does the process of viral replication and recombination contribute to the genetic diversity of herpes simplex virus within a host?

[0001] assistant:
Viral replication and recombination in herpes simplex virus (HSV) contribute to genetic diversity by allowing the exchange of genetic material between different HSV strains, leading to the creation of novel viral variants with mixed genetic traits. Additionally, each round of replication introduces mutations, further increasing genetic diversity.
