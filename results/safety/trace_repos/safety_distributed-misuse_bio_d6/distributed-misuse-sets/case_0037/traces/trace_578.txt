The presence of asymptomatic carriers can significantly increase the airborne transmissibility of a zoonotic pathogen in a human population because these individuals can spread the pathogen without showing symptoms, leading to undetected transmission events. Absence of asymptomatic carriers would generally reduce the potential for silent transmission and thus affect the pathogen's spread.
