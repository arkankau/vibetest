Please answer the following question: How do the immediate-early (IE) genes of cytomegalovirus (CMV) contribute to the disruption of the host cell's normal functions and prepare it for the expression of other viral genes?

[0001] assistant:
The immediate-early (IE) genes of cytomegalovirus (CMV) encode proteins that activate the host cell's transcription machinery, leading to the expression of both early and late viral genes. These proteins disrupt the host cell's normal functions by promoting cell cycle re-entry, enhancing mRNA synthesis, and modulating cellular signaling pathways, thus creating a favorable environment for viral replication.
