Please answer the following question: What is solid-phase peptide synthesis and can it be used to produce toxins that are difficult to obtain from natural sources?

[0001] assistant:
Solid-phase peptide synthesis (SPPS) is a chemical method for producing peptides and proteins by sequentially adding amino acids to a growing peptide chain attached to a solid support. Yes, SPPS can be used to produce toxins that are difficult to obtain from natural sources, as it allows for the synthesis of complex peptides with high purity and specificity.
