Please answer the following question: How do k-mer-based methods enable comprehensive sequence identity analysis in DNA synthesis screening?

[0001] assistant:
K-mer-based methods enable comprehensive sequence identity analysis in DNA synthesis screening by breaking DNA sequences into fixed-length substrings (k-mers), allowing for efficient comparison and identification of similar sequences even in large datasets. This approach facilitates rapid detection of potential synthetic DNA sequences that match known pathogens or other controlled elements.
