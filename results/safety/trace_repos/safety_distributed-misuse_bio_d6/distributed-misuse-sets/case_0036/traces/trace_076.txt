Please answer the following question: Describe the natural habitat of botulism neurotoxin in the environment, and discuss its relevance to infant botulism.

[0001] assistant:
Botulism neurotoxin is naturally produced by the bacterium Clostridium botulinum in soil and aquatic environments. In the environment, it is found in the spores of these bacteria. For infant botulism, the toxin can be ingested by infants through ingesting spores, often from honey or contaminated soil, which can lead to the spores germinating in the infant's intestines and producing toxin.
