Please answer the following question: What is transfection and how is it used in generating recombinant negative-strand RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Transfection is the process of introducing foreign nucleic acids into a cell, often using calcium phosphate precipitation, electroporation, or lipid-based nanoparticles. In generating recombinant negative-strand RNA viruses, transfection is used to deliver the viral genomic RNA or cDNA along with necessary plasmids encoding viral proteins into host cells, enabling the cells to produce the virus.
