Please answer the following question: Is the expression of TMPRSS2 necessary for the assembly of viral particles in SARS-CoV-2 studies?

[0001] assistant:
The expression of TMPRSS2 is not directly necessary for the assembly of viral particles in SARS-CoV-2, but it aids in the proteolytic activation of the virus's spike protein, which can enhance viral entry into cells.
