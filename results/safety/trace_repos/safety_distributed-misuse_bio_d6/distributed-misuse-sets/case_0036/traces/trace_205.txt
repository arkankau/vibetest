Please answer the following question: How does the HA2 subunit specifically interact with the HA1 subunit in the hemagglutinin glycoprotein of influenza A viruses?

[0001] assistant:
The HA2 subunit is responsible for fusion with the host cell membrane, while the HA1 subunit mediates receptor binding. The HA2 subunit specifically interacts with the HA1 subunit to facilitate the conformational changes necessary for viral entry into the host cell.
