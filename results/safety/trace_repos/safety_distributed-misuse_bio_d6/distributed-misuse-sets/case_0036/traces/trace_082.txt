Please answer the following question: What factors contribute to the lower risk of resistance in fungicides with single-site mode of action?

[0001] assistant:
Fungicides with single-site mode of action have a lower risk of resistance because pathogens require multiple mutations to overcome a single target site, which is less likely to occur compared to multiple target sites. Additionally, these fungicides often have a broader spectrum of activity, reducing the selection pressure for resistance.
