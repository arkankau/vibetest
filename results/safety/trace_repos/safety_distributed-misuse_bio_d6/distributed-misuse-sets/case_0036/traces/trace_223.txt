Please answer the following question: What role do arthropod ectoparasites play in the transmission of Bartonella between bats and other mammals?

[0001] assistant:
Arthropod ectoparasites, such as fleas and mites, can serve as vectors for Bartonella transmission between bats and other mammals by feeding on the blood of infected hosts and then transmitting the bacteria to uninfected hosts.
