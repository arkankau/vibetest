Please answer the following question: In the context of ADE, why is it important to test the ability of vaccine-induced antibodies to facilitate viral entry into Fc receptor-expressing cells?

[0001] assistant:
It is important to test the ability of vaccine-induced antibodies to facilitate viral entry into Fc receptor-expressing cells because this can help identify antibodies that might enhance viral infection rather than prevent it, a phenomenon known as Antibody-Dependent Enhancement (ADE).
