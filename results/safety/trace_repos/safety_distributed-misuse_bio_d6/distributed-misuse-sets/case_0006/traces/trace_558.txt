Please answer the following question: Is the mucociliary escalator involved in the transport of MV-infected cells to the upper respiratory tract for expulsion?

[0001] assistant:
The mucociliary escalator is involved in the transport of cells, including potentially infected cells, from the lower to the upper respiratory tract, where they can be expelled, but it specifically targets non-viable or less viable cells rather than actively transporting MV-infected cells.
