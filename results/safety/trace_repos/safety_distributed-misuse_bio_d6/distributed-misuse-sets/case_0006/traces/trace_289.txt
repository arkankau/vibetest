Apartheid-era South Africa's biological weapons program, known as Project Coast, focused on developing and stockpiling specific biological agents, including pathogens that could cause high mortality rates and long-term disabilities. Unlike conventional programs, it also experimented with agents that could be particularly debilitating but less lethal, aiming to incapacitate rather than kill its adversaries.
