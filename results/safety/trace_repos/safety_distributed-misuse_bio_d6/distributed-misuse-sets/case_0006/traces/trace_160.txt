Please answer the following question: Does vFLIP play a role in blocking apoptosis in infected cells, and if so, how does this relate to the development of Kaposi's sarcoma?

[0001] assistant:
vFLIP (viral FLICE-Inhibitory Protein) can play a role in blocking apoptosis in infected cells by inhibiting caspases. In the context of Kaposi's sarcoma, which is associated with human herpesvirus 8 (HHV-8) infection, vFLIP helps the virus to establish a persistent infection by preventing the host cell from undergoing apoptosis, thus promoting the development and progression of the tumor.
