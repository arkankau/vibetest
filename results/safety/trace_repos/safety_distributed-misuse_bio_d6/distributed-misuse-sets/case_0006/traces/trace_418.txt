Please answer the following question: What is the role of microRNAs (miRNAs) in gene regulation and how might they be associated with viral latency in herpesvirus research?

[0001] assistant:
MicroRNAs (miRNAs) play a crucial role in gene regulation by binding to messenger RNA (mRNA) and either inhibiting translation or promoting mRNA degradation, thus controlling gene expression. In herpesvirus research, miRNAs can be associated with viral latency by potentially modulating the host immune response and viral gene expression, helping the virus to remain dormant in the host.
