Please answer the following question: How do the cell lines used for producing adenovirus vectors differ between human and non-human serotypes, and what are the unique challenges associated with these cell lines?

[0001] assistant:
Human adenovirus vectors are typically produced using human 293 cells, which have been transformed from human embryonic kidney cells. Non-human adenovirus vectors often use different animal cell lines, such as monkey kidney cells (e.g., Vero cells). Unique challenges include species-specific immune responses and potential differences in gene expression and viral replication kinetics between human and non-human cell lines.
