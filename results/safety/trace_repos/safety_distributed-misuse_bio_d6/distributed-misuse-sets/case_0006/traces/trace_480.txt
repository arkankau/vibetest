Please answer the following question: What is the function of the viral thymidine kinase gene in the HSV amplicon vector and how is it related to propagation?

[0001] assistant:
The viral thymidine kinase gene in the HSV amplicon vector facilitates the vector's propagation by allowing it to replicate in cells that have been treated with ganciclovir, thereby providing a selective advantage for the vector during amplification.
