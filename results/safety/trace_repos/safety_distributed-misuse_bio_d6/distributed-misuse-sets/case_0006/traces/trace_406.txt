Please answer the following question: In CRISPR gene-drive systems, why are promoters from early embryonic genes (e.g., vasa, nanos) preferred over adult fat-body-specific promoters for minimizing somatic cleavage and the associated formation of mosaic resistance alleles?

[0001] assistant:
Promoters from early embryonic genes like vasa and nanos drive gene expression early in development, which helps ensure that the gene drive is active before somatic cells have a chance to form mosaic resistance. This minimizes somatic cleavage and the formation of mosaic resistance alleles.
