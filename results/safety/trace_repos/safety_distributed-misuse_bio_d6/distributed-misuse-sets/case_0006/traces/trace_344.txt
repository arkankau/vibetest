Please answer the following question: In reverse genetics studies, how does the interferon (IFN) system serve as a selective pressure for the evolution of viral resistance?

[0001] assistant:
In reverse genetics studies, the interferon (IFN) system serves as a selective pressure for viral resistance by inducing an antiviral state that limits viral replication. Viruses evolve mechanisms to counteract IFN effects, such as inhibiting IFN signaling or degrading IFN-induced proteins, thus facilitating resistance.
