Please answer the following question: What is the function of the glcB gene in Mycobacterium tuberculosis?

[0001] assistant:
The glcB gene in Mycobacterium tuberculosis encodes an isomerase involved in the glycolysis pathway, specifically isocitrate lyase, which plays a role in the metabolism of citrate and is important for the bacterium's survival under oxygen-limited conditions.
