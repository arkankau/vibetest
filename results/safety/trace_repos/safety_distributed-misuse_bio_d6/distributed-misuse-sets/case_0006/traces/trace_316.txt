Please answer the following question: How does the regulation of viral replication by viral haemagglutinin (HA) contribute to the virus life cycle?

[0001] assistant:
Viral hemagglutinin (HA) facilitates viral attachment and entry into host cells, which are crucial early steps in the viral life cycle. However, HA does not directly regulate viral replication; this is typically managed by other viral proteins that control the synthesis of viral components.
