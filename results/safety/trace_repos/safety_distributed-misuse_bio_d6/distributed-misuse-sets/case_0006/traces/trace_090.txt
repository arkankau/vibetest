Please answer the following question: What is the difference between ssRNA and dsDNA, and how does this difference relate to the observed results of the restriction enzyme digestion?

[0001] assistant:
ssRNA (single-stranded RNA) does not have a complementary strand to pair with, whereas dsDNA (double-stranded DNA) consists of two complementary strands. This difference means that restriction enzymes can cleave dsDNA at specific sequences, producing distinct fragments that can be visualized in a gel electrophoresis, but ssRNA is typically resistant to restriction enzyme digestion, resulting in no visible changes or specific fragment patterns.
