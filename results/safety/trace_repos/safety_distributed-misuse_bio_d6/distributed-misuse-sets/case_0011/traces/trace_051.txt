Please answer the following question: How do helper viruses support the transcription and replication of negative strand RNA viruses from cDNA templates?

[0001] assistant:
Helper viruses support the transcription and replication of negative strand RNA viruses by providing the necessary RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (RdRP) that these viruses lack. This enzyme is used to synthesize positive-sense RNA from the negative-sense RNA templates provided by the negative strand RNA viruses.
