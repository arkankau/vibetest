Please answer the following question: What is a plasmid-only-based reverse genetics system and how does it differ from other genetic systems?

[0001] assistant:
A plasmid-only-based reverse genetics system is a method used in molecular biology to study gene function by introducing plasmids into cells to express specific genes or knockouts. It differs from other systems, such as viral-based approaches, by not relying on a viral vector, thus potentially reducing the complexity and potential side effects in the host cells.
