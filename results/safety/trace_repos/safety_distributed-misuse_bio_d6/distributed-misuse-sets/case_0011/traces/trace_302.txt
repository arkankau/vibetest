Please answer the following question: How do antiviral drugs like acyclovir target the herpes simplex virus (HSV) during its replication cycle?

[0001] assistant:
Acyclovir targets the herpes simplex virus (HSV) by mimicking adenosine triphosphate (ATP) and interfering with the viral DNA polymerase, which leads to the termination of viral DNA synthesis and reduces viral replication.
