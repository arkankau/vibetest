Please answer the following question: How does a rescue system aid in the propagation of defective RNA viruses in cell culture during reverse genetics experiments?

[0001] assistant:
A rescue system aids in the propagation of defective RNA viruses by providing the missing genetic elements necessary for viral replication, allowing these viruses to replicate in cell culture even though they are defective. This is crucial for reverse genetics experiments where specific mutations or deletions are introduced to study viral function and pathogenesis.
