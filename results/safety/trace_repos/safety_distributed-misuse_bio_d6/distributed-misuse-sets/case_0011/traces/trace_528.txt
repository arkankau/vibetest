Please answer the following question: Describe the mechanism by which antiviral drugs targeting thymidine kinase inhibit viral replication.

[0001] assistant:
Antiviral drugs targeting thymidine kinase inhibit viral replication by blocking the enzyme's ability to convert antiviral nucleoside analogs into active triphosphate forms, which are then incorporated into viral DNA, leading to replication errors and viral genome instability.
