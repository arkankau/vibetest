Please answer the following question: What advantages does IFA offer over other methods of protein detection in analyzing viral protein expression?

[0001] assistant:
IFAA (Indirect Fluorescent Antibody Assay) offers advantages such as high specificity and sensitivity, rapid results, and the ability to detect multiple antigens simultaneously. However, it requires labeled antibodies, which can be expensive and may limit its accessibility in some settings.
