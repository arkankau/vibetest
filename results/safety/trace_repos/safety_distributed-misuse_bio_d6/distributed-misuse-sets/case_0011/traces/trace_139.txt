Please answer the following question: What is the role of post-translational modifications in enhancing the biochemical diversity and stability of animal venom toxins?

[0001] assistant:
Post-translational modifications enhance the biochemical diversity and stability of animal venom toxins by adding functional groups or altering existing structures, which can affect toxin activity, stability, and resistance to degradation. This diversity allows toxins to target a wider range of biological functions and survive within the complex environments encountered during venom delivery.
