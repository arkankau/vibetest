Please answer the following question: What are the known orthopox viruses, and how do they differ from smallpox in their genetic makeup?

[0001] assistant:
The known orthopox viruses include variola (the causative agent of smallpox), cowpox, vaccinia, goatpox, and horsepox. Variola differs from other orthopoxviruses in its genome, which is larger and has unique sequences, making it more virulent and causing the severe symptoms associated with smallpox.
