Yes, there are specific antiviral drugs that target the synthesis of viral DNA by herpesvirus-encoded DNA polymerase, such as acyclovir, ganciclovir, and valacyclovir. These drugs effectively inhibit the virus by acting as competitive inhibitors of the viral DNA polymerase, thereby preventing viral DNA synthesis.
