Please answer the following question: What is the importance of the packaging and envelope genes in creating a replication competent lentivirus, and how can they be moved to the transfer plasmid?

[0001] assistant:
The packaging and envelope genes are crucial for creating a replication-competent lentivirus because they enable the production of infectious viral particles. These genes can be moved to a transfer plasmid by cloning the relevant sequences into a vector designed for this purpose, typically using restriction enzymes and ligase.
