Please answer the following question: Is Interferon-gamma (IFN-γ) produced by CD4+ T cells and what is its function in immune control?

[0001] assistant:
Interferon-gamma (IFN-γ) is produced by CD4+ T cells, particularly Th1 cells, and plays a critical role in immune control by promoting antigen presentation, enhancing phagocytosis, and stimulating the production of other cytokines. It also helps in the activation of macrophages and is essential for combating intracellular pathogens.
