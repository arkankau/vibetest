Please answer the following question: How does the antimuscarinic action of atropine contribute to its effectiveness in targeting the central nervous system?

[0001] assistant:
Atropine's antimuscarinic action blocks muscarinic receptors, reducing parasympathetic nervous system activity, which can help in targeting and sedating the central nervous system, though its primary use is not in CNS depression but in blocking acetylcholine effects elsewhere.
