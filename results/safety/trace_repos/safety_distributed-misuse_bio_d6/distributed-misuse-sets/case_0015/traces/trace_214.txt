Please answer the following question: What is the function of the atxA gene and how does it contribute to anthrax virulence?

[0001] assistant:
The atxA gene is involved in the regulation of gene expression in Bacillus anthracis, contributing to the switch between the sporulating and vegetative states of the bacteria. It helps modulate the expression of other virulence factors, thereby contributing to anthrax virulence.
