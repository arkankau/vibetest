Please answer the following question: How does the cotE gene contribute to the exosporium formation in Bacillus anthracis spores?

[0001] assistant:
The cotE gene in Bacillus anthracis is involved in the modification of lipid A, which is a critical component for exosporium assembly. This modification is essential for the proper formation and function of the exosporium, a complex outer layer that protects the spore.
