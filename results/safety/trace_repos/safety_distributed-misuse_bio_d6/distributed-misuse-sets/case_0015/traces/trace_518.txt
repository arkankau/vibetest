The primary role of the spike protein in the viral life cycle is to bind to host cell receptors and mediate viral entry by fusing the viral and cellular membranes. Mutations in this protein can affect its binding affinity and fusion efficiency, potentially altering the virus's ability to infect cells and evade the immune system.
