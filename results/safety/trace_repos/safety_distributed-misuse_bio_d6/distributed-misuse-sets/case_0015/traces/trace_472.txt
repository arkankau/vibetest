[0001] assistant:
The SAα2,3-Gal and SAα2,6-Gal receptors in the upper respiratory tract of guinea pigs influence the host range of influenza viruses by determining where the virus can bind and replicate. Viruses that prefer SAα2,3-Gal are more likely to infect guinea pigs, affecting their susceptibility and thus the host range.
