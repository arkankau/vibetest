Please answer the following question: In what ways can insect cell expression systems express cysteine-rich peptides, and how do they compare to the capabilities of microbial systems in this regard?

[0001] assistant:
Insect cell expression systems can efficiently express cysteine-rich peptides due to their ability to perform post-translational modifications, such as disulfide bond formation, which is crucial for the proper folding and function of these peptides. Compared to microbial systems, insect cells often provide better structural integrity and activity for cysteine-rich peptides, though they may be more complex and expensive to use.
