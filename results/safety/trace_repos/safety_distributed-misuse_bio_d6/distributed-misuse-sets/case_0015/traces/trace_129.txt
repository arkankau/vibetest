Please answer the following question: Describe the segmented genome of rotaviruses and explain how this feature posed a technical challenge for developing reverse genetics.

[0001] assistant:
Rotaviruses have a segmented double-stranded RNA genome consisting of 11 distinct genomic segments. This feature posed a significant technical challenge for developing reverse genetics because it required the construction and manipulation of 11 separate cDNA clones to recreate the full viral genome.
