Please answer the following question: What evidence supports the idea that post-translational modifications regulate cellular metabolism in animal venom toxins?

Post-translational modifications (PTMs) in animal venom toxins have been shown to alter their activity, stability, and cellular localization, which suggests they play a role in regulating cellular metabolism. Specific examples include glycosylation and phosphorylation, which can affect toxin function and interactions with target cells.
