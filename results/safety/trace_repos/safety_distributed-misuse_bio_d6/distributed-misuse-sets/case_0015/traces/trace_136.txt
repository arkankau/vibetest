High titers of antibodies against rhabdoviruses like VSV can limit the survival and persistence of the virus by neutralizing viral particles and facilitating their clearance by the immune system. However, in some cases, high viral titers may also indicate an ongoing viral replication that could potentially overwhelm the host's immune response.
