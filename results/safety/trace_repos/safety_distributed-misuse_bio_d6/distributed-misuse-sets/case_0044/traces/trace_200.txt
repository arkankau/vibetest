Wolbachia is a bacterium that can manipulate reproductive processes in its host insects, facilitating the vertical transmission of the toxin protein by ensuring infected females only produce female offspring, thus passing the infection to the next generation. For horizontal transmission, Wolbachia can induce cytoplasmic incompatibility, allowing infected males to transfer the bacteria to uninfected females during mating. This persistence is ensured because Wolbachia provides essential functions for its host, such as protection against pathogens and metabolic benefits, which confer a selective advantage even in the absence of additional outside selective pressures.
