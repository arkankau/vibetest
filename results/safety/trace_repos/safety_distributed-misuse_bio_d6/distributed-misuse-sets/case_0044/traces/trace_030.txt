Please answer the following question: What is a Bacterial artificial chromosome (BAC) and how could it be used to store and manipulate genetic material from a human endogenous retrovirus?

[0001] assistant:
A Bacterial artificial chromosome (BAC) is a cloning vector derived from the F factor of Escherichia coli, capable of carrying up to 300 kb of foreign DNA. BACs can be used to store and manipulate genetic material from a human endogenous retrovirus by inserting the viral DNA into the BAC vector, allowing for its propagation, analysis, and manipulation in bacteria.
