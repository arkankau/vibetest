Please answer the following question: How do fluorescently labeled pathogen proteins interact with the glycan array during the study of pathogen receptor specificity?

[0001] assistant:
Fluorescently labeled pathogen proteins are used to identify specific interactions with glycan structures on the glycan array, allowing researchers to determine the receptor specificity of the pathogen. This is achieved by detecting which glycans bind to the pathogen proteins under controlled conditions.
