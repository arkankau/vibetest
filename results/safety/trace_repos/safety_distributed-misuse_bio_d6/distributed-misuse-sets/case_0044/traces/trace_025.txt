Please answer the following question: What is the function of the furin cleavage site in the flavivirus precursor membrane protein, and what is the effect of a mutation at this site on viral infectivity?

[0001] assistant:
The furin cleavage site in the flavivirus precursor membrane protein serves to facilitate maturation of the viral particle by cleaving the protein into its functional components. Mutations at this site can reduce viral infectivity by impairing the proper maturation and processing of the viral envelope proteins.
