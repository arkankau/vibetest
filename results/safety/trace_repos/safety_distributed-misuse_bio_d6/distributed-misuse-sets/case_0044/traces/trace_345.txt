Please answer the following question: What is the circular polymerase extension reaction and how might it be used to assemble the fragments of a single stranded RNA virus genome from synthesized DNA?

[0001] assistant:
The circular polymerase extension reaction (CPCR) is a method that uses a circular DNA template and a single primer to repeatedly extend and displace the primer, creating a circular DNA product. This technique can be used to assemble fragments of a single-stranded RNA virus genome by first synthesizing complementary DNA (cDNA) from the viral RNA, then using CPCR to circularize and amplify these cDNA fragments, ultimately facilitating their assembly into a complete viral genome.
