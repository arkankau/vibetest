Please answer the following question: Which rotavirus gene segments are recommended by the CDC as the principal qPCR targets for routine clinical surveillance, and why are these particular segments preferred over others?

[0001] assistant:
The CDC recommends using gene segments 10 and 11 as the principal qPCR targets for routine clinical surveillance of rotavirus. These segments are preferred because they are highly conserved across different genotypes, providing more reliable and consistent results for surveillance purposes.
