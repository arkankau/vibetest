Please answer the following question: What are the roles of universal linker and universal short tags in molecular cloning, and how do they relate to 'UL' and 'US' regions?

[0001] assistant:
Universal linker (UL) and universal short tags (US) are used to facilitate the cloning of PCR products into a vector. The UL and US regions serve as specific attachment sites that allow for the addition of these tags to DNA fragments, enabling them to be easily inserted into a vector for further manipulation.
