Please answer the following question: What are the steps involved in assembling full-length cDNA clones in plasmid vectors or bacterial/yeast artificial chromosome vectors for RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
The steps involve reverse transcription of viral RNA to cDNA, PCR amplification of the cDNA, cloning into plasmid vectors or BACs, transformation into E. coli or yeast cells, and selection of clones containing the full-length inserts.
