Please answer the following question: What is the role of post-translational modifications in viral proteins, and how can yeast perform these modifications, which might be advantageous in reverse genetics studies involving RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Post-translational modifications (PTMs) in viral proteins can affect their stability, localization, enzymatic activity, and interactions with host proteins, which are crucial for viral replication and pathogenesis. Yeast can perform PTMs such as glycosylation and phosphorylation, which can be advantageous in reverse genetics studies involving RNA viruses by providing a platform for the production of modified viral proteins that mimic those in the host, enhancing functional analysis.
