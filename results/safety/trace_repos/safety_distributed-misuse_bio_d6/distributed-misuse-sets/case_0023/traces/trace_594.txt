Please answer the following question: How do helper plasmids act as a source of selectable markers for transfected cells in reverse genetics systems?

[0001] assistant:
Helper plasmids provide additional genes that complement the deficiencies of the expression plasmid, ensuring proper replication and maintenance. These plasmids often carry selectable markers, which allow for the identification and selection of cells that have been successfully transfected with the expression plasmid.
