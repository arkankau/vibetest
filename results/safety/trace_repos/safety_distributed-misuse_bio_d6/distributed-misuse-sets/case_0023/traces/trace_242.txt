Please answer the following question: What are the key steps involved in performing a complementation assay to study gene function in a pathogen during reverse genetics research?

[0001] assistant:
The key steps in a complementation assay include creating a mutant strain with a gene of interest inactivated, transforming this mutant strain with a vector containing the wild-type version of the gene, and then selecting for phenotypic recovery of the wild-type function, which indicates successful gene complementation.
