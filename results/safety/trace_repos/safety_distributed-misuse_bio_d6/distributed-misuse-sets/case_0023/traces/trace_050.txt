The term 'rule of six' in RNA virus reverse genetics refers to the requirement that the viral genome must be divisible by six in length to facilitate efficient replication and translation. This is because the genome is transcribed and translated in frames of three nucleotides (codons), making the genome length a multiple of six crucial for correct protein synthesis.
