Please answer the following question: Can the olfactory epithelium serve as a primary site for virus replication, while the respiratory epithelium primarily acts as a secondary site?

[0001] assistant:
The olfactory epithelium can indeed serve as a primary site for virus replication, especially for viruses that target the nasal cavity. The respiratory epithelium often acts as a secondary site, where viral replication can occur as the infection progresses downward from the nasal cavity.
