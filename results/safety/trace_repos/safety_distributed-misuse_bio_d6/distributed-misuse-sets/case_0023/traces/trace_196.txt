Please answer the following question: What is the significance of plasmid replication in the construction of a reverse genetics system for a negative-sense RNA virus, and how can a self-cleaving element facilitate this process?

[0001] assistant:
Plasmid replication is crucial in reverse genetics systems for negative-sense RNA viruses as it allows for the stable propagation and manipulation of viral cDNA. A self-cleaving element, such as a hepatitis delta virus ribozyme, can facilitate this process by enabling the efficient release of viral RNA from the plasmid, enhancing the generation and use of viral cDNA clones.
