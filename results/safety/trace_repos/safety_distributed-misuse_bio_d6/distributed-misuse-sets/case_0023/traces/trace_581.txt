Please answer the following question: In the study of avian H5N1 influenza virus, which genes are known to enhance the virus's ability to infect mammalian cells and lead to mammal-to-mammal transmission?

[0001] assistant:
The genes known to enhance the H5N1 virus's ability to infect mammalian cells and facilitate mammal-to-mammal transmission include the hemagglutinin (HA) gene and the neuraminidase (NA) gene, particularly when they acquire mutations that allow for binding to human-like receptors.
