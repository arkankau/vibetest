Please answer the following question: What are the typical outcomes of saxitoxin poisoning compared to alcohol poisoning?

[0001] assistant:
Saxitoxin poisoning typically results in neurological symptoms such as tingling, numbness, and paralysis, often starting from the lips and tongue and spreading to the rest of the body. Alcohol poisoning usually leads to central nervous system depression, causing symptoms like vomiting, confusion, difficulty breathing, and unconsciousness.
