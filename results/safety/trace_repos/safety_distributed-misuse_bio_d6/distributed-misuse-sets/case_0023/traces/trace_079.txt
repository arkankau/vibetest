Please answer the following question: How does the cytokine storm syndrome (CSS) potentially lead to the recruitment of immune cells, and does this contribute to the resolution of SARS-CoV-2 infection?

[0001] assistant:
Cytokine storm syndrome (CSS) involves the rapid release of cytokines, which can lead to the recruitment of large numbers of immune cells to the site of infection. However, this excessive immune response can contribute to tissue damage and may not directly resolve SARS-CoV-2 infection; rather, it can complicate the disease course.
