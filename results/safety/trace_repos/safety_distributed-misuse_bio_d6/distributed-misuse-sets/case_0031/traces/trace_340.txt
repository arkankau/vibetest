Mancozeb acts by disrupting fungal cell metabolism. It is a multi-site contact fungicide, meaning it affects multiple targets within the fungal cell. Resistance to mancozeb can develop through various mechanisms, including enhanced detoxification, reduced penetration, and altered target sites.
