Please answer the following question: What is the mechanism by which the EBV latent membrane protein 1 (LMP1) activates the nuclear factor-κB (NF-κB) signaling pathway?

[0001] assistant:
EBV latent membrane protein 1 (LMP1) activates the NF-κB signaling pathway by mimicking the function of CD40, leading to the activation of receptor interacting protein (RIP) and subsequent recruitment of TNF receptor-associated factors (TRAFs), which in turn activate IKK complex and phosphorylate IκB, allowing NF-κB to translocate to the nucleus.
