Please answer the following question: Is miR122 involved in activating the interferon-mediated antiviral response against HCV in the liver?

[0001] assistant:
miR122 is not directly involved in activating the interferon-mediated antiviral response against HCV. However, miR122 is crucial for HCV replication, and its reduction can impair viral replication, indirectly supporting antiviral responses.
