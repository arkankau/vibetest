Please answer the following question: What does it mean for viral DNA to be 'excised' during the lytic phase, and how does this differ from the concept of an episome during latency?

[0001] assistant:
During the lytic phase, viral DNA is excised from the host genome and replicated independently, leading to the production of viral particles. In contrast, during latency, viral DNA is integrated into the host genome as an episome, remaining dormant and not actively transcribed or replicated until reactivated.
