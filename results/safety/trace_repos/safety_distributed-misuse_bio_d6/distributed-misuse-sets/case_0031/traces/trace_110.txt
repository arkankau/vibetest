Please answer the following question: How does the mecA gene contribute to antibiotic resistance in Staphylococcus aureus?

[0001] assistant:
The mecA gene contributes to antibiotic resistance in Staphylococcus aureus by encoding a modified penicillin-binding protein (PBP2a) that has a lower affinity for beta-lactam antibiotics, such as methicillin, making the bacteria resistant to these drugs.
