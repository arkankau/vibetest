Please answer the following question: Why is electroporation a preferred method for introducing positive-sense RNA viruses into host cells for reverse genetics studies, compared to other methods such as chemical transfection?

[0001] assistant:
Electroporation is preferred for introducing positive-sense RNA viruses into host cells for reverse genetics studies because it provides high efficiency and minimal genetic alteration, directly delivering the RNA into the cytoplasm where it can be translated. This method bypasses the need for complex vectors and allows for precise manipulation and analysis of gene function.
