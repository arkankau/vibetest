Please answer the following question: Under what circumstances are k-mer-based methods more suitable for sequences with nucleotide identities below 80%?

[0001] assistant:
K-mer-based methods are more suitable for sequences with nucleotide identities below 80% when high sensitivity and the ability to detect distant homologs are required, as k-mers can capture conserved short sequence patterns that might be missed by other methods.
