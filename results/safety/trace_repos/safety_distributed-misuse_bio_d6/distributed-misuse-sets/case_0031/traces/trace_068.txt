Please answer the following question: How does enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA) for common IAV antigens help in identifying novel IAV strains emerging from swine populations?

[0001] assistant:
ELISA for common IAV antigens can help identify novel IAV strains in swine populations by detecting the presence of known viral antigens, which can indicate the presence of similar or related novel strains. This method aids in surveillance and early detection, facilitating quicker response measures to potential pandemics.
