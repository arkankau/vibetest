Please answer the following question: What are the primary functions of transfer RNAs (tRNAs) in cellular processes and how do they relate to viral latency in herpesvirus research?

[0001] assistant:
Transfer RNAs (tRNAs) primarily function to carry specific amino acids to the ribosome during protein synthesis, where they are added to growing polypeptide chains. In the context of herpesvirus research, tRNAs can be used by the virus to enhance its latency by integrating their sequences into the viral genome, allowing the virus to persist in a dormant state within host cells.
