Please answer the following question: Can the EBV latent membrane protein 1 (LMP1) inhibit the lytic cycle of EBV, and if not, what is its role in maintaining viral latency?

[0001] assistant:
EBV latent membrane protein 1 (LMP1) does not inhibit the lytic cycle of EBV. Instead, LMP1 plays a role in maintaining viral latency by mimicking the signaling of the B-cell receptor, which is crucial for the survival and proliferation of latently infected B cells.
