Please answer the following question: What role do immediate-early (IE) genes play in the regulation of viral gene expression during the lytic and latent phases of herpesvirus infection?

[0001] assistant:
Immediate-early (IE) genes play a crucial role in the initial stages of herpesvirus infection by rapidly promoting the synthesis of viral proteins necessary for the transition from the latent to the lytic phase. These genes regulate viral gene expression by activating the transcription of other viral genes, facilitating the replication and spread of the virus.
