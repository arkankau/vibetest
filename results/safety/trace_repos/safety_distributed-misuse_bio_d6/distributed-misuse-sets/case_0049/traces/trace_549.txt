Please answer the following question: Do superantigens possess both glycosylation and proteolytic resistance as key characteristics?

[0001] assistant:
Superantigens are known for their ability to activate large numbers of T cells non-specifically and are often glycosylated, which can influence their activity. They also tend to be resistant to proteolytic degradation, which helps them persist in the environment and within the host. Therefore, glycosylation and proteolytic resistance are indeed key characteristics of superantigens.
