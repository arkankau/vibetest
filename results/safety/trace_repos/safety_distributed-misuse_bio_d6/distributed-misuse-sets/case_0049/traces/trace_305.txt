Please answer the following question: What is the function of thymidine kinase (TK) in the replication cycle of herpes simplex virus (HSV)?

[0001] assistant:
Thymidine kinase (TK) is essential for the replication cycle of herpes simplex virus (HSV) as it converts deoxythymidine monophosphate (dTMP) into deoxythymidine triphosphate (dTTP), which is then used to synthesize the viral DNA.
