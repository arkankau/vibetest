Please answer the following question: What are the advantages and limitations of using a mouse model in comparing the virulence of influenza strains, and how might these impact the results?

[0001] assistant:
Advantages include controlled experimental conditions, the ability to standardize infection protocols, and the availability of extensive genetic and immunological tools. Limitations involve differences between mouse and human immune responses, potential confounding factors due to strain-specific interactions, and the inability to fully replicate complex human conditions. These factors can impact the generalizability of the results to human influenza infections.
