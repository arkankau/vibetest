Please answer the following question: What is the function of viral proteins in the assembly of the viral capsid in KSHV?

[0001] assistant:
Viral proteins, specifically those encoded by the KSHV genome, play a crucial role in the assembly of the viral capsid by facilitating the correct arrangement and stabilization of capsid proteins. These proteins interact with each other and with nucleic acids to form the protective shell of the virus.
