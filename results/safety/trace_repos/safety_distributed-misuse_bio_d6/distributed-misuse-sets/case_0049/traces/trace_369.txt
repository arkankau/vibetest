Please answer the following question: How does the vRNP complex facilitate the assembly of influenza A virus particles?

[0001] assistant:
The vRNP (viral RNA polymerase complex) facilitates the assembly of influenza A virus particles by bringing together the viral RNA segments and the necessary viral proteins to form the viral ribonucleoproteins, which are then packaged into budding virions along with the viral envelope.
