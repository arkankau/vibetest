Please answer the following question: What is the JAK-STAT pathway and its role in cellular processes such as proliferation and survival?

[0001] assistant:
The JAK-STAT pathway is a signaling cascade where Janus kinases (JAKs) activate signal transducers and activators of transcription (STATs), which then translocate to the nucleus to regulate gene expression. This pathway plays crucial roles in cellular processes like proliferation, survival, and differentiation by integrating extracellular signals from various cytokines and growth factors.
