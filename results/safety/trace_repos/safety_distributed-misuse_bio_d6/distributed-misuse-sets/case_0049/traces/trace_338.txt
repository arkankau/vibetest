Please answer the following question: Is the olfactory epithelium more susceptible to virus entry and replication due to its unique structure and function compared to the respiratory epithelium?

[0001] assistant:
The olfactory epithelium is more susceptible to virus entry and replication due to its unique structure, which includes olfactory receptors and neuroblasts, that are directly exposed to inhaled viruses. This makes it a key initial site for viral infection compared to the more protective structure of the respiratory epithelium.
