Please answer the following question: What is the process of transfecting susceptible cells with RNA transcribed in vitro from the cloned cDNA, and is this a common method for recovering infectious virus in reverse genetics for RNA viruses?

This process is called in vitro transfection, where RNA transcribed from cloned cDNA is introduced into susceptible cells. This method is indeed common in reverse genetics for RNA viruses to recover infectious viruses.
