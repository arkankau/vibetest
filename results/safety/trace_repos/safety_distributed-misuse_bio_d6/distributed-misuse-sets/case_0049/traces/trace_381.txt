Please answer the following question: What is the significance of using cefoxitin diffusion disks in microbiological testing?

[0001] assistant:
Cefoxitin diffusion disks are used in microbiological testing to differentiate between susceptible and resistant strains of certain gram-negative bacteria, particularly those resistant to other beta-lactam antibiotics, due to their ability to produce extended-spectrum beta-lactamases.
