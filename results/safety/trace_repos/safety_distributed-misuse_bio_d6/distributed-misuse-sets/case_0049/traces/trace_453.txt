Please answer the following question: What is the significance of seroprevalence in the context of adenoviral vectors?

[0001] assistant:
Seroprevalence in the context of adenoviral vectors indicates the proportion of the population that has developed antibodies against adenoviruses, which is significant because it can affect the vectors' efficacy in delivering genetic material, especially in populations where pre-existing immunity may lead to immune clearance of the vector.
