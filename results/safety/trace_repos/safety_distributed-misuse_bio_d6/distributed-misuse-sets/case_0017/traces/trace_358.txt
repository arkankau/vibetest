Please answer the following question: What is the typical method of replication for negative-strand RNA viruses, and how does it differ from positive-strand RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Negative-strand RNA viruses typically replicate in the cytoplasm by synthesizing a positive-strand RNA using a negative-strand RNA as a template. This differs from positive-strand RNA viruses, which can directly serve as a template for protein synthesis and do not require an intermediate negative-strand RNA.
