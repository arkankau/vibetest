Please answer the following question: What is Cre-recombination-based AAV targeted evolution (CREATE) and how does it function in genetic engineering?

[0001] assistant:
CREATE is a method used in genetic engineering to optimize adeno-associated virus (AAV) vectors for efficient gene delivery. It involves using Cre recombinase to generate a library of AAV variants and then selecting for those with improved tropism and gene transfer efficiency.
