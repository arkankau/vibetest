Please answer the following question: What is the route of infection for inhalation botulism, and is it associated with the botulism neurotoxin (Botox)?

[0001] assistant:
Inhalation botulism occurs when spores of the bacterium Clostridium botulinum are inhaled, leading to the production of botulism neurotoxin in the lungs. Inhalation botulism is not associated with the Botox used in medical aesthetics; medical Botox is a purified form of the neurotoxin used in low doses for therapeutic and cosmetic purposes.
