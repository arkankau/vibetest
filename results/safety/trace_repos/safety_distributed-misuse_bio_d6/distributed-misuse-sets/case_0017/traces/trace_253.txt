Please answer the following question: Ampicillin, kanamycin, and cycloheximide have different antimicrobial properties. How do each of these help in selecting for B. anthracis?

[0001] assistant:
Ampicillin selects for bacteria that have a functional penicillin-binding proteins, kanamycin selects for resistance to aminoglycosides, and cycloheximide selects for ribosomes resistant to protein synthesis inhibition, effectively isolating B. anthracis strains that can survive in the presence of these antibiotics.
