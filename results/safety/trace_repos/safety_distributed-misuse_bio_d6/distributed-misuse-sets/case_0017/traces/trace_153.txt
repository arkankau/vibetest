Please answer the following question: Does UL54/ICP27 participate in the viral DNA replication process during herpesvirus infection?

[0001] assistant:
UL54 and ICP27 are both viral proteins involved in herpesvirus infection, but they have different functions. UL54 is a DNA polymerase that directly participates in viral DNA replication, while ICP27 has roles in regulating gene expression and DNA replication, but it does not directly engage in the replication process itself.
