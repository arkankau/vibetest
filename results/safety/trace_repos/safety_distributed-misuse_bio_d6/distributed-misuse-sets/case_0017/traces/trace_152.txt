Please answer the following question: How does an endpoint dilution assay help in quantifying the titer of infectious virus produced from transfected cells?

[0001] assistant:
An endpoint dilution assay helps in quantifying the titer of infectious virus produced from transfected cells by serially diluting the viral sample and determining the lowest dilution at which viral infection can still be detected in the cells. This provides a measure of the infectious viral particles present in the initial sample.
