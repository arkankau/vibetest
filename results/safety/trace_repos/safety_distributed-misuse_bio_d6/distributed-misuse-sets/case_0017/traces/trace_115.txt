Please answer the following question: Are endorphins strong bioregulators that can be synthesized from a protein and expressed by a single gene addition to a plant through biopharming?

[0001] assistant:
Endorphins are not typically synthesized from a single protein and a single gene addition in plants. They are complex peptides that arise from the processing of larger precursor proteins. While biopharming can be used to produce certain proteins in plants, the synthesis and expression of endorphins would likely require more complex genetic engineering.
