Please answer the following question: What experimental evidence or studies support the role of CD46 as the primary receptor for Measles virus entry into immune cells?

[0001] assistant:
Experimental evidence supports CD46 as the primary receptor for measles virus entry into immune cells, as demonstrated by studies showing that CD46 knockdown reduces virus infection, and that recombinant viruses with increased affinity for CD46 enhance viral entry.
