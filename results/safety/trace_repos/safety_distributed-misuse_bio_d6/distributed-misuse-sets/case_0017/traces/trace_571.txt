Please answer the following question: What is flow cytometry, and how can it be applied to detect transduced cells without a reporter gene?

[0001] assistant:
Flow cytometry is a technique that analyzes cells, particles, or molecules by suspending them in a stream of fluid and passing them through a detector that measures various physical and chemical properties. It can detect transduced cells without a reporter gene by assessing the presence of specific markers or changes in cell surface characteristics that result from the transduction process.
