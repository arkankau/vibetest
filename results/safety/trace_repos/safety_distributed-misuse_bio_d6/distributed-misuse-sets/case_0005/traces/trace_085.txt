Please answer the following question: How does the Psi packaging signal help in the selection of recombinant adenovirus vectors over helper virus during production?

[0001] assistant:
The Psi packaging signal helps in the selection of recombinant adenovirus vectors over helper virus by ensuring that only adenovirus vectors containing the signal are packaged into adenovirus capsids, while helper virus particles are not, thus facilitating the purification of recombinant vectors.
