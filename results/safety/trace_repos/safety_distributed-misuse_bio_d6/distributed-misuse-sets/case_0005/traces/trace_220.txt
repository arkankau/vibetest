Please answer the following question: How might the transfer of a virulence factor alter the interaction between a microbe and its host, leading to more severe disease?

[0001] assistant:
The transfer of a virulence factor, such as a toxin or adhesin, from one microbe to another can enhance the pathogen's ability to cause disease by increasing its ability to invade tissues, evade the immune system, or produce toxins. This transfer leads to more severe disease by making the microbe more effective at harming the host.
