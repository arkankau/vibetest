Please answer the following question: Do the UL and US segments contain inverted repeats, and if so, what is their significance?

[0001] assistant:
The UL and US segments of many RNA viruses, including some picornaviruses and caliciviruses, contain inverted repeats. These repeats are significant because they can form stem-loop structures that are involved in the regulation of viral RNA synthesis and replication.
