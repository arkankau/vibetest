Please answer the following question: What is the role of RNA interference in antiviral defense and how might ICP34.5 disrupt this process?

[0001] assistant:
RNA interference (RNAi) plays a crucial role in antiviral defense by helping to degrade viral RNA and silence viral genes. ICP34.5, a protein produced by some viruses like herpes simplex virus, can disrupt RNAi by affecting the expression of key RNAi components, thereby enhancing viral replication.
