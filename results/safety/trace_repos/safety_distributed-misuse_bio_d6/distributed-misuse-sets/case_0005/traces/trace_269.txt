The risks associated with the instability of cDNA inserts in vectors include loss of genetic material, reduced efficiency of transformation, and potential generation of incomplete or mutated viral particles. To manage these risks in the production of a genetic system for large viruses, strategies such as using stable cloning vectors, including selectable markers, and performing regular sequence validations can be employed.
