Please answer the following question: What evidence supports the involvement of viral microRNAs in the regulation of gene expression during the herpesvirus life cycle?

[0001] assistant:
Viral microRNAs (miRNAs) from herpesviruses have been shown to modulate host gene expression, affecting various aspects of the herpesvirus life cycle. For example, they can target host transcripts to promote viral replication and latency, or inhibit antiviral responses. This evidence comes from both in vitro and in vivo studies.
