Please answer the following question: What are the specific symptoms and their associated severity for inhalation anthrax, oropharyngeal anthrax, and gastrointestinal anthrax?

[0001] assistant:
Inhalation anthrax symptoms include flu-like symptoms (fever, malaise) followed by severe respiratory distress, cough, and possibly shock; oropharyngeal anthrax symptoms are milder and include sore throat, difficulty swallowing, and fever; gastrointestinal anthrax symptoms are more severe and include abdominal pain, vomiting of blood, and bloody diarrhea.
