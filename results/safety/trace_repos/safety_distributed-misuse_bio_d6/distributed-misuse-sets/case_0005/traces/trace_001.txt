Please answer the following question: What are the common misconceptions about what constitutes a vector for tick-borne diseases?

[0001] assistant:
Common misconceptions include the belief that only certain species of ticks, such as deer ticks, can transmit diseases, when in fact multiple tick species can carry pathogens. Another misconception is that vectors can only transmit disease when they bite, when some pathogens can be transmitted through tick saliva or indirectly.
