Please answer the following question: What is the effect of saturation mutagenesis on the stability of the viral genome during replication, and how is this stability measured?

[0001] assistant:
Saturation mutagenesis introduces a large number of random mutations into a viral genome, which can affect its stability during replication. Stability is often measured by assessing the virus's ability to replicate, its ability to produce infectious particles, and its fitness in competition assays with wild-type viruses.
