A reverse genetics system allows for the generation of viruses with specific mutations, facilitating the study of viral protein interactions with host cell proteins by isolating and analyzing the effects of these interactions in a controlled manner. The key advantages include the ability to study individual viral components in isolation, enhance understanding of viral pathogenesis, and accelerate the development of antiviral strategies.
