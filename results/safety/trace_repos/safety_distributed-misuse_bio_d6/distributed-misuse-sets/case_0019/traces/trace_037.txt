Please answer the following question: Is IFI16 involved in processing viral proteins in the context of HSV-1 infection?

[0001] assistant:
IFI16 (Interferon-Inducible Protein 16) is involved in processing viral proteins during HSV-1 infection by recognizing and promoting the clearance of viral DNA, which can lead to the activation of innate immune responses.
