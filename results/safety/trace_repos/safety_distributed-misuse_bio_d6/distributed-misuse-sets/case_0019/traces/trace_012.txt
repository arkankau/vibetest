Please answer the following question: How does ICP0 contribute to the immune evasion mechanisms of herpesviruses?

[0001] assistant:
ICP0 ( Immediate Early Protein 0) contributes to the immune evasion mechanisms of herpesviruses by promoting the degradation of cellular ubiquitinated proteins, including those involved in the interferon pathway, thus impairing the host's antiviral response.
