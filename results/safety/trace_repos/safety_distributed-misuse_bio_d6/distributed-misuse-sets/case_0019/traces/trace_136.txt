Please answer the following question: What is the mechanism by which inverted terminal repeats (ITRs) facilitate the binding of AAV vectors to cell surfaces?

[0001] assistant:
Inverted terminal repeats (ITRs) facilitate the binding of AAV vectors to cell surfaces by serving as binding sites for cellular receptors, although the exact receptor is specific to the serotype of AAV used. These repeats are essential for the viral life cycle and are recognized by co-receptors that mediate the entry of AAV into cells.
