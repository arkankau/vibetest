Please answer the following question: What is the significance of chaperone proteins in insect cells, and how do they contribute to the successful expression of venom toxins that require proper folding?

[0001] assistant:
Chaperone proteins in insect cells help ensure that venom toxins are correctly folded into their functional three-dimensional structures. They prevent misfolding and aggregation, thereby facilitating the successful expression and secretion of these toxins.
