The lack of cultured cells was a significant technical challenge for developing reverse genetics for rotaviruses because these viruses cannot grow in standard cell cultures, which are crucial for manipulating and studying viral genomes. This issue was addressed by developing in vitro packaging systems that allow the viral genes to be expressed in helper cells, enabling the production of infectious viral particles.
