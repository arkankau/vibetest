Please answer the following question: How does the presence of SAα2,3 and SAα2,6 affect the ability of influenza viruses to infect and replicate within pig tracheal epithelial cells?

[0001] assistant:
SAα2,3 and SAα2,6 glycans are critical for influenza virus binding and infection. The presence of SAα2,3 affects the binding of H1N1 and H3N2 influenza viruses, while SAα2,6 affects H5N1 and H7N9 viruses, facilitating their replication in pig tracheal epithelial cells.
