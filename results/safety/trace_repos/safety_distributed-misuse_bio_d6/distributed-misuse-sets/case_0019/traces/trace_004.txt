Please answer the following question: In what ways can reverse genetics be used to analyze the transcription and replication of filoviruses in the absence of VP40?

[0001] assistant:
Reverse genetics can be used to generate filovirus-like particles or replication competent viruses lacking the VP40 protein by expressing the remaining viral genes in cells, which can then be used to study transcription and replication mechanisms.
