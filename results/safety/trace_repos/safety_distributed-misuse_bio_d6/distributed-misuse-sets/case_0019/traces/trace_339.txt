Please answer the following question: Describe the process of mutation in viruses and how it can lead to a virus reaching pandemic potential.

[0001] assistant:
Mutation in viruses occurs through errors in their replication process. These mutations can confer new properties, such as increased transmissibility or virulence. If a mutation significantly enhances a virus's ability to spread or cause severe illness, it can lead to a pandemic by rapidly infecting a large population across a wide geographic area.
