Please answer the following question: How do coagulation factor activities typically change during DIC, and which assay would be best to measure these changes in an in vitro setting?

[0001] assistant:
During Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC), coagulation factor activities generally decrease due to consumption and consumption-induced inhibition. To measure these changes in an in vitro setting, the most appropriate assay is the coagulation factor activity assay, such as the activated partial thromboplastin time (aPTT) or prothrombin time (PT) tests, which can be used to quantify multiple coagulation factors.
