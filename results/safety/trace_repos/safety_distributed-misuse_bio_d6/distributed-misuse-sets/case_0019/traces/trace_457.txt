[0001] assistant:
Cell culture transfection is the process of introducing foreign genetic material into cells to alter their function or expression patterns. In the context of Mononegavirales rescue, it is used to introduce viral genes into host cells to study virus replication or to generate viral particles for research or vaccine development.
