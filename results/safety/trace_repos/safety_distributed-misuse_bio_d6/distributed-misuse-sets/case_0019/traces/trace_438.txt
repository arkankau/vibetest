Herpesviruses can mutate by acquiring new sequences through recombination or by point mutations, which can alter viral antigens, making them less recognizable by the host's immune system. Additionally, latency allows herpesviruses to persist in a dormant state within cells, where they are less likely to be targeted by the immune response, and they can re-activate with modified antigens to evade immunity.
