Botulinum toxin from Clostridium botulinum works by inhibiting the release of acetylcholine at neuromuscular junctions, leading to muscle paralysis. If expressed in a poxvirus, these mechanisms could still cause severe neurological symptoms by interfering with normal nerve function, potentially leading to muscle weakness and paralysis.
