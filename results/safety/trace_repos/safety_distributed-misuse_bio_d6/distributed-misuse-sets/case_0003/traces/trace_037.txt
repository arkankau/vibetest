Please answer the following question: Can bacterial toxins be distinguished from toxic effectors by their method of entry into host cells?

[0001] assistant:
Bacterial toxins can be distinguished from toxic effectors by their method of entry into host cells; for example, exotoxins are typically secreted and enter cells through binding to receptors or direct cell membrane penetration, while effectors are often delivered via bacterial type III, IV, or VI secretion systems directly into the cytoplasm of host cells.
