Please answer the following question: How does activation of the host cell cGAS-STING pathway contribute to the enhancement of interferon production and relate to the evasion of the host's innate immune system?

[0001] assistant:
Activation of the host cell cGAS-STING pathway recognizes and responds to cytosolic DNA, leading to the production of interferons and other antiviral cytokines. However, pathogens can evade this response by inhibiting cGAS or modulating STING activity, thus avoiding detection and subsequent immune activation.
