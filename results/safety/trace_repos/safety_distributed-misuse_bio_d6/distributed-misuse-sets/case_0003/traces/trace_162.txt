Please answer the following question: Can ELISA be used to measure the titer of replication-competent HSV, and if so, what is its limitation?

[0001] assistant:
ELISA can be used to measure the presence of antibodies against HSV but not the titer of replication-competent HSV. Its limitation is that it cannot detect the infectivity or replication capability of the virus particles.
