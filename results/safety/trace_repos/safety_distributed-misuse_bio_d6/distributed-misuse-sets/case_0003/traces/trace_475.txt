Please answer the following question: In what way does a complementation assay aid in the validation of gene function and the study of gene interactions in a pathogen's genetic background?

[0001] assistant:
A complementation assay helps validate gene function by determining if a mutant gene can be restored to wild-type function when introduced with a functional copy of the same gene. It also aids in studying gene interactions by identifying whether different genetic mutations in the same or different genes can compensate for each other's loss.
