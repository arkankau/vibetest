Please answer the following question: What is the purpose of using an F plasmid in the hybridization of bacterial genomes, and how would it affect the transformation process?

[0001] assistant:
The F plasmid, also known as the fertility plasmid, is used in the hybridization of bacterial genomes to enable conjugation, allowing F+ bacteria to transfer genetic material, including antibiotic resistance genes, to F- bacteria. This process enhances the transformation process by facilitating the direct transfer of genetic material between bacteria, increasing genetic diversity and the spread of traits.
