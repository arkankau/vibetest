Please answer the following question: What is the function of the Sigma70 promoter in reverse genetics systems, and why is transfection of host cells used with full-length viral cDNA?

[0001] assistant:
The Sigma70 promoter drives transcription in E. coli and is used in reverse genetics systems to express full-length viral proteins. Transfection of host cells with full-length viral cDNA is used to study viral replication and pathogenesis by introducing the genetic material into cells to produce infectious viruses.
