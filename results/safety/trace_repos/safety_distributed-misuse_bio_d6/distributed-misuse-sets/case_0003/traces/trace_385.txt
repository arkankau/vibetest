Please answer the following question: What is the significance of the MBCS in allowing the HPAI H5N1 virus to infect and replicate in various cell types throughout the body?

[0001] assistant:
The MBCS (Multi-Binding Cassette Sequence) in the HPAI H5N1 virus allows the hemagglutinin protein to bind to multiple sialic acid receptors, enabling the virus to infect and replicate in a wide range of cell types throughout the body.
