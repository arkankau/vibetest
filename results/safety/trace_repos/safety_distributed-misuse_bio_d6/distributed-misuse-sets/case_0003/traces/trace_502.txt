Please answer the following question: What are the necessary steps or conditions that must be met for a lentivirus to become replication competent, and how do these steps differ between first and second-generation systems?

[0001] assistant:
For a lentivirus to become replication competent, it must be recombined or transfected with all necessary viral genes, including gag, pol, and env, as well as packaging signals. First-generation systems require all these genes and signals to be provided exogenously, while second-generation systems include a modified viral genome that already contains some essential genes, reducing the number of plasmids needed for infection.
