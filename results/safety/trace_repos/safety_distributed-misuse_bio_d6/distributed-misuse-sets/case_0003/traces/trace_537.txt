Please answer the following question: What is the mechanism by which Vpu degrades human CD4, and how does this process impact the ability of HIV-1 group M to spread?

[0001] assistant:
Vpu degrades human CD4 by ubiquitinating it, marking it for proteasomal degradation. This process impairs CD4 surface expression, thereby enhancing the release of infectious HIV-1 particles and facilitating viral spread.
