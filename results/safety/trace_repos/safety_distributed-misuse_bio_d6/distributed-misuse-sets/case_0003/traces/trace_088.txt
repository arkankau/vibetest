Please answer the following question: Is Bacillus thuringiensis non-regulated and non-pathogenic, and can it serve as a model for B. anthracis?

[0001] assistant:
Bacillus thuringiensis is non-pathogenic and generally non-regulated. However, it is not a model for B. anthracis; B. anthracis is a separate species within the Bacillus genus.
