Please answer the following question: How does a phage integrase protein mediate site-specific recombination, and what target DNA features must be present in a recipient bacterial chromosome for successful integration of a cargo gene?

[0001] assistant:
Phage integrase proteins mediate site-specific recombination by recognizing and binding to specific DNA sequences called attP and attB, which are found in the phage and recipient bacterial chromosome, respectively. For successful integration, the recipient bacterial chromosome must contain the attB site, while the phage carries the attP site and the cargo gene.
