[0001] assistant:
A broad-host-range bacterial origin of replication typically includes sequences from plasmids that naturally replicate in both Gram-negative and Gram-positive bacteria, such as the RepA and RepB proteins from the pUB110 plasmid of Bacillus subtilis, or the RepA protein from the p15A plasmid of Escherichia coli. These sequences allow for efficient replication and stability in a wide range of bacterial hosts.
