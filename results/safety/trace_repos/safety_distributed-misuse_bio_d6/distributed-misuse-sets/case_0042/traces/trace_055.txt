Please answer the following question: What is the simian RV strain SA11 and why is it important in virology research?

[0001] assistant:
The simian RV strain SA11 is a attenuated strain of the simian immunodeficiency virus (SIV) used in research. It is important in virology research because it helps model HIV infection and aids in the development of vaccines and antiviral drugs.
