[0001] assistant:
The integrity of a pathogen's capsid or protein coat contributes to its environmental stability and airborne transmission by providing protection against environmental factors like humidity, temperature, and chemical agents, which can otherwise inactivate the pathogen. A robust capsid also facilitates aerosolization, enhancing the pathogen's ability to remain airborne and infect hosts over longer distances.
