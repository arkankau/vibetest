ORF-26 in KSHV (Kaposi's sarcoma-associated herpesvirus) is significant because it encodes a microRNA (miRNA) that contributes to the virus's ability to induce tumorigenesis by modulating host cell pathways and promoting viral latency and oncogenic processes.
