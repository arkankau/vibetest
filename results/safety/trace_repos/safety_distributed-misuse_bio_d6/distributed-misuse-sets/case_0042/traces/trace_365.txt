Please answer the following question: How can a plasmid-based system allow for the insertion of large genetic elements into the viral genome?

[0001] assistant:
A plasmid-based system can allow for the insertion of large genetic elements into the viral genome by first using restriction enzymes to cut the plasmid and the viral DNA at specific sites. Then, DNA ligase is used to join the new genetic element into the plasmid, which can be reintroduced into the virus, facilitating the insertion of the large genetic element.
