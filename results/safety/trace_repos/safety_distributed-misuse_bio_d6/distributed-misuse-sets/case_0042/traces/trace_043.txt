[0001] assistant:
The primary species of plankton that produce paralytic shellfish poisons are certain species of dinoflagellates, such as Alexandrium, Lingulodinium, and Gymnodinium. These toxins are produced through biosynthetic processes within the plankton cells, often as a defense mechanism against predators.
