Please answer the following question: How does the interferon (IFN) system provide a readout for the effectiveness of viral immune evasion strategies in reverse genetics studies?

[0001] assistant:
In reverse genetics studies, the interferon (IFN) system provides a readout for viral immune evasion strategies by measuring the induction of IFN-responsive genes. If a virus can evade the IFN response, it may not induce these genes, indicating the virus's ability to circumvent the immune system.
