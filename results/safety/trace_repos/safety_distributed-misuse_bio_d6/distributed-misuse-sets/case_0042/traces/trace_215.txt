Homologous recombination allows for the precise modification of genes by introducing specific changes at targeted locations, which is crucial for understanding gene interactions and pathways in reverse genetics. By modifying genes in a defined way, researchers can study the effects of these changes on cellular processes, thereby elucidating gene functions and their roles in biological pathways.
