[0001] assistant:
The autoproteolysis of the flavivirus precursor membrane protein is crucial for the formation of the mature envelope protein, which is necessary for viral budding and release. Mutations affecting this process can impair viral infectivity by interfering with the proper assembly and release of new virions.
