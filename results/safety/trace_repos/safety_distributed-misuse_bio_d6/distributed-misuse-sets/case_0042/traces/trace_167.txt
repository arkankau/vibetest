Please answer the following question: Does EBNA1 have any role in the modulation of the host immune response?

[0001] assistant:
Yes, EBNA1 (Epstein-Barr Virus Nuclear Antigen 1) plays a role in modulating the host immune response by interfering with processes such as antigen presentation and cytokine production, thereby helping the virus to establish a latent infection.
