Please answer the following question: What are the mechanisms by which error-prone DNA polymerase I can increase mutagenesis rates on a specific plasmid without affecting the rest of the genome?

[0001] assistant:
Error-prone DNA polymerase I increases mutagenesis rates on specific plasmids by introducing more errors during DNA replication due to its lower proofreading ability. This specificity is often achieved by local targeting of the polymerase to the plasmid, sparing the rest of the genome by not being present or active elsewhere.
