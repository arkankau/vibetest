Please answer the following question: What are the potential therapeutic applications of a rescue system in the context of reverse genetics for RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
A rescue system in reverse genetics for RNA viruses can be used to recover viable virus strains from cDNA clones, facilitating the study of viral pathogenesis and the development of antiviral therapies. It also enables the rapid generation of virus mutants for vaccine development and the assessment of viral tropism and host range.
