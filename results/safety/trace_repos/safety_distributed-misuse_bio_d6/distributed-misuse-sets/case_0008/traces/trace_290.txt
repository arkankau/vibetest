Please answer the following question: What role do mosquitoes play in the transmission of diseases, and are they capable of spreading B. anthracis spores to humans?

[0001] assistant:
Mosquitoes act as vectors for transmitting diseases such as malaria, dengue fever, and Zika virus by biting infected hosts and then biting other individuals, injecting the pathogens. They are not capable of spreading B. anthracis spores to humans; anthrax is primarily transmitted through skin contact with infected animals, inhalation of spores, or ingestion of contaminated food.
