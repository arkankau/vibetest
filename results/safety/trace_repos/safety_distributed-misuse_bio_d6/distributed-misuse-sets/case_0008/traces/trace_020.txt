Please answer the following question:  Are UL30 and UL42 proteins part of the viral DNA polymerase complex in the herpes simplex virus replication process?

[0001] assistant:
UL30 and UL42 are indeed part of the viral DNA polymerase complex in the herpes simplex virus replication process. UL30 is the DNA polymerase and UL42 is a processivity factor that enhances its activity.
