Please answer the following question: What is the function of the HA cleavage site in influenza viruses and how do polybasic amino acids affect this function?

[0001] assistant:
The HA cleavage site in influenza viruses allows the hemagglutinin protein to be cleaved into HA1 and HA2 subunits, which is necessary for the virus to infect host cells. Polybasic amino acids in this site enhance cleavage efficiency, facilitating viral infection and replication by making the cleavage more rapid and complete.
