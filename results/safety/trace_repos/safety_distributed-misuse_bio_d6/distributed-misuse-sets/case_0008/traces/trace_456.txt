Please answer the following question: What is sampling bias, and how can it occur in the process of selecting strains for the annual influenza vaccine?

[0001] assistant:
Sampling bias occurs when the sample used in a study does not accurately reflect the population it is intended to represent, leading to skewed results. In the context of selecting strains for the annual influenza vaccine, sampling bias can occur if the strains chosen do not adequately represent the viral strains prevalent in a given season, potentially because of limited geographic or temporal sampling, or preferential selection of certain strains.
