Please answer the following question: What is the role of vFLIP in the context of viral structural proteins and how does it differ from its role in KSHV-associated malignancies?

[0001] assistant:
vFLIP, or viral FLICE-inhibitory protein, is a regulatory protein in herpesviruses like KSHV that prevents apoptosis. In the context of viral structural proteins, vFLIP does not directly contribute to structural integrity but helps in viral replication by inhibiting host cell apoptosis. In KSHV-associated malignancies, vFLIP plays a role in oncogenesis by promoting cell survival and proliferation through its anti-apoptotic function.
