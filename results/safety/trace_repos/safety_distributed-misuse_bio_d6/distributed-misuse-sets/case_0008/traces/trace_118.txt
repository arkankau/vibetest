Please answer the following question: Why might herpesviruses have difficulty infecting neuronal cells, and how does this impact their use for gene therapy in the CNS?

[0001] assistant:
Herpesviruses have difficulty infecting neuronal cells because these cells have evolved mechanisms to restrict viral entry and replication, such as the presence of restriction factors like APOBEC3G and the lack of necessary receptors. This makes it challenging to use herpesviruses as gene therapy vectors in the central nervous system (CNS) because they may not efficiently deliver therapeutic genes to neuronal cells, potentially limiting their effectiveness in treating CNS diseases.
