Please answer the following question: What is the Gibson assembly method, and what are its advantages and limitations when assembling DNA fragments?

[0001] assistant:
The Gibson assembly method is a molecular biology technique that joins DNA fragments without the need for restriction enzymes or ligase. Its advantages include the ability to assemble multiple fragments at once, handle fragments with overhangs of up to 400 bp, and tolerate some GC content variations. Limitations include the requirement for a proofreading polymerase in the reaction, which can be costly, and it may not work well with very long or very short fragments.
