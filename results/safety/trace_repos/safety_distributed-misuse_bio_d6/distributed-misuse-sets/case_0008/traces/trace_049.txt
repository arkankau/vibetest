Integration of RNA into the host genome is not a concern in reverse genetics experiments on RNA viruses, as RNA typically does not integrate into the host genome like DNA does. A lipid-based transfection reagent does not prevent this, as it is designed to deliver the RNA into the host cells but does not address RNA integration into the genome.
