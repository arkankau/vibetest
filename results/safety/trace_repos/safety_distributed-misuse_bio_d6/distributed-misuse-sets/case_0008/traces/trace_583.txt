Please answer the following question: What is the primary function of the IRES element in the context of generating recombinant RNA viruses using reverse genetics techniques?

[0001] assistant:
The primary function of the IRES (Internal Ribosome Entry Site) element is to enable translation initiation at the start codon of a viral genome or cDNA without relying on the 5' cap structure, facilitating efficient protein synthesis during the generation of recombinant RNA viruses using reverse genetics techniques.
