[0001] assistant:
Genomic recombination is the process where genetic material from two different viruses is exchanged and integrated into a single viral genome, often occurring during coinfections of a host cell. This can increase genetic diversity and potentially enhance a virus's pathogenicity, transmissibility, or ability to escape host immune responses, thereby contributing to its pandemic potential.
