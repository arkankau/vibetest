Please answer the following question: What is the role of glycoprotein B (gB) in the assembly of the viral capsid during HSV infection?

[0001] assistant:
Glycoprotein B (gB) is involved in the fusion of the viral envelope with the host cell membrane during herpes simplex virus (HSV) infection, which is crucial for the release of the viral genome into the host cell but not directly in the assembly of the viral capsid.
