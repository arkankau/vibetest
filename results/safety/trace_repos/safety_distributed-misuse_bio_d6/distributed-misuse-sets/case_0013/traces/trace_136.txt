Please answer the following question: What is RNA editing, and what are the different types of RNA editing mechanisms that can occur in viruses?

[0001] assistant:
RNA editing is a post-transcriptional modification of RNA molecules that alters the genetic information encoded in the RNA. In viruses, RNA editing mechanisms include adenosine-to-inosine (A-to-I) editing by ADAR enzymes, which is the most common, and cytidine-to-uridine (C-to-U) editing, often mediated by viral proteins.
