Please answer the following question: Explain why in vitro transcription of cDNA into infectious RNA is considered a critical step in reverse genetics for positive-stranded RNA viruses like SARS-CoV-2.

[0001] assistant:
In vitro transcription of cDNA into infectious RNA is critical in reverse genetics for positive-stranded RNA viruses like SARS-CoV-2 because it allows for the synthesis of RNA with the exact sequence of the viral genome, enabling the creation of infectious viral particles for study. This process is essential for generating viruses with known genetic sequences for experimental manipulation and analysis.
