Please answer the following question: Is there evidence that exposure to water or soil during the rainy season in endemic areas increases the likelihood of melioidosis infection?

[0001] assistant:
Yes, there is evidence suggesting that exposure to water or soil during the rainy season in endemic areas increases the likelihood of melioidosis infection. The bacterium that causes melioidosis, *Burkholderia pseudomallei*, can survive in these environments and infect humans through skin wounds or inhalation.
