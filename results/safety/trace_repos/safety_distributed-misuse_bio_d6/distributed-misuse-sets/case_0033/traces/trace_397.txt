Please answer the following question: In what scenarios would a self-limiting vector be more effective than an inducible promoter for achieving temporal control of transgene expression?

[0001] assistant:
A self-limiting vector would be more effective than an inducible promoter for achieving temporal control of transgene expression in scenarios where the need for transgene expression is limited to a specific period and the expression level does not need to be precisely controlled. This is because self-limiting vectors inherently limit gene expression over time, whereas inducible promoters require external manipulation to control expression.
