Please answer the following question: Can reassortment in segmented RNA viruses lead to the generation of defective viral particles, and if so, how does this impact reverse genetics studies?

[0001] assistant:
Reassortment in segmented RNA viruses can indeed lead to the generation of defective viral particles if the reassortment results in incompatible gene segments. This can impact reverse genetics studies by complicating the analysis of genetic function and the generation of recombinant viruses, as it may produce viruses with altered phenotypes or non-functional genes.
