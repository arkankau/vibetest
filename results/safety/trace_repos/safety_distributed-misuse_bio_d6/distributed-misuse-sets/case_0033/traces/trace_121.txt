Please answer the following question: How do nonspecific interactions between the viral vector and sialic acids or serum factors like complement and natural antibodies influence the biodistribution of the viral vector?

[0001] assistant:
Nonspecific interactions between viral vectors and sialic acids on cell surfaces can lead to vector binding and internalization, potentially affecting the biodistribution. Interactions with serum factors like complement and natural antibodies can trigger opsonization and complement activation, which may enhance phagocytosis by immune cells, altering the vector's biodistribution and potentially decreasing its effectiveness in target tissues.
