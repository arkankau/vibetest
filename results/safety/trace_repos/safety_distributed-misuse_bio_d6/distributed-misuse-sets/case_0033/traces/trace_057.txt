TAR cloning offers advantages such as the ability to insert large DNA fragments directly, which is particularly useful for large viral genomes. This method also simplifies the cloning process by using a pre-miRNA sequence (TAR element) that can be easily ligated to the gene of interest, making it a preferred choice for some researchers.
