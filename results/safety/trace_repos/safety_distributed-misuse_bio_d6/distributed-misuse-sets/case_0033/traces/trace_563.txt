Please answer the following question: What is insertional mutagenesis, and how can it pose a risk when using herpesvirus vectors for gene therapy in the CNS?

[0001] assistant:
Insertional mutagenesis occurs when a viral vector inserts into a gene, potentially disrupting its function. When using herpesvirus vectors for gene therapy in the CNS, this risk arises because insertions can activate nearby oncogenes, potentially leading to tumor formation.
