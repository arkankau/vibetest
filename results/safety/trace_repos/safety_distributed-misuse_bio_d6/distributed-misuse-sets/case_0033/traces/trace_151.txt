[0001] assistant:
Upregulation of genes related to tissue repair and fibrosis generation during SARS-CoV-2 infection can promote the transition of alveolar macrophages from an M1 pro-inflammatory phenotype to an M2 anti-inflammatory phenotype, which is associated with tissue repair but can also lead to excessive fibrosis and scarring.
