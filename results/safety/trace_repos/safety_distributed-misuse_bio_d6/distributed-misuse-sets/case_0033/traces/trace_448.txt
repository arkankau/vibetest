Please answer the following question: How do inclusion bodies formed in bacterial systems typically contain the recombinant toxins, and what are the implications of this for purification?

[0001] assistant:
Inclusion bodies formed in bacterial systems typically contain the recombinant toxins as aggregate proteins that are insoluble. This makes them easier to purify by centrifugation and precipitation, but requires denaturation and renaturation steps, which can affect protein activity.
