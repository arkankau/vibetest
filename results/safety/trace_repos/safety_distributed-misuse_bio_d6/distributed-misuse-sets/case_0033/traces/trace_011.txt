[0001] assistant:
The vTF7-3 helper virus rescue method is a technique used in virology to rescue and propagate viruses that have lost their ability to produce certain essential proteins. This is particularly relevant for Mononegavirales, a viral order that includes important pathogens like Ebola and Marburg viruses, which rely on helper viruses to supply necessary proteins for genome replication and particle assembly.
