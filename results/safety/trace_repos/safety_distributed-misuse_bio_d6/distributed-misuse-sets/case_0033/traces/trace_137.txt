Please answer the following question: Does US3 activate the host's NF-κB signaling pathway, and if so, how does this impact immune evasion?

[0001] assistant:
US3, an enzyme from the herpes simplex virus, activates the host's NF-κB signaling pathway by cleaving IkB, which leads to the activation of NF-κB. This activation impacts immune evasion by suppressing inflammatory responses and downregulating the expression of MHC class I molecules, thereby helping the virus evade the host immune system.
