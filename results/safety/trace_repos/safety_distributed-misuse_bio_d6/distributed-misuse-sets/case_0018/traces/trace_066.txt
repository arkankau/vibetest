Please answer the following question: How does the integration capacity of adenoviral vectors influence the stability and expression of miRNAs delivered to cells?

[0001] assistant:
The integration capacity of adenoviral vectors does not directly influence the stability and expression of miRNAs because adenoviral vectors do not integrate into the host genome; they remain episomal. Therefore, the stability and expression of miRNAs delivered by adenoviruses are primarily determined by factors such as vector design, miRNA sequence, and cellular environment.
