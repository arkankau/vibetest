Please answer the following question: Does the NS1A protein of influenza A virus cleave host cell pre-mRNAs to generate primers for viral RNA synthesis?

[0001] assistant:
No, the NS1A protein of influenza A virus does not cleave host cell pre-mRNAs to generate primers for viral RNA synthesis. The NS1 protein, rather than NS1A, is involved in various functions including suppression of the host's antiviral interferon response, but its exact role in RNA synthesis is not to cleave host pre-mRNAs.
