[0001] assistant:
Using an IRES (Internal Ribosome Entry Site) sequence can lead to more efficient translation of the downstream gene because it allows for simultaneous translation from a single mRNA molecule, potentially increasing expression levels compared to identical promoters which generate separate mRNAs for each gene.
