Please answer the following question: What are the limitations of restriction fragment length polymorphism (RFLP) analysis when it comes to identifying specific mutations in rapidly mutating viruses like Ebola?

[0001] assistant:
RFLP analysis has limitations when identifying specific mutations in rapidly mutating viruses like Ebola because it requires known restriction enzyme sites and may not detect point mutations or insertions/deletions directly. Additionally, the process is labor-intensive and time-consuming, which is less suitable for monitoring rapid viral mutations.
