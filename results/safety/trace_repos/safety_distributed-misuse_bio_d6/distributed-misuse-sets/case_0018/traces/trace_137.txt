Please answer the following question: What is the primary function of the protease protein in herpesvirus and how does it relate to viral DNA synthesis?

[0001] assistant:
The primary function of the protease protein in herpesvirus is to cleave polyproteins into functional viral enzymes, including those involved in DNA synthesis. This cleavage is crucial for the maturation of viral proteins and the initiation of viral DNA replication within the host cell.
