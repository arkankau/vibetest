Please answer the following question: How does using a bacterial artificial chromosome (BAC) in reverse genetics ensure the high fidelity of the viral genome sequence?

[0001] assistant:
Using a bacterial artificial chromosome (BAC) in reverse genetics does not directly ensure high fidelity of the viral genome sequence. Instead, BACs are used to clone, manipulate, and maintain large segments of genomic DNA, which can then be introduced into cells to study gene function. High fidelity of the viral genome sequence is typically ensured through molecular cloning techniques and sequencing methods rather than the technology itself.
