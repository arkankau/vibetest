Adding transposon insertion sites into the target plasmid facilitates the random insertion of transposons, which can mutate the plasmid DNA more efficiently. This process does not affect the host genome because the transposons are confined to the plasmid, allowing for enhanced mutagenesis rates specifically within the plasmid DNA.
