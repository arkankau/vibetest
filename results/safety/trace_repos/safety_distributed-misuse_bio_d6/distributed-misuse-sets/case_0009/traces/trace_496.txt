Please answer the following question: Does TAR cloning eliminate the need for in vitro transcription of the viral genome?

[0001] assistant:
No, TAR cloning does not eliminate the need for in vitro transcription of the viral genome; it is used to create a circular plasmid that includes the viral genome and a TAR sequence for encapsidation, but the genome still needs to be transcribed in vitro to produce RNA for infection.
