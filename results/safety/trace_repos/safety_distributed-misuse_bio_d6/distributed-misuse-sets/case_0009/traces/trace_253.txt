Please answer the following question: Can the polarity of the RNA genome affect the stability or function of the viral RNA during in vitro transcription for a reverse genetics system?

[0001] assistant:
The polarity of the RNA genome can affect the stability and function of the viral RNA during in vitro transcription for a reverse genetics system. Typically, negative-sense RNA genomes require an RNA-dependent RNA polymerase to produce positive-sense RNA, which is functional, while positive-sense RNA genomes are directly infectious.
