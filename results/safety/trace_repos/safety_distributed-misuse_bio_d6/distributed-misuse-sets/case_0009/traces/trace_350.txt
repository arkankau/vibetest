Please answer the following question: What are the main subunits of the hemagglutinin (HA) glycoprotein of influenza A viruses, and what are their typical functions?

[0001] assistant:
The main subunits of the hemagglutinin (HA) glycoprotein of influenza A viruses are HA1 and HA2. HA1 is responsible for binding to sialic acid receptors on host cells, while HA2 is involved in fusion of the viral and cellular membranes.
