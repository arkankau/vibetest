Please answer the following question: How does a nonpolar mutation help in the study of gene function and gene interaction in pathogenic bacteria?

[0001] assistant:
A nonpolar mutation introduces a stop codon or creates a premature stop, which often inactivates the gene product without disrupting the rest of the gene sequence. This allows researchers to study the specific function of the gene and its interactions with other genes in a more controlled manner, as the mutation does not affect nearby coding sequences.
