Please answer the following question: How does nirmatrelvir interact with the enzyme it targets in SARS-CoV-2, and what is the therapeutic benefit of this interaction?

[0001] assistant:
Nirmatrelvir inhibits the main protease (Mpro) of SARS-CoV-2 by binding to its active site, preventing viral polyproteins from being cleaved into functional enzymes necessary for virus replication. This interaction blocks viral replication, reducing the spread of the virus within infected cells and lowering viral load, which is the therapeutic benefit.
