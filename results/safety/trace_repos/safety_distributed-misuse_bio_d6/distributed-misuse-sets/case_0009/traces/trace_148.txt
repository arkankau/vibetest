[0001] assistant:
Elevated levels of pro-inflammatory cytokines like IL-6 and TNF-α contribute to inflammatory injuries and fibrosis in the respiratory tracts during SARS-CoV-2 infection by promoting inflammation and tissue damage. These cytokines drive the M1 macrophage polarization, which leads to an inflammatory response, but can also transition to M2 macrophages that promote resolution and repair, thus balancing the inflammatory process.
