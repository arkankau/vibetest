Please answer the following question: Is the National Science and Engineering Board for Biosecurity involved in regulating dangerous pathogens in the United States, or is its role more focused on advisory functions?

[0001] assistant:
The National Science and Engineering Board for Biosecurity (NSEBB) provides advisory functions to the U.S. government on biosecurity, but it does not directly regulate dangerous pathogens. The regulatory functions are primarily carried out by agencies like the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) and the National Institutes of Health (NIH).
