Please answer the following question: What is the function of introns within the coding sequence and how might their presence affect mRNA surveillance by the cellular machinery?

[0001] assistant:
Introns are non-coding sequences within genes that are transcribed into RNA but must be removed by splicing before the mRNA is functional. Their presence can affect mRNA surveillance by potentially leading to the production of premature stop codons or other splicing errors, which the cellular machinery must recognize and correct to ensure proper gene expression.
