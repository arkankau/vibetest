Please answer the following question: How do immune responses pose a challenge in the use of adenoviral vectors for gene therapy, and what are the critical considerations to avoid them?

[0001] assistant:
Immune responses, particularly the production of neutralizing antibodies, can limit the efficacy and durability of adenoviral vectors in gene therapy by neutralizing the vector before it can deliver the therapeutic gene. To avoid these challenges, it is critical to use novel serotypes, develop vector modifications that evade immune detection, and employ prime-boost strategies with different vectors.
