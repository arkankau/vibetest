Please answer the following question: What is CRISPR/Cas9 technology and how does it enable the rapid replacement of large regions of a bacterial genome?

[0001] assistant:
CRISPR/Cas9 is a gene-editing technology that uses a Cas9 enzyme guided by RNA to make precise cuts in DNA. This allows for the rapid and efficient replacement of large regions of a bacterial genome by introducing new DNA sequences at the cut sites.
