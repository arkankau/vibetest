Please answer the following question: How does a ribosomal frameshift signal affect the translation of viral mRNA during the process of viral reverse genetics?

[0001] assistant:
A ribosomal frameshift signal in viral mRNA can alter the reading frame during translation, leading to the production of a non-functional protein. This is often used in viral reverse genetics to create a dominant-negative effect or to disrupt the protein's function, thereby inhibiting viral replication.
