The cytoplasm is crucial in gene therapy and viral gene expression because it contains the necessary cellular machinery for translating viral mRNA into proteins. Viral genetic material must be released into the cytoplasm for this process to occur, as the cytoplasm provides the ribosomes and tRNAs required for protein synthesis.
