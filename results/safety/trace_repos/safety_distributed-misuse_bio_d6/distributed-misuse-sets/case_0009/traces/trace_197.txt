Please answer the following question: How does the E1 glycoprotein act as a binding site for host cell receptors in CHIKV infection, and which receptors are primarily involved in this process?

[0001] assistant:
The E1 glycoprotein of Chikungunya virus (CHIKV) acts as a binding site for host cell receptors, primarily through interactions with toll-like receptor 7 (TLR7) and glycosaminoglycans (GAGs) like heparan sulfate. These interactions facilitate viral entry into the host cell.
