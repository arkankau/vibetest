Please answer the following question: How does inserting a therapeutic gene within the thymidine kinase gene serve as a selection marker for gene therapy, and what are the advantages and disadvantages of this approach?

[0001] assistant:
Inserting a therapeutic gene within the thymidine kinase (TK) gene serves as a selection marker because the TK gene confers sensitivity to ganciclovir, an antiviral drug. Cells containing the therapeutic gene, which disrupts TK function, survive when exposed to ganciclovir, allowing for the selection of cells that have taken up the therapeutic gene. Advantages include efficient targeting and selection of genetically modified cells. Disadvantages include potential cytotoxicity from ganciclovir and the risk of off-target effects from disrupting the TK gene.
