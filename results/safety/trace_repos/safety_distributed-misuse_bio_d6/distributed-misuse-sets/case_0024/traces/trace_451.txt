[0001] assistant:
Cytokine storm syndrome (CSS) in the context of SARS-CoV-2 infection is characterized by an excessive and uncontrolled release of pro-inflammatory cytokines and chemokines, leading to severe inflammation and potential organ damage. Unlike a normal inflammatory response, which is regulated and targeted, CSS involves a widespread and overwhelming immune reaction that can be harmful to the host.
