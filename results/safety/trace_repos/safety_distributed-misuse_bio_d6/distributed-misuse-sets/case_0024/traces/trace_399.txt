Please answer the following question: Is there a risk of RNA being highly stable and persisting in the environment for long periods when handling SARS-CoV-2 genomic RNA in a laboratory?

[0001] assistant:
RNA, including that of SARS-CoV-2, is generally less stable than DNA and can break down relatively quickly under environmental conditions. However, under certain conditions, such as low temperatures and low humidity, SARS-CoV-2 RNA can persist for longer periods in the laboratory environment.
