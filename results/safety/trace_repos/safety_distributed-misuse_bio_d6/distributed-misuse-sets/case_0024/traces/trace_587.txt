Tegument proteins of herpesviruses have been shown to play crucial roles in viral replication, immune evasion, and regulation of gene expression. Studies have demonstrated that these proteins interact with cellular factors to facilitate viral life cycle processes, although the specific mechanisms can vary between different herpesvirus species.
