Please answer the following question: What is the ability of lentivirus vectors to carry large gene expression cassettes, and how does this compare to the size of a typical CRISPR expression cassette?

[0001] assistant:
Lentivirus vectors can carry large gene expression cassettes, up to several kilobases. In contrast, a typical CRISPR expression cassette is relatively small, usually around 1-2 kilobases.
