Please answer the following question: What is the role of the envelope plasmid in the production of high-titer lentivirus when transfecting HEK293T cells?

[0001] assistant:
The envelope plasmid encodes viral envelope proteins that are necessary for the budding and release of the lentivirus from HEK293T cells, facilitating the production of high-titer lentivirus.
