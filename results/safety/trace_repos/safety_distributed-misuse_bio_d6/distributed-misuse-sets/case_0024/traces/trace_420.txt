Please answer the following question: What is the process of Transformation-associated recombination (TAR) cloning and can it be used for cloning viral genomes?

[0001] assistant:
Transformation-associated recombination (TAR) cloning is a molecular biology technique that uses error-prone DNA repair mechanisms to integrate exogenous DNA into plasmids. Yes, it can be used for cloning viral genomes, though it is not as commonly used as other methods due to potential insertional mutagenesis.
