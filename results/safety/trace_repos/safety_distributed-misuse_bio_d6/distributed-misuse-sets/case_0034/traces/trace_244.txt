Please answer the following question: What are the advantages of using a bidirectional transcription vector over other types of vectors in reverse genetics for influenza A virus vaccines?

[0001] assistant:
Bidirectional transcription vectors allow for the simultaneous expression of both the viral antigen and a selectable marker from a single plasmid, which simplifies the production process and enhances efficiency. This feature can lead to faster vaccine development and potentially higher yields of the desired viral antigens.
