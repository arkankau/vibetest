Please answer the following question: How do inverted terminal repeats (ITRs) assist in the integration of the viral genome into the host cell's DNA in AAV vectors?

[0001] assistant:
Inverted terminal repeats (ITRs) assist in the integration of the viral genome into the host cell's DNA by serving as recognition sites for the integrase enzyme, which facilitates the covalent attachment of the viral DNA to the host genome.
