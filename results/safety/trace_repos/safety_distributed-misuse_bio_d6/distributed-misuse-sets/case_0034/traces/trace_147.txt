Please answer the following question: What is the role of regulatory DNA in bacteriophages, and why is it significant in the context of a minimal-genome phage?

[0001] assistant:
Regulatory DNA in bacteriophages controls the expression of essential genes, ensuring that the phage can infect and replicate within the host bacterium. In the context of a minimal-genome phage, regulatory DNA is significant because it allows the phage to function with the smallest possible genome by efficiently managing gene expression without redundant genetic material.
