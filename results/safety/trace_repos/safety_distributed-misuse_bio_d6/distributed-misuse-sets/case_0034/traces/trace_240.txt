Please answer the following question: What is pulse-chase analysis and how is it used to measure the rate of viral protein synthesis?

[0001] assistant:
Pulse-chase analysis is a technique used in virology and molecular biology to measure the rate of viral protein synthesis. In this method, cells are first exposed to a radioactive or labeled amino acid (pulse), which allows newly synthesized proteins to be tracked. After a short period, labeled protein synthesis is halted by removing the labeled amino acids, and the cells are then cultured in unlabeled medium (chase). By analyzing the levels of radioactivity or labeled proteins over time, researchers can determine the rates of protein synthesis and degradation.
