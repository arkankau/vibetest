Please answer the following question: In the context of reverse genetics, what is the purpose of transfecting cells with plasmids containing viral RNA and proteins?

[0001] assistant:
The purpose of transfecting cells with plasmids containing viral RNA and proteins in reverse genetics is to generate and study the viral particles in a controlled manner, allowing for the analysis of viral gene function and the production of specific viral strains for research or vaccine development.
