Please answer the following question: Are the toxins found in nightshade leaves and solanum nigrum tea harmful to humans, and if so, what are their primary mechanisms of action?

[0001] assistant:
The toxins found in nightshade leaves, such as Atropine and Solanine, can be harmful to humans. Atropine can cause anticholinergic effects, leading to symptoms like dry mouth, blurred vision, and increased heart rate. Solanine can cause gastrointestinal symptoms, fever, and neurological issues by inhibiting acetylcholinesterase. Solanum nigrum tea, while generally safe in small quantities, may contain solanine and should be consumed with caution.
