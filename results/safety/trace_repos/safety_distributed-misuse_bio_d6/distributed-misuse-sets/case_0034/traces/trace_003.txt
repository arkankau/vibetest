A selectable marker gene in a viral vector expresses a protein that confers resistance to a specific selection pressure (like a drug) or confers a visible trait. Viral particles that have successfully integrated the desired genetic modifications also include this marker, allowing them to survive or be identified in the presence of the selection pressure or exhibit the visible trait.
