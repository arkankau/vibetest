Please answer the following question: Describe the characteristics of Madin-Darby canine kidney cells (MDCK cells) and their suitability for growing influenza viruses.

[0001] assistant:
MDCK cells are epithelial cells derived from the kidney of a Madin-Darby dog. They are commonly used for growing influenza viruses due to their ability to form syncytia and their suitability for viral propagation.
