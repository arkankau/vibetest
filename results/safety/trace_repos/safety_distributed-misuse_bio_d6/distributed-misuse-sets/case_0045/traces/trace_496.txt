Please answer the following question: What are some common symptoms observed in livestock that can indicate Rift Valley Fever (RVF) Virus infections?

[0001] assistant:
Common symptoms in livestock infected with Rift Valley Fever virus include fever, abortion in pregnant animals, stillbirths, and high mortality rates in newborns. Additionally, animals may exhibit lethargy, decreased milk production, and skin lesions.
