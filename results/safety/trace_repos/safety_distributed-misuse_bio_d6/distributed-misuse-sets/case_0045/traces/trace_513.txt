Please answer the following question: What are the key differences between proteins and biological toxins that could affect the use of protein purification methods on biological toxins?

Proteins are typically larger and more complex in structure compared to biological toxins, which can affect purification methods. Proteins often require more stringent conditions to avoid denaturation, whereas toxins may be more stable. Additionally, proteins are generally produced in larger quantities and have a wider range of physicochemical properties, necessitating different purification strategies compared to the often smaller and more specific toxins.
