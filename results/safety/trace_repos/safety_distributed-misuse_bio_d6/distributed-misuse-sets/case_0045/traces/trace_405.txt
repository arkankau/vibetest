Please answer the following question: How does the term 'syncytium' differ from the formation of inclusion bodies within the nucleus or cytoplasm of infected cells in herpesvirus research?

[0001] assistant:
In herpesvirus research, a syncytium refers to the formation of large, multinucleated cells due to the fusion of infected cells, while inclusion bodies are specific structures within the nucleus or cytoplasm of infected cells that contain viral components and are not related to cell fusion.
