Please answer the following question: How does IFA assist in identifying the timing and pattern of viral protein expression in infected cells?

[0001] assistant:
IFAR (Interferon Antiviral Response) assays can help identify the timing and pattern of viral protein expression by measuring the induction of interferon-stimulated genes (ISGs) in infected cells. This allows researchers to correlate specific viral protein expression stages with cellular antiviral responses.
