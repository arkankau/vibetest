Please answer the following question: What is the advantage of using axenic culture followed by incubation with host cells and observation for cell death to determine the infectious nature of C. burnetii?

[0001] assistant:
The advantage of this method is that it allows for the specific identification and isolation of the infectious agent Coxiella burnetii by first growing it in a pure culture (axenic culture) and then observing its ability to cause cell death in host cells, which indicates its virulence and infectivity.
