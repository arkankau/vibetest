Please answer the following question: How do oncogenic herpesviruses typically manipulate the host cell cycle to evade the host immune system?

[0001] assistant:
Oncogenic herpesviruses typically manipulate the host cell cycle by expressing viral proteins that interfere with key regulatory proteins, such as p53 and RB, leading to cell proliferation. This allows the viruses to replicate and evade immune detection.
