Please answer the following question: How can the use of synonymous mutations aid in the tracking and analysis of recombinant viruses during reverse genetics studies?

[0001] assistant:
Synonymous mutations, which replace one codon with another for the same amino acid, do not affect protein sequence but can change RNA structure or stability. These mutations can serve as molecular "barcodes" to track recombinant viruses during reverse genetics studies by allowing the differentiation of viral strains while the protein function remains unchanged.
