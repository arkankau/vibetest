Please answer the following question: How does vIL-10 influence the cytotoxic activity of natural killer (NK) cells, and what is the impact on immune clearance of infected cells?

[0001] assistant:
vIL-10 inhibits the cytotoxic activity of NK cells by suppressing their degranulation and cytokine production. This reduces their ability to clear virus-infected cells efficiently.
