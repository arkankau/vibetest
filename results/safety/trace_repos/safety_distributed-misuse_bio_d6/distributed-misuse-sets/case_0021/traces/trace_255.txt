Please answer the following question: How does EBNA1 contribute to the stabilization and maintenance of the Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) genome within the host cell?

[0001] assistant:
EBNA1 (Epstein-Barr Nuclear Antigen 1) contributes to the stabilization and maintenance of the EBV genome by interacting with the viral genome and chromatin, facilitating its episomal persistence within the host cell, and regulating gene expression.
