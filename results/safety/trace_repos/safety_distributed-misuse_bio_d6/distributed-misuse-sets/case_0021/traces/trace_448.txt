Please answer the following question: How does the interaction between PBP-2A and β-lactam antibiotics lead to antibiotic resistance in Staphylococcus aureus?

[0001] assistant:
PBP-2A, an antibiotic-inactivating β-lactamase, interacts with β-lactam antibiotics by hydrolyzing the β-lactam ring, rendering the antibiotics ineffective. This interaction leads to antibiotic resistance in Staphylococcus aureus by allowing the bacteria to survive and reproduce in the presence of these antibiotics.
