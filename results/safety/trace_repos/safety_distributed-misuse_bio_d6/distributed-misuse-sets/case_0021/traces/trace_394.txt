Please answer the following question: How does a complementation assay assist in understanding the phenotypic effects of gene mutations in a pathogen during reverse genetics studies?

[0001] assistant:
A complementation assay helps determine whether two mutations in the same gene are in different alleles, indicating that the genes are the same. If one mutant allele can restore the wild-type phenotype when paired with another mutant allele, it suggests that the mutations are in the same gene and can aid in understanding the gene's function and the effects of mutations on the pathogen's phenotype.
