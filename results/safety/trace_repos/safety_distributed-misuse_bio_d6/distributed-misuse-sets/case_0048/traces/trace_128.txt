Please answer the following question: How does the full-length spike (S) protein-based subunit vaccine typically induce an immune response, and what are the potential limitations of this approach?

[0001] assistant:
The full-length spike (S) protein-based subunit vaccine typically induces an immune response by presenting the S protein to the immune system, which then produces antibodies and T-cell responses against the S protein of the virus. Potential limitations include the need for multiple doses and possibly lower efficacy compared to mRNA vaccines.
