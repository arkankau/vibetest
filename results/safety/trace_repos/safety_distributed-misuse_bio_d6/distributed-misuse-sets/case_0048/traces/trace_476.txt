Please answer the following question: How does the 5' termini sequence ensure accurate initiation of transcription by the RNA-dependent RNA polymerase?

[0001] assistant:
The 5' termini sequence does not directly ensure accurate initiation of transcription by RNA-dependent RNA polymerase; this function is primarily related to DNA-dependent RNA polymerases. The 5' termini of viral RNA genomes, however, often have specific sequences that are recognized by the RNA-dependent RNA polymerase to initiate replication.
