Please answer the following question: What is the evidence linking SARS-CoV2 to pangolins as a possible host?

[0001] assistant:
There is evidence suggesting that pangolins can be infected with SARS-CoV2 or similar coronaviruses, but the virus does not seem to replicate efficiently in them. This, along with genetic analysis, has led researchers to propose pangolins as a potential intermediate host between bats (the likely original host) and humans. However, the exact role of pangolins in the emergence of SARS-CoV2 remains speculative.
