Please answer the following question: What is the F2A sequence and how does it function to enable the expression of two proteins from a single mRNA transcript?

[0001] assistant:
The F2A sequence is a fusion sequence that allows for the co-expression of two proteins from a single mRNA transcript. It functions by enabling ribosomes to read through a short internal stop codon, translating the mRNA to produce a chimeric protein that contains both proteins.
