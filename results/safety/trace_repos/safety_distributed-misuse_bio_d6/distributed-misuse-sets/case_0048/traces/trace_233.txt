Please answer the following question: Is there evidence that measles virus spreads systemically via infected circulating CD150+ immune cells, and if so, how does this mechanism compare to the others in terms of significance?

[0001] assistant:
There is evidence suggesting that measles virus can spread systemically through infected CD150+ (SLAM) T cells, which can circulate in the blood. This mechanism is significant because it allows the virus to evade the initial immune response and spread to new tissues, contributing to the severe complications associated with measles. However, its exact role compared to other mechanisms, such as direct cell-to-cell transmission or spread via infected dendritic cells, is still being researched and may vary in significance depending on the context and stage of infection.
