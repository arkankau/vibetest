[0001] assistant:
A rescue system in reverse genetics experiments provides a way to restore gene function in a knockout virus, allowing researchers to study the effects of the gene in the presence of its wild-type counterpart. This contributes to understanding viral gene functions and interactions by elucidating the roles of specific genes and how they interact with the host cell and other viral genes.
