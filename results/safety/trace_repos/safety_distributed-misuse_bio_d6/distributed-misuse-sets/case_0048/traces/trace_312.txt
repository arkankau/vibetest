Please answer the following question: What is the P/V gene, and how is it present in human parainfluenza viruses?

[0001] assistant:
The P/V gene in human parainfluenza viruses refers to a polymerase gene, which is part of the viral genome responsible for producing the viral RNA polymerase. This gene is present in all four types of human parainfluenza viruses and is essential for the viral replication process.
