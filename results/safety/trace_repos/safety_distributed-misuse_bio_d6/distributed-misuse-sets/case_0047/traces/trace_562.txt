Please answer the following question: What is the role of agarose in gel electrophoresis, and how could changes in agarose concentration affect the observed fragments?

[0001] assistant:
Agarose in gel electrophoresis provides the solid matrix through which DNA or protein samples are separated. Increasing agarose concentration results in a more viscous gel, which can slow down larger fragments more significantly, while smaller fragments may move more freely.
