Please answer the following question: How does the viral nuclear egress complex (NEC) ensure the successful egress of HSV from the nucleus to the cytoplasm of the infected cell?

[0001] assistant:
The viral nuclear egress complex (NEC) ensures the successful egress of HSV from the nucleus to the cytoplasm by facilitating the degradation of nuclear membrane proteins and restructuring the nuclear pores to allow viral egress. This process involves the action of viral proteins that interact with cellular factors to remodel the nuclear environment, enabling the release of virions into the cytoplasm.
