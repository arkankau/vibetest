Please answer the following question: What is the function of RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (RdRp) in the replication cycle of influenza A viruses, considering that it degrades the host cell's mRNA to facilitate the takeover of the cellular machinery?

[0001] assistant:
RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (RdRp) in influenza A viruses plays a critical role in synthesizing negative-strand RNA from the positive-strand RNA genome, which is essential for viral replication. This enzyme also degrades the host cell's mRNA to clear the way for viral mRNA to be translated, facilitating the takeover of the cellular machinery for viral protein synthesis.
