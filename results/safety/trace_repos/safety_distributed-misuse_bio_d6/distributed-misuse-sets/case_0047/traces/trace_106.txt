Please answer the following question: What impact does alternating intense rainfall and dry conditions have on the survival and transmission of B. anthracis to livestock?

[0001] assistant:
Alternating intense rainfall and dry conditions can increase the survival of B. anthracis spores in the soil, thereby enhancing the risk of livestock exposure and infection. Wet periods help spores germinate and survive, while dry periods facilitate their dispersal through hoof action and other means.
