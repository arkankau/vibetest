Please answer the following question: Do ferrets exhibit susceptibility to SARS-CoV-2, influenza A, and Clostridium difficile in laboratory studies?

[0001] assistant:
Ferrets are susceptible to SARS-CoV-2 and can transmit the virus to other ferrets, making them useful for studying COVID-19. They are also susceptible to influenza A and can be used to study its effects. However, there is no evidence that ferrets are naturally susceptible to Clostridium difficile.
