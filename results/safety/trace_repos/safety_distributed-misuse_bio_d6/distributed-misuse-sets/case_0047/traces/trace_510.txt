Please answer the following question: What is the mechanism of action of Delta-ctenitoxin-Pn1a from Phoneutria nigriventer, CARDS toxin from Mycoplasma pneumoniae, and epsilon toxin from Clostridium perfringens, and does any of these mechanisms involve the inhibition of protein synthesis in cells?

[0001] assistant:
Delta-ctenitoxin-Pn1a from Phoneutria nigriventer acts by blocking voltage-gated sodium channels, CARDS toxin from Mycoplasma pneumoniae inhibits the eukaryotic translation initiation factor 2α (eIF2α), and epsilon toxin from Clostridium perfringens enters cells and inactivates the ADP-ribosylation factor 6 (Arf6). Only the CARDS toxin involves the inhibition of protein synthesis by disrupting the eIF2α, which is crucial for translation initiation.
