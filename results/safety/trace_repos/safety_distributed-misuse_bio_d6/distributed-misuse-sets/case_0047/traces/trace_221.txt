Please answer the following question: How can qPCR be used to quantify the number of viral copies in a sample, and what are its limitations without a reporter gene?

[0001] assistant:
qPCR can be used to quantify viral copies by amplifying viral DNA sequences and comparing the cycle threshold (Ct) values to a standard curve of known viral concentrations. Without a reporter gene, qPCR is limited in accuracy because it cannot account for differences in viral genome copy number or potential inhibition of the reaction, leading to potentially inaccurate quantification.
