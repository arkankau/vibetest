Please answer the following question: What is the known relationship between heparan sulfate and viral entry into host cells, and how does the upregulation of heparanase affect this relationship?

[0001] assistant:
Heparan sulfate proteoglycans (HSPGs) on the cell surface act as receptors for many viruses, facilitating their entry into host cells. Upregulation of heparanase, an enzyme that degrades heparan sulfate, can disrupt these interactions, potentially inhibiting viral entry.
