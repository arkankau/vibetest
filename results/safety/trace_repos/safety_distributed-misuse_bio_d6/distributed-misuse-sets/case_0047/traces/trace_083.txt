Please answer the following question: How does reverse genetics enable the study of the effects of mutations on viral fitness and pathogenicity in a controlled environment?

[0001] assistant:
Reverse genetics allows for the introduction of specific mutations into viral genomes, enabling researchers to study how these changes affect viral fitness and pathogenicity under controlled conditions. By synthesizing viral RNA from known sequences and infecting cells, scientists can directly test the consequences of genetic modifications.
