Please answer the following question: How does the antigen presentation of viral peptides contribute to the immune response against SARS-CoV-2?

[0001] assistant:
Viral peptides from SARS-CoV-2 are presented on the surface of infected cells by MHC molecules, which are recognized by T cells. This process helps activate both CD4+ and CD8+ T cells, facilitating the immune response that can eliminate infected cells and provide memory for future responses.
