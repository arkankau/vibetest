[0001] assistant:
The promoter of arenaviruses, particularly the leader promoter, has a sequence known as the transcription initiation site (TIS), which can specifically interact with the viral replication complex. Key residues involved include the U-rich region, which is crucial for binding to the viral RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (RdRP) complex.
