Please answer the following question: Does EBNA1 facilitate the entry of the virus into the host cell during the EBV life cycle?

[0001] assistant:
EBNA1 does not directly facilitate the entry of the virus into the host cell. Its primary function is to maintain the episome in the host cell and regulate viral gene expression during the latent phase of the Epstein-Barr Virus (EBV) life cycle.
