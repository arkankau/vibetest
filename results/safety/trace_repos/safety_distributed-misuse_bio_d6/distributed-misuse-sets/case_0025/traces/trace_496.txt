Please answer the following question: What is the role of a T7 promoter in the context of reverse genetics systems, and how is it used with full-length viral cDNA or RNA?

[0001] assistant:
The T7 promoter is used to drive the expression of full-length viral cDNA or RNA in a host cell that has been transfected with T7 RNA polymerase. This allows for the synthesis of viral genetic material in the absence of the virus itself, facilitating the study of viral genes and functions.
