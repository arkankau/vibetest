Please answer the following question: What are the advantages and limitations of using restriction enzyme digestion followed by gel purification in recombinant HSV generation for gene therapy?

[0001] assistant:
Advantages include precise insertion of genetic material, easy to standardize, and allows for the removal of uncut DNA. Limitations involve potential loss of DNA during purification, complexity in multiple cloning sites, and the need for restriction sites in the therapeutic gene.
