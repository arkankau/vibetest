Please answer the following question: Are there any other viral genes that are commonly used for the insertion of foreign genes, and if so, what are their characteristics?

[0001] assistant:
Yes, besides retroviruses like lentiviruses, adenoviruses and adeno-associated viruses (AAVs) are commonly used for inserting foreign genes. AAVs are known for their high transduction efficiency, lack of immunogenicity, and ability to infect non-dividing cells. Adenoviruses are good at delivering large genetic material and can be easily produced in high titers.
