Please answer the following question: What is the LPxTG secretion/surface display motif and how does it contribute to the successful display of proteins on the outer surface of B. anthracis spores?

[0001] assistant:
The LPxTG motif is a sequence found in proteins that B. anthracis uses for secretion and surface display. It contributes to the successful display of proteins on the outer surface of B. anthracis spores by facilitating their transport across the bacterial membrane and anchoring them to the spore coat.
