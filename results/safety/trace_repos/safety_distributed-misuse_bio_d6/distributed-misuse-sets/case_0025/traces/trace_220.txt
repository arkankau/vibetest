[0001] assistant:
The primary function of tegument proteins in herpesvirus infection is to modulate the host cell environment, facilitating viral replication and spread. UL13 is a tegument protein that plays a role in regulating viral gene expression and may be involved in protecting the viral genome from host immune responses.
