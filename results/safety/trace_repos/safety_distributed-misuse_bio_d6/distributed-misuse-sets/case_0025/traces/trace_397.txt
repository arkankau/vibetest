Please answer the following question: What was the historical context and timeline for the application of reverse genetics to Poliomyelitis virus, and was it the first virus to have this technique applied?

[0001] assistant:
Reverse genetics was first applied to the Poliovirus in 1989 by researchers who constructed a poliovirus genome from cDNA and infected cells with it. This technique allowed for the study of viral gene function and the development of vaccines. While Poliovirus was one of the early viruses to which reverse genetics was applied, it was not the first; the technique was previously applied to influenza virus in 1983.
