[0001] assistant:
Residue 587 in the adeno-associated virus (AAV) capsid is a lysine (K587). This residue plays a crucial role in viral attachment and tropism by interacting with heparan sulfate proteoglycans on cell surfaces, facilitating the initial binding and subsequent internalization of the virus.
