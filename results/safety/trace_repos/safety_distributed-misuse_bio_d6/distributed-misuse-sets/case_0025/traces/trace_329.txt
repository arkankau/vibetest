Please answer the following question: How do high-concentration antibiotics affect viral replication and selection in packaging cells during viral reverse genetics?

[0001] assistant:
High-concentration antibiotics can inhibit bacterial growth in packaging cells, which is beneficial for maintaining a sterile environment. However, they do not directly affect viral replication or selection; this is more influenced by the presence of antibiotics in the cell culture medium rather than their action on viruses.
