Please answer the following question: Describe the function of 5' and 3' terminal sequences in the assembly of the viral capsid and how they relate to the formation of virions.

[0001] assistant:
The 5' and 3' terminal sequences are crucial for the packaging of viral genomes into capsids. Typically, the 3' sequence acts as a signal for the viral polymerase to initiate genome synthesis, while the 5' sequence may help in initiating RNA synthesis or in the correct positioning of the genome within the capsid. These sequences ensure that the viral genome is properly assembled and packaged, facilitating the formation of infective virions.
