Please answer the following question: Can the sgRNA act as a primer for reverse transcription in CRISPR-Cas9 experiments with RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
I don't know. The sgRNA in CRISPR-Cas9 systems is typically single-stranded RNA designed to guide the Cas9 enzyme to a specific genomic locus. It is not designed to serve as a primer for reverse transcription. Reverse transcription is typically carried out by reverse transcriptase using a DNA or RNA primer, not an sgRNA.
