Prion diseases are characterized by progressive neurodegeneration leading to symptoms such as cognitive decline, motor dysfunction, and behavioral changes. Outcomes are typically fatal. If a researcher is infected, they may experience similar symptoms, and the infection could be transmitted through certain procedures, posing a risk to health and safety.
