Please answer the following question: What is the potential for reduced contamination when using primary cells or organ cultures in reverse genetics studies of viruses, and how does this compare to continuous cell lines?

[0001] assistant:
Using primary cells or organ cultures in reverse genetics studies of viruses can reduce contamination compared to continuous cell lines because primary cells and organ cultures are derived directly from the organism and are less prone to carrying or supporting a wide range of contaminants. Continuous cell lines, on the other hand, are more susceptible to contamination due to their indefinite proliferative capacity and frequent use in research settings.
