Please answer the following question: What is the role of including replication and capsid genes in the AAV genome, and is it essential for the protein vector?

[0001] assistant:
The replication and capsid genes in the AAV genome are not typically included in the final vector because AAV is designed to be replication-deficient. The capsid genes provide the necessary proteins to assemble the viral particle, while replication genes are not essential once the vector is properly constructed.
