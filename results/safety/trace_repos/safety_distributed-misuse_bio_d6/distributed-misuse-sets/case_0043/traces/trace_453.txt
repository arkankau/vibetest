Experimental techniques in reverse genetics for manipulating the HSV (Herpes Simplex Virus) genome include homologous recombination, transgenesis, and CRISPR-Cas9 editing. These methods allow for precise modification of the viral DNA to study gene function and viral pathogenesis.
