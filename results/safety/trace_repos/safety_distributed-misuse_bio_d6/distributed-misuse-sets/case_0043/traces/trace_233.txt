Please answer the following question: Does the hepatitis C virus use proteins made of peptides that are unable to be loaded onto MHC molecules to impair the immune response and persist as chronic infections?

[0001] assistant:
The hepatitis C virus (HCV) does use strategies to impair the immune response, but it primarily achieves this by interfering with the presentation of viral peptides on MHC molecules, not by using peptides that cannot be loaded onto MHC molecules. HCV targets and modifies the host's antigen presentation pathway to its advantage.
