[0001] assistant:
Yes, Candida auris shows a high prevalence of resistance to azole antifungal drugs. The specific mechanisms of resistance include overexpression of efflux pumps, mutations in the erg11 gene that encodes the target enzyme lanosterol 14α-demethylase, and modifications in the drug-binding site.
