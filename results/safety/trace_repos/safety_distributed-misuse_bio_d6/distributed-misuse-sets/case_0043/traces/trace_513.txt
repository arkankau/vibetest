Please answer the following question: What is the importance of particle size in ensuring effective delivery of aerosolized medications to the alveoli?

[0001] assistant:
Particle size is crucial for effective delivery of aerosolized medications to the alveoli because particles that are too large may deposit in the upper airways, while those that are too small may be exhaled before reaching the lungs. Particles in the 1-5 micrometer range are generally most effective for alveolar deposition.
