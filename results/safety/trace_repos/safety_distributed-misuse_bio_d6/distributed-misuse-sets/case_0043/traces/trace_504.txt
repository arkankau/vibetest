Please answer the following question: What specific features of viral proteins can be analyzed using IFA, and how does this relate to the study of recombinant viruses?

[0001] assistant:
IFA (Immunofluorescence Assay) can be used to analyze the localization and expression levels of specific viral proteins within host cells. This technique is particularly useful in the study of recombinant viruses as it allows researchers to track and identify the presence and distribution of introduced viral proteins, thereby facilitating the understanding of viral replication and host-virus interactions.
