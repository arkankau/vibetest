Please answer the following question: How does lysozyme affect gram-positive and gram-negative bacteria and what is its role in selecting for B. anthracis?

[0001] assistant:
Lysozyme affects gram-positive bacteria more effectively by breaking down their cell walls, which are rich in peptidoglycan. Gram-negative bacteria are less susceptible due to their additional outer membrane. Lysozyme does not directly select for B. anthracis; however, it can influence bacterial populations, potentially favoring growth conditions that B. anthracis can thrive in, such as those with reduced bacterial competition.
