Please answer the following question: In reverse genetics studies, how does the introduction of synonymous mutations contribute to the overall understanding of viral gene function and regulation?

[0001] assistant:
In reverse genetics studies, introducing synonymous mutations helps identify which codons are functionally important by preserving the amino acid sequence while altering the mRNA. This allows researchers to distinguish between codons that are tolerated and those that are critical for gene function and regulation.
