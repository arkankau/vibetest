Please answer the following question: What is the role of the ICP4 gene in the herpes simplex virus (HSV) life cycle and why is it important to avoid disrupting it in gene therapy vectors?

[0001] assistant:
The ICP4 gene in herpes simplex virus (HSV) is crucial as it encodes a transcription factor that activates the expression of other viral genes, essential for viral replication. Avoiding disruption of ICP4 in gene therapy vectors is important because it ensures the virus can still replicate and deliver therapeutic genes effectively.
