Please answer the following question: How does the interferon (IFN) system act as a source of antiviral factors for in vitro culture systems in reverse genetics studies?

[0001] assistant:
In reverse genetics studies, the interferon (IFN) system acts as a source of antiviral factors by inducing the expression of proteins that inhibit viral replication. This enhances the antiviral state of cells in in vitro culture systems, allowing for the study of viral gene functions and the effects of antiviral treatments.
