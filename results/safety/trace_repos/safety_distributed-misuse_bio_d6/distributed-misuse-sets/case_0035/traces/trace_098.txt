Please answer the following question: What does the concentration of viral particles measured by an endpoint dilution assay indicate about the efficiency of transfection?

[0001] assistant:
The concentration of viral particles measured by an endpoint dilution assay indicates the viral titer, which can reflect the efficiency of transfection, but not directly the efficiency of transfection itself. Transfection efficiency would need to be assessed separately, often through reporter gene expression or other markers.
