Please answer the following question: What is the significance of binding the poliovirus receptor in the context of poliovirus infection, and how does cold temperature (4C) influence this process?

[0001] assistant:
Binding the poliovirus receptor is crucial for poliovirus infection as it allows the virus to attach to and enter host cells. At 4°C, the cold temperature reduces the binding affinity of the virus for its receptor, making it more difficult for the virus to infect cells and effectively inhibiting its entry into the host.
