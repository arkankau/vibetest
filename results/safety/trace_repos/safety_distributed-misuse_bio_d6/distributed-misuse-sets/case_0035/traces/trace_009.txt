Please answer the following question: What is the mechanism of action of Ricin toxin from Ricinus communis, Shiga toxin from Shigella dysenteriae, and mistletoe lectin 1 from Viscum album, and does any of these mechanisms involve the inhibition of protein synthesis in cells?

[0001] assistant:
Ricin inactivates ribosomal RNA, Shiga toxin inhibits protein synthesis by binding to the 60S subunit of the ribosome, and mistletoe lectin 1 induces apoptosis through multiple mechanisms including disruption of the endoplasmic reticulum. All three mechanisms involve the inhibition of protein synthesis in cells.
