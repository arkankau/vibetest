Please answer the following question: What is heterologous expression and how can it be used to produce toxins from rare or difficult-to-maintain animals?

[0001] assistant:
Heterologous expression involves expressing genes from one organism within another, typically using bacteria, yeast, or mammalian cells. This method can be used to produce toxins from rare or difficult-to-maintain animals by inserting the toxin-producing genes into these host cells, allowing for easier and more controlled production of the toxins.
