Please answer the following question: How does the NF-κB pathway typically become activated in response to LMP1 expression and what role does it play in oncogenesis?

[0001] assistant:
The NF-κB pathway becomes activated in response to LMP1 expression by LMP1 interacting with receptors that signal through NF-κB, leading to its nuclear translocation and transcriptional activation. This activation plays a significant role in oncogenesis by promoting cell survival, proliferation, and inhibiting apoptosis, thereby contributing to tumor development.
