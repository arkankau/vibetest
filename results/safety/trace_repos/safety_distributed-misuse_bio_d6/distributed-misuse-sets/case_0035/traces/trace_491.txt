Insect cells can utilize key metabolic pathways such as glycosylation, ubiquitination, and SUMOylation for post-translational modifications of proteins. These modifications can be advantageous for expressing animal venom toxins compared to microbial systems because they allow for more accurate folding and post-translational modifications, which are crucial for the function of venom toxins.
