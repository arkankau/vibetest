Please answer the following question: How do immediate-early (IE) genes influence the expression of other viral genes during the reactivation phase of herpesvirus life cycle?

[0001] assistant:
Immediate-early (IE) genes are the first to be expressed during the reactivation phase of herpesvirus life cycles. They encode proteins that rapidly activate the transcription of other viral genes, including late genes, by establishing a permissive environment within the host cell.
