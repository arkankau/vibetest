Please answer the following question: How does electroporation temporarily permeabilize the cell membrane, and what is the benefit of this process for RNA transduction in reverse genetics studies?

[0001] assistant:
Electroporation involves applying an electric field to cells, which creates transient pores in the cell membrane, allowing molecules like RNA to enter the cell. This process is beneficial for RNA transduction in reverse genetics studies because it enables direct introduction of RNA into cells, facilitating the study of gene functions by modulating gene expression.
