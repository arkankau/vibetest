Please answer the following question: What is biosecurity, and how does it relate to the prevention of the misuse of biological agents such as viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Biosecurity is a set of practices and policies designed to protect living organisms from harmful biological agents. It plays a crucial role in preventing the misuse of biological agents like viruses by controlling access to potentially dangerous materials and facilities, ensuring proper containment and handling practices, and implementing security measures to prevent deliberate misuse.
