Common methods for extracting and identifying ancient viral DNA include PCR amplification, next-generation sequencing, and bioinformatics analysis. These methods can be applied to Neanderthal DNA by extracting viral DNA from skeletal remains or sedimentary samples. For wastewater DNA, viral sequences are extracted from sewage samples to trace viral prevalence. In mouse lymphocyte DNA, viruses can be detected by analyzing the lymphocytes' genomic material.
