Please answer the following question: What is the role of the T6SS gene cluster in Vibrio parahaemolyticus and how is it different from the T3SS gene clusters in relation to virulence and acute gastroenteritis?

[0001] assistant:
The T6SS gene cluster in Vibrio parahaemolyticus plays a role in bacterial competitiveness and defense against phage and predatory protists, but it is not directly involved in pathogenesis. In contrast, the T3SS gene clusters are crucial for virulence, facilitating the secretion of toxins that cause acute gastroenteritis in infected hosts.
