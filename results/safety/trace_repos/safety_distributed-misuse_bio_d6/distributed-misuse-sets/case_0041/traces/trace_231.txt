Please answer the following question: Are eukaryotic expression vectors commonly used in reverse genetics systems for DNA viruses, and if so, for what purpose?

[0001] assistant:
Yes, eukaryotic expression vectors are commonly used in reverse genetics systems for DNA viruses to facilitate the overexpression of viral genes, aid in the functional analysis of viral proteins, and support the generation of viral mutants for studying virus-host interactions and infection mechanisms.
