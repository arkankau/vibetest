Please answer the following question: What are the primary methods for introducing large DNA fragments into host cells in reverse genetics, and how does BAC compare to these methods?

[0001] assistant:
Primary methods for introducing large DNA fragments into host cells in reverse genetics include biolistic gene delivery, electroporation, and transfection using plasmid vectors. BAC (Bacterial Artificial Chromosome) vectors offer advantages such as large insert capacity (up to 300 kb), high copy number, and the ability to maintain large DNA segments in bacteria, making them particularly useful for studying complex genomes. Compared to other methods, BAC provides a more stable and reliable system for maintaining and manipulating large DNA fragments.
