Please answer the following question: How does the herpes simplex virus use its encoded ribonucleotide reductase under conditions of cellular stress?

[0001] assistant:
The herpes simplex virus encodes a ribonucleotide reductase that helps maintain nucleotide balance during cellular stress by synthesizing deoxyribonucleotides, which are essential for DNA replication and repair under stressful conditions.
