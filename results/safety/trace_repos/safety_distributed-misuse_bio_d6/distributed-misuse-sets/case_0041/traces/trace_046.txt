Please answer the following question: Cycloheximide, polymyxin B, and X-Indoxyl-choline phosphate are potential components. How do these agents, individually and in combination, aid in the selective isolation of B. anthracis?

[0001] assistant:
Cycloheximide and polymyxin B are used to inhibit protein synthesis and prevent cell wall synthesis, respectively, which helps in the selective isolation of B. anthracis by reducing growth of other bacteria. X-Indoxyl-choline phosphate serves as an iron source, which is advantageous for B. anthracis due to its iron-scavenging mechanisms. Combining these agents can enhance the selective growth of B. anthracis in culture.
