Computational modeling of RBD evolution under immune pressure involves simulating the interactions between the receptor-binding domain (RBD) of a virus and the immune system to predict how the virus might mutate to avoid immune recognition. This process can help identify potential escape mutations, guiding the design of more effective vaccines.
