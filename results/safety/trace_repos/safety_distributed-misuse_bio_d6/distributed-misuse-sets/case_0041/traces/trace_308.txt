Please answer the following question: What is the mechanism by which RNR supports the synthesis of deoxyribonucleotides needed for DNA replication?

[0001] assistant:
RNR (Ribonucleotide Reductase) reduces ribonucleotides to deoxyribonucleotides by catalyzing the conversion of ribonucleotides to their deoxy counterparts through a series of redox reactions. This mechanism is crucial for maintaining adequate levels of deoxyribonucleotides, which are required for DNA replication and repair.
