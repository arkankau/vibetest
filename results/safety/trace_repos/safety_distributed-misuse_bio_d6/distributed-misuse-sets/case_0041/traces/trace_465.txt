Firmicutes are a large phylum of bacteria commonly found in soil, water, plants, and the gastrointestinal tracts of animals, including humans. They exhibit diverse behaviors, such as fermentation and spore formation. While many Firmicutes are harmless or even beneficial (like those in the human gut), some species can contain infectious agents, particularly those in environments where they come into contact with humans or animals.
