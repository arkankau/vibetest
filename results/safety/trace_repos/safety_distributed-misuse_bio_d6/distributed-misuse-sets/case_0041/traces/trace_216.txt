Please answer the following question: How does reassortment allow for the exchange of gene segments between different viral strains in reverse genetics studies, and what is its importance for studying gene function?

[0001] assistant:
Reassortment allows for the exchange of gene segments between different viral strains by infecting cells simultaneously with two or more viruses, enabling the creation of recombinant viruses with mixed gene segments. This is important for studying gene function because it allows researchers to introduce specific gene segments into viruses to investigate their roles and interactions within the viral lifecycle.
