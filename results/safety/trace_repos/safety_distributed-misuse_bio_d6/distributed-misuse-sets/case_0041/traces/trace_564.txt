[0001] assistant:
Low-copy-number plasmids, like BACs (Bacterial Artificial Chromosomes), reduce the risk of spontaneous mutations in the viral genome because they are replicated fewer times per cell cycle, leading to less exposure to mutagenic agents and replication errors.
