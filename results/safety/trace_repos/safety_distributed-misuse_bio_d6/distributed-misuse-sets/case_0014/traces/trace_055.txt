Please answer the following question: What are the advantages of using a yeast shuttle vector over other methods in generating infectious cDNA clones of viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Yeast shuttle vectors allow for the easy cloning and propagation of cDNA inserts in yeast, facilitating the generation of infectious cDNA clones of viruses. This method is advantageous because it ensures high-fidelity cloning and can be efficiently screened and characterized in yeast before being transferred to mammalian cells for virus recovery.
