Please answer the following question: What is reverse genetics and how is it used in studying viruses, specifically human parainfluenza viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Reverse genetics is a method that allows researchers to generate viral genomes from cDNA, enabling the study of viral functions and the effects of genetic changes. In the context of human parainfluenza viruses, reverse genetics is used to introduce specific mutations or insert genes to understand viral pathogenesis and to develop vaccines and antiviral therapies.
