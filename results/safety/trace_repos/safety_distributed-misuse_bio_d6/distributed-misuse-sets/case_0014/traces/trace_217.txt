The NS1 protein of Zika virus (ZIKV) can influence infectivity in mosquitoes by interacting with the mosquito's endosomal and lysosomal pathways, potentially altering the viral life cycle and enhancing viral replication within the mosquito. Additionally, NS1 may interfere with the mosquito's antiviral responses, such as RNA interference, thereby promoting viral persistence.
