Please answer the following question: What is the significance of K27-/K29-linked ubiquitination in the context of herpesvirus infection and the antiviral response?

[0001] assistant:
K27- and K29-linked ubiquitination plays a crucial role in modulating the antiviral response during herpesvirus infection by influencing the stability and function of antiviral factors, such as interferon-stimulated genes and their transcription factors. This form of ubiquitination can either enhance or suppress the antiviral state, depending on the specific viral and cellular factors involved.
