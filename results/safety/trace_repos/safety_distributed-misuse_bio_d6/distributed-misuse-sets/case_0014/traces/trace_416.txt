[0001] assistant:
Different promoters (T7, T3, SP6, Sigma70) are used in in vitro transcription to generate RNA from a template DNA, with each having specific binding sites for its respective RNA polymerase. Reverse genetics using these promoters allows for the manipulation and study of gene function by introducing genomic RNA to host cells through in vitro transcription and subsequent transfection or injection.
