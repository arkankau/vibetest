Please answer the following question: Can direct injection of viral DNA into bacterial cells be effectively used to clone large DNA genomes of baculoviruses and herpesviruses into BACs?

[0001] assistant:
Direct injection of viral DNA into bacterial cells can be used for cloning, but it is more commonly associated with small DNA fragments. Cloning large DNA genomes of baculoviruses and herpesviruses into BACs (bacterial artificial chromosomes) typically requires more controlled methods such as restriction enzyme digestion and ligation, rather than direct injection.
