Please answer the following question: What is the function of the viral inhibitor of caspase activation (vICA) in the context of herpesvirus infection?

[0001] assistant:
The viral inhibitor of caspase activation (vICA) in the context of herpesvirus infection functions to inhibit caspase activation, thereby preventing the induction of apoptosis in infected cells and promoting viral propagation.
