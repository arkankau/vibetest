Please answer the following question: How does RNA interference work, and can it be used to engineer AAV variants that can traverse the blood-brain barrier?

[0001] assistant:
RNA interference (RNAi) works by introducing small interfering RNAs (siRNAs) that bind to and degrade specific messenger RNAs (mRNAs), thereby silencing the expression of particular genes. While RNAi has potential for treating brain disorders, engineering AAV variants to traverse the blood-brain barrier using RNAi is challenging and not yet widely applied due to delivery and safety issues.
