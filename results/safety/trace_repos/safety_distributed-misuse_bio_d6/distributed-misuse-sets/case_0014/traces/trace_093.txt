Polymerase cycling assembly (PCA) and yeast homologous recombination are both powerful techniques for synthesizing and assembling large viral genomes. PCA offers rapid and flexible assembly, while yeast homologous recombination provides high-fidelity integration and can handle larger DNA fragments, making both methods suitable for complex viral genome construction.
