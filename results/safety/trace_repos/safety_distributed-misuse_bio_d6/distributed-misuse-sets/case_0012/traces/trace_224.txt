Please answer the following question: What is the primary function of BZLF1 in the life cycle of herpesvirus and how does it contribute to the transition from latency to lytic infection?

[0001] assistant:
BZLF1 is a latency-breaking gene in herpesviruses. Its primary function is to initiate the transition from latency to lytic infection by expressing immediate-early proteins that activate the viral replication cycle.
