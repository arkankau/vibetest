Please answer the following question: How does cloning loxP sites in the same orientation in head-to-tail concatemers facilitate efficient circularization?

[0001] assistant:
Cloning loxP sites in the same orientation in head-to-tail concatemers facilitates efficient circularization by allowing Cre recombinase to act on all loxP sites sequentially around the circular DNA molecule, effectively joining the ends of the concatemer.
