Please answer the following question: Does the IRES element play a role in the integration of viral RNA into the host genome during the generation of recombinant RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
I don't know. The IRES (Internal Ribosome Entry Site) element is involved in the initiation of translation of certain viral mRNAs, but it is not typically associated with the integration of viral RNA into the host genome. That process is more commonly linked to retroviruses and their integrase enzyme.
