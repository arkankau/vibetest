Please answer the following question: How does the Nef protein in HIV-1 groups interact with the Vpu protein to counteract human tetherin?

[0001] assistant:
The Nef protein interacts with the Vpu protein to counteract human tetherin by promoting the degradation of tetherin through the ubiquitin-proteasome pathway. Nef enhances Vpu's ability to remove tetherin from the cell surface, thereby allowing HIV-1 to bud and release from infected cells more efficiently.
