Please answer the following question: What is the NLRP3 inflammasome and what is its primary function in the immune system?

[0001] assistant:
The NLRP3 inflammasome is a protein complex that plays a crucial role in the innate immune response. Its primary function is to detect pathogens and damaged cells, leading to the activation of inflammatory responses and the processing of pro-IL-1β and pro-IL-18 into their active forms.
