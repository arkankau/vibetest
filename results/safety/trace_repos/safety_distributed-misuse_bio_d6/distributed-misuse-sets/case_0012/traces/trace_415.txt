Please answer the following question: How does the use of a low-fidelity RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (RdRP) facilitate the study of viral evolution and adaptation in reverse genetics studies?

[0001] assistant:
A low-fidelity RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (RdRP) facilitates the study of viral evolution and adaptation in reverse genetics by introducing a high frequency of mutations, allowing researchers to rapidly generate and analyze a diverse pool of viral variants. This enables the tracking of evolutionary trajectories and the identification of traits associated with adaptation.
