Please answer the following question: How does the E1 glycoprotein contribute to the release of viral RNA into the host cell cytoplasm after the initial endocytosis step?

[0001] assistant:
The E1 glycoprotein of enveloped viruses, such as HIV, plays a crucial role in fusion between the viral and cellular membranes, facilitating the release of viral RNA into the host cell cytoplasm after endocytosis.
