Please answer the following question: Why is it necessary to monitor the genetic stability of a recombinant virus during multiple passages in cell culture?

[0001] assistant:
Monitoring the genetic stability of a recombinant virus during multiple passages in cell culture is necessary to ensure that the viral characteristics remain consistent and that the therapeutic or experimental properties are maintained. Genetic drift can lead to changes that may affect the virus's efficacy or safety.
