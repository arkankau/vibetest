Please answer the following question: What is the incubation period and the rapidity of progression for inhalation anthrax, oropharyngeal anthrax, and gastrointestinal anthrax?

The incubation period for inhalation anthrax is typically 1 to 6 days, with a rapidity of progression to severe illness within a week. Oropharyngeal anthrax has an incubation period of 1 to 7 days, progressing more slowly than inhalation anthrax. Gastrointestinal anthrax has an incubation period of 1 to 21 days, with a more gradual onset of symptoms compared to the other forms.
