Please answer the following question: Does glycoprotein B (gB) play a role in the regulation of viral gene expression during HSV infection?

[0001] assistant:
Glycoprotein B (gB) is involved in virus entry and cell fusion but does not directly regulate viral gene expression during HSV infection. Its primary role is in the early stages of infection, facilitating the spread of the virus to uninfected cells.
