Please answer the following question: How does the blood-brain barrier (BBB) impact the efficacy of direct intraparenchymal injections for brain-wide gene transfer in non-human primates?

[0001] assistant:
The blood-brain barrier (BBB) can limit the efficacy of direct intraparenchymal injections for brain-wide gene transfer in non-human primates by restricting the passage of large molecules and viruses used for gene delivery. This limitation can lead to localized gene transfer rather than achieving a uniform distribution throughout the brain.
