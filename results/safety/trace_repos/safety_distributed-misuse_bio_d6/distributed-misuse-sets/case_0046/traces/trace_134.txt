The process of integration of the viral genome into the host cell chromosome involves the viral enzyme integrase, which cuts the host DNA and inserts the viral DNA. This integration allows the herpesvirus to remain dormant within specific host cells, such as sensory neurons, without causing immediate disease symptoms but enabling potential reactivation later.
