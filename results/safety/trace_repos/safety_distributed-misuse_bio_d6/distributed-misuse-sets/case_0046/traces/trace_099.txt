Please answer the following question: What is the role of inverted terminal repeats (ITRs) in the replication and packaging of adeno-associated virus (AAV) vectors?

[0001] assistant:
Inverted terminal repeats (ITRs) serve as recognition sites for the viral replication proteins and are essential for the replication and packaging of adeno-associated virus (AAV) vectors, providing the necessary signals for the viral DNA to be copied and encapsidated into virus-like particles.
