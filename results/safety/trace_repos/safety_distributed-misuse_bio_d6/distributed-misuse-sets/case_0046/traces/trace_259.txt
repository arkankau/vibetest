Please answer the following question: What is the role of the common gamma chain in the IL-2 receptor complex and how might mutating it affect the immune response?

[0001] assistant:
The common gamma chain (γc) is a component of the IL-2 receptor complex that is shared by several cytokine receptors, including those for IL-2, IL-4, IL-7, IL-9, IL-15, and IL-21. Mutating the common gamma chain can impair the signaling of these cytokines, potentially leading to weakened immune responses, particularly in T-cell and B-cell activation and proliferation.
