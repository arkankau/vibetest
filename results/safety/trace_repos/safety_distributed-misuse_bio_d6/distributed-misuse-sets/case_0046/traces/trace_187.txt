Please answer the following question: What are the limitations of using Northern blot analysis for detecting segmented RNA in identifying novel IAV strains emerging from swine populations?

[0001] assistant:
Northern blot analysis has limitations in identifying novel Influenza A virus (IAV) strains emerging from swine populations because it requires prior knowledge of the specific RNA segments to design probes, and it may not detect novel or reassortant strains effectively.
