Please answer the following question: How does the exonuclease activity affect the accuracy and efficiency of DNA polymerase during DNA synthesis?

[0001] assistant:
Exonuclease activity allows DNA polymerase to proofread and remove incorrectly incorporated nucleotides, thereby increasing the accuracy of DNA synthesis. This activity also helps maintain the efficiency of DNA replication by facilitating the removal of mismatched or incorrectly paired nucleotides.
