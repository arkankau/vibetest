Please answer the following question: What are the typical characteristics of integrative and conjugative elements that might make them suitable for transferring pathogenicity islands and resistance cassettes?

[0001] assistant:
Integrative and conjugative elements, such as integrative conjugative elements (ICEs) and mobilizable elements, are typically mobile genetic elements that can integrate into and transfer DNA between bacterial cells. Their characteristics that make them suitable for transferring pathogenicity islands and resistance cassettes include conjugative transfer mechanisms, integration sites in bacterial chromosomes, and the ability to replicate independently.
