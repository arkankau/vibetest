Please answer the following question: How does the selection marker ensure that only cells infected with the recombinant virus survive antibiotic treatment?

[0001] assistant:
The selection marker ensures that only cells infected with the recombinant virus survive antibiotic treatment by being part of a gene that confers antibiotic resistance. When the recombinant virus integrates into the host cell genome, it carries this resistance gene, allowing those cells to survive in the presence of antibiotics, while uninfected cells die.
