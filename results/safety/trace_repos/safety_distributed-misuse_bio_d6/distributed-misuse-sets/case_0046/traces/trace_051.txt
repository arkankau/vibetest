Please answer the following question: List the mechanisms by which antiviral drugs targeting DNA polymerase inhibit viral DNA synthesis and explain why this makes DNA polymerase a key target.

[0001] assistant:
Antiviral drugs targeting DNA polymerase inhibit viral DNA synthesis by either directly binding to the enzyme and blocking its active site, thereby preventing the incorporation of nucleotides into the growing DNA strand, or by incorporating into the growing DNA strand as misincorporated nucleotides, causing premature chain termination. DNA polymerase is a key target because it is essential for viral DNA replication, and inhibiting it effectively halts the virus's ability to replicate its genetic material.
