Please answer the following question: What is the function of Integrase in the context of retroviruses and how does this relate to HSV-1?

[0001] assistant:
Integrase is a viral enzyme that integrates the viral DNA into the host cell's genome. While Integrase is specific to retroviruses, HSV-1 (Herpes Simplex Virus-1) does not use Integrase; instead, it uses viral proteins to facilitate its DNA entry and integration into the host cell, though this process is different from that of retroviruses.
