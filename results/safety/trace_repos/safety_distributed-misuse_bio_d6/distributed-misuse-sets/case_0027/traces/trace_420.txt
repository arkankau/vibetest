Please answer the following question: How does the EBV latent membrane protein 1 (LMP1) influence T cell-mediated immune responses and what are the implications for oncogenesis?

[0001] assistant:
EBV latent membrane protein 1 (LMP1) influences T cell-mediated immune responses by mimicking the signaling of CD40, leading to B cell survival and proliferation. This can impair T cell function and promote immune evasion. The implications for oncogenesis include chronic immune stimulation and the establishment of a permissive microenvironment that supports tumor growth.
