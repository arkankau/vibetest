Please answer the following question: What is the Gibson assembly method and how does it facilitate the assembly of multiple DNA fragments without the need for restriction enzymes?

[0001] assistant:
The Gibson assembly method is a DNA assembly technique that uses Gibson enzymes to join DNA fragments with overhangs that are compatible with the enzyme's activity, facilitating the assembly of multiple DNA fragments without the need for restriction enzymes or ligase. This method relies on exonuclease and polymerase activities to create and fill in gaps between DNA fragments, ensuring precise and efficient assembly.
