[0001] assistant:
Acquiring mammalian transmissibility in H5N1 viruses could change the receptors they bind to, shifting from avian receptors (which are primarily found in the intestines) to human receptors (found in the respiratory tract). This change is significant because it would allow the virus to spread more efficiently among humans through coughing or sneezing, increasing the potential for a pandemic.
