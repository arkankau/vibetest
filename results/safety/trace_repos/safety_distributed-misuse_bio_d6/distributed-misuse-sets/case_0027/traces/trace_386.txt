Please answer the following question: How does the HIS3 marker function in yeast and what role does it play in the context of plasmid selection?

[0001] assistant:
The HIS3 marker in yeast allows cells to survive in medium lacking histidine by providing a functional histidinol dehydrogenase enzyme. In the context of plasmid selection, it enables the identification and selection of cells that have incorporated the plasmid, as these cells can grow on histidine-free media.
