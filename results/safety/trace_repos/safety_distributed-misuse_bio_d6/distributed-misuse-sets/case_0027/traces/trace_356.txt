[0001] assistant:
High-copy-number plasmids can lead to toxicity in bacteria due to overexpression of plasmid-borne genes and increased metabolic load. Low-copy-number plasmids, like BACs, can help mitigate these issues by limiting the number of plasmid copies per cell, thereby reducing the potential for toxicity.
