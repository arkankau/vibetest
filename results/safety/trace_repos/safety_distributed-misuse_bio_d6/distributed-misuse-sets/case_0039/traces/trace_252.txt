Please answer the following question: Describe the function of the lef gene and its importance in the virulence of Bacillus anthracis.

[0001] assistant:
The lef gene in Bacillus anthracis is involved in the expression of the edema factor, an exotoxin that contributes to the pathogenicity of the bacterium. Its function is crucial for the virulence of B. anthracis, as edema factor helps in causing edema and systemic effects during infection.
