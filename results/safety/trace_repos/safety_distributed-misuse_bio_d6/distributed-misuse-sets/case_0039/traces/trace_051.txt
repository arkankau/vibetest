[0001] assistant:
Specific surface loops on an AAV vector can enhance its ability to cross the blood-brain barrier (BBB) by modifying the viral capsid to better interact with receptors on the barrier's endothelial cells. These modifications can improve the vector's tropism for the central nervous system (CNS) and spinal cord, potentially increasing transduction efficiency in these regions.
