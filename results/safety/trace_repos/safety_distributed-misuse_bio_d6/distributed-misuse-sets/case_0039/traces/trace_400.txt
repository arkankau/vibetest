Epidemiological modeling of variant spread patterns aims to predict and understand the dynamics of virus transmission in different populations. It provides specific information about factors such as increased transmissibility, such as higher reproductive numbers, more efficient aerosol transmission, or changes in symptomatology that affect contact rates.
