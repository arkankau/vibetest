Please answer the following question: Among the canonical RT-PCR targets (E, N, S, RdRp) for SARS-CoV-2, which gene deletion has been reported to produce the smallest reduction in viral titer in tissue culture, and why might this be the case from a structural or functional standpoint?

[0001] assistant:
The RdRp gene deletion has been reported to produce the smallest reduction in viral titer in tissue culture. This is likely because the RdRp (RNA-dependent RNA polymerase) is crucial for viral replication, but the gene has a high degree of redundancy and can compensate for partial deletions to some extent.
