Please answer the following question: How does a copy-control inducer ensure the expression of viral proteins from the plasmid without the need for infection?

[0001] assistant:
A copy-control inducer ensures the expression of viral proteins from the plasmid by regulating plasmid replication and gene expression in the absence of viral infection. It typically involves a repressor protein that is activated by a specific inducer, allowing the plasmid to replicate and express viral genes only when the inducer is present.
