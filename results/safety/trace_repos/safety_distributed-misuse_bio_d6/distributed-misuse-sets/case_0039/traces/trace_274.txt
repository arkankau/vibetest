Please answer the following question: What is TAR cloning and why is it useful for assembling large DNA fragments when traditional methods are not feasible?

[0001] assistant:
TAR cloning uses a specific RNA sequence to facilitate the ligation of DNA fragments into a vector, making it useful for assembling large DNA fragments. This method is particularly useful when traditional cloning methods are not feasible due to the size of the DNA fragments or their complexity.
