Please answer the following question: In reverse genetics studies, are yeast cells used because they can replicate viral genomes without a mammalian host, but is this the primary reason for using TAR systems in infectious clone assembly?

[0001] assistant:
Yeast cells are used in reverse genetics studies because they can stably maintain and propagate viral genomes as plasmids without a mammalian host. However, this is not the primary reason for using Trans-Activator of Transcription (TAR) systems in infectious clone assembly, which mainly serve to enhance viral RNA synthesis and rescue.
