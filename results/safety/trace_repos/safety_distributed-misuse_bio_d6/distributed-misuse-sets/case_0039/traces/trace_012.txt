Please answer the following question: What is the minimal infectious unit of negative-strand RNA viruses, and how does it relate to the rescue from cloned DNA?

[0001] assistant:
The minimal infectious unit of negative-strand RNA viruses is a single viral genome encapsulated in a capsid. Rescue from cloned DNA refers to the process where the viral genome is synthesized from a DNA copy, often in cells, to produce functional negative-strand RNA and infectious virus particles.
