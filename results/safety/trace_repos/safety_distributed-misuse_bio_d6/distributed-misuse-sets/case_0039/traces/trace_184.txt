The Spike (S) protein from SARS-CoV, MERS-CoV, and SARS-CoV-2 mediates viral entry into host cells by binding to angiotensin-converting enzyme 2 (ACE2) receptors and facilitating membrane fusion. When expressed in a poxvirus, these S proteins can alter host cell entry mechanisms, potentially leading to enhanced viral spread or altered immune responses.
