Please answer the following question: What is the function of the IL-2 receptor beta subunit in the immune response and how does it interact with IL-2?

[0001] assistant:
The IL-2 receptor beta subunit (IL-2Rβ) is crucial for high-affinity IL-2 binding and signaling in T cells. It interacts with IL-2 to enhance receptor signaling, which is essential for T cell activation and proliferation.
