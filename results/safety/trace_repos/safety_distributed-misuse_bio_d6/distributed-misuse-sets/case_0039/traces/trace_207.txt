Polymyxin B is effective against gram-negative bacteria by disrupting their cell membranes. It helps in isolating Bacillus anthracis from complex samples by selectively inhibiting the growth of gram-negative bacteria, thereby enhancing the visibility and isolation of Bacillus anthracis.
