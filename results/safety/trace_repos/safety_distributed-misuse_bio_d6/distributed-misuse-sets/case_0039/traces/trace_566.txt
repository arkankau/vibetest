Please answer the following question: How does the preferential infection of ATII cells by SARS-CoV-2 suggest a risk of systemic viral spread?

[0001] assistant:
The preferential infection of alveolar type II (ATII) cells by SARS-CoV-2 suggests a risk of systemic viral spread because ATII cells are located in the lungs and can serve as sites for viral replication, which can then lead to inflammation and damage. This can impair lung function and allow the virus to enter the bloodstream, facilitating systemic infection and dissemination to other organs.
