Please answer the following question: During routine probe-based RT-PCR design, how many mismatches in the probe-binding region are typically required to cause complete failure of fluorescent signal detection, and does it matter whether the mismatches are synonymous or non-synonymous with respect to the encoded protein?

[0001] assistant:
Typically, more than 2-3 mismatches in the probe-binding region can cause complete failure of fluorescent signal detection. Whether the mismatches are synonymous or non-synonymous with respect to the encoded protein does not matter; the key factor is the impact on probe binding efficiency.
