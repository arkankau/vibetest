Please answer the following question: Is herpes simplex virus (HSV) characterized by low immunogenicity in the central nervous system, and could this contribute to its advantage in gene delivery?

[0001] assistant:
HSV is characterized by its high immunogenicity, not low, but it can still be used as a vector for gene delivery due to its ability to efficiently infect neurons and persist in the central nervous system. However, its immunogenicity can pose challenges in repeated administrations.
