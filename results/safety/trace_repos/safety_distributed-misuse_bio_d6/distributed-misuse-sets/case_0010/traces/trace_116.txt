Please answer the following question: What does the term 'helper virus' refer to in the context of reverse genetics for RNA viruses, and how is it used in recovering infectious virus from cloned cDNA?

[0001] assistant:
The term 'helper virus' refers to a secondary virus that provides essential genes or functions missing in a cloned cDNA construct. In the context of reverse genetics for RNA viruses, helper viruses are used to recover infectious virus from cloned cDNA by supplying necessary elements that the cloned genome cannot provide on its own.
