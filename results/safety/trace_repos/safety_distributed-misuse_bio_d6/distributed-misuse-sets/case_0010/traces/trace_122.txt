Please answer the following question: What are the potential risks of exposing the leishmanial culture medium to higher or lower temperatures during shipment, and how can these be mitigated?

[0001] assistant:
Exposing leishmanial culture medium to higher temperatures can kill the parasites or degrade the medium, while lower temperatures can slow down growth and decrease viability. To mitigate these risks, the medium should be stored and shipped at optimal temperatures, typically between 2-8°C, and insulated packaging with temperature monitoring devices should be used.
