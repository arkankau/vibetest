An episome in the context of herpesvirus research refers to the viral DNA that can exist either in an integrated or circular form within the host cell's nucleus. Its location is flexible, allowing it to exist either as an extra chromosome or as an independent element, which enables the virus to persist in a latent state without immediately causing symptoms.
