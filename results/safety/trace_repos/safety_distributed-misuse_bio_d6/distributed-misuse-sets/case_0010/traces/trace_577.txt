Live animal imaging allows for the real-time visualization of viral infection dynamics within living organisms, providing temporal information on disease progression and spatial information on viral distribution and tissue tropism. This technique helps assess the altered virus's ability to cause severe disease by monitoring changes in health status, viral load, and tissue damage over time.
