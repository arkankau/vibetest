Please answer the following question: What are the secretion mechanisms for disulfide-rich toxins from the periplasm to the culture medium in E. coli?

[0001] assistant:
Disulfide-rich toxins from the periplasm of E. coli are typically secreted through the Sec machinery and may undergo further modification in the periplasm before being released into the culture medium. Some toxins can also be secreted via the twin-arginine translocation (Tat) system or the secretin pathway.
