Please answer the following question: How does the CDN-binding region of STING contribute to the overall anti-viral immune response against herpesvirus infection?

[0001] assistant:
The CDN-binding region of STING helps recognize viral cyclic dinucleotides, which are produced by pathogens including herpesviruses. This recognition triggers an intracellular signaling cascade that leads to the activation of interferon and other immune responses to combat the infection.
