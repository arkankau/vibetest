Please answer the following question: What is the role of EBNA1 in the EBV life cycle and is it known to mimic a constitutively active CD40 receptor in EBV-associated malignancies?

[0001] assistant:
EBNA1 (Epstein-Barr Virus Nuclear Antigen 1) plays a crucial role in the EBV life cycle by maintaining episomal viral DNA in infected B cells and regulating gene expression. It is known to mimic the function of a constitutively active CD40 receptor, contributing to the transformation of B cells in EBV-associated malignancies.
