Please answer the following question: In what way does the presence of receptors in pigs' respiratory tract that can bind to a variety of IAVs facilitate the reassortment of swine-, human-, and avian-lineage IAVs?

[0001] assistant:
The presence of receptors in pigs' respiratory tract that can bind to a variety of influenza A viruses (IAVs) facilitates the reassortment of swine-, human-, and avian-lineage IAVs by creating an environment where multiple viral strains can co-infect the same cell. This co-infection increases the likelihood of genetic reassortment, potentially leading to the emergence of new virus strains with pandemic potential.
