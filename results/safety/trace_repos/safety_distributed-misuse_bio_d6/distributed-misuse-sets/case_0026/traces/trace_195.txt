Please answer the following question: Are there any known reassortment events or genetic exchanges between avian and swine influenza viruses that could have contributed to the emergence of the PB2 gene in the H5HA-mutant/pdm09 influenza virus?

[0001] assistant:
Yes, reassortment events and genetic exchanges between avian and swine influenza viruses have contributed to the emergence of the PB2 gene in the H5N1-mutant/pdm09 influenza virus. This gene, originally from avian influenza viruses, was passed to the pandemic 2009 H1N1 virus through reassortment.
