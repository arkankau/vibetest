Please answer the following question: How does the self-inactivating (SIN) feature of lentiviral vectors contribute to the overall efficiency and accuracy of reverse genetics studies?

[0001] assistant:
The self-inactivating (SIN) feature of lentiviral vectors removes viral promoter elements that are necessary for vector replication, reducing the risk of vector integration into active genes and thus minimizing the potential for insertional mutagenesis. This contributes to the overall efficiency and accuracy of reverse genetics studies by allowing for safer and more precise gene knockdown or knockout.
