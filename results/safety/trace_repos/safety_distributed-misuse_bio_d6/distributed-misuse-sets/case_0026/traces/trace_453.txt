Please answer the following question: What is the definition of intertypic recombinant viruses and how do they typically arise in viral populations?

[0001] assistant:
Intertypic recombinant viruses are viruses that result from the exchange of genetic material between different viral types, typically from different species or genera. They arise in viral populations through recombination events during co-infection of a host cell by multiple viral types, leading to the formation of new viral variants with mixed genetic traits.
