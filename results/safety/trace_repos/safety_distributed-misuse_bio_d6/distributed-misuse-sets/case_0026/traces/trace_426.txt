[0001] assistant:
Flanking sequences in herpesvirus vectors can provide specific regulatory elements such as promoters and enhancers that control the temporal and spatial expression of the inserted gene. These elements ensure that the gene is expressed in the appropriate host cells and at the correct time during the viral infection process.
