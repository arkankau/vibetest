Please answer the following question: How does the interaction of replication-competent oncolytic viruses (OVs) with the mononuclear phagocytic system (MPS) complicate PK and PD analysis?

[0001] assistant:
The interaction of replication-competent oncolytic viruses (OVs) with the mononuclear phagocytic system (MPS) can complicate PK and PD analysis because the MPS can sequester and degrade the viruses before they can reach their target tumors, leading to variable virus distribution and reduced therapeutic efficacy.
