Please answer the following question: What are the common symptoms and signs of food borne botulism in a patient?

[0001] assistant:
The common symptoms and signs of foodborne botulism in a patient include double vision, blurred vision, drooping eyelids, slurred speech, difficulty swallowing, dry mouth, and muscle weakness. These symptoms typically appear 12 to 36 hours after eating contaminated food.
