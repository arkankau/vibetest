Please answer the following question: What are the characteristics of the influenza A virus subtype responsible for the 1968 pandemic that distinguish it from the other options?

[0001] assistant:
The 1968 pandemic, also known as the Hong Kong flu, was caused by the H3N2 subtype of the influenza A virus. This subtype is distinguished by the H3 hemagglutinin and N2 neuraminidase antigens, which differentiate it from other influenza subtypes like H1N1 and H5N1.
