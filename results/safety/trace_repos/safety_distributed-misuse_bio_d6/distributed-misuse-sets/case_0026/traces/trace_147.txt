Please answer the following question: In in vitro transcription systems, how does T7 RNA polymerase initiate transcription of a DNA template?

[0001] assistant:
T7 RNA polymerase initiates transcription by binding to the T7 promoter sequence in the DNA template. It then synthesizes RNA from the template in the 5' to 3' direction, starting at the promoter site.
