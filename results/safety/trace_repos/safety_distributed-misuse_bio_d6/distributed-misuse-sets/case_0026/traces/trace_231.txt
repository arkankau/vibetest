Please answer the following question: During reactivation from latency, does the herpesvirus protein ICP0 act as a viral protease to process the viral polyprotein?

[0001] assistant:
ICP0 acts as a viral ubiquitin ligase, not a protease, to promote the degradation of cellular and viral proteins, aiding in the reactivation of the herpesvirus.
