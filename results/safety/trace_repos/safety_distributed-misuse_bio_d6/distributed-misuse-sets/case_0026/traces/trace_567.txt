Please answer the following question: How do viral microRNAs contribute to the establishment of latency and modulation of the host immune response in herpesvirus research?

[0001] assistant:
Viral microRNAs contribute to the establishment of latency by targeting host genes that would otherwise activate the immune response, thereby allowing the herpesvirus to persist. They also modulate the host immune response by silencing genes involved in immune recognition and antiviral defense mechanisms.
