Please answer the following question: Does DNA-launched infectious systems require the host cell machinery for replication, and if not, what is its significance in comparison to RNA-launched systems?

[0001] assistant:
DNA-launched infectious systems do not require the host cell machinery for replication. This is significant because it allows for more controlled and stable genetic delivery compared to RNA-launched systems, which depend on host cell machinery for replication and can be less stable due to RNA's susceptibility to degradation.
