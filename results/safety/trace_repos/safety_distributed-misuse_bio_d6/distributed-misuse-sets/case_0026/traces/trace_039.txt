Please answer the following question: What is the role of the Orf7a protein from SARS-CoV-2 in the interaction with host chemokines and how does it aid the virus during infection?

[0001] assistant:
The Orf7a protein from SARS-CoV-2 interacts with host chemokine receptors to block the release of chemokines that recruit immune cells to the site of infection. This aids the virus by suppressing the host's immune response and preventing the activation of interferon signaling pathways, thus creating a more favorable environment for viral replication.
