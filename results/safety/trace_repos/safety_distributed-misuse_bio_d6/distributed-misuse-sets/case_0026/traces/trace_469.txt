Please answer the following question: Can measles virus (MV) infect cells other than tracheo-bronchial epithelial cells, and if so, how does this affect its overall transmission?

[0001] assistant:
Yes, measles virus can infect cells other than tracheo-bronchial epithelial cells, including macrophages and monocytes. This broader cell tropism allows the virus to spread via the blood to different organs, enhancing its transmission and ability to cause a systemic infection.
