Please answer the following question: How do sialyloligosaccharides SAα2,3 and SAα2,6 influence the adaptation of avian influenza viruses to mammalian hosts?

[0001] assistant:
Sialyloligosaccharides SAα2,3 and SAα2,6 influence the adaptation of avian influenza viruses to mammalian hosts by determining the receptor specificity for viral hemagglutinin. These glycans are more abundant on human cells with SAα2,3 linkages, facilitating viral entry and replication, which aids in the virus's adaptation to mammals.
