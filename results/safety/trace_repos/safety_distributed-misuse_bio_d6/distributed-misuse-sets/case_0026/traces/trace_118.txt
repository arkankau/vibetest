Please answer the following question: What is CD150 and what role does it play in viral entry into cells, specifically for Measles virus?

[0001] assistant:
CD150, also known as SLAM (Signaling Lymphocytic Activation Molecule), is a receptor that plays a role in the entry of Measles virus into cells. It acts as a co-receptor, enhancing viral attachment and internalization.
