Please answer the following question: What is the role of hemaggultinin (HA) in the influenza virus, and is it related to resistance to adamantane-class antivirals?

[0001] assistant:
Hemagglutinin (HA) is a surface protein of the influenza virus responsible for binding the virus to the host cell and initiating infection. Yes, HA is related to resistance to adamantane-class antivirals, as mutations in the HA gene can confer resistance to these drugs.
