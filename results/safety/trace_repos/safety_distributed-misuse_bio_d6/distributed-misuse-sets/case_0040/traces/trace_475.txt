There are known limitations with using miRNA targeting sequences in viral vectors for specificity, including potential off-target effects and the need for highly sequence-specific miRNAs, which can be difficult to achieve. Additionally, the stability and delivery efficiency of these miRNAs within the target cells can also pose challenges.
