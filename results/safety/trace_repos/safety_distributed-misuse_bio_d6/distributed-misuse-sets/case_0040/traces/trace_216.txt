Please answer the following question: What are the advantages of using an adeno-associated virus (AAV) vector for large gene inserts?

[0001] assistant:
One key advantage of using AAV vectors for large gene inserts is their ability to deliver up to 4-5 kilobases of genetic material, which is more than many other viral vectors. Additionally, AAV vectors have a low immune response and can achieve long-term gene expression, making them suitable for therapeutic applications involving large genes.
