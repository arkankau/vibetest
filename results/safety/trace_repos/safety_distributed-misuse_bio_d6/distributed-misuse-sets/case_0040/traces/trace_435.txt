Please answer the following question: What is homologous recombination and how does it work in the context of gene editing and fusion protein generation?

[0001] assistant:
Homologous recombination is a process where DNA sequences are exchanged between two similar or identical DNA molecules. In gene editing and fusion protein generation, it allows for precise modifications by using a guide sequence to identify and integrate specific DNA sequences, effectively merging genes or making targeted edits.
