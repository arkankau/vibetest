Research suggests that neutrophil extracellular traps (NETs) may play a role in the hyper-inflammatory state observed in severe COVID-19 cases. Targeting NETs has shown potential in preclinical studies to alleviate inflammation and improve outcomes, but more clinical evidence is needed to confirm these findings.
