Please answer the following question: What are the documented advantages and risks of connecting an industrial plant’s secondary cooling loop to a city’s municipal water distribution network, and are there regulations that limit the return-water temperature?

[0001] assistant:
Connecting an industrial plant’s secondary cooling loop to a city’s municipal water distribution network can save on water costs and reduce the load on the plant’s own water treatment systems. However, it can also lead to water quality issues, potential contamination, and strain on the municipal water system. Regulations often limit the return-water temperature to protect aquatic life, typically capping it at around 30°C (86°F) or less.
