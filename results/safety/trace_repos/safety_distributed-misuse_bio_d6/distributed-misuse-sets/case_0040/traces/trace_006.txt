Please answer the following question: What is the role of viral glycoprotein B (gB) in the life cycle of HSV, and does it play a critical role in egress?

[0001] assistant:
Viral glycoprotein B (gB) is crucial for the entry of HSV into host cells. It plays a critical role in egress by mediating the fusion of viral and cellular membranes, facilitating the release of newly formed virus particles from the host cell.
