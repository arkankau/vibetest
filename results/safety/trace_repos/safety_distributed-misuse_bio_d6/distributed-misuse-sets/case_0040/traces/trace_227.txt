Some continuous or fed-batch chemical plants schedule their highest heat-releasing steps at night to take advantage of lower electricity rates and reduced competition for grid capacity. The typical reduction in peak cooling-tower load can be significant, often ranging from 20% to 50%, depending on the specific process and local electricity pricing.
