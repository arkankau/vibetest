Please answer the following question: Which biocontrol agents are commonly used to control weeds or illicit plants, and what are their known mechanisms of action?

[0001] assistant:
Common biocontrol agents for weeds include insects, pathogens, and plants that compete with the target weeds. For example, the yellow starthistle weevil can reduce the growth of yellow starthistle by feeding on its seeds. Pathogens such as fungi and bacteria can also be used to target specific weeds, often by causing disease that weakens or kills the plant.
