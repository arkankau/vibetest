Please answer the following question: In what way does the function of thymidine kinase (TK) make it a target for antiviral therapies against herpes simplex virus (HSV)?

[0001] assistant:
Thymidine kinase (TK) is essential for herpes simplex virus (HSV) DNA synthesis, as it phosphorylates viral thymidine to form 2',3'-cyclic monophosphate, which is then incorporated into the viral DNA. This makes TK a target for antiviral therapies because inhibiting it prevents viral replication.
