Please answer the following question: What other cardiovascular effects might be associated with the overproduction of angiotensinogen in individuals with preexisting cardiovascular conditions?

[0001] assistant:
Overproduction of angiotensinogen can lead to increased angiotensin II production, which may exacerbate hypertension and further strain the heart, potentially leading to left ventricular hypertrophy and cardiac remodeling.
