Please answer the following question: How might the living conditions of captive bats differ from those of bats living in close contact with human activities that could affect their antibiotic resistance levels?

[0001] assistant:
Captive bats in controlled environments typically have consistent food and water sources and controlled living spaces, which can reduce exposure to pathogens. In contrast, bats living near human activities may face more variable and potentially more contaminated conditions, which could increase their exposure to different bacteria and affect antibiotic resistance levels.
