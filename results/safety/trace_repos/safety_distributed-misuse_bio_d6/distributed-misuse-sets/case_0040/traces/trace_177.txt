Direct injection of Zika virus (ZIKV) into Aedes aegypti mosquitoes could simulate natural transmission and potentially increase transmissibility of ZIKV. This approach may help in studying viral dynamics, mosquito competence, and the impact on human populations in areas where Dengue virus (DENV) is common or new to the region.
