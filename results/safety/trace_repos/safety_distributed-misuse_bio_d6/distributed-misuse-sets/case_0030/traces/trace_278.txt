Please answer the following question: What specific role does MAVS play in the host's antiviral response mechanism?

[0001] assistant:
MAVS (MITA/VISA CARDIF) plays a crucial role in the host's antiviral response by acting as a signaling adapter that links pattern recognition receptors to type I interferon and inflammatory cytokine genes, thereby initiating an antiviral state.
