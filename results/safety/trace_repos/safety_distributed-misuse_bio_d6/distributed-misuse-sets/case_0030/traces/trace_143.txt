Please answer the following question: What are the common methods used in reverse genetics systems for positive-sense RNA viruses, and how do they differ from systems requiring viral RNA polymerase in trans?

[0001] assistant:
Common methods in reverse genetics for positive-sense RNA viruses include cDNA cloning and transfection. These methods involve generating a cDNA copy of the viral genome and introducing it into cells, allowing for the production of virus-like particles or infectious clones. These systems differ from those requiring viral RNA polymerase in trans because the latter need an additional exogenous RNA polymerase to transcribe the cDNA into RNA, whereas the former rely on the cell's own RNA polymerase.
