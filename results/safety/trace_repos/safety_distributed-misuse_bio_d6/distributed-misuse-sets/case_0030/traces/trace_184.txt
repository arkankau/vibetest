[0001] assistant:
An essential gene locus in a herpesvirus vector is crucial for the virus's replication and spread, as these genes are necessary for its life cycle. Inserting a therapeutic gene into a non-essential gene locus instead may not pose immediate risks for the vector's replication but could lead to lower vector stability and potentially reduced gene transfer efficiency.
