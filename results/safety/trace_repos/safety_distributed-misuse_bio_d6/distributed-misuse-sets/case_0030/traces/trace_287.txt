Please answer the following question: What is the role of the NS1 protein in flavivirus infections and how does it interact with human endothelial cells?

[0001] assistant:
The NS1 protein of flaviviruses, such as dengue and Zika viruses, plays a crucial role in viral replication by increasing viral RNA synthesis and interfering with the host cell's antiviral response. It interacts with human endothelial cells by promoting viral assembly and release, potentially leading to increased viral spread and contributing to the development of vascular leakage observed in flavivirus infections.
