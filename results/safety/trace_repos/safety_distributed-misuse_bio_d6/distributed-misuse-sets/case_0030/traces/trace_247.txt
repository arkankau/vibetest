Please answer the following question: Does the hepatitis C virus rely on a high diversity of exposed sites that antibodies can target to impair the immune response and persist as chronic infections?

[0001] assistant:
The hepatitis C virus (HCV) does not rely on a high diversity of exposed sites for antibodies to target. Instead, HCV evades the immune response through mechanisms such as rapid mutation and encapsidation within the host cell, which helps it persist as a chronic infection.
