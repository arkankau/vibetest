Please answer the following question: What are the potential applications of using a bicistronic expression vector in reverse genetics for manipulating viral genomes?

[0001] assistant:
Bicistronic expression vectors in reverse genetics can be used to simultaneously express two genes from a single promoter, allowing for the study of viral gene functions and interactions. This approach is useful for generating viruses with altered gene expression patterns or knocked-out genes, facilitating the understanding of viral pathogenesis and the development of antiviral strategies.
