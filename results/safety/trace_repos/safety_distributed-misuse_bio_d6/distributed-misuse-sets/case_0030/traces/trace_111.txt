Overlapping genes allow for the encoding of more information using less genomic space by utilizing the same sequence of DNA for different genes, potentially reducing the overall length of the genetic material. The fitness advantages include increased compactness of the genome, potentially faster transcription and translation processes, and the ability to maintain genetic diversity through alternative gene reading frames.
