Please answer the following question: What is the natural host of the bacterium Bacillus anthracis, and from which animals have the most virulent strains of B. anthracis been historically isolated?

[0001] assistant:
The natural host of Bacillus anthracis is the domestic and wild ruminants such as cattle, sheep, and deer. The most virulent strains of B. anthracis have been historically isolated from cattle and sheep.
