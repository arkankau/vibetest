Please answer the following question: What is the significance of the hemagglutinin (HA) cleavage in the context of an influenza plaque assay?

[0001] assistant:
The cleavage of hemagglutinin (HA) is significant in an influenza plaque assay as it indicates the virus has become fully infectious and can spread to neighboring cells, which is crucial for the formation of plaques and assessing viral replication and pathogenicity.
