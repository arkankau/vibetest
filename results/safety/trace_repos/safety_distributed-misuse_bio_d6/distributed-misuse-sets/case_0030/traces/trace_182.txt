Transcription of genomic DNA into mRNA in the reverse genetics process for positive-stranded RNA viruses like SARS-CoV-2 is not typical, as these viruses do not have a DNA intermediate. Instead, reverse genetics involves synthesizing viral RNA from a cDNA copy to generate infectious virus particles, allowing for the study and manipulation of the virus.
