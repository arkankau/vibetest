Please answer the following question: What are the key differences between random generation of viral RNA using cell-free systems and assembling full-length cDNA clones for reverse genetic studies?

[0001] assistant:
Random generation of viral RNA using cell-free systems involves direct transcription of viral RNA without generating a DNA intermediate, while assembling full-length cDNA clones involves creating a DNA copy of the viral genome, which can then be amplified and used to generate viral RNA. The latter provides more precise control over the viral sequence.
