Please answer the following question: What is the role of pathogen proteins in glycan microarrays when studying the specificity of pathogens like influenza?

[0001] assistant:
Pathogen proteins in glycan microarrays are used to identify and study the specific glycan-binding properties of pathogens like influenza, which can help in understanding how these pathogens interact with host cells and potentially in developing targeted therapeutics or vaccines.
