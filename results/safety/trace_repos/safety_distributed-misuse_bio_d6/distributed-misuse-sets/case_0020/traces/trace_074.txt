Please answer the following question: How do cells with a higher sialic acid content, specifically with 2,6-linked sialic acid, influence the attachment and replication of human influenza viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Cells with a higher content of 2,6-linked sialic acid are more susceptible to attachment and replication of human influenza viruses because these viruses preferentially bind to this type of sialic acid, which is more abundant on human cells compared to the 2,3-linked sialic acid found on avian cells.
