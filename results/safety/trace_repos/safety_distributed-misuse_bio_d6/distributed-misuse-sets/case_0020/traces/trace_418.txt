Please answer the following question: How does the herpes simplex virus (HSV) protein ICP47 interact with the complement system to evade immune detection?

[0001] assistant:
The herpes simplex virus (HSV) protein ICP47 interacts with the complement system by binding to and inhibiting the function of the human immunodeficiency virus (HIV)-binding protein 1 (TAP1), which prevents the presentation of viral peptides on MHC class I molecules. This interaction helps the virus evade immune detection by reducing the activation of complement-mediated immune responses.
