Please answer the following question: What role does the vRNP complex play in the mechanisms of action of antiviral drugs targeting influenza A viruses?

[0001] assistant:
The vRNP complex, consisting of viral RNA, nucleoprotein, and polymerase, is crucial for viral replication. Antiviral drugs targeting influenza A viruses often aim to interfere with this complex to prevent viral assembly and release, thus inhibiting virus propagation.
