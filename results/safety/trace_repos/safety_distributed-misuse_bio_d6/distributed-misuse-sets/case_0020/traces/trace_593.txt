Please answer the following question: Does Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) use the latency-associated nuclear antigen (LANA) for maintaining its viral genome during latency?

[0001] assistant:
Yes, Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) uses the latency-associated nuclear antigen (LANA) for maintaining its viral genome during latency. LANA binds to the EBV genome and chromosomal DNA, helping to stabilize the viral genome within the host cell.
