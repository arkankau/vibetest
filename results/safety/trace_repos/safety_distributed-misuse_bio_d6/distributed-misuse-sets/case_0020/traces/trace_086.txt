Please answer the following question: How do bacterial artificial chromosomes (BACs) aid in the cloning and amplification of large DNA fragments, and what are their advantages?

[0001] assistant:
BACs aid in cloning and amplifying large DNA fragments by inserting them into a circular plasmid derived from E. coli, which can then replicate inside bacterial cells. This allows for the large DNA segments to be copied numerous times, facilitating their study and manipulation. Advantages include the ability to handle DNA fragments up to several hundred kilobases, stability of inserted DNA, and ease of manipulation.
