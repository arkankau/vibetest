Please answer the following question: How do ubiquitous cellular receptors differ from rare cellular receptors in terms of their role in the infection process of AAV vectors?

[0001] assistant:
Ubiquitous cellular receptors are more widely expressed on cell surfaces, facilitating the infection of a larger number of cells by AAV vectors. In contrast, rare cellular receptors are less commonly found, limiting the infectivity of AAV vectors to cells that express them.
