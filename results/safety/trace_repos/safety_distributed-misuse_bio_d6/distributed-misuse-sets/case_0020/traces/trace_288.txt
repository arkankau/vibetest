Please answer the following question: How does the asymmetric lipid bilayer structure of the bacterial outer membrane allow for the easy penetration of hydrophobic molecules?

[0001] assistant:
The asymmetric lipid bilayer of the bacterial outer membrane contains a high concentration of lipopolysaccharides and other hydrophobic molecules on the outer leaflet, which facilitates the easy penetration of similarly hydrophobic molecules through the membrane.
