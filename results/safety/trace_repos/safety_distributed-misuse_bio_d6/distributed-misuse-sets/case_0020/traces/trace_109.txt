Quantifying viral load using plaque assays helps in assessing the impact of gene knockouts on viral replication by providing a measure of infectious virus produced, which can reveal how essential a gene is for viral replication. By comparing the plaque formation in cells with and without the gene knockout, one can determine the gene's role in viral propagation.
