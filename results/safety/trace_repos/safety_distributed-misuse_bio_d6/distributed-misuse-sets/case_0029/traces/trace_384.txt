[0001] assistant:
The I-SceI meganuclease is a site-specific endonuclease derived from the yeast I-SceI intron. Its mechanism of action involves recognizing a specific 19-22 base pair sequence and cleaving DNA at this site, which can be utilized to induce double-strand breaks in genetic engineering for homologous recombination or to enhance the efficiency of gene targeting in cells.
