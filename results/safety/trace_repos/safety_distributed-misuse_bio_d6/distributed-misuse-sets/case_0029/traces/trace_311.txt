Please answer the following question: What are the main challenges associated with ensuring the specificity of Cre recombinase expression in astrocytes?

[0001] assistant:
One main challenge is ensuring that Cre recombinase is expressed exclusively in astrocytes and not in other cell types, which can be difficult due to the overlap in gene expression between different cell types in the brain. Another challenge is the potential for Cre recombination to occur in non-astrocytic cells if Cre driver lines are not perfectly specific.
