Please answer the following question: In what way do selectable markers benefit the construction and maintenance of plasmids in reverse genetics systems for DNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Selectable markers benefit the construction and maintenance of plasmids in reverse genetics systems for DNA viruses by allowing easy selection of plasmid-containing cells, facilitating the identification and propagation of cells that have been successfully transformed or transduced with the desired genetic material.
