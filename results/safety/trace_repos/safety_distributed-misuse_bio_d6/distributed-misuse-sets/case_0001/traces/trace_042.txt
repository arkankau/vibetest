TALENs, I-SceI meganuclease, and CRISPR-Cas9 systems can be used to remove antibiotic resistance markers by specifically targeting and cleaving the DNA at the site of the resistance marker, leading to its deletion. This targeted cleavage activates the cell's DNA repair mechanisms, which can result in the loss of the resistance gene.
