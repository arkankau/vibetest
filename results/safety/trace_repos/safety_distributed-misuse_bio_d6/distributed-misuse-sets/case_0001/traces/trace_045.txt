Please answer the following question: What is the bacterial artificial chromosome (BAC) stepwise insertion synthesis technique, and how is it used in genetic assembly?

The bacterial artificial chromosome (BAC) stepwise insertion synthesis technique is a method used in molecular biology to construct large DNA fragments by sequentially inserting DNA fragments into a BAC vector using bacteria. This technique is commonly used in genetic assembly to create complex genomes or large DNA constructs by adding DNA pieces one by one in an ordered manner.
