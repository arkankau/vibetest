Please answer the following question: What is the application of CRISPR-Cas9 and bacterial artificial chromosomes in the context of synthesizing and assembling large viral genomes?

[0001] assistant:
CRISPR-Cas9 and bacterial artificial chromosomes (BACs) are used to precisely edit and assemble large viral genomes by facilitating the insertion, deletion, and correction of genetic sequences. BACs provide a stable vector for cloning large DNA fragments, while CRISPR-Cas9 enables targeted genome modifications.
