Please answer the following question: How does the process of serial passage under selection lead to the generation of recombinant negative-strand RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Serial passage under selection pressure can lead to genetic reassortment or point mutations in negative-strand RNA viruses, potentially generating recombinant viruses. This process is facilitated by the error-prone RNA-dependent RNA polymerase of these viruses and the presence of multiple viral genomes in the same host cell.
