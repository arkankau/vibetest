Please answer the following question: What are serotype-specific adenovirus capsids, and how do they differ from serotype-independent ones in terms of structure and function?

[0001] assistant:
Serotype-specific adenovirus capsids are virus particles that are unique to a particular serotype and contain specific proteins that interact with host cell receptors, mediating infection for that specific serotype. In contrast, serotype-independent adenovirus capsids lack these specific proteins and can infect cells regardless of serotype, although they may still have some serotype-preference due to minor structural differences.
