[0001] assistant:
ICP0, a protein from the herpes simplex virus (HSV), can directly interfere with host immune responses by ubiquitinating and degrading key transcription factors like p53 and IRF3, thereby inhibiting the expression of interferons and other antiviral genes. Additionally, ICP0 disrupts the interferon signaling pathway and modulates the NF-κB pathway, further suppressing the immune response.
