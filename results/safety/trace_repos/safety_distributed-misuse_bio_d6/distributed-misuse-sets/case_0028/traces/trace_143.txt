Please answer the following question: How does culturing cells using ethyl methanesulfonate (EMS) or UV light influence mutagenesis rates specifically on a plasmid and not the rest of the genome?

[0001] assistant:
Ethyl methanesulfonate (EMS) and UV light induce mutagenesis by damaging DNA, which is then repaired or replicated with errors, increasing mutagenesis rates specifically in the plasmid DNA. This targeted mutagenesis can be particularly useful in genetic studies and transformations where plasmid DNA is of interest.
