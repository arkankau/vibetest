Please answer the following question: How do the loxP and loxB sites function in the context of vector production and helper virus contamination?

[0001] assistant:
loxP and loxB sites function as recognition sequences for the Cre recombinase, which is used to manipulate DNA in vectors. They help avoid helper virus contamination by allowing for the removal of viral genes that could otherwise contaminate the vector, thus improving the safety and utility of the vector in gene therapy applications.
