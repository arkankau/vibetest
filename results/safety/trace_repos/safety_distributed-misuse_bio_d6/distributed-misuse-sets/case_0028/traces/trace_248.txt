Please answer the following question: What is the transmembrane domain of the IL-2 receptor alpha subunit and how does truncating it create a soluble receptor?

[0001] assistant:
The transmembrane domain of the IL-2 receptor alpha subunit is responsible for attaching the receptor to the cell membrane. Truncating this domain creates a soluble form of the receptor that can circulate freely in the bloodstream without being anchored to the cell surface.
