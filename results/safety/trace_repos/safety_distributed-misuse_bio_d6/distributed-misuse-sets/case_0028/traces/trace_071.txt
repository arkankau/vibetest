Please answer the following question: How do viral vectors persist and spread within an insect population, and what is the specific mechanism of introduction and transmission?

[0001] assistant:
Viral vectors persist and spread within an insect population through vertical transmission (from parent to offspring) and horizontal transmission (between individuals). The specific mechanism often involves encapsulation of the virus within the insect's eggs or transmission via saliva during feeding, allowing the virus to spread both vertically and horizontally among the population.
