Please answer the following question: What is the function of the T7 promoter and how does it relate to plasmid expression?

[0001] assistant:
The T7 promoter is a sequence recognized by the T7 RNA polymerase, which binds to initiate transcription of genes on a plasmid. When a T7 expression system is used, an engineered cell is provided with T7 RNA polymerase, allowing for the specific and efficient expression of genes under the control of the T7 promoter on the plasmid.
