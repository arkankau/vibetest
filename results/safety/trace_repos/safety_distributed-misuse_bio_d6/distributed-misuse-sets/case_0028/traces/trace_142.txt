Please answer the following question: What is the role of US3 in degrading host antiviral proteins, and how does this mechanism evade the host's immune response?

[0001] assistant:
US3, an enzyme from certain strains of herpesvirus, degrades host antiviral proteins such as interferon-induced protein with tetratricopeptide repeats 1 (IFIT1), thereby evading the host's immune response. By inhibiting these proteins, US3 allows the virus to replicate more efficiently without being detected or fought by the host's immune system.
