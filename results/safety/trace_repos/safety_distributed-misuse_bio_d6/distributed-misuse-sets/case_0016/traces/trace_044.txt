Please answer the following question: Can sampling bias lead to a mismatch between the vaccine and the circulating strains of influenza virus, and if so, how?

[0001] assistant:
Sampling bias can lead to a mismatch between the vaccine and circulating influenza strains because the vaccine strains are chosen based on predictions made from surveillance data that may not accurately represent the strains that will circulate in the upcoming season. This can result from incomplete or biased surveillance data.
