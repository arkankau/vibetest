Please answer the following question: What is the role of the N protein in the measles virus life cycle and how might its ablation or overexpression affect measles virus replication in interferon-deficient cells?

[0001] assistant:
The N protein in the measles virus life cycle is the nucleocapsid protein that binds viral RNA, playing a crucial role in genome packaging and stability. Ablation of the N protein can severely impair virus replication, while overexpression might enhance viral RNA binding but could also lead to genome instability or interfere with other viral functions, potentially affecting replication in interferon-deficient cells.
