Please answer the following question: What is the role of the HSV protein ICP4 in regulating the expression of viral genes, particularly early and late viral genes?

[0001] assistant:
HSV protein ICP4 acts as a master regulator of viral gene expression, promoting the transcription of early viral genes and indirectly facilitating the expression of late viral genes by establishing a permissive environment for late gene expression.
