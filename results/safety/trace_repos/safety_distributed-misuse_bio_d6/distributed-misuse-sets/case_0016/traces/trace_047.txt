Please answer the following question: What are the specific benefits or advantages of using HPXV for vaccine development over VACV?

[0001] assistant:
HPXV (Hic et Prohibitum X-numbered virus) offers advantages such as reduced risk of recombination and pathogenicity, making it a safer platform for vaccine development compared to VACV (Variola virus, the causative agent of smallpox). Additionally, HPXV can more easily be genetically engineered and lacks some of the complex regulatory elements of VACV, simplifying vaccine production.
