Primary cells or organ cultures offer advantages such as maintaining the natural infection environment and preserving host cell functions, which can provide more accurate models for studying host-virus interactions and immune responses compared to cell lines. These systems allow for the investigation of virus-host interactions under conditions that more closely mimic the in vivo situation.
