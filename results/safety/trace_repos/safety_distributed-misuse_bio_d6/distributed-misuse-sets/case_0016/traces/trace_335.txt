Please answer the following question: What is the primary state in which the herpesvirus DNA exists within a host cell during latency, and how does this relate to the term 'episome'?

[0001] assistant:
The primary state in which the herpesvirus DNA exists within a host cell during latency is as an episome. An episome is an extrachromosomal circular DNA molecule that can replicate independently of the chromosome and is maintained in the host cell.
