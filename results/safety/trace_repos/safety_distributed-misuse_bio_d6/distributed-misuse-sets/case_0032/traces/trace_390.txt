Please answer the following question: What is the process of transforming yeast cells with plasmids and how do the markers (TRP1, HIS3, URA3) assist in this process?

[0001] assistant:
The process involves transforming yeast cells with plasmids using lithium acetate and heat shock. Markers like TRP1, HIS3, and URA3 assist by allowing selection of transformed cells on media lacking tryptophan, histidine, and uracil, respectively, as cells with integrated plasmids can grow where others cannot.
