Please answer the following question: How does the quorum sensing system associated with the agr locus influence the behavior of S. aureus in a cell density-dependent manner?

[0001] assistant:
The agr locus in S. aureus controls the production of quorum-sensing molecules, which increase in concentration as cell density rises. Once a threshold is reached, these molecules trigger a shift in gene expression, leading to the production of virulence factors and biofilm formation.
