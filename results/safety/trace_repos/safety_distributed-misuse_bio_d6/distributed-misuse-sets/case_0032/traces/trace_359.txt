[0001] assistant:
A fitness landscape is a conceptual model representing the evolutionary fitness of all possible genotypes of an organism in a given environment. Its dynamic nature, as genotypes and environments change over time, poses a significant challenge in accurately modeling pathogen evolution because it requires constant updates and cannot capture real-time evolutionary dynamics.
