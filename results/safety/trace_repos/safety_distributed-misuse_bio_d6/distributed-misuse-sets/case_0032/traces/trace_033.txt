Please answer the following question: How does transcriptional activation, the initiation of RNA synthesis by RNA polymerase at specific DNA sequences, relate to introducing new genes into the Vaccinia virus genome?

[0001] assistant:
Transcriptional activation can be used to introduce and express new genes into the Vaccinia virus genome by ensuring that the viral RNA polymerase initiates RNA synthesis at specific promoters associated with these foreign genes, thereby allowing them to be transcribed and expressed.
