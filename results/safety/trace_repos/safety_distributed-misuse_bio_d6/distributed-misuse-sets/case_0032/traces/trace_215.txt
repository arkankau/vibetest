The process of using environmental DNA (eDNA) swabs involves collecting water or soil samples from an ecosystem to obtain genetic material from organisms living in that environment. This method is generally less expensive and more efficient than traditional sampling methods like trawling or trapping, as it requires fewer personnel and resources.
