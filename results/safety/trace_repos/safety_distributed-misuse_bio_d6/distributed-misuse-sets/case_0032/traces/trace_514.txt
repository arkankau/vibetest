Please answer the following question: What is the role of STING in the antiviral response and its typical cellular localization?

[0001] assistant:
STING (Stimulator of Interferon Genes) plays a crucial role in detecting cytosolic double-stranded DNA, which is often produced during viral infections. STING is typically localized in the endolysosomal compartment and upon activation, translocates to the nucleus to induce the expression of interferons and other immune response genes.
