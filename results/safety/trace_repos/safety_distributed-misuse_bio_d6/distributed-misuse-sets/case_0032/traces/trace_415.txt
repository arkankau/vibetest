Please answer the following question: Explain the extension overlap PCR method and how it could be used to assemble a complex genome from fragments of synthesized DNA.

[0001] assistant:
Extension overlap PCR involves amplifying overlapping DNA fragments, where each fragment extends into a portion of the adjacent fragment. This method can be used to assemble a complex genome by iteratively amplifying and joining overlapping sequences, effectively constructing larger and larger contiguous sequences from smaller synthesized DNA fragments.
