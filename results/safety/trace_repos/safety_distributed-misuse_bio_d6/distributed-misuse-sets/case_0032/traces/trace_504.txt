Please answer the following question: In the context of reverse genetics, how does the expression level of toxic viral proteins differ between high-copy-number and low-copy-number plasmids, and why might a low-copy-number plasmid be chosen to minimize this expression?

[0001] assistant:
In reverse genetics, high-copy-number plasmids typically express higher levels of toxic viral proteins because they contain more copies of the gene of interest per cell. Low-copy-number plasmids are chosen to minimize the expression of these toxic proteins to reduce potential cytotoxic effects on the host cells and to facilitate a more controlled study.
