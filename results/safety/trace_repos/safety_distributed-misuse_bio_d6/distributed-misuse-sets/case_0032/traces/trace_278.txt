Please answer the following question: How do botulinum toxin and nerve gases affect the nervous system in terms of their mechanism of action?

[0001] assistant:
Botulinum toxin works by inhibiting the release of acetylcholine at neuromuscular junctions, leading to muscle paralysis. Nerve gases, such as sarin, inhibit acetylcholinesterase, accumulating acetylcholine and causing overstimulation of nerve receptors, leading to symptoms like muscle spasms and paralysis.
