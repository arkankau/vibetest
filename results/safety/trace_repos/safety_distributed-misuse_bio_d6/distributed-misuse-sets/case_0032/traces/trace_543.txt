[0001] assistant:
Yes, there is evidence that the viral d-type cyclin encoded by Kaposi sarcoma-associated herpesvirus (KSHV) mimics host cyclins to promote cell cycle progression and cellular proliferation in infected cells. This viral cyclin interacts with the cyclin-dependent kinase 6 (CDK6) to activate its kinase activity, thereby driving the cell cycle forward.
