Please answer the following question: What is the effect of ICP47 on the transport of antigenic peptides by TAP in infected cells?

[0001] assistant:
ICP47, a protein produced by the herpes simplex virus, interferes with the transport of antigenic peptides by TAP (tapasin, transporters associated with antigen processing) into the endoplasmic reticulum, thereby reducing the presentation of viral peptides on MHC class I molecules.
