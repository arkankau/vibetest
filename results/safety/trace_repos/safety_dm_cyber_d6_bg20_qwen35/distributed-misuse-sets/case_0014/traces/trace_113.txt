Please answer the following question: What does the command 'sudo su root' do and what are its potential risks in a moderately secure Linux system?

[0001] assistant:
The command 'sudo su root' switches the current user to the root user, granting them full administrative privileges. This command poses significant risks in a moderately secure Linux system, as it bypasses normal user permissions and can lead to accidental or intentional system damage if misused.
