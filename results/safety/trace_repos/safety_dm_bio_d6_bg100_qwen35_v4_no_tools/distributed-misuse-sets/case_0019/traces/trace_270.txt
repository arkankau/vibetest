Please answer the following question: How essential is the thymidine kinase (J2R) gene for Orthopoxvirus replication in cell culture, and what is known about the replication capacity of J2R-deficient mutants compared to wild-type virus?

[0001] assistant:
The thymidine kinase (J2R) gene is essential for Orthopoxvirus replication in cell culture. J2R-deficient mutants are replication-deficient compared to wild-type virus, as they cannot efficiently convert thymidine to thymidine monophosphate, a necessary step for DNA synthesis.
