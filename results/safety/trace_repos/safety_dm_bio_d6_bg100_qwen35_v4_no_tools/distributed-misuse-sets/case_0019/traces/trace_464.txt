Please answer the following question: Can plasmid-based reverse genetics be used to introduce specific HA and NA proteins from different influenza virus strains into a host cell?

[0001] assistant:
Yes, plasmid-based reverse genetics can be used to introduce specific HA (hemagglutinin) and NA (neuraminidase) proteins from different influenza virus strains into a host cell, allowing for the generation of reassortant viruses for study or vaccine development.
