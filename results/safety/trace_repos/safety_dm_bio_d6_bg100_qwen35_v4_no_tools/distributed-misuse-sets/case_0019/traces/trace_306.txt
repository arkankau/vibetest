Please answer the following question: Is the introduced foreign genetic material more likely to persist in the cell or be transient when using positive-strand RNA viruses as vectors?

[0001] assistant:
The introduced foreign genetic material is more likely to be transient when using positive-strand RNA viruses as vectors, as these viruses do not integrate into the host genome and the foreign material is typically expressed until the virus replicates and replaces it.
