Please answer the following question: What are the known functions of the V protein in the measles virus, and how might its presence or absence impact the virus's replication in interferon-deficient cells?

[0001] assistant:
The V protein in the measles virus functions as an interferon antagonist, inhibiting the host's interferon response. Its presence in interferon-deficient cells likely does not have a significant impact since the cells already lack interferon, but in normal cells, the V protein helps the virus evade the immune system and enhances viral replication by blocking type I interferon signaling.
