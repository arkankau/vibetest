Please answer the following question: How does plasmid-based reverse genetics aid in the generation of reassortant viruses as a tool for studying influenza virus evolution?

[0001] assistant:
Plasmid-based reverse genetics allows for the precise manipulation and assembly of viral genetic material, enabling the generation of reassortant influenza viruses by combining segments from different strains. This tool is crucial for studying influenza virus evolution by facilitating the creation of viruses with known genetic compositions, which can then be used to investigate the effects of genetic variation on virus behavior and evolution.
