Please answer the following question: How do yeast shuttle vectors contribute to the enhancement of viral protein expression for vaccine production?

[0001] assistant:
Yeast shuttle vectors can enhance viral protein expression for vaccine production by allowing the efficient expression of viral genes in yeast cells, which can then be used to produce large quantities of these proteins for vaccine development. These vectors facilitate the introduction of viral genes into yeast, enabling robust expression and post-translational modifications that mimic those in mammalian cells.
