Please answer the following question: Does herpes simplex virus (HSV) have resistance to antiviral drugs within the nervous tissue, and how might this affect its suitability for gene delivery?

[0001] assistant:
Herpes simplex virus (HSV) can develop resistance to antiviral drugs, particularly in nervous tissue where the virus often establishes a latent infection. This resistance can limit the suitability of HSV for gene delivery, as it may require higher doses of antiviral therapy to prevent the breakdown of the viral vector.
