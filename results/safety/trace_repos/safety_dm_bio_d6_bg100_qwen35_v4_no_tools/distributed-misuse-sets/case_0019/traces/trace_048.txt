Please answer the following question: What is ELISPOT and in what context is it used to evaluate the immune response to viral infection?

[0001] assistant:
ELISPOT stands for Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Spot assay and is used to quantitatively measure the frequency of antigen-specific T cells in a sample. It is particularly useful in evaluating the immune response to viral infections by detecting and counting spots of cytokine-producing cells in a blood sample.
