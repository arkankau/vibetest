Please answer the following question: Are ICP0 and ICP4 proteins involved in the regulation of host cell transcription, and if so, how?

[0001] assistant:
ICP0 and ICP4 are proteins encoded by the herpes simplex virus 1 (HSV-1). Both proteins are involved in the regulation of host cell transcription. ICP4 acts as a transcriptional activator, enhancing the expression of viral and some cellular genes, while ICP0 disrupts cellular regulatory mechanisms, including the ubiquitin-proteasome system, to facilitate viral gene expression and replication.
