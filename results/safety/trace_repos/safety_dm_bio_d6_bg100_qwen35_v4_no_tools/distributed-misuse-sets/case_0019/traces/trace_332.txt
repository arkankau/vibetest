Please answer the following question: What is the known impact of the 591R amino acid substitution on the replication of influenza viruses in mammalian cells?

[0001] assistant:
The 591R amino acid substitution in the matrix protein M1 of influenza viruses has been shown to enhance viral replication in mammalian cells by increasing the stability of the viral ribonucleoprotein complex and promoting viral uncoating.
