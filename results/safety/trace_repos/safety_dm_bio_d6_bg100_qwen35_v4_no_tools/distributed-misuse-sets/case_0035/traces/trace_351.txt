Please answer the following question: What is the role of the Nucleocapsid (N) protein in the SARS-CoV-2 virus and how does it differ from the 3CL protease?

[0001] assistant:
The Nucleocapsid (N) protein in SARS-CoV-2 primarily binds to the viral RNA to form the viral ribonucleoprotein complex, which is essential for viral replication. The 3CL protease, on the other hand, is involved in processing polyproteins required for viral replication, while the N protein does not have enzymatic activity.
