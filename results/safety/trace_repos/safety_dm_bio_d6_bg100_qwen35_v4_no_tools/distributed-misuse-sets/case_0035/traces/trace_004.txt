Please answer the following question: What specific mechanisms do the Vpu proteins of HIV-1 group M and group N use to interact with human tetherin?

[0001] assistant:
The Vpu proteins of HIV-1 group M interact with human tetherin through a mechanism that involves ubiquitination and degradation of tetherin by the proteasome. In contrast, Vpu proteins from group N HIV-1 do not interact with or antagonize human tetherin, likely due to differences in the tetherin protein in humans.
