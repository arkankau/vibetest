[0001] assistant:
The primary challenge in purifying viruses from infected leaf tissue is the variability in viral load and the risk of contamination. However, benefits include the ability to generate large amounts of potentially infective material and the relative simplicity of the process. Compared to other methods, such as cell culture or transgenic plants, purification from leaf tissue is generally quicker and less resource-intensive but may yield lower viral titers.
