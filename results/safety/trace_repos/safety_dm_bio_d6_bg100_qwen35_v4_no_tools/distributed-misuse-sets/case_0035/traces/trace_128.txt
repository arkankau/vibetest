Please answer the following question: How can the three-dimensional structure of viral proteins identified through structural modelling be used to pinpoint potential antigenic epitopes?

[0001] assistant:
The three-dimensional structure of viral proteins can be used to identify potential antigenic epitopes by mapping regions that are highly conserved and exposed on the protein surface, as these are likely to be recognized by the immune system. Modeling tools can predict which parts of the protein are most accessible to antibodies, helping to pinpoint these critical sites.
