Please answer the following question: How does the Epstein-barr virus (viral) IL-10 homologue evade the immune system compared to the traditional IL-10, which has both stimulating and inhibitory effects?

[0001] assistant:
The Epstein-barr virus (EBV) IL-10 homologue, called EB VIRALIL-10 (EBIL-10), primarily functions to inhibit immune responses, specifically by suppressing T-cell activation and cytokine production. Unlike traditional IL-10, which can have both stimulatory and inhibitory effects depending on the context, EBIL-10 tends to exert more consistent immunosuppressive effects, helping the virus evade detection and destruction by the immune system.
