Please answer the following question: Is glycoprotein D necessary for herpes simplex virus (HSV) to release new viral particles from infected cells?

[0001] assistant:
Glycoprotein D is necessary for herpes simplex virus (HSV) to enter host cells, but it is not directly involved in the release of new viral particles from infected cells. The release of new viral particles is primarily facilitated by other viral proteins and cellular machinery.
