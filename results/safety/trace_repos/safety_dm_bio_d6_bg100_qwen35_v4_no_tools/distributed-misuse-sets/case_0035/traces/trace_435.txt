Please answer the following question: What ecological or evolutionary implications could arise from the observed lower level of antibiotic resistance in captive bats compared to bats in close contact with human activities?

[0001] assistant:
Captive bats may have lower levels of antibiotic resistance due to controlled environments with fewer exposure opportunities to antibiotics. This could imply that human activities and interactions, which might expose bats to antibiotic residues, could select for higher resistance levels. Evolutionarily, this suggests that selective pressures from human activities can drive rapid changes in bacterial resistance within bat populations.
