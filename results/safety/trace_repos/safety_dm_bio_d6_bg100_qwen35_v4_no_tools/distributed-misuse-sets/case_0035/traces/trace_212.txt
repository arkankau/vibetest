Please answer the following question: What is the IRES sequence and how does it affect the expression of the downstream gene in a viral vector when linked with another gene?

[0001] assistant:
The IRES (Internal Ribosome Entry Site) sequence is a non-polyadenylated sequence that allows for cap-independent translation initiation. When linked to another gene in a viral vector, it enables the downstream gene to be expressed independently of the cap-binding machinery, thus facilitating simultaneous translation of both genes.
