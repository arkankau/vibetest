Please answer the following question: What are epigenetic modifications and how do they affect the DNA and gene expression in the context of herpesvirus latency and reactivation?

[0001] assistant:
Epigenetic modifications, such as DNA methylation and histone modifications, do not alter the DNA sequence but change how genes are expressed. In the context of herpesvirus latency and reactivation, these modifications help maintain viral DNA in a quiescent state during latency and facilitate viral gene expression and replication during reactivation.
