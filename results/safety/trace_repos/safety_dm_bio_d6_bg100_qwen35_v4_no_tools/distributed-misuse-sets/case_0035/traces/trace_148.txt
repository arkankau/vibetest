Please answer the following question: In what ways does relative humidity influence the size and behavior of droplets containing airborne pathogens?

[0001] assistant:
Relative humidity can influence the size and behavior of droplets containing airborne pathogens by affecting the rate of evaporation. Higher humidity reduces evaporation, leading to larger droplets that may remain suspended in the air for longer periods. Lower humidity accelerates evaporation, resulting in smaller droplets that can remain airborne and be more easily dispersed.
