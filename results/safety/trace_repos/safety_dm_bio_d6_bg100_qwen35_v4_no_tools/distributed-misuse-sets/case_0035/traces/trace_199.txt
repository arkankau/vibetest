Please answer the following question: What is the primary function of Viral protein kinase (VPK) in herpesvirus infection and does it interfere with interferon signaling?

[0001] assistant:
Viral protein kinase (VPK) in herpesviruses primarily functions to phosphorylate and inactivate interferon-induced proteins, thereby interfering with interferon signaling and helping the virus to establish infection.
