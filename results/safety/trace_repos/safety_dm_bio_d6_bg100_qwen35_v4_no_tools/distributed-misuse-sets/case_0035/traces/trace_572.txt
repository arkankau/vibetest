The introduction of mutations through a low-fidelity RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (RdRP) helps in the study of specific genetic changes in RNA viruses by increasing the rate of random mutations, allowing researchers to identify and characterize the effects of particular mutations on viral function and evolution.
