Please answer the following question: Which of the listed enzymes is known to be involved in the integration of viral DNA into the host cell chromosomes and how does it function in this process?

[0001] assistant:
Integrase is the enzyme known to be involved in the integration of viral DNA into the host cell chromosomes. Integrase catalyzes the recombination of viral DNA into the host genome, typically by first cleaving two sites on the viral DNA and then integrating the resulting ends into the host DNA.
