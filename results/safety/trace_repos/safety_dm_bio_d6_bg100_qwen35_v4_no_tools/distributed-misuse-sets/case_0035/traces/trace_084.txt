Please answer the following question: How does the inability of herpesviruses to infect neuronal cells limit their effectiveness for gene therapy in the CNS, and are there any strategies to overcome this limitation?

[0001] assistant:
The inability of herpesviruses to infect neuronal cells limits their effectiveness for gene therapy in the central nervous system (CNS) because these viruses primarily target glial cells, not neurons. Strategies to overcome this limitation include engineering the viruses to enhance neuronal tropism or using a combination of viral and non-viral vectors to target both glial cells and neurons.
