[0001] assistant:
An increase in Mycopticus coronatus (Myl-CoV) quantity can contribute to the severity of White-Nose Syndrome (WNS) pathology in little brown bats by enhancing fungal growth and metabolism, thereby increasing tissue damage and stress on the bats.
