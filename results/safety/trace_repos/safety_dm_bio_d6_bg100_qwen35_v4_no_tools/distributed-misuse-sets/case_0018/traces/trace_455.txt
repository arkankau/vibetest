Yes, EBNA1 plays a significant role in mediating the replication of the viral genome in Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) by binding to specific sequences in the Epstein-Barr nuclear antigen region and facilitating the initiation and maintenance of viral chromatin during the latent and lytic phases of infection.
