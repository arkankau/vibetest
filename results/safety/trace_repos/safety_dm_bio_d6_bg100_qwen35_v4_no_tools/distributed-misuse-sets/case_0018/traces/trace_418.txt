Please answer the following question: Can each viral RNA segment of influenza A viruses be separately cloned into plasmids and co-transfected into cells to generate an infectious virus?

Yes, each viral RNA segment of influenza A viruses can be separately cloned into plasmids and co-transfected into cells to generate an infectious virus. This technique is known as reverse genetics and allows for the production of viruses with specific genetic modifications.
