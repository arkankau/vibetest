[0001] assistant:
OVs (oncolytic viruses) have complex pharmacodynamics due to their ability to modulate immune responses, which can vary between patients and tumors. This immunomodulatory nature presents challenges for precise pharmacodynamic characterization compared to traditional drugs, as it involves multiple interacting biological pathways.
