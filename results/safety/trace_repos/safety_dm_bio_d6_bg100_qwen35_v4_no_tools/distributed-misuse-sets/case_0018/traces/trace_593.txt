Please answer the following question: What is the PB2 protein in the context of the avian-origin influenza A H7N9 virus and its function?

[0001] assistant:
The PB2 protein in the avian-origin influenza A H7N9 virus is a viral RNA-dependent RNA polymerase subunit that plays a crucial role in the replication and transcription of viral RNA. It is essential for the virus's ability to reproduce and is a target for antiviral drugs.
