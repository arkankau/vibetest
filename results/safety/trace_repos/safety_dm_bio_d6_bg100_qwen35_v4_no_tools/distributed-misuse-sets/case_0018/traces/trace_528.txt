Please answer the following question: How do polycistronic vectors enable the expression of multiple genes from a single promoter?

[0001] assistant:
Polycistronic vectors enable the expression of multiple genes from a single promoter by having a single promoter that drives the transcription of a single mRNA molecule carrying multiple open reading frames (ORFs) that are separated by intervening sequences. These vectors take advantage of bacterial ribosomal binding sites to ensure that the mRNA is translated into the correct proteins in the correct order.
