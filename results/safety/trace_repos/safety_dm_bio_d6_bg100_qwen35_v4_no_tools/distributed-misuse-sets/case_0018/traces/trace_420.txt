Please answer the following question: What is the primary cause of wound botulism, and is it linked to the botulism neurotoxin (Botox)?

[0001] assistant:
The primary cause of wound botulism is the introduction of Clostridium botulinum spores into a wound, which then grow and produce botulism neurotoxin. Wound botulism is not linked to Botox, as Botox is a purified form of botulinum toxin used therapeutically and not associated with infections.
