Please answer the following question: Which protein of Varicella-zoster virus (VZV) is known to inhibit apoptosis in infected cells and contribute to oncogenesis?

The protein known to inhibit apoptosis in infected cells and contribute to oncogenesis in Varicella-zoster virus (VZV) is pp30 (also known as vMIR or vmiR).
