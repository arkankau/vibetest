Please answer the following question: What role does the hemagglutinin gene from H1N1 play in viral attachment and entry into host cells?

[0001] assistant:
The hemagglutinin gene from H1N1 encodes a protein that facilitates viral attachment to host cells by binding to sialic acid receptors, and it also mediates the fusion of the viral and cellular membranes, allowing viral entry into the host cell.
