[0001] assistant:
Advantages of BACs include high copy number and ease of manipulation, but they can be costly and labor-intensive. Overlapping ssDNA oligos offer flexibility and cost-effectiveness but require accurate design and assembly. Gene fragment synthesis and assembly allow for precise construction but can be limited by the size of fragments. Environmental DNA swabs are non-invasive and can capture diverse viral elements, but they might introduce contamination and require stringent controls.
