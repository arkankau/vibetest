Please answer the following question: What is Multiplex automatable genome engineering and how is it used in genetic modifications of bacteria?

[0001] assistant:
Multiplex automatable genome engineering (MAGE) is a technique that allows for the simultaneous editing of multiple DNA sites in a genome. It is used in genetic modifications of bacteria by precisely editing multiple genes at once, facilitating the creation of strains with specific traits for research and biotechnological applications.
