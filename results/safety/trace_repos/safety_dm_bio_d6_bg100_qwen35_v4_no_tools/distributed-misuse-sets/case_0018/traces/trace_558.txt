Please answer the following question: Are there any specific environmental or clinical factors that make Candida auris more likely to develop azole resistance compared to the other listed pathogens?

[0001] assistant:
Candida auris is more likely to develop azole resistance due to its ability to acquire and express efflux pumps and mutations in target enzymes, which are less common in other Candida species. Environmental factors such as intensive care unit settings and the use of broad-spectrum antifungals also contribute to its resistance development.
