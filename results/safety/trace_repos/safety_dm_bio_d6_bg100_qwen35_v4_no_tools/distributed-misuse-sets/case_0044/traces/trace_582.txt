Please answer the following question: How does the acquisition of novel genetic material through re-assortment or recombination enhance a pathogen's ability to adapt to a new host and become airborne?

Re-assortment and recombination can introduce new genetic traits that allow a pathogen to better adapt to a new host, potentially enhancing its virulence or transmission methods. By acquiring genetic material from other pathogens, a virus can sometimes gain the ability to become airborne, which can significantly increase its transmissibility.
