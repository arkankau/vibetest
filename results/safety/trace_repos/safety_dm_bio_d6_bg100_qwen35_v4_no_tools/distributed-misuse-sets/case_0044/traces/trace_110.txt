Please answer the following question: What is the in vitro CRISPR-Cas9 method and how can it be applied to generate fusions of fluorescent proteins to VP16?

[0001] assistant:
The in vitro CRISPR-Cas9 method involves using the Cas9 enzyme and guide RNAs to edit DNA sequences in vitro. This method can be applied to generate fusions of fluorescent proteins to VP16 by designing specific guide RNAs to target the VP16 gene, then fusing a fluorescent protein to the VP16 sequence during the editing process.
