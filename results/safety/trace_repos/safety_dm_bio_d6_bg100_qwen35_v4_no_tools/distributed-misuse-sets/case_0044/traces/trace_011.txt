Please answer the following question: Which method of recovering infectious virus from cloned cDNA is commonly utilized in reverse genetics for RNA viruses, and what are the key steps involved?

[0001] assistant:
The method commonly utilized is recombination between a cloning vector and a viral cDNA. Key steps include cloning the viral cDNA into a plasmid, recombination with a helper plasmid to generate infectious virus, and selection of recombinant virus particles.
