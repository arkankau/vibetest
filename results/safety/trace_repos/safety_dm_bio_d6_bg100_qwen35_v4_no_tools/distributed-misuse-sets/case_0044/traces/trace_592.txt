Please answer the following question: How do the immediate-early (IE) genes of cytomegalovirus (CMV) regulate the expression of early and late viral genes during the viral replication cycle?

[0001] assistant:
The immediate-early (IE) genes of cytomegalovirus (CMV) encode transcription factors that activate the expression of early viral genes, which in turn regulate the expression of late viral genes. These IE proteins establish the viral transcriptional program necessary for the progression of the viral replication cycle.
