Please answer the following question: How does the VP16 protein interact with cellular transcription factors to promote viral gene expression during the initial stages of HSV infection?

[0001] assistant:
The VP16 protein from HSV interacts with cellular transcription factors, such as the retinoblastoma protein (pRb), to recruit the general transcription machinery to viral promoters, thereby enhancing the transcription of viral genes.
