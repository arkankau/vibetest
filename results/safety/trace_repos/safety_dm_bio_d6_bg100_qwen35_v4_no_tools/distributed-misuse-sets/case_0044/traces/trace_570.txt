Please answer the following question: Can a lentivirus vector accommodate a 51 kb gene insert, and what are its primary limitations?

[0001] assistant:
A lentivirus vector typically has a capacity limit of around 8.5 to 10 kb for gene inserts, so it cannot accommodate a 51 kb gene insert. The primary limitations are the vector's packaging capacity and the risk of insertional mutagenesis due to random integration.
