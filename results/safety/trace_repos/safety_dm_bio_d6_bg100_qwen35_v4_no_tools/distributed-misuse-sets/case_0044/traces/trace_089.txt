Please answer the following question: What are the technical complexities involved in Cell-Free Protein Synthesis that might make it less feasible for classical biochemistry laboratories?

[0001] assistant:
Cell-Free Protein Synthesis can be technically complex due to the need for precise control over reaction conditions, including temperature, pH, and ion concentrations. Additionally, ensuring the stability and activity of the ribosomes and other cellular components outside their native environment can be challenging.
