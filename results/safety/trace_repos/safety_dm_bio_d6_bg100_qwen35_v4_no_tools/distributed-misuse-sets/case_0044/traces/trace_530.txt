Please answer the following question: The viral interferon regulatory factor (vIRF) is known to interfere with interferon signaling. How does it affect the host's interferon response?

[0001] assistant:
The viral interferon regulatory factor (vIRF) interferes with the host's interferon response by suppressing the activation of interferon-stimulated genes, thereby reducing the host cell's antiviral state and allowing the virus to replicate more efficiently.
