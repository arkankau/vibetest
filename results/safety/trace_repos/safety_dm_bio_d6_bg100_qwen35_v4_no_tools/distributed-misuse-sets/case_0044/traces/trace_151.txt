Please answer the following question: How does the periplasmic environment of E. coli support the stability and functionality of disulfide-rich toxins?

[0001] assistant:
The periplasmic environment of E. coli contains oxidizing conditions, specifically due to the presence of peroxiredoxins and Dsb enzymes, which facilitate the formation and maintenance of disulfide bonds necessary for the stability and functionality of disulfide-rich toxins.
