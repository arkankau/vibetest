[0001] assistant:
OVs (oncolytic viruses) can exhibit unique pharmacokinetic behaviors due to their selective replication in tumor cells, leading to variable virus distribution and persistent viral titers. This can complicate traditional PK modeling and characterization due to the non-uniform viral spread and potential immune responses.
