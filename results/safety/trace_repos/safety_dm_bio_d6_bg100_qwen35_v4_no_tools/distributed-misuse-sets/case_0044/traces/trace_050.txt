Gene drives can be used to ensure the transmission of a particular gene to the next generation at a higher frequency than normal, allowing for the potential manipulation of population genetics. For example, they can be designed to spread genes for pest control, such as making mosquitoes resistant to malaria, or to control invasive species by driving them to local extinction.
