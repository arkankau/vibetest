Please answer the following question: What is the function of type II interferon in the immune response to SARS-CoV-2 infection?

[0001] assistant:
Type II interferon, specifically interferon-gamma (IFN-γ), plays a role in enhancing immune responses to SARS-CoV-2 by activating macrophages and T cells, and promoting anti-viral states in infected cells.
