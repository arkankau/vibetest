Please answer the following question: What is the specific function of RNR in the context of DNA replication, as opposed to other viral processes like capsid assembly or integration?

[0001] assistant:
RNR stands for Ribonucleotide Reductase, which is crucial for DNA replication as it converts ribonucleotides to deoxyribonucleotides, the building blocks needed for DNA synthesis. This function is specific to DNA replication and is not involved in capsid assembly or integration processes.
