Please answer the following question: How do ambisense plasmids facilitate the expression of viral RNA and proteins in reverse genetics systems?

[0001] assistant:
Ambisense plasmids contain genes that can be transcribed from both strands of the DNA, facilitating the expression of viral RNA and proteins. This bidirectional transcription is crucial for reverse genetics systems, allowing for the efficient production of viral genomes and proteins needed for studying viral replication and function.
