Please answer the following question: Is herpes simplex virus (HSV) known to have resistance to antiviral drugs within the nervous tissue, and could this be an advantage for gene delivery?

[0001] assistant:
HSV can develop resistance to antiviral drugs, particularly in nervous tissue, which can complicate treatment. However, this same tropism for nervous tissue makes HSV a valuable vector for gene delivery, as it can efficiently transport genetic material to neurons.
