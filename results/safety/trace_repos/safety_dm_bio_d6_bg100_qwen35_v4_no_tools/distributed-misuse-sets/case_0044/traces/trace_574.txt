Please answer the following question: How do restriction enzyme markers facilitate the monitoring and confirmation of gene segments during the development of a reverse genetics-based influenza virus vaccine?

[0001] assistant:
Restriction enzyme markers facilitate the monitoring and confirmation of gene segments by allowing specific, targeted cutting of DNA at known sites, which helps in accurately assembling and verifying the correct integration of genetic material into the vaccine. This ensures that the correct gene segments are present and correctly oriented for the development of an effective vaccine.
