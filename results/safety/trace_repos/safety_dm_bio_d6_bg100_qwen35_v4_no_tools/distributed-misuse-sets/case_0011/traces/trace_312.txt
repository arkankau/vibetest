Please answer the following question: How do B lymphocytes contribute to the immune response against HSV, and are they the primary cells involved in the early response in HSK?

[0001] assistant:
B lymphocytes contribute to the immune response against HSV by producing antibodies that neutralize the virus and help clear it from the body. However, they are not the primary cells involved in the early response in Herpetic Stromal Keratitis (HSK); that role is primarily played by innate immune cells such as dendritic cells and natural killer cells.
