[0001] assistant:
The Pol I promoter can be used to replicate viral RNA in the cytoplasm, but this is less common and less efficient compared to other methods such as the Pol III or Pol II promoters. The Pol I promoter is typically used for nuclear gene expression and is not naturally involved in cytoplasmic RNA replication.
