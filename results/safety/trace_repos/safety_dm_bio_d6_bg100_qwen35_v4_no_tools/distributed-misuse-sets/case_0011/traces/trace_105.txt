Burkholderia mallei and Burkholderia pseudomallei both produce various proteins that contribute to their pathogenicity, but the flagellin protein is frequently cited for its role in tissue tropism and severity of infection in murine models. Additionally, the type III secretion system (T3SS) effectors are often highlighted for their impact on host cell interactions and disease severity.
