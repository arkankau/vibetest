Please answer the following question: Explain the role of the transmembrane domain in viral entry and its potential impact on infectivity.

[0001] assistant:
The transmembrane domain of viral proteins, such as envelope glycoproteins, helps the virus fuse with or insert into the host cell membrane, facilitating viral entry. Its proper function is crucial for infectivity, as any disruption can impair the virus's ability to enter and infect host cells.
