Please answer the following question: Which plants can serve as hosts for the maize dwarf mosaic virus, and how do they contribute to the virus's spread in agricultural settings?

[0001] assistant:
Maize and other cereal crops like wheat and sorghum can serve as hosts for the maize dwarf mosaic virus. These plants contribute to the virus's spread through insect vectors, particularly leafhoppers, which feed on the plants and transmit the virus to healthy crops as they move between them.
