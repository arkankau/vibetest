Please answer the following question: What does gB do in herpesvirus infection and is it known to affect MHC class I molecules?

[0001] assistant:
gB is a glycoprotein of herpesvirus that aids in viral entry by interacting with host cell receptors. It is known to affect MHC class I molecules, promoting the downregulation of these molecules on the infected cell surface, which can help evade the host's immune response.
