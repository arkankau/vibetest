Please answer the following question: What are the transmission routes of enterovirus/rhinovirus, and how do these routes contribute to the persistence of the virus in the human population?

[0001] assistant:
Enterovirus and rhinovirus are primarily transmitted through respiratory droplets when an infected person coughs or sneezes, and through fecal-oral route due to contaminated food or water. These transmission routes contribute to their persistence in the human population by facilitating easy person-to-person spread, especially in close-contact settings like schools and households.
