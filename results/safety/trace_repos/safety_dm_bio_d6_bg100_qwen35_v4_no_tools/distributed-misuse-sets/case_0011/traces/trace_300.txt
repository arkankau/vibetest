Please answer the following question: What are Mononegavirales, and why are their virions resistant to protease digestion for nucleic acid release?

[0001] assistant:
Mononegavirales is a order of viruses that includes families like Paramyxoviridae and Rhabdoviridae. Their virions are resistant to protease digestion because the viral envelope glycoproteins are covalently linked to the nucleocapsid, which protects the nucleic acid from proteases during infection.
