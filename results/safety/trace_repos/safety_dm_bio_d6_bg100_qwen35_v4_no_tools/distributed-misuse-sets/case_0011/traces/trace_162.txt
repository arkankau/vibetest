Please answer the following question: What is the T2A sequence and how does it function to enable the expression of two proteins from a single mRNA transcript?

[0001] assistant:
The T2A sequence, also known as a ribosome skipping sequence, is a type of internal ribosome entry site (iRIS) that allows for the co-expression of two proteins from a single mRNA transcript. This sequence functions by enabling the ribosome to skip from the first open reading frame (ORF) to the second ORF, ensuring that both proteins are expressed efficiently.
