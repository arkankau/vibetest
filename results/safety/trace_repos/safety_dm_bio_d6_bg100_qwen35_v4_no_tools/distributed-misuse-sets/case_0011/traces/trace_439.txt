Please answer the following question: How does a Master Donor Virus (MDV) contribute to the understanding of viral envelope proteins in co-infection scenarios within reverse genetics research?

[0001] assistant:
A Master Donor Virus (MDV) serves as a backbone vector in reverse genetics, providing a background strain for the expression of foreign viral genes, including envelope proteins. In co-infection studies, MDV helps elucidate how different viral envelope proteins interact and influence each other's function or the host immune response.
