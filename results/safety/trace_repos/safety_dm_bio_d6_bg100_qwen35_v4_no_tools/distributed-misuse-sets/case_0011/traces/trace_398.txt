Please answer the following question: How does the spike protein of SARS-CoV-2 facilitate viral entry into host cells and what makes it a target for the immune system?

[0001] assistant:
The spike protein of SARS-CoV-2 facilitates viral entry into host cells by binding to angiotensin-converting enzyme 2 (ACE2) receptors on the cell surface, then fusing the viral and cellular membranes. This protein is a target for the immune system because it is highly variable and antigenic, allowing the body to mount an effective immune response against the virus.
