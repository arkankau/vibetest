Please answer the following question: Explain the role of DNA polymerase in the viral DNA synthesis process and identify why it is a key target for antiviral drugs.

[0001] assistant:
DNA polymerase is crucial for viral DNA synthesis as it synthesizes new DNA strands by adding nucleotides to a primer, extending the growing DNA strand. It is a key target for antiviral drugs because inhibiting this enzyme can prevent viral replication, thus stopping the spread of the virus.
