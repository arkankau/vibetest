[0001] assistant:
In the context of a chimeric virus used for gene therapy, endosomes primarily function to internalize and temporarily sequester the viral particles. Endosomes typically handle these viral particles by fusing with late endosomes or lysosomes, where the viral envelope is usually degraded, releasing the viral genome into the cytoplasm for gene delivery.
