Please answer the following question: How does tangential flow filtration of spent growth media alter the concentration of residual nutrients and cell debris before the filtrate is sent to drain, and what effect does this have on downstream wastewater detectability?

[0001] assistant:
Tangential flow filtration reduces the concentration of residual nutrients and cell debris in the spent growth media by filtering the liquid phase while allowing larger particles, including cell debris, to be retained. This results in a cleaner filtrate that is less detectable in downstream wastewater treatment processes.
