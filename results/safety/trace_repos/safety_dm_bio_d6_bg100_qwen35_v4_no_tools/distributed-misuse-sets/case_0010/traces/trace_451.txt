Please answer the following question: Can the upregulation of heparanase promote immune responses in the host cell, and if so, what are the mechanisms involved?

[0001] assistant:
Heparanase upregulation can promote immune responses in the host cell by enhancing matrix degradation, which releases heparan sulfate proteoglycans that act as immunomodulators. These molecules can activate immune cells and influence cytokine production, thereby contributing to inflammation and immune cell recruitment.
