Yes, studies suggest that the number of Bacillus anthracis spores needed to cause significant disease can vary widely depending on the route of exposure. Inhalation exposure typically requires fewer spores (likely fewer than 10,000) compared to cutaneous exposure, which can be more resistant to infection (potentially requiring 1,000 to 500,000 spores).
