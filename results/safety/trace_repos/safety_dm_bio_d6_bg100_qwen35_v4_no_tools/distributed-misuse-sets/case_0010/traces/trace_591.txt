[0001] assistant:
Pre-existing immunity to related non-zoonotic pathogens can influence the pathogen's transition to human-to-human transmission by either enhancing or inhibiting it, depending on the specific interactions between the pathogens and the immune responses. If the pre-existing immunity provides some cross-reactive protection, it may reduce the severity of disease and transmission. Conversely, if the immunity does not provide protection or if it drives the pathogen to evade the immune system, it could facilitate more efficient transmission.
