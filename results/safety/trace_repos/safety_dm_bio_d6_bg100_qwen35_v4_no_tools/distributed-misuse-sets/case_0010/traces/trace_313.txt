Please answer the following question: Does the transport of viral RNA into the nucleus facilitate the recovery of infectious virus from cDNA in segmented negative-strand RNA viruses, and if so, how?

[0001] assistant:
Transport of viral RNA into the nucleus is crucial for the recovery of infectious virus from cDNA in segmented negative-strand RNA viruses. This process allows for the synthesis of positive-strand RNA, which serves as a template for viral protein synthesis and genome replication, facilitating the production of infectious virus particles.
