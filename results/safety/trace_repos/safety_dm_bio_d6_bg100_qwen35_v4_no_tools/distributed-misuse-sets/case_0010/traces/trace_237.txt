Please answer the following question: What are the key features of first generation adenovirus vectors?

[0001] assistant:
First generation adenovirus vectors are generally non-replicative and use a deleted E1 region to allow for transcription of the inserted gene. They can stably transduce dividing and non-dividing cells but have a limited capacity for foreign DNA (typically up to 7-8 kb).
