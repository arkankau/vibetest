Please answer the following question: What does PCR amplification involve and how can it be used to detect infectious viruses in VAC-BAC experiments?

[0001] assistant:
PCR amplification involves using enzymes to make multiple copies of a specific DNA sequence. In VAC-BAC experiments, it can be used to detect infectious viruses by amplifying viral DNA from a sample, making it possible to identify and quantify the presence of the virus.
