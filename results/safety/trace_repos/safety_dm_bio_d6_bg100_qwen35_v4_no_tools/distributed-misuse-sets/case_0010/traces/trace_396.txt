Please answer the following question: What are the advantages of yeast systems in producing larger quantities of RNA compared to bacterial systems in the context of reverse genetics studies involving RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Yeast systems can produce larger quantities of RNA compared to bacterial systems because yeast has RNA polymerases that can transcribe longer and more complex sequences efficiently. Additionally, yeast allows for the expression of eukaryotic post-transcriptional regulatory elements, which can affect RNA stability and processing, providing more accurate model systems for RNA viruses.
