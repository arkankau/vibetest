Please answer the following question: What are the morphological characteristics of Clostridium spores, and how do they stain with malachite green?

[0001] assistant:
Clostridium spores are highly spherical or oval, with a thick, tough outer coat and a inner cortex. They stain with malachite green through the malachite green exclusion test, appearing as non-staining (pink) spores against a green background.
