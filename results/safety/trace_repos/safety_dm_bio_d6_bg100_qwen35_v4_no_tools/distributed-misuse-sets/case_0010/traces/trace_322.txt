Please answer the following question: How might the viral d-type cyclin encoded by Kaposi sarcoma-associated herpesvirus (KSHV) contribute to viral latency in infected cells?

[0001] assistant:
The viral d-type cyclin encoded by Kaposi sarcoma-associated herpesvirus (KSHV) contributes to viral latency by promoting cell cycle progression and inhibiting cell death, which helps maintain the infected cell in a state where it continues to produce viral proteins without undergoing apoptosis.
