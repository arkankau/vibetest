Current anti-epidemic measures for enterovirus/rhinovirus include frequent hand washing, wearing masks, social distancing, and avoiding close contact with sick individuals. Measures like asymptomatic testing are also being used. Some measures, such as antiviral medications, are often ineffective against these RNA viruses due to their high mutation rates.
