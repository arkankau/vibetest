[0001] assistant:
The MVA-T7 helper virus rescue method involves using a modified vaccinia virus Ankara (MVA) platform to rescue and propagate mononegavirales, such as Ebola or Marburg viruses, which are otherwise difficult to manipulate due to their inherent stability. This method utilizes the T7 RNA polymerase to transcribe viral RNA from cloned DNA templates, enabling the rescue of these viruses for study and vaccine development.
