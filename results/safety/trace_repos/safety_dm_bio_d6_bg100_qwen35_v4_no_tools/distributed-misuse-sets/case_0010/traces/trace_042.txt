Please answer the following question: How does the concept of stochastic mutation and selection apply to the modeling of disease spread in epidemic dynamics?

[0001] assistant:
Stochastic mutation and selection in epidemic dynamics models account for random genetic changes in pathogens and the survival of more fit variants, which can lead to changes in the disease's transmissibility or virulence over time. These processes are often modeled using stochastic simulations to capture the variability and unpredictability in pathogen evolution during an epidemic.
