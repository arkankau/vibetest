Please answer the following question: What is the role of the matrix (M) protein in the influenza virus, and is it related to resistance to adamantane-class antivirals?

[0001] assistant:
The matrix (M) protein in the influenza virus is involved in assembly and budding of new virus particles by organizing the viral RNA and nucleoproteins. Yes, the M2 protein, which is associated with the matrix protein, is related to resistance to adamantane-class antivirals.
