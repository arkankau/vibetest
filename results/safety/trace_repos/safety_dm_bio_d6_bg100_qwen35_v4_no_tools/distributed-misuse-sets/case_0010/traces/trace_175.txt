A bidirectional transcription vector allows for the simultaneous expression of both viral RNA and mRNA by using two promoters that drive transcription in opposite directions. This facilitates the production of both viral antigens and adjuvant mRNA, enhancing the immune response in influenza A virus vaccine development.
