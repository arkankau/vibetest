Please answer the following question: How does TAR cloning facilitate the assembly of large DNA molecules into yeast centromeric plasmids?

TAR cloning facilitates the assembly of large DNA molecules into yeast centromeric plasmids by using a two-step PCR process to generate adaptors that bridge the gap between the large DNA fragment and the plasmid, allowing for efficient and specific recombination in yeast.
