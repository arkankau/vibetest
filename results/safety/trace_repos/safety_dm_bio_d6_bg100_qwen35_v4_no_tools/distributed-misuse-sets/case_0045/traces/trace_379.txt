Please answer the following question: Can the insertion process of exogenous DNA be completely random, and what are the implications of this randomness for mutations?

[0001] assistant:
Insertion of exogenous DNA is not completely random; it can be influenced by factors such as target site preference of the integrating enzyme. This non-randomness can have implications for mutations, potentially concentrating mutations in certain regions and affecting gene function more significantly than if insertions were random.
