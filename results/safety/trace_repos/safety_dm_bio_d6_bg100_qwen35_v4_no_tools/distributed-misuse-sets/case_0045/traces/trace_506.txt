Please answer the following question: How does a yeast shuttle vector facilitate the integration of viral cDNA into the yeast genome?

[0001] assistant:
A yeast shuttle vector contains sequences that allow it to replicate in both yeast and mammalian cells, facilitating the transfer of viral cDNA into yeast. Once inside yeast, these vectors can integrate the viral cDNA into the yeast genome through homologous recombination.
