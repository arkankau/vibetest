Please answer the following question: What are the benefits of using organ-on-a-chip technology in studying viral entry and replication in diverse human cell types?

[0001] assistant:
Organ-on-a-chip technology allows for the study of viral entry and replication in diverse human cell types under conditions that closely mimic in vivo environments, providing more accurate and predictive data compared to traditional cell culture methods. This technology reduces the need for animal models and can accelerate drug development and toxicity testing.
