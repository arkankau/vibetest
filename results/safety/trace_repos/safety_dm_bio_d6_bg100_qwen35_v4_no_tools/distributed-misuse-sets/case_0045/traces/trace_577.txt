Please answer the following question: What is the effect of LMP1 activation on the JAK-STAT pathway in EBV-infected cells, and how does it lead to cellular proliferation?

[0001] assistant:
LMP1 activation in EBV-infected cells activates the JAK-STAT pathway, leading to the production of cytokines and other growth factors that promote cellular proliferation. This activation results in the phosphorylation of STAT proteins, which then translocate to the nucleus to induce the expression of genes involved in cell growth and survival.
