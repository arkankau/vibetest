Please answer the following question: Does the HSV protein ICP4 play a role in the inhibition of host cell apoptosis and how does it do so?

[0001] assistant:
ICP4, a viral transcription factor from the HSV genome, plays a role in inhibiting host cell apoptosis by upregulating anti-apoptotic genes and downregulating pro-apoptotic genes, thereby promoting viral replication and survival.
