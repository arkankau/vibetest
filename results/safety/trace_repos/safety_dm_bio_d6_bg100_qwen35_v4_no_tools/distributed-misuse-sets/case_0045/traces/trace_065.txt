Please answer the following question: What are the reasons why the absence of suitable restriction enzyme sites in the viral genome is or is not a common challenge in constructing infectious clones of flaviviruses like Dengue virus?

[0001] assistant:
The absence of suitable restriction enzyme sites in flaviviruses like Dengue virus can be a challenge in constructing infectious clones because it limits the precise integration of genetic elements needed for replication. However, this is not a common challenge due to the development of techniques such as overlap extension PCR and site-directed mutagenesis that can create these sites.
