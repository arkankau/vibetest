Please answer the following question: How does the use of whole-body aerosol exposure systems prevent the spread of the aerosolized virus to other mice in the same cage?

[0001] assistant:
Whole-body aerosol exposure systems prevent the spread of aerosolized virus to other mice in the same cage by exposing each mouse individually to the aerosol, thereby minimizing direct contact and reducing the risk of viral transmission among cage mates.
