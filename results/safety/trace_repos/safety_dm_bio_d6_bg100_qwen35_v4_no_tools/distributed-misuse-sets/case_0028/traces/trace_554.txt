Please answer the following question: Does the RNAi machinery process VA RNAs into microRNAs (mivaRNAs) that might affect cellular gene expression during the late phase of infection?

[0001] assistant:
I don't know. VA RNAs are not typically processed into microRNAs (miRNAs) by the RNAi machinery. The RNAi machinery processes small interfering RNAs (siRNAs) and miRNAs to regulate gene expression, but VA RNAs (viral antisense RNAs) are usually involved in other viral processes.
