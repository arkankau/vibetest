Please answer the following question: What is the known role of Galactosidase in biological systems and does it influence cytokine production?

[0001] assistant:
Galactosidase is an enzyme that catalyzes the hydrolysis of galactoside substrates. It does not directly influence cytokine production, though its role in glycosylation processes can indirectly affect cellular functions and signaling, which may in turn influence cytokine production.
