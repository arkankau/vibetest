Please answer the following question: When researchers try to introduce a circular piece of DNA into a heterogeneous mixture of Gram-negative and Gram-positive bacteria (such as activated-sludge samples), what host factors most often prevent that DNA from becoming stably inherited after entry?

[0001] assistant:
The host factors most often preventing stable inheritance of introduced circular DNA in a mixture of Gram-negative and Gram-positive bacteria include restriction-modification systems and differences in plasmid partitioning mechanisms between the two bacterial types.
