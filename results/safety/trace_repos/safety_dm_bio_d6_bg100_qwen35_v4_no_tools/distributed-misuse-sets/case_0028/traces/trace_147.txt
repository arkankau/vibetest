Please answer the following question: What is the process of using alternative codons to alter the DNA sequence while maintaining the amino acid sequence and function of the protein, and how does it help in obfuscating the sequence?

[0001] assistant:
The process is called codon optimization or codon rewriting. By using alternative codons that are less common in an organism's genome, the process obfuscates the DNA sequence without changing the protein's amino acid sequence or function, which can help in avoiding detection by certain molecular biology tools or in reducing the likelihood of recognition by restriction enzymes.
