Please answer the following question: Is it known that the Measles virus (MeV) exploits the ACE2 receptor in the upper respiratory tract similar to how SARS-CoV-2 infects cells?

[0001] assistant:
Yes, it is known that the Measles virus (MeV) exploits the ACE2 receptor for infection, although it primarily uses the CD150 (SLAMF1) receptor. However, MeV can also use ACE2, particularly in the upper respiratory tract, in a manner similar to how SARS-CoV-2 infects cells.
