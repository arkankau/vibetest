[0001] assistant:
In the study of herpesvirus infection, syncytium formation is significant because it indicates widespread viral spread within host cells, leading to the fusion of multiple cell membranes and the creation of a single large multinucleated cell. This process is often associated with high viral replication and can be observed in infected tissue cultures and is used as a marker for certain herpesvirus infections.
