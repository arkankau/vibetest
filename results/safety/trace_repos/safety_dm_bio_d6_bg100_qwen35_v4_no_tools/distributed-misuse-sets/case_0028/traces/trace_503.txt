Please answer the following question: In environmental microbiology, what genetic features distinguish mobile genetic elements that are known to colonize many different bacterial genera from those that are restricted to a single genus?

[0001] assistant:
Mobile genetic elements that can colonize many different bacterial genera often possess broader host range determinants and can integrate into multiple types of bacterial genomes. In contrast, those restricted to a single genus typically have more specific host range factors and integration sites.
