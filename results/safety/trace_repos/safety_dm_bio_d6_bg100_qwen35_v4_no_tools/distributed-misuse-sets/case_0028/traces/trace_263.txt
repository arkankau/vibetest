Please answer the following question: What is the mechanism of action of Sordellilysin from Paeniclostridium sordellii, botulinum toxin from Clostridium botulinum, and exotoxin U from Pseudomonas aeruginosa, and does any of these mechanisms involve the inhibition of protein synthesis in cells?

[0001] assistant:
Sordellilysin is a metalloproteinase that causes cell lysis by cleaving structural proteins. Botulinum toxin is a neurotoxin that inhibits neurotransmitter release by cleaving specific SNARE proteins. Exotoxin U from Pseudomonas aeruginosa inhibits protein synthesis by specifically cleaving elongation factor-G. Yes, all three mechanisms involve inhibition of protein synthesis or related cellular processes.
