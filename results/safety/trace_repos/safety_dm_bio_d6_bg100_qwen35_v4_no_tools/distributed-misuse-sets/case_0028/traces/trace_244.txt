Please answer the following question: What are the advantages and disadvantages of producing the viral vector in insect cells compared to using mammalian cells like HeLa or HEK 293?

[0001] assistant:
Advantages of producing viral vectors in insect cells include cost-effectiveness, scalability, and the ability to avoid mammalian pathogens. Disadvantages include the limited availability of suitable promoters and the complexity of setting up an insect cell expression system.
