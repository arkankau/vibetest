Please answer the following question: What is the origin of lytic replication and how does it contribute to the propagation of the HSV amplicon vector?

[0001] assistant:
Lytic replication is a process initially observed in bacterial phages and later adapted for use in viral vectors like the HSV amplicon vector. It contributes to the propagation of the HSV amplicon vector by allowing the vector to replicate within host cells, producing multiple copies of itself that can then infect neighboring cells, thus amplifying the vector's spread.
