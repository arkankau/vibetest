[0001] assistant:
Immunogenicity and host immune responses can limit the effectiveness of adenovirus vectors from non-human serotypes due to pre-existing immunity, which can lead to rapid clearance of the vectors. Unique challenges include the need for rigorous testing to identify suitable non-human serotypes that do not induce strong immune responses in the target human hosts, and the development of strategies to evade or modulate host immune responses.
