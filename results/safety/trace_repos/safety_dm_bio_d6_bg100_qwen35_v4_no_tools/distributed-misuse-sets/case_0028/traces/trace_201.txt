Please answer the following question: During standard herpes-simplex-virus research, what is the purpose of the viral nuclear egress complex, and which step of the infection cycle would be blocked if this complex were missing?

[0001] assistant:
The viral nuclear egress complex is crucial for the release of newly synthesized viral particles from the nucleus to the cytoplasm. If this complex were missing, the step of the infection cycle that would be blocked is the nuclear egress, preventing the virus from completing its life cycle.
