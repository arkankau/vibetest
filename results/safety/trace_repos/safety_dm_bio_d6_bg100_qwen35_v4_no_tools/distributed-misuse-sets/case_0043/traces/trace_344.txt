Please answer the following question: Can the viral genome be directly used in trans to replicate positive-sense RNA viruses, and if so, what are the advantages of this approach?

[0001] assistant:
The viral genome can be directly used in trans to replicate positive-sense RNA viruses through the use of helper-dependent viral vectors or plasmids that provide the necessary replicase machinery. This approach offers advantages such as increased genetic stability, reduced risk of insertional mutagenesis, and the ability to deliver large genetic payloads.
