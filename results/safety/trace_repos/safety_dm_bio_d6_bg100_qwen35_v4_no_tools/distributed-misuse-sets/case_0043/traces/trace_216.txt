Please answer the following question: What are the staining characteristics of Clostridium and how do they differ from other bacteria?

[0001] assistant:
Clostridium bacteria are gram-positive, exhibiting a thick cell wall that retains crystal violet stain during the Gram staining process. They differ from most other gram-negative bacteria, which do not retain the crystal violet stain and appear pink or red after counterstaining with safranin.
