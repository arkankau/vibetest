Please answer the following question: Is NPC1 an intracellular or extracellular receptor, and how does this affect its role in Ebola virus entry?

[0001] assistant:
NPC1 is an intracellular receptor. Its intracellular location affects its role in Ebola virus entry by facilitating the virus's interaction with cellular membranes, as it allows the virus to access the endosomal environment where it can fuse with the membrane and release its genetic material into the cell.
