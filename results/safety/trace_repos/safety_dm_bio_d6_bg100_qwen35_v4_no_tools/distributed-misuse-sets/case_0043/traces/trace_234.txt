Please answer the following question: Can guinea pigs be used to test for susceptibility to SARS-CoV-2, influenza A, and Clostridium difficile?

[0001] assistant:
Guinea pigs can be used to test for susceptibility to SARS-CoV-2 and influenza A, but they are not typically used for Clostridium difficile as this bacterium primarily affects mammals like humans and other primates.
