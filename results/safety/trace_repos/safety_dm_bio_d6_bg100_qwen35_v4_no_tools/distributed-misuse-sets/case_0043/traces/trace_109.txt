Please answer the following question: How does the in vivo recombination method with host RNA polymerase II work, and what are its advantages and limitations for virus research?

[0001] assistant:
In vivo recombination using host RNA polymerase II involves inserting DNA sequences into the genome of a host cell, which are then transcribed and recombined to generate viral particles. Advantages include high efficiency and minimal interference with host gene expression. Limitations include potential off-target effects and difficulties in controlling the recombination process.
