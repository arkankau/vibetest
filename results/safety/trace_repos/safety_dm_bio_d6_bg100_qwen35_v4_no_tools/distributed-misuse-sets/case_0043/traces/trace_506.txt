Please answer the following question: What is the role of neuraminidase (NA) in the influenza virus, and is it related to resistance to adamantane-class antivirals?

[0001] assistant:
Neuraminidase (NA) helps the influenza virus escape from infected cells by cleaving sialic acid receptors. It is not directly related to resistance to adamantane-class antivirals, which primarily target the M2 protein.
