Please answer the following question: What is the mechanism by which vIL-10 modulates the immune response to evade detection and clearance by the host?

[0001] assistant:
vIL-10 (vesicular IL-10) modulates the immune response by suppressing the activation and function of antiviral immune cells, such as NK cells and dendritic cells, thereby helping viral pathogens evade detection and clearance.
