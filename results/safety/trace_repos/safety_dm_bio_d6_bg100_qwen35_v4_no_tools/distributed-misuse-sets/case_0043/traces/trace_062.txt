Please answer the following question: What is the process of selecting for bacterial colonies that have successfully taken up the plasmid during cloning, and how does it involve the selection marker?

[0001] assistant:
The process involves using a selection marker on the plasmid, which is a gene that confers resistance to antibiotics. Colonies that have taken up the plasmid survive and grow on agar plates containing the corresponding antibiotic, while those without the plasmid die.
