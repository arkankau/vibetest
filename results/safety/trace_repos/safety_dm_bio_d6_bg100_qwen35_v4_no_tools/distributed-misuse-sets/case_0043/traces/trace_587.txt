Please answer the following question: How might encoding its own ribonucleotide reductase help the herpes simplex virus evade the host immune response?

[0001] assistant:
Encoding its own ribonucleotide reductase allows the herpes simplex virus to synthesize deoxyribonucleotides independently, avoiding the limitation of relying on the host's reduced dNTP pools. This independence can help the virus maintain high replication rates and persist without being completely eliminated by antiviral host responses.
