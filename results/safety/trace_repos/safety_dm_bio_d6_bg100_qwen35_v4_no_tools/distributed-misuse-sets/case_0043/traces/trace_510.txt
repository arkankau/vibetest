Please answer the following question: What is the significance of the H5N1ΔMBCS virus's requirement for trypsin in the context of viral entry mechanisms?

[0001] assistant:
The H5N1ΔMBCS virus requires trypsin for viral entry because trypsin cleaves a specific site on the viral hemagglutinin protein, facilitating the fusion of the viral envelope with the host cell membrane. This requirement helps the virus infect cells in the lower respiratory tract, where trypsin is abundant, making it more pathogenic in avian species.
