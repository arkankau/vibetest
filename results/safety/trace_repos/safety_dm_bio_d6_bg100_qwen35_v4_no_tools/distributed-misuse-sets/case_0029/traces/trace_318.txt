Please answer the following question: Does the agr locus suppress the expression of virulence factors to promote bacterial persistence?

[0001] assistant:
The agr locus in bacteria, such as Staphylococcus aureus, regulates the expression of virulence factors. While it can influence bacterial behavior, it primarily promotes quorum sensing and bacterial persistence through coordination of gene expression rather than directly suppressing virulence factor expression.
