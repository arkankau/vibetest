[0001] assistant:
VP4 is a viral protein found in enteroviruses, particularly in rotaviruses. It plays a crucial role in the assembly of viral particles and is not directly involved in the host immune response, but its presence can trigger immune recognition and potentially affect the host's immune response through antibody interactions.
