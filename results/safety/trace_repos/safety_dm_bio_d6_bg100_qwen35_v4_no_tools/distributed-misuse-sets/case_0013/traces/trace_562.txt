Please answer the following question: What is Gibson cloning and how does it enable the assembly of DNA fragments?

[0001] assistant:
Gibson cloning is a PCR-based assembly technique that enables the seamless ligation of multiple DNA fragments into a plasmid without the need for restriction enzymes. It achieves this by using PCR primers with overlapping sequences that anneal to adjacent fragments, and a DNA polymerase that can add or remove nucleotides, facilitating the assembly of these fragments into a cohesive sequence.
