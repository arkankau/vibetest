Please answer the following question: What is the role of cysteine (Cys) in a cysteine protease active site and how might a mutation to alanine (Ala) affect the enzyme's activity?

[0001] assistant:
Cysteine (Cys) in a cysteine protease active site typically acts as a nucleophile, participating in the catalytic mechanism by forming a thioester bond with the substrate. A mutation to alanine (Ala) could disrupt this catalytic mechanism, as alanine lacks the reactive thiol group of cysteine, likely reducing or abolishing the enzyme's activity.
