[0001] assistant:
Latency-associated transcripts (LATs) are non-coding RNA molecules that are expressed during the latent phase of HSV infection. They differ from other gene types in that their transcription is constitutive and not regulated by immediate-early (IE) viral proteins, allowing them to be expressed even when the virus is in a latent state.
