Please answer the following question: What is the significance of an in vitro transcription system in producing viral proteins for vaccine development in the context of reverse genetics for RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
An in vitro transcription system is crucial in reverse genetics for RNA viruses as it allows for the production of specific viral mRNA molecules, which can be used to generate viral proteins necessary for vaccine development. This system enables scientists to manipulate viral genomes and produce infectious clones for studying virus-host interactions and developing vaccines.
