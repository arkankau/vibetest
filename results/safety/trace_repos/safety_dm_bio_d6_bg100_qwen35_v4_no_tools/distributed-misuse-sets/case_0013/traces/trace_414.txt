Please answer the following question: How do HDACi influence the cellular response to viral infection in EBV-associated malignancies?

[0001] assistant:
HDACi (histone deacetylase inhibitors) can enhance the cellular response to viral infections like EBV by increasing the expression of interferons and other antiviral genes, thereby boosting the immune response and reducing viral replication and latency in EBV-associated malignancies.
