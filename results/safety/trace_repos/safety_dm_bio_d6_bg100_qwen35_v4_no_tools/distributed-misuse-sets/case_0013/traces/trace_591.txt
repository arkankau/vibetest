Using a mouse model to quantify the pathogenicity of influenza strains allows researchers to assess virulence, including measures like lung virus burden, clinical signs of illness, and mortality. To compare a new strain with its nearest relative, specific measurements such as viral load in lung tissues, body weight changes, and lung macroscopic scores should be taken and analyzed.
