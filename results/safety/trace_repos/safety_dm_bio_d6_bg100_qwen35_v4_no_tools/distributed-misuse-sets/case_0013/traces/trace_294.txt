Please answer the following question: How does the overproduction of angiotensinogen lead to hypertension and heart hypertrophy in individuals with preexisting cardiovascular conditions?

[0001] assistant:
Overproduction of angiotensinogen leads to increased angiotensin II production, which causes vasoconstriction and elevated blood pressure, contributing to hypertension. Angiotensin II also stimulates cardiac myocyte growth and collagen deposition, leading to heart hypertrophy.
