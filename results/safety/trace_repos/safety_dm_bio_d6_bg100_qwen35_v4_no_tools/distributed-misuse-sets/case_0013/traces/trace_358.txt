Please answer the following question: What is the purpose of nested PCR in the context of detecting KSHV DNA in clinical samples?

[0001] assistant:
Nested PCR is used to increase the sensitivity and specificity of detecting KSHV DNA in clinical samples by using two sets of primers that target different regions of the viral DNA, thereby reducing nonspecific amplification and improving the detection of low-copy number targets.
