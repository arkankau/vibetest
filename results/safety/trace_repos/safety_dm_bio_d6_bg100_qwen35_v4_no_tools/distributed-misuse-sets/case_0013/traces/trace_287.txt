Please answer the following question: What are the toxic effects of consuming multiple nightshade berries and how do they impact human health?

[0001] assistant:
Nightshade berries, such as those from the Solanum genus, contain solanine and other glycoalkaloids which can be toxic in large quantities. Consuming multiple berries can cause gastrointestinal issues, nausea, vomiting, and in severe cases, neurological symptoms and fever.
