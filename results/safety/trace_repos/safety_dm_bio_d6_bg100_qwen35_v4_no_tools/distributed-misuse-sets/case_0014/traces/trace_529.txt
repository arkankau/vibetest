Please answer the following question: When designing a pan-orthopoxvirus PCR assay, which of the following loci—E9L (DNA polymerase), HA (hemagglutinin), ATI (A-type inclusion body), or TK (thymidine kinase)—is considered the most genetically stable across multiple species and therefore preferred as a primer target?

[0001] assistant:
The E9L (DNA polymerase) locus is considered the most genetically stable across multiple species of orthopoxviruses and is therefore preferred as a primer target for a pan-orthopoxvirus PCR assay.
