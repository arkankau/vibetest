Please answer the following question: What are the roles of CD4+ T helper cells in the immune response to HSV infection, and are they primarily involved in the early response in HSK?

[0001] assistant:
CD4+ T helper cells play a crucial role in the immune response to HSV infection by helping activate other immune cells like B cells and cytotoxic T cells. They are primarily involved in the late stages of HSV infection, including the resolution of herpes simplex keratitis (HSK), rather than the early response.
