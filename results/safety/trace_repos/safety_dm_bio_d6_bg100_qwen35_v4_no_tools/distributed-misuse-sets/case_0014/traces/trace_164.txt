Please answer the following question: How does the Tumor Necrosis Factor Receptor (TNFR) function in cellular signaling?

[0001] assistant:
The Tumor Necrosis Factor Receptor (TNFR) functions in cellular signaling by binding to its ligands, such as TNF-alpha, which then recruits adaptor proteins like TRADD and RIPK to initiate signaling cascades leading to various cellular responses including apoptosis, inflammation, and immune responses.
