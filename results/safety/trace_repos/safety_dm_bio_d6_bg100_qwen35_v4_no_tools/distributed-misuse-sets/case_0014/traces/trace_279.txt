Please answer the following question: How does the genetic diversity of avian influenza viruses in wild populations influence their potential for mammalian adaptation, and what surveillance methods can help capture this diversity?

[0001] assistant:
The genetic diversity in wild avian influenza viruses can influence their potential to adapt to mammals by facilitating the emergence of strains with the ability to infect and replicate in mammalian hosts. Surveillance methods such as regular sampling of wild bird populations, genetic sequencing of sampled viruses, and monitoring of virus spread patterns can help capture this diversity and track potential zoonotic threats.
