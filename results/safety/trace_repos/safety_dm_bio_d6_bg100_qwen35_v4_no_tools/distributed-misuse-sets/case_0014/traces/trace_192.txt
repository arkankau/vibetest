Please answer the following question: Can Galliform species act as intermediate hosts for influenza viruses, and if so, how might this influence the virus's adaptation and evolution in CAFOs?

[0001] assistant:
Galliform species, such as chickens and turkeys, can act as intermediate hosts for influenza viruses. In CAFOs (concentrated animal feeding operations), this can facilitate the virus's adaptation and evolution by providing a constant reservoir of hosts, increasing the likelihood of genetic reassortment and mutation.
