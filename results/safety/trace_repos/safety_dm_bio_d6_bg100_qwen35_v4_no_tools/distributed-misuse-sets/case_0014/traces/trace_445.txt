Please answer the following question: What is the primary role of early lytic proteins in the EBV lytic cycle and are they involved in DNA replication?

[0001] assistant:
The primary role of early lytic proteins in the EBV lytic cycle is to reprogram the infected B cell, facilitating the expression of other viral genes necessary for virus replication. Yes, they are involved in DNA replication, as they help establish the viral replication factory.
