Experimental evidence could be obtained through in vitro studies where specific point mutations are introduced into the viral capsid protein gene using site-directed mutagenesis. The mutated viruses would then be tested for their ability to form stable capsids and infect cells, with disrupted life cycles indicated by reduced viral particle production or impaired infectivity. These studies are typically conducted using plaque assays or quantitative viral titer measurements to assess the impact of the mutation.
