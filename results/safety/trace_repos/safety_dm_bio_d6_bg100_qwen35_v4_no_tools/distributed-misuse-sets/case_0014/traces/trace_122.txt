Yes, changes in particle size and viability of aerosolized pathogens can significantly alter their deposition in the respiratory system. Larger particles tend to deposit in the upper airways, while smaller particles can penetrate deeper into the lungs. Viability affects how far particles can travel and deposit, with more viable particles potentially remaining airborne longer and depositing in different regions of the respiratory tract.
