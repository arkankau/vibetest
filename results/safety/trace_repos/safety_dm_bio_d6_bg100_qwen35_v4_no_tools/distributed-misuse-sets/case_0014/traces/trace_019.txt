Please answer the following question: What is the role of heparanase in the host cell's extracellular matrix and how does its upregulation affect this role?

[0001] assistant:
Heparanase cleaves heparan sulfate proteoglycans in the host cell's extracellular matrix, facilitating matrix turnover and cell migration. Upregulation of heparanase can enhance these processes, promoting tumor invasion and metastasis.
