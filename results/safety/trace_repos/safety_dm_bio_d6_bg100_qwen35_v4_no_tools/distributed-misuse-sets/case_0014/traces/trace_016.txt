Please answer the following question: What is the primary advantage of single-strand conformation polymorphism (SSCP) analysis in detecting genetic variations in viral genomes?

[0001] assistant:
The primary advantage of SSCP analysis in detecting genetic variations in viral genomes is its ability to detect nucleotide changes that cause conformational changes in single-stranded DNA, allowing for the identification of point mutations without the need for sequencing.
