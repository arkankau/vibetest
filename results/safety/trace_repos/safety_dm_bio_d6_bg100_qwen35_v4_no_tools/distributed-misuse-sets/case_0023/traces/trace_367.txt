Please answer the following question: Is measles virus known to spread through receptor-independent mechanisms such as in-cell infection, and if so, does this contribute to systemic spread to other organs and tissues?

[0001] assistant:
Measles virus primarily spreads through receptor-dependent mechanisms. However, it can also spread through receptor-independent mechanisms, including in-cell infection, which can contribute to systemic spread to other organs and tissues.
