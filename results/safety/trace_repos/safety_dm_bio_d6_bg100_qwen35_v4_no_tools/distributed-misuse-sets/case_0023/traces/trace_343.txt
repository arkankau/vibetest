Please answer the following question: Describe the process of syncytium formation in herpesvirus infections and explain how it contributes to the spread of the virus within the host.

[0001] assistant:
In herpesvirus infections, syncytium formation occurs when infected cells fuse with each other, creating multinucleated giant cells. This process allows the virus to spread rapidly within the host by increasing the number of cells harboring the virus.
