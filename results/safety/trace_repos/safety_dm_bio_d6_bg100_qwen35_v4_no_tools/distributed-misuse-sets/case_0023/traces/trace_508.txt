[0001] assistant:
The BAC (Bacterial Artificial Chromosome) system is used in herpesvirus research to facilitate the manipulation of the herpesvirus genome for the generation of recombinant viruses. This system allows for precise genetic modifications and the propagation of the modified viral genomes.
