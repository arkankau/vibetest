Please answer the following question: In routine microbiology labs, how is each of the following agricultural byproducts typically employed: wheat bran, corn steep liquor, soy peptone, and potato dextrose agar?

[0001] assistant:
Wheat bran is used as a carbon source in certain media. Corn steep liquor provides nitrogen, vitamins, and minerals. Soy peptone serves as a protein source and nutrient for bacterial growth. Potato dextrose agar is used as a growth medium for culturing fungi and some bacteria.
