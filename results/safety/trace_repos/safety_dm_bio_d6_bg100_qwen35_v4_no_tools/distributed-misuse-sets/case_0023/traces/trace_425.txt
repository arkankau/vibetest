Please answer the following question: How does hibernation influence the immune response of little brown bats, and how might this be affected by WNS?

[0001] assistant:
Hibernation in little brown bats suppresses immune functions to conserve energy, making them more susceptible to infections. White-nose syndrome (WNS) further compromises their immune response, exacerbating the bats' vulnerability to pathogens and reducing their survival rates.
