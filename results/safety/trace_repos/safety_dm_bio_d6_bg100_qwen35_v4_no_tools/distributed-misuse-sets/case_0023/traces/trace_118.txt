The primary antigenic determinants of the E1 glycoprotein in CHIKV infection are found in its N-terminal domain, which is crucial for viral entry. These determinants significantly influence the host immune response by driving the production of neutralizing antibodies and T-cell responses, which are essential for controlling viral infection and providing immunity.
