Partial cross-protection in innate immune responses often occurs due to the recognition of similar molecular patterns between different pathogens. For instance, pattern recognition receptors (PRRs) like Toll-like receptors (TLRs) can respond to conserved microbial molecules, leading to a non-specific protective effect against unrelated pathogens. This cross-priming and cross-talk between PRR pathways provide evidence for the role of innate immune responses in partial cross-protection.
