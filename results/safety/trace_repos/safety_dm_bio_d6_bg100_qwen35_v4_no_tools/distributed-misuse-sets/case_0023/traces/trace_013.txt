Please answer the following question: What is the role of the URA3 marker in yeast and how does it contribute to the selection of yeast cells with the plasmid?

[0001] assistant:
The URA3 marker in yeast confers resistance to the drug 5-fluoroorotic acid (5-FOA). Yeast cells containing the URA3 marker on a plasmid can survive when grown on media containing 5-FOA, as they can use the URA3 gene to synthesize orotic acid from uracil, thus surviving selection.
