Please answer the following question: What is the MVA-T7 method, and what is its significance in virology research?

[0001] assistant:
The MVA-T7 method involves modifying the Modified Vaccinia Virus Ankara (MVA) vector to express and produce the T7 RNA polymerase, which is used for in vivo transcription of therapeutic genes. This method is significant in virology research for its potential in generating and delivering RNA molecules for gene therapy and vaccine development.
