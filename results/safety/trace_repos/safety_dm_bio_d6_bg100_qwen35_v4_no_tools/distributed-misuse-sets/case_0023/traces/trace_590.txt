Please answer the following question: How does SARS-CoV-2 interact with the NLRP3 inflammasome to cause persistent activation?

[0001] assistant:
SARS-CoV-2 interacts with the NLRP3 inflammasome by inducing the production of pro-inflammatory cytokines and promoting its activation through the release of potassium and hydrogen ions, leading to persistent inflammation.
