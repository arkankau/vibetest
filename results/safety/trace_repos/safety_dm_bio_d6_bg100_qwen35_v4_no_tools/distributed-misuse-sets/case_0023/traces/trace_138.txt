Please answer the following question: What is the relative importance of impaction and sedimentation for particles with an aerodynamic diameter greater than 1 to 2 μm in the upper and lower respiratory regions?

[0001] assistant:
Impaction and sedimentation are both important for particles with an aerodynamic diameter greater than 1 to 2 μm in both the upper and lower respiratory regions, but sedimentation becomes more significant in the lower respiratory regions due to the longer residence time and stronger gravitational effects.
