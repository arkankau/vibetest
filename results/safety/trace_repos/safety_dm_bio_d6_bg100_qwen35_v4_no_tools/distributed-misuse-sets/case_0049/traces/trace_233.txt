Please answer the following question: How do immediate-early (IE) genes contribute to the establishment of a chronic infection and the maintenance of herpesvirus latency in host cells?

[0001] assistant:
Immediate-early (IE) genes of herpesviruses are among the first to be expressed upon viral reactivation, contributing to the establishment of chronic infections and maintenance of latency by regulating the host cell environment, enhancing viral gene expression, and modulating the immune response.
