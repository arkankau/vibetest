Please answer the following question: How does vFLIP potentially contribute to immune evasion by HHV-8, and what is its impact on the development of Kaposi's sarcoma?

[0001] assistant:
vFLIP (viral FLICE-inhibitory protein) in HHV-8 potentially contributes to immune evasion by inhibiting apoptosis and modulating immune responses. This protein plays a role in the development of Kaposi's sarcoma by promoting cell survival and tumor progression.
