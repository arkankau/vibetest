Please answer the following question: How can gain-of-function mutations in recombinant viruses potentially lead to the rapid evolution of resistance to antiviral drugs?

[0001] assistant:
Gain-of-function mutations in recombinant viruses can introduce new traits or enhance existing ones, allowing the virus to more effectively evade or resist antiviral drugs. This can lead to rapid evolution by increasing the virus's ability to survive and replicate in the presence of these drugs.
