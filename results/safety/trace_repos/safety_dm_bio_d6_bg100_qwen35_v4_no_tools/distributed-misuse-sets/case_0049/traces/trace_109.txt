Please answer the following question: Do Botulism and Nerve Gases have a similar duration of effect on the body?

[0001] assistant:
Botulism and nerve gases can both cause similar symptoms by interfering with acetylcholine, but their durations of effect are different. Botulism toxin produces symptoms that can last for weeks to months, while nerve gases can have immediate and rapid onset with effects that may last for hours to days, depending on the type and dose.
