Please answer the following question: How does vIL-10 affect the expression of MHC-I, ICAM-1, and B7 on monocytes, and what is the impact on immune recognition?

[0001] assistant:
vIL-10 increases the expression of MHC-I and ICAM-1 on monocytes but downregulates B7. This results in enhanced antigen presentation and improved recognition by T cells, while reducing co-stimulation necessary for full T cell activation.
