Please answer the following question: How might adding .1% EDTA to the Lipofectamine 3000/DNA mixture affect cell adherence to the tissue culture plate during transfection?

[0001] assistant:
Adding 0.1% EDTA to the Lipofectamine 3000/DNA mixture may reduce cell adherence to the tissue culture plate, as EDTA is a chelating agent that can interfere with calcium and magnesium ions necessary for cell adhesion and spreading.
