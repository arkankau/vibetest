Please answer the following question: In what scenarios are TALENs more suitable compared to other gene insertion methods like AAV vectors or RNAi technology?

[0001] assistant:
TALENs are more suitable for creating large deletions, insertions, or substitutions in the genome, making them ideal for knocking out or modifying specific genes with high precision. They are particularly advantageous when precise genome editing is required in a variety of cell types and organisms, including those not easily transduced by AAV vectors, and when the outcomes need to be stable and heritable, unlike transient RNAi effects.
