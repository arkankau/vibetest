Reassortment of viral genetic material can significantly increase a virus's pandemic potential by allowing the exchange of genetic segments between different viral strains, potentially creating new strains with enhanced transmissibility or virulence. This process can lead to sudden changes in the virus that humans have not previously encountered, making the population more susceptible and enabling rapid spread across the globe.
