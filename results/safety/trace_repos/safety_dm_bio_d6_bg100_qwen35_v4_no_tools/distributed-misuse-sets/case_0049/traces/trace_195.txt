Please answer the following question: How important is the integration of the viral vector into the host DNA for the success of gene therapy treatments using adenoviral vectors?

[0001] assistant:
The integration of the viral vector into the host DNA is not typically required for adenoviral vector-based gene therapy treatments, as these vectors do not integrate into the host genome. Instead, they use episomal DNA, which can lead to transient expression of the therapeutic gene.
