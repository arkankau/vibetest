Please answer the following question: What RNA secondary-structure or codon-usage features can cause a viral template to be consistently under-amplified in real-time RT-PCR even when the primers appear to match the sequence?

[0001] assistant:
RNA secondary-structure features, such as hairpins or G-quadruplexes, can hinder primer binding and thus cause under-amplification. Additionally, poor codon usage or bias in the viral RNA can affect translation efficiency, indirectly impacting amplification.
