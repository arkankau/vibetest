Please answer the following question: How does the pagA gene contribute to the virulence of Bacillus anthracis and what specific functions does it perform?

[0001] assistant:
The pagA gene in Bacillus anthracis contributes to virulence by facilitating the production of polyglutamic acid, which is essential for spore formation and survival in the environment. This gene also plays a role in biofilm formation and cell aggregation, further enhancing bacterial survival and infection.
