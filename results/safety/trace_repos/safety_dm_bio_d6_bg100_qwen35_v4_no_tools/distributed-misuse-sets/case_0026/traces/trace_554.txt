The advantages of using an endpoint dilution assay in reverse genetics studies involving viral RNA transfection include its ability to quantitatively determine viral titers with high precision, allowing for the precise measurement of viral replication and the effect of genetic modifications. This method also enables the identification of viral mutants with specific phenotypes by diluting viral stocks and assessing the resulting infection levels.
