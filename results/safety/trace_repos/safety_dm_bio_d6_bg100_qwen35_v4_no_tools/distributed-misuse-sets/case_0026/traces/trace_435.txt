Please answer the following question: How does the HSV protein ICP4 contribute to the overall life cycle of the herpes simplex virus, and which specific stages of the virus does it affect?

[0001] assistant:
HSV protein ICP4 is a transcriptional activator that plays a crucial role in the viral life cycle by enhancing the transcription of both early and late viral genes. It affects the early and late stages of the virus, promoting the production of viral proteins and the assembly of new virus particles.
