Please answer the following question: What is the role of EDTA in inhibiting the growth of certain microorganisms and how might it impact the isolation of B. anthracis?

[0001] assistant:
EDTA (ethylene-diamine tetraacetic acid) chelates metal ions necessary for the growth of certain microorganisms, thus inhibiting their growth. In the context of isolating B. anthracis, EDTA might interfere with the isolation process by also chelating essential metal ions required for the growth of this bacterium, potentially making its isolation more challenging.
