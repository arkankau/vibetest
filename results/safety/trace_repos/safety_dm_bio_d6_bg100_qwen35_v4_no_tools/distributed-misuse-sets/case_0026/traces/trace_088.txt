Please answer the following question: What are the key components typically found in a lentivirus plasmid system that are necessary for replication competency?

[0001] assistant:
The key components typically found in a lentivirus plasmid system for replication competency include the viral genes gag, pol, and env, as well as accessory genes like tat and rev. These components are necessary for the production and replication of lentiviral particles.
