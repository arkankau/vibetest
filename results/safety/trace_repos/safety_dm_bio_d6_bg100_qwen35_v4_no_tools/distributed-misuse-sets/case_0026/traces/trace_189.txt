Please answer the following question: How does the expression of T7 RNA polymerase facilitate the rescue of RNA viruses from cDNA templates in a reverse genetics system?

[0001] assistant:
T7 RNA polymerase is expressed in cells to synthesize viral RNA from cDNA templates using a T7 promoter sequence in the plasmid. This allows for the production of infectious viral particles from cloned viral genomes.
