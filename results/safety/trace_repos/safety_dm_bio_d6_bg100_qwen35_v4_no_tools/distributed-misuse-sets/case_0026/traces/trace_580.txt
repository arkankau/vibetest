Please answer the following question: What does it mean for a reporter gene to have a strong viral promoter, and how does this help in preventing the reporter gene from being silenced during viral replication?

[0001] assistant:
A strong viral promoter in a reporter gene ensures high and consistent expression of the reporter gene. This helps prevent silencing during viral replication by maintaining the reporter gene's activity, even as the virus tries to shut down host cell processes.
