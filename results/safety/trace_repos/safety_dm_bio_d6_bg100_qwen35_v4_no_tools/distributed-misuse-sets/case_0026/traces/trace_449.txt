Please answer the following question: How does the combination of a long lifespan with prolonged viral evolution contribute to the pig's role as a 'mixing vessel' for IAVs?

[0001] assistant:
Pigs have a long lifespan and can be infected with multiple strains of influenza A viruses (IAVs) from different species, which allows for prolonged viral evolution and recombination within the pig, making them a 'mixing vessel' for IAVs.
