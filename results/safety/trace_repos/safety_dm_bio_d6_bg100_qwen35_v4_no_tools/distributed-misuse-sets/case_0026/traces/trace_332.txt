Please answer the following question: In what ways can the use of transfection with viral RNA help in the study of viral gene functions and interactions?

[0001] assistant:
Transfection with viral RNA can help in studying viral gene functions and interactions by allowing researchers to introduce specific viral genes into host cells, enabling the analysis of gene products and their effects. This method facilitates the examination of gene function, host-virus interactions, and the role of viral proteins in viral replication and pathogenesis.
