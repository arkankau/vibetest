[0001] assistant:
T7 RNA polymerase can transcribe RNA from a DNA template under the control of a T7 promoter, which is often used in the production of recombinant viruses. However, the term "antigenomic RNA" typically refers to RNA complementary to the viral genome, and its production would depend on the specific DNA template and promoter sequence used.
