Please answer the following question: What is the role of BAC in maintaining the integrity of the viral genome during reverse genetics experiments?

[0001] assistant:
BAC (Bacterial Artificial Chromosome) is used to maintain the integrity of the viral genome by cloning it into bacteria, where it can be replicated and preserved without the risk of recombination or degradation that might occur during propagation in eukaryotic cells.
