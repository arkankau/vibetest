Please answer the following question: How does a two-part AAV delivery system work, and what are its advantages in delivering CRISPR constructs, particularly in the context of genome disruption?

[0001] assistant:
A two-part AAV delivery system involves splitting the CRISPR construct between two AAV vectors, which allows for higher transgene titers and reduced immune response. This method enhances genome disruption efficiency by avoiding toxic levels of Cas9 expression and improving delivery to specific cell types.
