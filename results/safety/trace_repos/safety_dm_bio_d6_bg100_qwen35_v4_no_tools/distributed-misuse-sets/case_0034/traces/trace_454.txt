Please answer the following question: What is the role of a rescue system in facilitating the recovery of infectious RNA viruses from cloned cDNA in reverse genetics studies?

[0001] assistant:
The rescue system is crucial in reverse genetics studies as it allows the recovery of infectious RNA viruses from cloned cDNA by providing necessary elements such as promoters and replication machinery that are not present in the cDNA but are required for virus replication.
