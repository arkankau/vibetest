Please answer the following question: What is the purpose of saturation mutagenesis in the context of directed evolution of viral proteins, and how does it differ from other mutagenesis techniques?

[0001] assistant:
Saturation mutagenesis in directed evolution of viral proteins aims to generate a library with all possible mutations at a specific site to identify the most beneficial ones for the desired trait. It differs from other mutagenesis techniques like error-prone PCR or transposon mutagenesis by systematically targeting a single residue, potentially providing more controlled and comprehensive information about the protein's functional landscape.
