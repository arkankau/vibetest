Please answer the following question: Compare and contrast the characteristics of latency and active replication in the context of herpesvirus research.

[0001] assistant:
Latency in herpesviruses involves the virus remaining dormant in the host without causing immediate disease, whereas active replication leads to the production of viral particles and the onset of disease symptoms. Active replication is associated with acute infections and virus spread, while latency allows for long-term persistence and potential reactivation.
