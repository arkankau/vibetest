[0001] assistant:
Replacing the E1 and E2 genes with those from other Alphaviruses could help elucidate how these proteins contribute to cross-species transmission, viral infectivity, and immune evasion in Chikungunya virus (CHIKV). This genetic manipulation allows researchers to study specific functional differences and similarities in these proteins across different Alphaviruses, providing insights into the molecular mechanisms underlying CHIKV pathogenesis.
