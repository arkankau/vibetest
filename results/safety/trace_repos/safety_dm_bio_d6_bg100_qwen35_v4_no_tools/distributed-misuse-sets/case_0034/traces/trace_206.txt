Please answer the following question: Do UL30 and UL42 proteins play a role in the viral DNA polymerase complex during the replication of herpes simplex virus?

[0001] assistant:
UL30 and UL42 proteins are indeed involved in the viral DNA polymerase complex during herpes simplex virus replication. UL30 is a DNA polymerase processivity factor, and UL42 is a single-strand binding protein that helps maintain the stability of the viral DNA template.
