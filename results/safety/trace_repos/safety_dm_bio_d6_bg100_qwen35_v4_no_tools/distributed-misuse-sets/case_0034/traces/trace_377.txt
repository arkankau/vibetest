Please answer the following question: Is either incubation at 90 degrees Celsius for 7 minutes or incubation with 5 mM EDTA sufficient to inactivate Botox?

[0001] assistant:
Neither incubation at 90 degrees Celsius for 7 minutes nor incubation with 5 mM EDTA is sufficient to inactivate Botox. Botox (botulinum toxin) requires higher temperatures, typically above 100 degrees Celsius, for a sufficient period to be inactivated.
