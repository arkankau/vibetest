Please answer the following question: Does the viral nucleoprotein (NP) play a role in the cleavage and use of host cell mRNA to initiate viral mRNA synthesis during influenza A virus replication?

[0001] assistant:
The viral nucleoprotein (NP) does not directly cleave and use host cell mRNA; however, it is part of the viral ribonucleoprotein complex that is essential for the transcription and replication of viral mRNA.
