Please answer the following question: How does the 'CD150-blind' recombinant measles virus (rMV) differ from the wild-type measles virus in terms of its ability to infect respiratory epithelial cells?

[0001] assistant:
The CD150-blind recombinant measles virus (rMV) lacks the ability to bind to CD150 (also known asplexin A1), which is a critical receptor for viral entry into respiratory epithelial cells. In contrast, the wild-type measles virus uses CD150 for initial attachment and entry into these cells.
