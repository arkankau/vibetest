Please answer the following question: How does the herpes simplex virus (HSV) protein ICP47 interact with the transporter associated with antigen processing (TAP)?

[0001] assistant:
ICP47, a viral protein from HSV, interacts with the transporter associated with antigen processing (TAP) to reduce the presentation of viral peptides on MHC class I molecules, thereby inhibiting the host immune response.
