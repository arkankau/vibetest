Please answer the following question: What are miRNA targeting sequences and how do they function in the context of viral vectors?

[0001] assistant:
miRNA targeting sequences are specific RNA sequences designed to bind to miRNAs, which can then regulate gene expression. In the context of viral vectors, these sequences can be used to target and modulate the expression of specific genes, potentially enhancing vector stability, reducing immunogenicity, or controlling viral gene expression levels.
