Please answer the following question: What are the known effects of heparanase activity on the remodeling of the extracellular matrix and how can this remodeling impact the viral infection process?

[0001] assistant:
Heparanase activity leads to the degradation of heparan sulfate proteoglycans in the extracellular matrix, facilitating cell migration and metastasis. This remodeling can enhance viral infection by providing a more accessible and permissive environment for viruses to attach and enter host cells.
