Answer: A reverse genetics system allows for the introduction of specific mutations into viral genomes, enabling the generation of viruses with desired genetic alterations in the protein of interest. This capability is significant because it allows for precise manipulation and study of viral proteins, facilitating a deeper understanding of viral biology and potentially leading to the development of new antiviral strategies.
