Please answer the following question: Is it necessary to include a reverse transcriptase gene when creating an AAV vector, and why or why not?

[0001] assistant:
No, it is not necessary to include a reverse transcriptase gene when creating an AAV vector because AAV (adeno-associated virus) does not require reverse transcription to integrate into the host genome. AAV vectors are based on AAV DNA, which is already a double-stranded DNA and does not undergo reverse transcription.
