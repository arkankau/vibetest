Please answer the following question: What is the guanylyltransferase assay and how does it relate to the study of viral RNA capping enzymes?

[0001] assistant:
The guanylyltransferase assay is a biochemical test used to measure the activity of enzymes that add a guanine nucleotide to the 5' end of an RNA molecule, a process crucial for the formation of the 5' cap in eukaryotic mRNA. This assay is essential in studying viral RNA capping enzymes as many viruses possess their own capping enzymes that help them hijack the host's cellular machinery, and the guanylyltransferase assay can help characterize these enzymes and their mechanisms.
