Please answer the following question: What is the role of galectins in the inflammatory response and can they contribute to lethal inflammatory responses?

[0001] assistant:
Galectins are lectins that bind to beta-galactosyl residues on glycoproteins and glycolipids, playing a role in the inflammatory response by modulating cell adhesion, proliferation, and cytokine production. Yes, galectins can contribute to lethal inflammatory responses by amplifying inflammatory signals and promoting tissue damage.
