Please answer the following question: What are the primary mechanisms by which TALENs can achieve targeted gene insertion into a viral genome?

[0001] assistant:
TALENs achieve targeted gene insertion into a viral genome by recognizing specific DNA sequences through their TALE (Transcription Activator-Like Effector) domains and creating a double-strand break at the desired location. This break is then repaired using homologous recombination, introducing the desired gene into the viral genome.
