Please answer the following question: How does the sgRNA interact with Cas9 to achieve targeted genome editing in RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
sgRNA (single-guide RNA) interacts with Cas9 to achieve targeted genome editing in RNA viruses by guiding Cas9 to the specific viral RNA sequence through base pairing. Once bound, Cas9 makes a double-strand break, which can lead to insertion, deletion, or other modifications in the viral genome.
