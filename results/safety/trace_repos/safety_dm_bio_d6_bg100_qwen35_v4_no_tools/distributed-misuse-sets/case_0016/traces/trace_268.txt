Please answer the following question: How does relative humidity influence the transmission of airborne pathogens through air currents and ventilation systems?

[0001] assistant:
Relative humidity affects the transmission of airborne pathogens by influencing the moisture content of the air, which can alter the stability and viability of droplets containing pathogens. Higher humidity can lead to larger, heavier droplets that fall more quickly to surfaces, reducing airborne transmission, while lower humidity can keep droplets suspended for longer periods, potentially increasing transmission.
