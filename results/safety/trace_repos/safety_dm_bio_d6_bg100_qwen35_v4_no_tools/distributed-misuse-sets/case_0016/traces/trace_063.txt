Please answer the following question: What is the general nature of Eukaryota and how does it relate to the likelihood of containing an infectious agent for humans?

[0001] assistant:
Eukaryota are organisms with complex cells containing a nucleus. While many eukaryotic organisms can contain or be infectious agents for humans (such as certain fungi, protozoa, and nematodes), not all do, as many are harmless or even beneficial.
