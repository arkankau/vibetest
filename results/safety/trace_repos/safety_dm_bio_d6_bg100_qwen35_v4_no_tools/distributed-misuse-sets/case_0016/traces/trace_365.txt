Please answer the following question: How does the EBV latent membrane protein 1 (LMP1) interact with the nuclear factor-κB (NF-κB) pathway to support cell survival and proliferation in the context of oncogenesis?

[0001] assistant:
EBV LMP1 interacts with the NF-κB pathway by constitutively activating it, leading to the continuous production of pro-survival and pro-proliferative cytokines. This activation supports cell survival and proliferation by mimicking the effects of bacterial and viral pathogens.
