Please answer the following question: How does the use of a cDNA clone from a virulent strain facilitate the examination of the virus's ability to evade the host's immune system?

[0001] assistant:
A cDNA clone from a virulent strain allows researchers to produce large quantities of the viral genetic material, enabling detailed analysis of how the virus interacts with and evades the host's immune system. This facilitates the study of specific viral genes and their effects on immune evasion.
