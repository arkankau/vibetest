Please answer the following question: How does the presence or absence of glucose affect the growth of C. botulinum in the media?

[0001] assistant:
The presence of glucose inhibits the growth of C. botulinum because it competes with the bacterium for limited carbon sources, favoring other bacteria that can use glucose. In the absence of glucose, C. botulinum can grow and potentially produce botulinum toxin.
