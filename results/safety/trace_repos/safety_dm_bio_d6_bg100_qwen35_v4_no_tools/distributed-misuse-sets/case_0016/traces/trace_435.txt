Please answer the following question: What is the role of Interleukin-6 (IL-6) in immune responses to viral infections, including KSHV?

[0001] assistant:
Interleukin-6 (IL-6) plays a role in enhancing the immune response to viral infections by promoting inflammation and stimulating the production of cytokines. In the context of KSHV (Kaposi's sarcoma-associated herpesvirus), IL-6 contributes to immune modulation and chronic inflammation.
