Please answer the following question: What is the function of natural killer (NK) cells in the immune response to HSV, and are they the primary cells involved in the early response in HSK?

[0001] assistant:
Natural killer (NK) cells play a role in the immune response to HSV by secreting cytokines and directly killing infected cells. However, they are not the primary cells involved in the early response in herpetic stromal keratitis (HSK); that role is typically filled by cytotoxic T lymphocytes.
