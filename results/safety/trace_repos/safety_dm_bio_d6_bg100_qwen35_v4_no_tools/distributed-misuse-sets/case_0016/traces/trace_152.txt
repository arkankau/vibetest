Please answer the following question: Can you provide an example of how the SIR model is applied to simulate inter-host propagation in an epidemic scenario?

[0001] assistant:
The SIR model divides the population into three compartments: Susceptible (S), Infected (I), and Recovered (R). It simulates inter-host transmission by tracking the rates at which individuals move between these states, using differential equations to model the spread of infection within the population.
