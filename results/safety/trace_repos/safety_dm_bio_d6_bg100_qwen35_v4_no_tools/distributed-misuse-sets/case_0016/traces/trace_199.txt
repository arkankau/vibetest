Please answer the following question: What are the primary advantages of reverse genetics in the context of viral research and vaccine development?

[0001] assistant:
The primary advantages of reverse genetics in viral research and vaccine development include the ability to generate viruses with defined mutations, allowing for the study of gene function and the identification of essential genes for virus replication. This technology also facilitates the rapid production of live-attenuated vaccines and can help in understanding viral pathogenesis.
