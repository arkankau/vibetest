Yes, the EBV BamHI C promoter (CP) is known for its ability to drive gene expression specifically in EBV-infected cells. It achieves this specificity through the binding of the Epstein-Barr nuclear antigen 1 (EBNA1), which is exclusively expressed in EBV-infected cells and enhances the activity of the CP.
