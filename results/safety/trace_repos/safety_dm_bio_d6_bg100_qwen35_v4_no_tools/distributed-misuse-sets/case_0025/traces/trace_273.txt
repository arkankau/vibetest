Please answer the following question: How do loss-of-function mutations in the transmembrane domain of HIV-1 Vpu proteins influence the sensitivity of primary human CD4+ T cells to interferons (IFN)?

[0001] assistant:
Loss-of-function mutations in the transmembrane domain of HIV-1 Vpu proteins can decrease the ability of Vpu to downregulate CD4 and other coreceptors on the surface of infected cells. This results in enhanced sensitivity of primary human CD4+ T cells to interferons, as the cells are better able to respond to IFN signals.
