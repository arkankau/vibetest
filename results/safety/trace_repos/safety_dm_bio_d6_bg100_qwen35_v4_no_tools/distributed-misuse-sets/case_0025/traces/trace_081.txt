Please answer the following question: What is electroporation and how does it work in the context of introducing cDNA into host cells?

[0001] assistant:
Electroporation is a method used to introduce DNA into cells by briefly exposing them to an electric field, which creates temporary pores in the cell membrane, allowing DNA to enter the cell. In the context of introducing cDNA (complementary DNA) into host cells, electroporation enhances the uptake of the DNA, facilitating genetic transformation and expression of the introduced cDNA.
