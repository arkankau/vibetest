Please answer the following question: What is the known function of YopJ in Y. pestis, and how does it modify residues?

[0001] assistant:
YopJ is a protease in Yersinia pestis that modifies proteins by phosphorylating certain residues, specifically by removing a phosphate group from threonine residues, which can alter the function of these proteins and help the bacterium evade the host immune response.
