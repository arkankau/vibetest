Please answer the following question: How does cold temperature (4C) affect nuclease activity, and what is the significance of this for poliovirus infection?

[0001] assistant:
Cold temperature (4C) generally decreases nuclease activity, which can help stabilize viral RNA and reduce degradation. This stabilization is significant for poliovirus infection as it may enhance viral survival and replication under cold conditions, potentially contributing to more efficient viral spread.
