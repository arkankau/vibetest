Please answer the following question: How does the viral nucleoprotein (N) act as an RNA chaperone to prevent premature RNA folding during the recovery of infectious virus from cDNA in segmented negative-strand RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
The viral nucleoprotein (N) acts as an RNA chaperone by binding to the viral RNA in a way that prevents premature folding. This interaction helps maintain the RNA in a single-stranded conformation, which is necessary for the recovery of infectious virus from cDNA during the replication cycle of segmented negative-strand RNA viruses.
