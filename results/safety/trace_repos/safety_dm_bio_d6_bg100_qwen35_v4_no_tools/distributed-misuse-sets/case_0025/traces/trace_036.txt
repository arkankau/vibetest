BAC (Bacterial Artificial Chromosome) contributes to the accuracy and reliability of reverse genetics experiments by providing a large cloning capacity, allowing for the precise manipulation and insertion of genetic sequences. This ensures that the generated recombinant viruses closely match the original viral genomes, enhancing the accuracy and reliability of the experiments.
