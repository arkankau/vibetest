Please answer the following question: How does a copy-control inducer affect the replication rate of plasmids in bacterial cells?

[0001] assistant:
A copy-control inducer affects the replication rate of plasmids in bacterial cells by regulating the number of plasmid copies per cell. When present, it typically suppresses plasmid replication to maintain a stable, low copy number, preventing over-replication and ensuring balanced bacterial growth.
