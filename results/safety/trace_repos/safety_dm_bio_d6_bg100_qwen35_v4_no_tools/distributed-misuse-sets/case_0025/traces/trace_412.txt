Please answer the following question: What is the typical method of initiating the production of E1-deleted adenovirus vectors in mammalian cell culture systems?

[0001] assistant:
The typical method of initiating the production of E1-deleted adenovirus vectors involves co-transfecting E1-expressing helper plasmids with the E1-deleted adenovirus vector plasmid into mammalian cells, usually HEK293 cells, to allow for viral replication and packaging.
