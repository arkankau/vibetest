[0001] assistant:
Assessing the longevity of the antibody response and its correlation with protection against reinfection helps identify whether a vaccine elicits a robust and lasting immune response, which is crucial for long-term protection. This information is important for ruling out the risk of antibody-dependent enhancement (ADE), where antibodies non-specifically enhance viral entry and infection.
