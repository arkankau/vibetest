Please answer the following question: How does saturation mutagenesis facilitate the identification of viral receptors on host cells compared to other methods?

[0001] assistant:
Saturation mutagenesis allows for the systematic introduction of a wide range of mutations in a protein, which can help identify regions of a viral protein that interact with host cell receptors. This method is more comprehensive than other approaches like site-directed mutagenesis, as it does not require prior knowledge of potential binding sites.
