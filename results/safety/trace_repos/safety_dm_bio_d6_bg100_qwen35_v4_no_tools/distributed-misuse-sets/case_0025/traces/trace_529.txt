Please answer the following question: Which mosquito species is not commonly associated with the transmission of dengue, yellow fever, West Nile, Zika, or chikungunya viruses?

[0001] assistant:
The Anopheles mosquito species is not commonly associated with the transmission of dengue, yellow fever, West Nile, Zika, or chikungunya viruses; these are primarily transmitted by Aedes and Culex species.
