Please answer the following question: What is diphthamide, and how does it interact with diphtheria toxin protein?

[0001] assistant:
Diphthamide is a modified amino acid found in histidine residues of proteins, including diphtheria toxin. It plays a crucial role in enhancing the toxicity of diphtheria toxin by stabilizing the toxin's ADP-ribosyltransferase activity, which is essential for the toxin's mechanism of action.
