Please answer the following question: What is the Biological and Chemical Toxins Convention, and does it regulate dangerous pathogens in the United States?

[0001] assistant:
The Biological and Toxins Weapons Convention (BTWC) is an international arms control treaty aimed at prohibiting the development, production, stockpiling, and use of biological and toxin weapons. It does not regulate dangerous pathogens in the United States but rather serves as a framework for national measures and international cooperation to prevent biological threats.
