Please answer the following question: What are CpG motifs and how might their incorporation into a synthetic virus influence genetic surveillance systems?

[0001] assistant:
CpG motifs are sequences in DNA where a cytosine nucleotide occurs immediately preceding a guanine nucleotide. Their incorporation into a synthetic virus could potentially alter how the virus is detected by genetic surveillance systems, as these systems often rely on specific sequences to identify and track viruses.
