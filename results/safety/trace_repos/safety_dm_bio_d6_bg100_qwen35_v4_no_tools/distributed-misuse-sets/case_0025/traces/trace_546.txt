Please answer the following question: How does the H5N1 influenza A virus interact with host cells, and are there specific cellular mechanisms that lead to severe disease and high mortality?

[0001] assistant:
The H5N1 influenza A virus interacts with host cells by binding to sialic acid-containing receptors on the cell surface, primarily in the respiratory tract. This interaction triggers endocytosis, followed by uncoating and release of viral RNA into the cytoplasm. Severe disease and high mortality are associated with an overactive immune response, known as a cytokine storm, which can cause widespread inflammation and organ failure.
