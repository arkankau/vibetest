Please answer the following question: What are the advantages and limitations of using inducible promoters to control the expression of a transgene in a viral vector over time?

[0001] assistant:
Advantages include precise temporal and spatial control of gene expression, reduced side effects due to controlled expression levels, and the ability to turn gene expression on or off as needed. Limitations involve the complexity of designing and maintaining inducible systems, potential instability of the inducible promoters, and the risk of undetectable expression if the inducer is not properly administered.
