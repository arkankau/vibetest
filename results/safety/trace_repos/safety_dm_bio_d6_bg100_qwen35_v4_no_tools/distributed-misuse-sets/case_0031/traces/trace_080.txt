Please answer the following question: What are the main challenges associated with directly introducing RNA into host cells without electroporation, and how does electroporation address these challenges?

[0001] assistant:
The main challenges with directly introducing RNA into host cells without electroporation include low transfection efficiency and rapid RNA degradation. Electroporation addresses these by creating temporary pores in the cell membrane, allowing for more efficient RNA uptake and protection from degradation.
