Please answer the following question: EMS treatment was performed in rich liquid media. Could the EMS have been transferred to the plate, and if so, how could this affect colony growth on fluconazole?

[0001] assistant:
EMS (ethyl methanesulfonate) can be transferred to a plate if the treated cells are streaked or spread onto it. This would increase the likelihood of fluconazole-resistant colonies due to induced mutations.
