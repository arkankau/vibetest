Please answer the following question: What is the function of the T3SS effector protein VopQ in Vibrio parahaemolyticus and how is it related to virulence and acute gastroenteritis?

[0001] assistant:
VopQ is an effector protein of the Type III Secretion System (T3SS) in Vibrio parahaemolyticus that modulates host immune responses, contributing to the bacterium's virulence. It is involved in the pathogenesis of acute gastroenteritis by impairing host cell functions and facilitating bacterial colonization and invasion.
