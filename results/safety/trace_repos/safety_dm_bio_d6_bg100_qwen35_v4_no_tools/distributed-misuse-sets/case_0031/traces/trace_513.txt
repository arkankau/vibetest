Please answer the following question: How does saturation mutagenesis enable the generation of a comprehensive library of mutants, and what is the importance of this library in directed evolution?

[0001] assistant:
Saturation mutagenesis introduces a large number of random mutations into a gene or gene pool, creating a comprehensive library of mutants. This library is crucial in directed evolution as it allows for the selection of variants with improved or novel properties, such as increased activity or stability.
