Please answer the following question: What is the mechanism by which vFLIP blocks apoptosis in infected cells, and why is this significant in the context of KSHV-associated malignancies?

[0001] assistant:
vFLIP (viral FLIP) blocks apoptosis in infected cells by inhibiting the caspase-8 and caspase-10 activation pathways, which are crucial for death receptor-induced apoptosis. This is significant in the context of KSHV-associated malignancies because it allows infected cells to evade host immune responses and persist, contributing to the development and progression of cancers such as Kaposi's sarcoma.
