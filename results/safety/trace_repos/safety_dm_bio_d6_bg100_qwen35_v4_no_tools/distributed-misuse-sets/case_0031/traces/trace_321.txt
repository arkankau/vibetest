Please answer the following question: What is the importance of inverted terminal repeats (ITRs) in the overall function and efficiency of a reverse genetics system for a DNA virus?

[0001] assistant:
Inverted terminal repeats (ITRs) are crucial in reverse genetics systems for DNA viruses as they serve as replication origins, ensuring the correct initiation of viral DNA replication. They also play a role in maintaining viral genome stability and are essential for the in vitro packaging of viral DNA.
