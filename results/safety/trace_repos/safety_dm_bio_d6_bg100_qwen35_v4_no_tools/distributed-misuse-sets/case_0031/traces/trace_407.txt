Please answer the following question: What is the role of Vpu in suppressing the transcription factor NF-κB in HIV-1 group M, and how might this affect viral replication and spread?

[0001] assistant:
Vpu in HIV-1 group M enhances the degradation of the protein Tat, a key transcription factor for HIV-1, and it also downregulates the expression of the protein BST-2/tetherin, indirectly suppressing NF-κB signaling. This suppression likely aids in viral replication and spread by reducing cellular defenses and promoting viral release from infected cells.
