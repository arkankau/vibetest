Please answer the following question: Does TMPRSS2 play a part in the transcription of viral RNA in SARS-CoV-2 infection?

[0001] assistant:
TMPRSS2 does not directly play a part in the transcription of viral RNA in SARS-CoV-2 infection. Its role is more related to helping the virus enter host cells by cleaving the spike protein.
