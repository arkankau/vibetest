Please answer the following question: What is the typical viral replication process in the context of synthetic genomics and how does it compare to the BAC-based system?

[0001] assistant:
In synthetic genomics, viral replication often involves the use of plasmids or other vectors to express viral genes, whereas BAC (Bacterial Artificial Chromosome) systems provide a larger capacity for viral genome insertion and can offer more stable replication. BAC systems typically allow for better maintenance and manipulation of large viral genomes compared to smaller plasmids.
