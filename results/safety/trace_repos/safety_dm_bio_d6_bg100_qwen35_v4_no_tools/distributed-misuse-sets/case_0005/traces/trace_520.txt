Please answer the following question: How do miRNA targeting sequences impact the delivery and expression of therapeutic cargo in viral vectors?

[0001] assistant:
MiRNA targeting sequences can enhance the specificity and efficiency of therapeutic cargo delivery by viral vectors. By designing these sequences to match specific miRNA targets in the host cells, the vectors can be regulated at the post-transcriptional level, ensuring that the therapeutic cargo is expressed only in relevant cells.
