The polymerase fidelity of a virus affects its mutation rate by determining the accuracy with which genetic material is replicated. Higher fidelity polymerases lead to lower mutation rates, reducing adaptability, while lower fidelity polymerases increase mutation rates, enhancing adaptability through genetic diversity.
