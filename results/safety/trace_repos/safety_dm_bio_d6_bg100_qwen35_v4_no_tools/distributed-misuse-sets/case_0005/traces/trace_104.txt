Please answer the following question: Do ICP0 and ICP4 proteins form the viral capsid structure, and if not, what is their function in viral replication?

[0001] assistant:
ICP0 and ICP4 are not components of the viral capsid. Instead, they play regulatory roles in viral replication, enhancing transcription and interfering with host cell functions to facilitate viral gene expression and spread.
