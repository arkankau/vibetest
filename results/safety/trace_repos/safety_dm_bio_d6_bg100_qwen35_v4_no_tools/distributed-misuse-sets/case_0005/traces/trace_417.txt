Please answer the following question: How does the herpesvirus protein ICP0 influence the transcriptional activity of viral genes during reactivation from latency?

[0001] assistant:
ICP0 enhances the transcriptional activity of viral genes during reactivation from latency by promoting the deubiquitination and degradation of transcriptional repressors, thus facilitating the expression of viral genes necessary for viral replication.
