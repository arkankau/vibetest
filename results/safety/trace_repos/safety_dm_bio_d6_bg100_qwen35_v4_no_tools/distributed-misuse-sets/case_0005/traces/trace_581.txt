Please answer the following question: What is the primary function of the RNAi machinery in the context of viral infections, and how does it interact with viral RNA?

[0001] assistant:
The primary function of the RNAi machinery in the context of viral infections is to degrade viral RNA, thereby interfering with viral replication. This is achieved by the formation of RNA-induced silencing complexes (RISC) that target and cleave viral RNA molecules.
