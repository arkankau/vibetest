Please answer the following question: Are mouse lymphocyte DNA samples likely to contain ancient viral DNA from ancestral viruses like herpesviruses and papillomaviruses that could be used for reconstruction?

[0001] assistant:
Mouse lymphocyte DNA samples are likely to contain ancient viral DNA from ancestral viruses, including herpesviruses and papillomaviruses, which could potentially be used for reconstruction. However, the feasibility of reconstruction depends on the preservation and accessibility of this ancient viral DNA within the samples.
