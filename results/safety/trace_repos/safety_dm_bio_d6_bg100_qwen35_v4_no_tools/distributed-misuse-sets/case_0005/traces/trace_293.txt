Please answer the following question: What is the nature of the full-length spike (S) protein used in subunit vaccines against SARS-CoV and how does it contribute to the vaccine's effectiveness?

[0001] assistant:
The full-length spike (S) protein used in subunit vaccines against SARS-CoV-1 and SARS-CoV-2 mimics the natural protein that the virus uses to enter cells. This protein effectively triggers an immune response, helping the body to recognize and fight off the virus if encountered.
