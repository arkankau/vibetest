Please answer the following question: How do phage display libraries contribute to the development of vaccine platforms based on bacteriophage capsid proteins?

[0001] assistant:
Phage display libraries allow for the presentation of a vast array of peptides and proteins on the surface of bacteriophages, which can be used to identify and select peptides or proteins that elicit a strong immune response. This capability is leveraged to develop vaccine platforms based on bacteriophage capsid proteins that can serve as effective antigens, potentially leading to the generation of protective immunity against specific pathogens.
