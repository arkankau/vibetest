Please answer the following question: What is the synthetic genomics assembly method and how does it differ from the BAC-based system in terms of genome manipulation?

[0001] assistant:
Synthetic genomics assembly involves constructing genomes from chemically synthesized DNA fragments. This method differs from the BAC-based system in that it can handle larger DNA fragments directly and does not require intermediate cloning into Bacterial Artificial Chromosomes (BACs), allowing for more straightforward and efficient genome manipulation and assembly.
