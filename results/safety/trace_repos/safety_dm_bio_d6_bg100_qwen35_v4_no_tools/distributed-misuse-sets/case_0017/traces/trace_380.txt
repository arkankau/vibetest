Please answer the following question: How does Brucella superoxide dismutase (BsuSOD) differ from general SOD in terms of substrate specificity?

[0001] assistant:
BsuSOD, the superoxide dismutase from Brucella, has a broader substrate specificity than general SOD, as it can also catalyze the dismutation of peroxynitrite, which is not typically a substrate for general SOD enzymes.
