Please answer the following question: What is the mechanism by which a self-cleaving 2A peptide facilitates the expression of multiple genes in a polycistronic vector?

[0001] assistant:
A self-cleaving 2A peptide facilitates the expression of multiple genes in a polycistronic vector by allowing the ribosome to bypass the coding sequence of one gene and initiate translation of the next gene immediately downstream, effectively cleaving the translated polypeptide chain at the peptide's site.
