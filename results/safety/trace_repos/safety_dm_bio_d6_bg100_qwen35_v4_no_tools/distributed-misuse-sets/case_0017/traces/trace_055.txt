Please answer the following question: How does the absence or presence of ACE2 affect the overall susceptibility of a host to SARS-CoV-2 infection?

[0001] assistant:
The presence of ACE2 receptors increases the susceptibility of a host to SARS-CoV-2 infection, as the virus uses these receptors to enter cells. Conversely, the absence or low expression of ACE2 receptors can reduce the risk of infection.
