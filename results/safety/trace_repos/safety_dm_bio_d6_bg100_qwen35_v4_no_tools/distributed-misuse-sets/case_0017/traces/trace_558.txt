Please answer the following question: What are the advantages of using adenoviral vectors in gene therapy that make them suitable for delivering miRNAs to cells?

[0001] assistant:
Adenoviral vectors are advantageous for delivering miRNAs because they can efficiently transduce both dividing and non-dividing cells, have a high transduction efficiency, and can be produced in large quantities. However, their transient expression and immune responses are notable drawbacks.
