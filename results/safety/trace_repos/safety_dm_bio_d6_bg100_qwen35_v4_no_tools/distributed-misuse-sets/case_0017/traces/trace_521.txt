The synthesis of orthopox viruses raises significant regulatory and ethical considerations, including biosafety and biosecurity concerns, potential misuse, and the need for rigorous review by institutional biosafety committees and regulatory agencies like the NIH's Recombinant DNA Advisory Committee (RAC).
