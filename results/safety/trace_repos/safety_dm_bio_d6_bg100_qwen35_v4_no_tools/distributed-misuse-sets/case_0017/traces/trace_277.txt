Please answer the following question: What are the functions of co-stimulatory molecules in T cell activation and how can their degraders be used in the virus to reduce T cell activation?

[0001] assistant:
Co-stimulatory molecules enhance T cell activation by providing the second signal required for full T cell activation, alongside antigen recognition. Their degraders can be used in viruses to reduce T cell activation by targeting and degrading these molecules, thereby dampening the immune response.
