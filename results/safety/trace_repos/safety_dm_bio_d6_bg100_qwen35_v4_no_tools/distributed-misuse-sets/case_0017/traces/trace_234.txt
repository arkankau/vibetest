Please answer the following question: How do cytokines participate in the inflammatory response and can they be lethal?

[0001] assistant:
Cytokines play a crucial role in the inflammatory response by promoting inflammation through various mechanisms such as recruiting immune cells to the site of infection or injury, and increasing blood flow. While they are essential for fighting infections and healing, excessive or prolonged cytokine activity can lead to severe inflammation and organ damage, potentially causing lethality.
