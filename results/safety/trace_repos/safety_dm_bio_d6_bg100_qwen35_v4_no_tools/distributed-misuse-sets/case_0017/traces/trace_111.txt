Please answer the following question: What is the significance of the RNA-dependent RNA polymerase in the replication cycle of negative-strand RNA viruses, and how does it affect the rescue from cloned DNA?

[0001] assistant:
The RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (RdRp) is crucial for replicating the negative-strand RNA genomes of these viruses. During the rescue from cloned DNA, RdRp synthesizes the negative-strand RNA from the positive-strand DNA template, facilitating the production of new viral RNA genomes.
