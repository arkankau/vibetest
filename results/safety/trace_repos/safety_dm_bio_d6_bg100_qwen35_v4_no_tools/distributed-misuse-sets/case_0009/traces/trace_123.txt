Please answer the following question: What are cyclic dinucleotides (CDNs) and how are they involved in the innate immune response to viral infections like herpesvirus?

[0001] assistant:
Cyclic dinucleotides (CDNs) are small molecules that serve as second messengers in cells, particularly recognized by the innate immune system. They are involved in the innate immune response to viral infections like herpesvirus by activating innate immune signaling pathways, leading to the production of interferons and other antiviral factors.
