Please answer the following question: What is the role of eIF2α in the host's antiviral response and how might ICP34.5 dephosphorylate it to prevent translational arrest?

[0001] assistant:
eIF2α (eukaryotic initiation factor 2α) plays a crucial role in the host's antiviral response by mediating translational arrest to inhibit viral protein synthesis. ICP34.5, a protein produced by certain herpesviruses like herpes simplex virus, dephosphorylates eIF2α, thereby preventing its inhibition and allowing for continued viral protein synthesis.
