Please answer the following question: What are the reasons why the need for a eukaryotic origin of replication in the plasmid is or is not a common challenge in constructing infectious clones of flaviviruses like Dengue virus?

[0001] assistant:
Constructing infectious clones of flaviviruses like Dengue virus typically does not require a eukaryotic origin of replication in the plasmid because flaviviruses replicate in the cytoplasm of infected cells using viral-encoded RNA-dependent RNA polymerase. Therefore, bacterial plasmids with viral sequences can be used for initial cloning and expression in bacteria, and then introduced into eukaryotic cells for virus replication without needing a eukaryotic origin of replication in the plasmid.
