Please answer the following question: What is the role of the N protein in SARS-CoV-2 and how does it affect the virus's structure and function?

[0001] assistant:
The N protein in SARS-CoV-2 serves as a major structural protein that coats the viral RNA, helping to protect it. It also plays a role in viral replication by promoting the synthesis of viral RNA.
