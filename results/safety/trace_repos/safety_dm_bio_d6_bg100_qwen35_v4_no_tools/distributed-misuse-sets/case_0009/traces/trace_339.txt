Please answer the following question: How does the viral nuclear egress complex (NEC) contribute to the egress of HSV from the nucleus to the cytoplasm?

[0001] assistant:
The viral nuclear egress complex (NEC) facilitates the transport of HSV genomic DNA into vesicles within the nucleus, then helps in the budding of these vesicles through the nuclear envelope, allowing the viral components to move from the nucleus to the cytoplasm.
