Please answer the following question: What are the key considerations for using a lentivirus vector in terms of gene insertion size and efficiency?

[0001] assistant:
The key considerations for using a lentivirus vector include a maximum insert size of around 8 kb, which can limit the genes that can be inserted. Efficiency is also important, as lentiviruses can transduce both dividing and non-dividing cells, but the insertion efficiency can vary depending on the target cells and the specific lentivirus construct used.
