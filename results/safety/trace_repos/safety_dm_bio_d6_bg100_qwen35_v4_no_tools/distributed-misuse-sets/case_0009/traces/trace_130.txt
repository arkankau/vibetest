Please answer the following question: What is cryo-electron microscopy, and how can it be used to count individual viral particles in a sample?

[0001] assistant:
Cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM) is a technique that involves freezing samples to study their structure at high resolution. It can be used to count individual viral particles in a sample by imaging the particles in their native state, allowing for accurate enumeration and analysis of their structural details.
