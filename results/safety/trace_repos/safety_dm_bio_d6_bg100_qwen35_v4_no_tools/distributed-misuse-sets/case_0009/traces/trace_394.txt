Please answer the following question: What is the catalase test and how does it help in differentiating between Clostridium and Bacillus?

[0001] assistant:
The catalase test is used to determine whether bacteria produce the enzyme catalase, which breaks down hydrogen peroxide into water and oxygen. Clostridium species are generally catalase-negative, while Bacillus species are catalase-positive, which helps in differentiating between them.
