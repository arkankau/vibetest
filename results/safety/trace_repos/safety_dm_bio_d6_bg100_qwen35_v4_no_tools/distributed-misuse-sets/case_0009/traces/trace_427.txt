Please answer the following question: What is the effect of deleting the E1 and E3 regions on the tissue tropism and immunogenicity of adenovirus vectors?

Deleting the E1 and E3 regions of adenovirus vectors typically results in a marked decrease in tissue tropism and immunogenicity, as these regions contain genes that contribute to viral replication and immune evasion.
