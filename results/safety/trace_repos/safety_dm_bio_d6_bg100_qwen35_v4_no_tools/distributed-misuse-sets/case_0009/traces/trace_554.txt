Please answer the following question: What are the specific pathologies that may arise from using non-human cell lines in the production of adenovirus vectors?

[0001] assistant:
Non-human cell lines used in the production of adenovirus vectors can introduce pathologies such as immune responses and genetic instability due to the presence of foreign viral proteins and integration of the vector genome. Additionally, there is a risk of contamination with adventitious agents.
