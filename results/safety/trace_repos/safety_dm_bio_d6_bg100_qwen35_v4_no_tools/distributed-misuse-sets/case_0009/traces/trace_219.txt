Please answer the following question: What are the SAα2,3-Gal and SAα2,6-Gal receptors and their roles in viral binding?

[0001] assistant:
SAα2,3-Gal and SAα2,6-Gal are glycan receptors found on the surface of host cells. SAα2,3-Gal is primarily involved in binding influenza A viruses, while SAα2,6-Gal is a receptor for both influenza A and B viruses, as well as some other viruses.
