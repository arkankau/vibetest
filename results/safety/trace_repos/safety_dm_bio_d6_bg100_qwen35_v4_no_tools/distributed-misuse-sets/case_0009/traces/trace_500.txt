[0001] assistant:
Yes, the latency-associated transcript (LAT) is involved in enhancing the immune evasion of the infected cell in the context of HSV latency by downregulating major histocompatibility complex (MHC) class I molecules, which reduces the ability of cytotoxic T cells to recognize and kill infected cells.
