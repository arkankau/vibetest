Please answer the following question: What is the process of reassortment in segmented RNA viruses and how does it contribute to genetic diversity within a viral population during reverse genetics studies?

[0001] assistant:
Reassortment in segmented RNA viruses occurs when two or more different viral strains infect the same cell, allowing segments of RNA to mix and create new viral particles with novel combinations of genetic material. This process significantly contributes to genetic diversity within a viral population, facilitating rapid evolution and adaptation.
