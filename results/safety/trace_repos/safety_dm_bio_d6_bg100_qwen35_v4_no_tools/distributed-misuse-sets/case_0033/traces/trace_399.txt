Please answer the following question: What are the potential methods for modifying the viral capsid to achieve conditional release of the transgene and thus temporal control of its expression?

[0001] assistant:
Potential methods for modifying the viral capsid include engineering specific peptides or ligands onto the capsid surface that can bind to cell-surface receptors or small molecules only under certain conditions, thereby achieving conditional release of the transgene. Another approach involves using light-inducible systems, such as optogenetic tools, to control the release of the viral contents.
