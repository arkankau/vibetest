Please answer the following question: What is the purpose of a counterselectable marker in a suicide vector for bacterial mutagenesis?

[0001] assistant:
The purpose of a counterselectable marker in a suicide vector for bacterial mutagenesis is to allow the selection of bacteria that have been successfully transformed with the vector while eliminating those that have not taken up the vector, effectively "suiciding" the untransformed cells.
