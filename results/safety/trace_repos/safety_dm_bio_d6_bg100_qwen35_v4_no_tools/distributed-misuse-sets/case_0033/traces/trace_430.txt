Please answer the following question: Is UL54/ICP27 involved in the regulation of viral gene expression at the level of mRNA splicing?

[0001] assistant:
UL54 and ICP27 are proteins associated with herpesviruses, but UL54 is not directly involved in mRNA splicing. ICP27, however, can affect viral gene expression including splicing through its interactions with cellular factors.
