Please answer the following question: What are the primary effects of using whole-body aerosol exposure systems that do not involve the use of anesthesia, and how do they compare to the effects of intranasal inoculation?

[0001] assistant:
Whole-body aerosol exposure systems can lead to systemic effects such as respiratory irritation, inflammation, and changes in lung function without the need for anesthesia. Compared to intranasal inoculation, aerosol exposure typically affects a broader area of the respiratory tract and can be more efficient in delivering particles to the lower lungs, but both methods can induce similar immune responses and lung pathology.
