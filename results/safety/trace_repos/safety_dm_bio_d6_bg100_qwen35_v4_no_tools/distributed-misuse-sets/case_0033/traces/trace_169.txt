Please answer the following question: How does a ribosomal frameshift signal facilitate the assembly of viral particles in reverse genetics studies?

[0001] assistant:
A ribosomal frameshift signal, often created by a specific sequence in the viral RNA, allows for the production of truncated viral proteins. In reverse genetics studies, this facilitates the assembly of viral particles by enabling the incorporation of these proteins, which can mimic natural viral components, aiding in the study and manipulation of viral life cycles.
