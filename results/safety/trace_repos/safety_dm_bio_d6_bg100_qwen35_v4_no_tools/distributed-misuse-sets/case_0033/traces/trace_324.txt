Please answer the following question: What specific mechanism does ICP47 use to interfere with the host immune response in herpesvirus infection?

[0001] assistant:
ICP47 from herpesviruses interferes with the host immune response by degrading major histocompatibility complex class I (MHC-I) molecules, thus preventing the presentation of viral peptides to cytotoxic T lymphocytes.
