Please answer the following question: Are BZLF1 and BRLF1 proteins involved in the initiation of the lytic cycle of EBV?

[0001] assistant:
Yes, BZLF1 and BRLF1 are involved in the initiation of the lytic cycle of Epstein-Barr Virus (EBV). BZLF1 encodes Zta, which is a master regulator that initiates the lytic cycle, while BRLF1 encodes Rta, which has similar functions and works synergistically with Zta.
