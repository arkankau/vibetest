Please answer the following question: What is the known function of ICP0 in HSV-1 and how does it relate to the host cell's ubiquitin-proteasome system?

[0001] assistant:
ICP0 in HSV-1 is a transactivator that interferes with the host cell's ubiquitin-proteasome system, promoting the degradation of key antiviral proteins and enhancing viral gene expression and replication.
