Yersinia pestis is suitable for isolation from soil/water samples using selective media due to its ability to grow on media containing bile salts and crystal violet, which inhibit the growth of many other bacteria. Additionally, it ferments sucrose but not lactose, allowing it to be distinguished from other Enterobacteriaceae.
