Please answer the following question: What is the 'Trojan horse' mechanism and how is it used in the context of studying BBB crossing with reverse genetics?

[0001] assistant:
The 'Trojan horse' mechanism involves encapsulating molecules or cells in a non-toxic, biodegradable material that can cross the blood-brain barrier (BBB). In reverse genetics, this is used to deliver genetic material, such as shRNA or CRISPR/Cas9 components, to specific brain regions for functional studies or gene editing.
