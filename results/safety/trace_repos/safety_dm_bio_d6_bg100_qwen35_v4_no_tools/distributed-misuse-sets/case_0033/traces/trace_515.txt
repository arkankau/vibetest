Please answer the following question: How should the RNA synthesized from poliovirus cDNA be introduced into the cells and what method is commonly used?

[0001] assistant:
The RNA synthesized from poliovirus cDNA should be introduced into cells via transfection methods. A commonly used method is electroporation, which involves briefly exposing cells to an electric field to create transient pores in the cell membrane, allowing the RNA to enter the cells.
