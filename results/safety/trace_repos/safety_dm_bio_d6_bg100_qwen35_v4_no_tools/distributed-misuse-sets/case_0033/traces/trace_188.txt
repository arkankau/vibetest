Please answer the following question: What is the role of the transporter associated with antigen processing (TAP) in presenting antigens to cytotoxic T lymphocytes (CTLs)?

[0001] assistant:
The role of TAP is to transport peptides from the cytosol into the endoplasmic reticulum, where they combine with MHC class I molecules for presentation to cytotoxic T lymphocytes.
