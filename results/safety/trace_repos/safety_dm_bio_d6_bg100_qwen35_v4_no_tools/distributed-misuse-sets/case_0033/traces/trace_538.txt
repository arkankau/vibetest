Please answer the following question: How does the mechanism of action of saxitoxin family of toxins differ from that of alcohol?

[0001] assistant:
Saxitoxin family toxins block sodium channels in nerve and muscle cells, preventing the influx of sodium ions and thus inhibiting action potential generation. Alcohol, on the other hand, affects GABA-A receptors and inhibits neurotransmitter release, leading to a depressant effect on the central nervous system.
