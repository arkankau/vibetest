Please answer the following question: What is the significance of high LD in the context of identifying genetic associations with disease resistance in a genome-wide association study?

[0001] assistant:
High linkage disequilibrium (LD) in a genome-wide association study indicates that genetic markers are inherited together more frequently than expected by chance, suggesting they are physically close on the same chromosome. This can help identify genetic regions associated with disease resistance but may also complicate fine-mapping of specific disease-risk variants.
