Please answer the following question: What is the function of the gerB gene in B. anthracis spores and how does it affect spore germination?

[0001] assistant:
The gerB gene in B. anthracis is involved in the production of the spore cortex. Its function is crucial for proper spore germination, as it helps in the assembly and stability of the spore cortex, which is essential for the transition from the dormant spore state to the active bacterial cell.
