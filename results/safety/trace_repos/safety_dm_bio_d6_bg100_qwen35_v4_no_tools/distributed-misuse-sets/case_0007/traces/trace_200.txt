Please answer the following question: How does glycoprotein E (gE) contribute to the fusion of the viral envelope with the host cell membrane during VZV infection?

[0001] assistant:
Glycoprotein E (gE) contributes to the fusion of the viral envelope with the host cell membrane by interacting with cellular receptors and other viral glycoproteins, particularly glycoprotein I and II (gI and gII), to mediate the entry of the virus into the cell.
