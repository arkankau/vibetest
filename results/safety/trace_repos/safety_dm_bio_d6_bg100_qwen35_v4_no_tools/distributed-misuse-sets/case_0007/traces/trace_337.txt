Please answer the following question: What are the limitations of continuous cell lines that make primary cells or organ cultures more suitable for reverse genetics studies of viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Continuous cell lines often lack genetic diversity and may not accurately reflect the response of primary cells or tissues, making them less suitable for reverse genetics studies of viruses. Primary cells and organ cultures, in contrast, are more representative of in vivo conditions and can provide more accurate data for such studies.
