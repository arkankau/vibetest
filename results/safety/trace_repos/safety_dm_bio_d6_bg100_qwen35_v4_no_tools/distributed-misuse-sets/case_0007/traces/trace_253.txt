Please answer the following question: What is the standard method for assembling synthesized DNA into a circular construct, and how does this method facilitate the transcription of viral RNA with a constitutive promoter to generate a single-stranded RNA virus?

[0001] assistant:
The standard method for assembling synthesized DNA into a circular construct is often achieved using Gibson assembly or Golden Gate cloning. This method facilitates the transcription of viral RNA with a constitutive promoter by creating a circular plasmid containing the viral RNA genome and the promoter sequence, which drives continuous transcription of the viral RNA when introduced into host cells.
