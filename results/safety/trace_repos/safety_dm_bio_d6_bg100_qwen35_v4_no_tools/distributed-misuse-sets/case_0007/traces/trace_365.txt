Please answer the following question: Can saturation mutagenesis be effectively used to integrate foreign genes into the viral genome, and what is the primary method for doing so?

[0001] assistant:
Saturation mutagenesis is not typically used to integrate foreign genes into the viral genome. The primary method for integrating foreign genes into a viral genome involves homologous recombination or site-specific integration using enzymes like integrases.
