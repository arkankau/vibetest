Please answer the following question: What is the definition of reverse genetics and how does it relate to RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Reverse genetics is a molecular biology technique used to generate a synthetic gene and then express it in a host cell to produce a specific gene product, allowing for the study of gene function. In the context of RNA viruses, reverse genetics enables the creation of infectious clones of viral genomes, facilitating the study of viral replication and the development of vaccines and antiviral therapies.
