Please answer the following question: What is the mechanism by which thymidine kinase (TK) is involved in the antiviral therapy of herpes simplex virus (HSV)?

[0001] assistant:
Thymidine kinase (TK) is involved in antiviral therapy of herpes simplex virus (HSV) by converting acyclic guanosine monophosphate (ACPM) to 5'-triphosphate acyclic guanosine (ATP), which is then incorporated into the viral DNA, causing premature termination of replication.
