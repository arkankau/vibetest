Please answer the following question: Does the Epstein-barr virus (viral) IL-10 homologue mimic the effects of traditional IL-10, but with a bias towards inhibitory or stimulating effects, and how does this impact immune evasion?

[0001] assistant:
The Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) encodes a protein called EBV latent membrane protein 1 (LMP1) that mimics the effects of IL-10 but with a bias towards stimulating rather than inhibitory effects. This bias contributes to immune evasion by promoting a pro-inflammatory environment that can suppress antiviral immune responses.
