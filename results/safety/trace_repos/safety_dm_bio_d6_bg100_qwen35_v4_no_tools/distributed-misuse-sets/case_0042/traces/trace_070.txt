Please answer the following question: What is the role of the viral vector in the production of high-titer lentivirus when transfecting HEK293T cells?

[0001] assistant:
The viral vector, typically based on HIV-1, carries the genetic material to be delivered into the HEK293T cells. When transfecting HEK293T cells, it facilitates the production of high-titer lentivirus by providing the necessary viral proteins for packaging the genetic cargo.
