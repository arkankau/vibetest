Please answer the following question: What are the mechanisms by which EBV nuclear antigen 1 (EBNA1) can contribute to oncogenesis through the NF-κB pathway?

[0001] assistant:
EBNA1 can contribute to oncogenesis through the NF-κB pathway by binding to and transactivating NF-κB-responsive promoters, thereby enhancing the transcription of NF-κB target genes that promote cell survival and proliferation. Additionally, EBNA1 can inhibit the activity of NF-κB inhibitors, further activating the NF-κB pathway.
