Please answer the following question: What is the function of vFLIP in the lifecycle of HHV-8 and how does it affect the virus's ability to establish latency in host cells?

[0001] assistant:
vFLIP (viral FLICE-inhibitory protein) in HHV-8 is a protein that inhibits apoptosis in the host cells, thereby promoting the survival of the infected cells. This function allows HHV-8 to establish and maintain latency, as the infected cells can persist without being eliminated by the host's immune system.
