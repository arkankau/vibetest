Please answer the following question: What are the key characteristics of a self-inactivating (SIN) lentiviral vector that make it suitable for use in reverse genetics experiments?

[0001] assistant:
A self-inactivating (SIN) lentiviral vector is suitable for reverse genetics experiments because it contains deletions or modifications in the viral genes (like Vpr and U3 region of theLTR) that prevent the expression of viral genes, thus reducing vector-induced toxicity and integration site interference. This makes it safer and more precise for studying gene function by allowing stable integration of the vector into the host genome without activating the viral genome.
