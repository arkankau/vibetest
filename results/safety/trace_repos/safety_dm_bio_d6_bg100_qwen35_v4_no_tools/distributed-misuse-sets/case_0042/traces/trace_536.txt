Please answer the following question: How does the polyprotein, a nonstructural protein, contribute to virulence activity between hosts and is it relevant to the modulation of host range in picornaviruses?

[0001] assistant:
The polyprotein in picornaviruses is cleaved into smaller functional proteins that contribute to virulence by modulating host cell responses and facilitating viral replication. This process is crucial for the virus to effectively infect and spread between hosts, thus impacting the virus's host range.
