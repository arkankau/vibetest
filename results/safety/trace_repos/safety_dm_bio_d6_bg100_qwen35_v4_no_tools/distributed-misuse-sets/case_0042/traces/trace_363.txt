Please answer the following question: Do both Botulism and Nerve Gases block acetylcholine transmission in the body?

[0001] assistant:
Botulism does block acetylcholine transmission by inhibiting the release of acetylcholine, but nerve gases typically work by inactivating acetylcholinesterase, leading to acetylcholine accumulation. Therefore, while they have different mechanisms, both can effectively interfere with acetylcholine transmission.
