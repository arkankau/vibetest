Please answer the following question: What is structural modelling and how does it help in understanding the three-dimensional structure of viral proteins?

[0001] assistant:
Structural modeling involves creating computational models of the three-dimensional structures of proteins based on their amino acid sequences and other available biochemical data. This helps in understanding the three-dimensional structure of viral proteins by predicting their shape and interactions, which is crucial for studying their function, potential drug targets, and vaccine development.
