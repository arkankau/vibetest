Please answer the following question: Can you provide examples or studies that confirm the association between Human herpesvirus 8 (HHV-8) and Kaposi's sarcoma in immunocompromised individuals?

[0001] assistant:
Human herpesvirus 8 (HHV-8) is widely recognized as the causative agent of Kaposi's sarcoma, particularly in immunocompromised individuals. Studies have consistently shown that the presence of HHV-8 DNA in biopsy samples from Kaposi's sarcoma lesions is highly correlated with the development of the disease in these populations.
