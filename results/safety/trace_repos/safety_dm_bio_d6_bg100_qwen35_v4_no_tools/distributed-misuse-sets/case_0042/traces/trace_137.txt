Please answer the following question: Can fleas effectively transmit the bacteria that causes the bubonic plague to other animals and humans, making them the primary vector?

[0001] assistant:
Fleas can effectively transmit the bacteria that causes the bubonic plague, but they are not the primary vector. The primary vector is the flea's host, typically rodents, which become infected and then pass the bacteria to fleas. When a flea bites an infected rodent and then bites a human or another animal, it can transmit the bacteria.
