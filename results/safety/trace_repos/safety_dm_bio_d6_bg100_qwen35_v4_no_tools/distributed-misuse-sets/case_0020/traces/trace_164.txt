Please answer the following question: What role does CD150 play in the entry of wild-type MV into host cells, and how does this relate to the use of 'CD150-blind' rMV in pathogenesis studies?

[0001] assistant:
CD150 (also known as SLAM or CD278) is a receptor that mediates the entry of wild-type measles virus (MV) into host cells. In pathogenesis studies, 'CD150-blind' rMV strains lack this receptor binding capacity, allowing researchers to study the virus's effects without the initial cellular entry step.
