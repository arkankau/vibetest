Please answer the following question: In what ways can single-strand conformation polymorphism (SSCP) analysis complement or be used in conjunction with other molecular techniques to study viral evolution?

[0001] assistant:
SSCP analysis can complement other molecular techniques, such as PCR and sequencing, by providing a quick screening method to detect DNA sequence variations. It can be used in conjunction with sequencing to confirm and identify specific polymorphisms involved in viral evolution.
