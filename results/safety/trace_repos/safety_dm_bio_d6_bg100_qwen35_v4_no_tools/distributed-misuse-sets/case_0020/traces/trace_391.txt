Please answer the following question: What are the specific mechanisms by which RNA surveillance might identify and degrade mRNA, and how might these be impacted by the other proposed modifications?

[0001] assistant:
RNA surveillance can identify and degrade mRNA through mechanisms such as nonsense-mediated mRNA decay (NMD), which targets mRNAs containing premature termination codons, and decay mediated by miRNAs or siRNAs that bind to complementary sequences in the mRNA. These processes can be influenced by various modifications, such as methylation or pseudouridylation, which may alter RNA structure and stability, thereby impacting surveillance and degradation.
