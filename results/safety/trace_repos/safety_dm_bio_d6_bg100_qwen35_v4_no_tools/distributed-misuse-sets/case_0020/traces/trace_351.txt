Please answer the following question: What are the steps involved in the NF-κB pathway and how might ICP34.5 interfere with these steps to promote cell survival?

[0001] assistant:
The NF-κB pathway involves activation through various stimuli, leading to the degradation of IκB proteins, nuclear translocation of NF-κB, and transcription of pro-inflammatory and survival genes. ICP34.5, a viral protein from herpes simplex virus, interferes by enhancing ubiquitination and degradation of IκB proteins, thus promoting cell survival and inhibiting the NF-κB pathway.
