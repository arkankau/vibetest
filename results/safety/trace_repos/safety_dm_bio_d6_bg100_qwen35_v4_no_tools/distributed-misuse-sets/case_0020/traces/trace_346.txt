Please answer the following question: How does the plaque assay work and what kinds of viruses is it commonly used to quantify?

[0001] assistant:
The plaque assay works by measuring the number of viral particles that can replicate and form plaques on a monolayer of cells in a petri dish. It is commonly used to quantify viruses that can form plaques, such as poxviruses, adenoviruses, and herpesviruses.
