[0001] assistant:
One example is the H1N1 influenza virus (swine flu) that emerged in 2009, resulting from a reassortment of viruses from pigs, birds, and humans. Another example is HIV, which has evolved through recombination and has pandemic potential due to its high mutation rate and ability to infect a wide range of hosts.
