Please answer the following question: What is a virulence factor and how does it contribute to the harmful effects of a microbe on its host?

[0001] assistant:
A virulence factor is a molecule produced by a microbe that contributes to its ability to cause disease by damaging host tissues or interfering with host defenses. These factors can facilitate invasion, colonization, and evasion of the host immune response, thereby enhancing the harmful effects of the microbe.
