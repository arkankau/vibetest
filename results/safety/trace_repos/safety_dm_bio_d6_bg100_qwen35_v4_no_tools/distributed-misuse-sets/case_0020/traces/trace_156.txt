Please answer the following question: How does the reassortment between segmented RNA genomes of avian and human influenza viruses contribute to the emergence of new influenza viruses with pandemic potential, as evidenced by historical pandemics?

[0001] assistant:
Reassortment between segmented RNA genomes of avian and human influenza viruses allows for the exchange of genetic material, creating new virus strains with novel combinations of surface proteins. This process can result in viruses that humans have not been previously exposed to, leading to new pandemics, as seen in the 1918 Spanish flu and 2009 H1N1 pandemic.
