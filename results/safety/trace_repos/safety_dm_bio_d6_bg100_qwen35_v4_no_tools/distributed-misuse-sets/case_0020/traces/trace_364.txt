Please answer the following question: What is E. coli homologous recombination (ET recombination) and how is it typically used in molecular biology?

[0001] assistant:
ET recombination is a site-specific recombination system derived from E. coli that allows for precise DNA rearrangements. It is typically used in molecular biology for genetic engineering, gene targeting, and constructing complex genetic circuits due to its specificity and efficiency.
