Please answer the following question: What is the role of ribonucleotide reductase in maintaining the correct balance of deoxyribonucleotides (dNTPs) during viral DNA synthesis?

[0001] assistant:
Ribonucleotide reductase plays a crucial role in converting ribonucleotides to deoxyribonucleotides, which is essential for maintaining the correct balance of dNTPs during viral DNA synthesis. This ensures that the viral DNA can be synthesized accurately without depleting the host cell's dNTP pool too much.
