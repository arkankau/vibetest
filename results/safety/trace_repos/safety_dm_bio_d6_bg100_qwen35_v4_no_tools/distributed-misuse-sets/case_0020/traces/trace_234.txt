Please answer the following question: What are the advantages of using the flanking loxP site approach for creating recombinant herpesviruses in terms of gene expression and viral replication?

[0001] assistant:
The flanking loxP site approach allows for precise control over gene deletion or insertion, which can enhance gene expression by removing regulatory elements or inserting promoters. This method also facilitates viral replication by ensuring the integrity of essential viral genes while enabling the study of gene function.
