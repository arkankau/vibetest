Please answer the following question: What role does thymidine kinase play in the viral life cycle and why is it a significant target for antiviral drugs?

[0001] assistant:
Thymidine kinase plays a crucial role in the viral life cycle by converting viral deoxythymidine triphosphate (dTTP) into dTMP, which is then incorporated into viral DNA during replication. It is a significant target for antiviral drugs because inhibiting this enzyme can block viral DNA synthesis, thereby preventing the virus from replicating and spreading.
