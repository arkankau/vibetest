[0001] assistant:
In Thioglycolate broth, oxygen-soluble components like glucose and other organic nutrients are typically found at the top, while sulfur compounds and other reduced substances accumulate at the bottom. This distribution allows aerobic bacteria to thrive at the top, while anaerobic bacteria can survive in the oxygen-depleted lower layers.
