Please answer the following question: What is the ELISA assay and how does it measure the presence of viruses in a sample?

[0001] assistant:
The ELISA (Enzyme-Linked Immunosorbent Assay) is a laboratory test used to detect and measure antibodies or antigens in a sample. It measures the presence of viruses by detecting specific viral antigens or antibodies that the immune system produces in response to a viral infection.
