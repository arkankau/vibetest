Please answer the following question: What is the role of MAVS in the host immune response to viral infections, specifically in relation to antiviral signaling pathways?

[0001] assistant:
MAVS (Mitochondrial Antiviral Signaling Protein) plays a crucial role in the host immune response to viral infections by serving as a key sensor and adaptor in innate antiviral signaling pathways. It detects viral components like double-stranded RNA and triggers the activation of type I interferons and other antiviral responses.
