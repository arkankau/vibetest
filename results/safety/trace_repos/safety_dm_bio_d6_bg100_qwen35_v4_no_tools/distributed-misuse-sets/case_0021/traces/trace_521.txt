Please answer the following question: Is reassortment an error-prone process that leads to high mutation rates in the viral genome during reverse genetics studies of segmented RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Reassortment is not an error-prone process and does not inherently lead to high mutation rates in the viral genome. It involves the exchange of genetic segments between different virus particles, which can increase genetic diversity but is not characterized by errors or high mutation rates.
