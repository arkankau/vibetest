Please answer the following question: How does T7 RNA polymerase synthesize RNA from a DNA template in reverse genetics studies?

[0001] assistant:
T7 RNA polymerase synthesizes RNA from a DNA template by binding to a T7 promoter region in the DNA, initiating transcription, and synthesizing RNA in the 5' to 3' direction. This process is commonly used in reverse genetics to produce large amounts of specific RNA molecules for functional studies.
