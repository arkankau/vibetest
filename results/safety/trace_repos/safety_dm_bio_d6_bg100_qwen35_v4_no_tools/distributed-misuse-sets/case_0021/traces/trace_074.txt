Please answer the following question: Do UL30 and UL42 proteins serve as scaffold proteins for the assembly of the herpes simplex virus?

[0001] assistant:
UL30 and UL42 proteins are not known to serve as scaffold proteins for the assembly of the herpes simplex virus. The specific roles of these proteins in viral assembly are not as well defined as some other viral proteins, but they are involved in DNA replication and repair rather than capsid assembly.
