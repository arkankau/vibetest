Please answer the following question: How does the close proximity of pigs to both avian and human populations contribute to their role as 'mixing vessels' for IAVs?

[0001] assistant:
Pigs can serve as mixing vessels for IAVs because they can be infected with both avian and human influenza viruses, allowing these viruses to reassort and potentially generate new strains that can infect humans. Their close proximity to both avian and human populations facilitates this viral mixing.
