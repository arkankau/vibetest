Please answer the following question: What are the challenges in regaining bioactivity of misfolded proteins during the refolding process, and how do these proteins relate to the purification of toxins?

[0001] assistant:
Regaining bioactivity of misfolded proteins during refolding can be challenging due to the protein's tendency to form non-native aggregates or misfold again. These proteins can relate to the purification of toxins as some toxins are misfolded proteins that need to be refolded or properly denatured to be neutralized or purified.
