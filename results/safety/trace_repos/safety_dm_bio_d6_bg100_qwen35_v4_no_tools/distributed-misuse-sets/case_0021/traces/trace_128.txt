Please answer the following question: Is aflatoxin a strong bioregulator that can be synthesized from a protein and expressed by a single gene addition to a plant through biopharming?

[0001] assistant:
Aflatoxin is not a bioregulator and is not synthesized from a protein or expressed by a single gene addition in plants through biopharming. It is a toxic metabolite produced by certain fungi, particularly under unfavorable growth conditions.
