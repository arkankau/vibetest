Please answer the following question: What is the role of an antibiotic resistance marker in synthetic gene circuits and why might it not be included in circuits controlling the expression of a virulence factor?

[0001] assistant:
An antibiotic resistance marker in synthetic gene circuits allows for the selection of cells that have been successfully transformed with the plasmid carrying the gene of interest. It might not be included in circuits controlling the expression of a virulence factor to avoid conferring resistance to antibiotics, which could pose a risk in therapeutic applications or unintended environmental release.
