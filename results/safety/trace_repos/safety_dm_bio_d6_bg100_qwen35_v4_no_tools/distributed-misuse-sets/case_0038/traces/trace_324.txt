Please answer the following question: What is the mechanism by which ICP47 downregulates MHC class I molecules on the cell surface?

[0001] assistant:
ICP47 from herpes simplex virus competes with MHC class I molecules for binding to TAP transporters, thereby preventing the transport of peptides to the endoplasmic reticulum for MHC class I loading and presentation on the cell surface.
