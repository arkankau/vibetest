Please answer the following question: What are the characteristics of mutants obtained from insertions of exogenous DNA, and can they always be null mutants?

[0001] assistant:
Mutants obtained from insertions of exogenous DNA can exhibit various characteristics depending on the nature and location of the insertion, such as gene activation, gene disruption, or altered gene expression. These mutants are not always null mutants; the effect can vary from no change to a complete loss of function, or to changes in gene expression levels.
