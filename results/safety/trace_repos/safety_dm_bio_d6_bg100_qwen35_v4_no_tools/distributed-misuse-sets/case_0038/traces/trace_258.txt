Please answer the following question: How does the activity of thymidine kinase (TK) affect the replication of herpes simplex virus (HSV) in the presence of acyclovir?

[0001] assistant:
Thymidine kinase (TK) converts acyclovir to a monophosphate form, which is then activated further by viral and cellular kinases to become effective against herpes simplex virus (HSV). In the presence of functional TK, acyclovir inhibits HSV replication by forming a trimer that interferes with viral DNA synthesis.
