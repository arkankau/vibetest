Please answer the following question: What are the typical roles of cell binding receptors, replication proteins, nonstructural proteins, and lytic proteins in the viral life cycle?

[0001] assistant:
Cell binding receptors help viruses attach to host cells. Replication proteins are involved in viral genome replication. Nonstructural proteins typically function in viral RNA synthesis or modulation of the host cell environment. Lytic proteins are responsible for causing the host cell to lyse, releasing new viral particles.
