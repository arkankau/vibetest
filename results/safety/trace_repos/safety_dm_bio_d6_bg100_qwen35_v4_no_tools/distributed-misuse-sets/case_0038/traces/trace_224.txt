[0001] assistant:
Evaluating neutralizing activity of vaccine-induced antibodies against SARS-CoV-2 variants helps identify which antibodies can effectively neutralize new variants, ensuring the vaccine remains effective. This process also aids in understanding potential for antibody-dependent enhancement (ADE), by assessing whether antibodies might inadvertently help the virus enter cells, which is crucial for vaccine safety.
