In the context of reverse genetics for RNA viruses, the cloned cDNA cannot be directly injected into a host organism to recover an infectious virus because RNA viruses typically require a replication intermediate (such as a negative-strand RNA for negative-sense viruses) that is not directly produced from cDNA. An additional step, such as transfection into permissive cells, is usually required to generate an infectious virus.
