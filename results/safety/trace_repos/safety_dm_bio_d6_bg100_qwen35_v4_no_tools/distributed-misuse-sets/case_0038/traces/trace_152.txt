Please answer the following question: How does plasmid-based reverse genetics facilitate viral genome sequencing in the study of influenza viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Plasmid-based reverse genetics allows for the construction of infectious clones of viral genomes, which can be used to generate viruses for sequencing and studying influenza virus genetics and pathogenesis. This technique facilitates the rapid generation and modification of viral genomes, enabling detailed analysis and sequencing.
