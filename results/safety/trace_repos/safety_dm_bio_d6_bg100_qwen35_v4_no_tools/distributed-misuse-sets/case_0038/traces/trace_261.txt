Please answer the following question: How does a packaging signal sequence function in the propagation of viral vectors like the HSV amplicon?

[0001] assistant:
A packaging signal sequence in viral vectors like the HSV amplicon helps ensure that only the desired genetic material is packaged into the viral particles. This sequence guides the correct integration of the vector's genetic material into the viral capsid, facilitating efficient propagation and delivery of the gene of interest.
