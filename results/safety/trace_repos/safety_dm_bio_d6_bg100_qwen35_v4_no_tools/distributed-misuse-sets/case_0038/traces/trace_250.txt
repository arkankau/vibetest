Please answer the following question: Which genes are categorized as late genes in the context of HSV replication and what is their typical expression pattern?

[0001] assistant:
Late genes in the context of HSV (Herpes Simplex Virus) replication are expressed late in the viral life cycle, typically after the early genes. They include genes that encode structural proteins necessary for the assembly of new virions, such as the virion proteins VP5, VP6, and VP7. Their expression pattern is characterized by increasing levels of viral protein production as the infected cell progresses towards lysis.
