Please answer the following question: What is the role of macrophages in the immune response to HSV, and are they the primary cells involved in the early response in HSK?

[0001] assistant:
Macrophages play a crucial role in the immune response to HSV by phagocytosing virus particles and secreting cytokines that recruit other immune cells. They are not the primary cells involved in the early response in HSK; that role is typically carried out by dendritic cells and NK cells.
