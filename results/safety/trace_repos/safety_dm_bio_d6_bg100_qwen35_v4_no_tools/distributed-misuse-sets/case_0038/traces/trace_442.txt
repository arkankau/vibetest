Please answer the following question: How does bovine serum albumin (BSA) protect viral envelopes during lyophilization, and what weight-to-weight ratio (BSA:virus) is typically required to prevent ≥2 log loss of titer after rehydration?

[0001] assistant:
Bovine serum albumin (BSA) protects viral envelopes during lyophilization by stabilizing the viral particles and preventing aggregation and structural damage. Typically, a BSA:virus weight-to-weight ratio of 5-10:1 is required to prevent ≥2 log loss of titer after rehydration.
