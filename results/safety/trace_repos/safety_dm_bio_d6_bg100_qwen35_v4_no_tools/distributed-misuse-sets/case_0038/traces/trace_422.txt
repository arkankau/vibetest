Multiplex Automatable Genome Engineering (MAGE) allows for the simultaneous modification of multiple genes, increasing efficiency in genome editing. However, it is generally slower and less precise than CRISPR-Cas9 for large-scale genome replacement due to the need for multiple rounds of transformation and selection.
