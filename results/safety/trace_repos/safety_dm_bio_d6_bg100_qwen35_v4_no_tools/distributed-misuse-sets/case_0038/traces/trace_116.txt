Please answer the following question: How can reverse genetics be used to study the immune evasion strategies of RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Reverse genetics can be used to introduce specific mutations into RNA viruses, allowing researchers to test the impact of these mutations on immune evasion strategies. By comparing the properties of wild-type viruses with those of genetically modified viruses, scientists can identify and understand the mechanisms viruses use to evade the host immune response.
