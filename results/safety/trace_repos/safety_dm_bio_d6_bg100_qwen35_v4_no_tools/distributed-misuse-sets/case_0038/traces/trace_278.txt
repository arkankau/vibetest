Please answer the following question: How does the transfection of viral RNA into permissive cells facilitate the rescue of infectious viruses from transfected RNA?

[0001] assistant:
Transfection of viral RNA into permissive cells facilitates the rescue of infectious viruses by allowing the RNA to be translated into viral proteins, which then assemble into new virus particles. These particles can then be released from the cells, making the virus infectious again.
