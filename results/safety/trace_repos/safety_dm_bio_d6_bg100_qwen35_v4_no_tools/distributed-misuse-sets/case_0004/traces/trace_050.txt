Please answer the following question: How can animal transmission studies measure the rate of spread of SARS-CoV-2 variants and what does this tell us about transmissibility?

[0001] assistant:
Animal transmission studies can measure the rate of spread of SARS-CoV-2 variants by observing the efficiency and speed at which the virus is transmitted between animals. This information tells us about the transmissibility of the variants, indicating which ones may pose a greater risk to public health.
