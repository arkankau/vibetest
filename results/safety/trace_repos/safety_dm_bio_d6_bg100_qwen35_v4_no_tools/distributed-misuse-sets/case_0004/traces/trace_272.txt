[0001] assistant:
Primary cells or organ cultures allow for the study of viral interactions with the host genome by providing a more natural environment compared to cell lines, enabling researchers to observe viral replication and host responses in a context closer to the in vivo situation. This approach is crucial for understanding viral pathogenesis as it can reveal host factors that influence viral infection and replication, which may not be apparent in simpler systems.
