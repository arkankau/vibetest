Botulism typically causes symptoms such as double vision, blurred vision, drooping eyelids, weakness, difficulty swallowing, and slurred speech. In the context of Botox usage, symptoms are usually localized to the area treated, such as temporary paralysis of facial muscles leading to a frozen or asymmetrical appearance.
