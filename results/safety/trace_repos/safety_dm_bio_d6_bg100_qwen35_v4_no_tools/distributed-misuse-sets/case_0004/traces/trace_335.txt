[0001] assistant:
Yes, this approach is commonly used in virology to test for neutralizing antibodies against an engineered virus. It involves coating plates with virus-like particles (VLPs) or recombinant viral capsids and then incubating them with human serum to detect bound antibodies using a fluorophore.
