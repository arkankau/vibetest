The Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor (EGFR) pathway promotes cell proliferation and survival by activating various intracellular signaling cascades that enhance cell cycle progression and inhibit apoptosis. This leads to increased cell growth and survival, which can be exploited in cancer therapy targeting this pathway.
