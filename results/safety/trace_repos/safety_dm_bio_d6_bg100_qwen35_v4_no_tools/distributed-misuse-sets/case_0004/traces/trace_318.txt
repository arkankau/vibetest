Please answer the following question: What evidence supports the idea that the spike protein is more immunogenic than the other proteins in SARS-CoV-2, such as the membrane and nucleocapsid proteins?

[0001] assistant:
Studies have shown that the spike protein induces a stronger immune response than the membrane and nucleocapsid proteins, likely due to its role in viral entry and its high degree of variability. This is supported by the fact that most COVID-19 vaccines target the spike protein.
