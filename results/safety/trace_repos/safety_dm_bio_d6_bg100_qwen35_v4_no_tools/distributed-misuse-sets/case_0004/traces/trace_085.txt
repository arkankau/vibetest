Cell-Free Protein Synthesis (CFPS) requires specialized reagents and equipment, such as purified enzymes and ribosomes, which are not typically available in classical biochemistry laboratories. Additionally, optimizing reaction conditions for CFPS can be challenging due to the sensitivity of the components involved.
