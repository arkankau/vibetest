Please answer the following question: Can electroporation integrate viral RNA into the host genome, and if not, what is the role of other methods?

[0001] assistant:
Electroporation does not integrate viral RNA into the host genome. Instead, it is used to increase the permeability of cell membranes to facilitate the uptake of DNA or RNA, which can then be used for gene delivery or expression. Other methods, such as transduction by viral vectors, are used for integrating viral genetic material into the host genome.
