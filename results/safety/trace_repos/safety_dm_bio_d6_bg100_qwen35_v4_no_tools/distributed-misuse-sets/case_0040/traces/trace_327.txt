Please answer the following question: Describe the process of active replication and production of new virions by a virus in the context of herpesvirus research.

[0001] assistant:
Herpesviruses enter host cells and use the cellular machinery to replicate their DNA. The viral DNA then undergoes transcription and translation to produce viral proteins. These components assemble into new virions, which are released from the cell to infect other cells.
