Genetic recombination of ZIKV with DENV in vitro can simulate some aspects of natural transmission but is more controlled and may not fully replicate complex interactions. Serial passaging of wild-type ZIKV between Aedes albopictus C6/36 cells and Ifnar1−/− mice better simulates natural transmission dynamics and can increase transmissibility by selecting for more virulent strains.
