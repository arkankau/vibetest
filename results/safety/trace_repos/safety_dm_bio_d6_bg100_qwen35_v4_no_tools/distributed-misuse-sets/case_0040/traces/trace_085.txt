Please answer the following question: What similarities and differences exist between VACV and HPXV that could affect their effectiveness as vaccines?

VACV (VacV) and HPXV (Herpesvirus of Xenopus) are both large DNA viruses from different families. VACV is used as a live attenuated vaccine in veterinary medicine and shows broad-spectrum protection. HPXV, on the other hand, is not used as a vaccine and has different immunogenic properties. Therefore, the effectiveness as vaccines would differ due to their distinct viral structures, immune responses they elicit, and specific host cell interactions.
