Please answer the following question: How do insect cells typically handle complex protein folding compared to microbial systems, and what implications does this have for expressing venom toxins?

[0001] assistant:
Insect cells can handle complex protein folding more efficiently than microbial systems due to the presence of a more sophisticated endoplasmic reticulum and Golgi apparatus. This is advantageous for expressing venom toxins, as it leads to higher yields of correctly folded proteins, which are crucial for the function and stability of these toxins.
