Please answer the following question: How is surface plasmon resonance used to determine the binding affinity of viral proteins to host receptors?

[0001] assistant:
Surface plasmon resonance (SPR) is used to determine the binding affinity of viral proteins to host receptors by detecting real-time changes in mass at the sensor surface, which correspond to the binding and dissociation of molecules. This technique measures the interaction kinetics and affinity with high sensitivity and specificity, providing dynamic information about the binding process.
