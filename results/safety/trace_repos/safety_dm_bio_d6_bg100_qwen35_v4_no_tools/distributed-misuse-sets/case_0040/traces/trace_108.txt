Please answer the following question: Can you explain how the selective impermeability of the outer membrane affects the ability of antibiotics to reach and interact with intracellular targets?

[0001] assistant:
The outer membrane of Gram-negative bacteria acts as a selective barrier, allowing only small molecules to pass through. This impermeability restricts the entry of larger antibiotics, thereby limiting their ability to reach and interact with intracellular targets.
