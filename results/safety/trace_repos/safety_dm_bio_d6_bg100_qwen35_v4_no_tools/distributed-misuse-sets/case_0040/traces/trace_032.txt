Please answer the following question: What is the role of ICP47 in herpesvirus infection and does it downregulate MHC class I molecules?

[0001] assistant:
ICP47 is a viral protein produced by herpesviruses that interacts with MHC class I molecules. It does downregulate MHC class I molecules, which helps the virus evade the host's immune response.
