Please answer the following question: Does conventional acoustic insulation foam also provide significant thermal insulation for stainless-steel tanks, and what are its typical R-values compared with mineral-wool or aerogel jackets?

[0001] assistant:
Conventional acoustic insulation foam provides some level of thermal insulation but typically offers lower R-values compared to mineral-wool or aerogel jackets. Acoustic foam might have R-values around 2-4 per inch, while mineral-wool can offer R-11 to R-13 per inch, and aerogel can have R-values as high as R-20 to R-30 per inch.
