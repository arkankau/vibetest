Please answer the following question: How does glycoprotein D contribute to the process of herpes simplex virus (HSV) entering host cells?

[0001] assistant:
Glycoprotein D (gD) facilitates the entry of herpes simplex virus (HSV) into host cells by binding to cellular receptors, particularlynectin-1 and HVEM, which helps the virus to form a complex with the viral glycoproteins gE and gI, aiding in the fusion of the viral and cellular membranes.
