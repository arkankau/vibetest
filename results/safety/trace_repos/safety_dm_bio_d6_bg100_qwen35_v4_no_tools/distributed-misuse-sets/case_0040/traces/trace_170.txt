Please answer the following question: In the context of herpesvirus research, how does the aggregation of viral particles at the cell surface relate to the concept of syncytium?

[0001] assistant:
The aggregation of herpesvirus particles at the cell surface can lead to the formation of syncytia, which are multinucleated giant cells resulting from the fusion of infected cells. This process is associated with the spread of the virus within the host.
