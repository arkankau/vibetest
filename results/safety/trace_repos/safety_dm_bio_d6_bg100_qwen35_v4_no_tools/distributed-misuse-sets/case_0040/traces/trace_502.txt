Please answer the following question: What is the function of RIG-I in the context of antiviral responses?

[0001] assistant:
RIG-I (Retinoic Acid Early Antigen Induced Gene I) is an innate immune receptor that detects viral RNA and initiates an antiviral response by activating the innate immune signaling pathway, leading to the production of type I interferons and other antiviral cytokines.
