Please answer the following question: How can the integrated phage genome be reactivated to produce phage particles that contain bacterial genes?

[0001] assistant:
The integrated phage genome, or prophage, can be reactivated through various mechanisms such as UV radiation, certain antibiotics, or changes in environmental conditions like temperature shifts, which can lead to the excision of the prophage from the bacterial chromosome and subsequent phage particle production containing the bacterial genes.
