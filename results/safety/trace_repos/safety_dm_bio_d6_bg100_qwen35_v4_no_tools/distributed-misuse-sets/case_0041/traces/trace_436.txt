Please answer the following question: What is the role of the CMV promoter in controlling the timing of viral genome transcription in the presence of tetracycline?

[0001] assistant:
The CMV promoter is used in conjunction with tetracycline-controlled transcription systems to finely regulate the timing of viral genome transcription. In the presence of tetracycline, the promoter's activity is inhibited, slowing or stopping transcription.
