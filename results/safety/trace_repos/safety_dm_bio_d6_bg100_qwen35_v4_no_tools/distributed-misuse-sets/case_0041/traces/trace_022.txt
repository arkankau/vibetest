Please answer the following question: What is the role of loxP sites in molecular biology and how do they facilitate recombination events?

[0001] assistant:
LoxP sites are specific DNA sequences recognized and recombined by the Cre recombinase enzyme. They facilitate site-specific recombination events, allowing for the precise removal, inversion, or deletion of genetic sequences in transgenic organisms and cell cultures.
