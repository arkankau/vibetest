Please answer the following question: What is the function of the thymidine kinase (TK) locus in the herpes simplex virus (HSV) genome?

[0001] assistant:
The thymidine kinase (TK) locus in the herpes simplex virus (HSV) genome is essential for the synthesis of viral DNA, as it phosphorylates thymidine, facilitating DNA synthesis during infection.
