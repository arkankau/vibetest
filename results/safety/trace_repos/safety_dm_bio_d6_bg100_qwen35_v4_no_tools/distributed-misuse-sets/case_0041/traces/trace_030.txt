Please answer the following question: What is the importance of thymidine kinase (TK) in the activation of acyclovir and its effectiveness in antiviral therapy?

[0001] assistant:
Thymidine kinase (TK) is crucial in the activation of acyclovir, converting it into its mono-phosphorylated form, which is then further converted by cellular kinases. This activation is essential for acyclovir's effectiveness in antiviral therapy, as the activated form can inhibit viral DNA synthesis.
