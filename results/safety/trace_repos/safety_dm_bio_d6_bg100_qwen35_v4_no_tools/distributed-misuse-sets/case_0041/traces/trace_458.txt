Please answer the following question: When rescuing a filovirus whose native glycoprotein (GP) gene has been deleted, what is the most reliable way to supply a recombinant GP in trans using a plasmid vector driven by a CMV promoter after co-transfection into Vero E6 cells, and how can one confirm that the resulting virions incorporate the new GP into their envelopes?

[0001] assistant:
The most reliable way to supply a recombinant GP in trans is by co-transfecting Vero E6 cells with a plasmid encoding the GP gene under a CMV promoter and a plasmid encoding the filovirus genome. To confirm that the resulting virions incorporate the new GP into their envelopes, one can perform immunofluorescence assays using GP-specific antibodies or analyze the viral particles by Western blotting with the same antibodies.
