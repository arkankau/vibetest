Please answer the following question: Does glycoprotein D (gD) play a role in cleaving host membrane proteins to facilitate endocytosis in the context of HSV-1?

[0001] assistant:
Yes, glycoprotein D (gD) of HSV-1 interacts with host cell receptors and plays a role in facilitating the attachment and entry of the virus into host cells, though it does not directly cleave host membrane proteins; this process is primarily mediated by other viral glycoproteins.
