Please answer the following question: What is the significance of inverted terminal repeats (ITRs) in the transcription of early viral genes in the context of a reverse genetics system for a DNA virus?

Inverted terminal repeats (ITRs) are essential for the transcription of early viral genes in the context of a reverse genetics system for a DNA virus because they serve as primer binding sites for RNA polymerase during replication. They ensure that the viral genome is properly transcribed and replicated.
