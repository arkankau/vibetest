[0001] assistant:
The origin of replication in a herpesvirus vector is a DNA sequence that initiates viral DNA replication. Positioning a therapeutic gene adjacent to the origin of replication can enhance packaging efficiency because it allows the host cell to replicate both the viral DNA and the therapeutic gene together, increasing the likelihood that the recombinant vector will be packaged into viral particles.
