Please answer the following question: In the context of reverse genetics studies, what are the advantages of using electroporation over other transfection methods when working with positive-sense RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Electroporation offers high transfection efficiency and is particularly useful for positive-sense RNA viruses because it can introduce nucleic acids directly into cells, bypassing the need for complex vectors. This method is also faster and more reliable, especially for difficult-to-transfect cell types.
