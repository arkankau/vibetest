Non-invasive monitoring techniques, such as blood tests and imaging, can provide insights into a virus's pathogenicity by measuring parameters like white blood cell counts, inflammation markers, and organ function without the need for invasive procedures. Specific physiological parameters that can be measured include temperature, oxygen saturation, heart rate, and liver and kidney function.
