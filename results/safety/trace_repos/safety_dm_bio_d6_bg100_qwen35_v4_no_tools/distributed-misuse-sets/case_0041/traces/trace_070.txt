Filovirus glycoproteins require host-cell factors such as proteases (e.g., furin), chaperones (e.g., calnexin), and lipid micro-domains (e.g., cholesterol and sphingolipids) for correct cleavage, trafficking, and envelope incorporation. These factors can indeed differ significantly between Vero E6, HEK293T, and insect cells, potentially affecting viral replication efficiency.
