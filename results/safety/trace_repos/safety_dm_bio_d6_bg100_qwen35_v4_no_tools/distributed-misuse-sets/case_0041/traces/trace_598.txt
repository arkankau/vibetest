Please answer the following question: How does the sensitivity of serologic tests for visceral leishmaniasis compare to that for cutaneous leishmaniasis?

[0001] assistant:
The sensitivity of serologic tests for visceral leishmaniasis is generally higher than for cutaneous leishmaniasis. This is because visceral leishmaniasis involves a systemic infection, leading to a more robust immune response that can be detected by serologic tests.
