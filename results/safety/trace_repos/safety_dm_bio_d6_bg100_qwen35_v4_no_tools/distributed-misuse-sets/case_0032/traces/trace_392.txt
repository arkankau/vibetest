Please answer the following question: What is the replication of viral genomes within a newly infected host cell and how does it differ from de novo synthesis?

[0001] assistant:
Viral genome replication within a newly infected host cell involves the production of viral genetic material using the host's machinery, while de novo synthesis refers to the host cell synthesizing its own genetic material from scratch. The key difference is that viral replication uses existing viral genetic material as a template, whereas de novo synthesis does not require any pre-existing genetic material.
