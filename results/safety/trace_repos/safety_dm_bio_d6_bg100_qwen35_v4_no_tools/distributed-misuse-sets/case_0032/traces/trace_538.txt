In the context of herpesvirus genetic engineering, 'UL' (Unique Long) and 'US' (Unique Short) regions refer to specific segments of the viral genome. The UL region contains larger, more complex genes, while the US region includes smaller, often regulatory genes. Unique long and unique short regions are terms used to describe the locations of these genes within the virus, distinguishing them from other regions like immediate-early (IE) or late (L) regions.
