Please answer the following question: What is the role of the packaging plasmid in the production of high-titer lentivirus when transfecting HEK293T cells?

[0001] assistant:
The packaging plasmid is used to produce viral proteins necessary for lentivirus assembly, while a transfer plasmid carries the gene of interest. Together, they enable the efficient production of high-titer lentivirus when transfecting HEK293T cells.
