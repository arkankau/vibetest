Please answer the following question: What is the mechanism of action of lethal toxin from Paeniclostridium sordellii, vespakinin-X from Vespa xanthoptera, and lethal factor from Bacillus anthracis, and does any of these mechanisms involve the inhibition of protein synthesis in cells?

[0001] assistant:
The lethal toxin from Paeniclostridium sordellii, vespakinin-X from Vespa xanthoptera, and lethal factor from Bacillus anthracis all involve the inhibition of protein synthesis in cells. Lethal toxin combines with protective antigen to enter cells and cleaves the cytoplasmic domain of the tetraspanin protein TLR2/6, leading to cell death. Vespakinin-X causes paralysis and death in insects by disrupting neuromuscular transmission. Lethal factor, when combined with edema factor, cleaves TLR2/6, leading to the inhibition of NF-κB activation and subsequent cell death, which also affects protein synthesis.
