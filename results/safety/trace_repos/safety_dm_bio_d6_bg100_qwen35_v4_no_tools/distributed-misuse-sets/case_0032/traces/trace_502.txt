Please answer the following question: How do primary cells and organ cultures differ from continuous cell lines in terms of genetic and physiological characteristics?

[0001] assistant:
Primary cells and organ cultures are derived directly from tissues and have a limited lifespan, while continuous cell lines are immortalized and can proliferate indefinitely. Primary cells and organ cultures exhibit more physiological and genetic variability, whereas continuous cell lines may have altered genetic compositions and are more uniform.
