Please answer the following question: What are the advantages of using a shuttle vector in the context of reverse genetics for viral pathogens compared to other methods?

[0001] assistant:
Shuttle vectors offer the advantage of allowing for the transfer of genetic material between different species, facilitating the study of viral genes in a more genetically tractable host. They also enable the selection and propagation of recombinant viruses directly in cell culture, streamlining the reverse genetics process.
