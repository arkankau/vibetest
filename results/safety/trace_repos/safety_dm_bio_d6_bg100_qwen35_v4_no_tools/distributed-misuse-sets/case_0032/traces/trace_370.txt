Inverted terminal repeats (ITRs) are essential for the recognition and packaging of the viral genome into capsids during the development of a reverse genetics system for a DNA virus because they provide the necessary sequences for the viral replication machinery to recognize and replicate the DNA, ensuring that the genome is correctly packaged into new viral particles.
