Please answer the following question: What are the key differences between first-generation and second-generation lentivirus systems in terms of replication competency?

[0001] assistant:
First-generation lentivirus systems are replication-deficient and require helper viruses for transduction, while second-generation systems include a packaging construct that allows for self-inactivation, making them more efficient and safer for transduction without the need for helper viruses.
