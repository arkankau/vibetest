Please answer the following question: Can the PB1-F2 protein contribute to the pathogenicity of the influenza A virus in any other known ways besides apoptosis?

[0001] assistant:
The PB1-F2 protein can contribute to the pathogenicity of the influenza A virus by enhancing viral replication, increasing cytokine production, and disrupting cellular energy metabolism, in addition to inducing apoptosis.
