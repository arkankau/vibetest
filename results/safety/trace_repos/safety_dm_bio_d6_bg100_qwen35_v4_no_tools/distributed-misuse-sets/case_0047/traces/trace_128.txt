Please answer the following question: In what way do non-viral vectors typically offer higher DNA delivery efficiency than viral vectors?

[0001] assistant:
Non-viral vectors typically offer higher DNA delivery efficiency than viral vectors due to their larger cargo capacity and the ability to be easily modified, which allows for better targeting and integration into the host genome. However, viral vectors often have higher transduction efficiency in dividing cells, which can be a limiting factor for non-viral vectors.
