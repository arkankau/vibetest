Please answer the following question: How does the endosomal cysteine protease cathepsin L (CatL) contribute to the proteolysis of viral glycoproteins during the entry of the EBOV Makona strain?

[0001] assistant:
Cathepsin L (CatL) contributes to the proteolysis of viral glycoproteins during the entry of the EBOV Makona strain by cleaving these glycoproteins within endosomes, facilitating the release of the viral capsid into the host cell cytoplasm.
