Please answer the following question: What are the effects of a blood-borne virus able to remove ACE2 receptors on angiotensinogen levels and cardiovascular health in individuals with preexisting cardiovascular conditions?

[0001] assistant:
If a blood-borne virus removes ACE2 receptors, it could lead to increased angiotensinogen levels and impaired cardiovascular health, as the ACE2 receptors play a crucial role in regulating angiotensin II, which is important for blood pressure and cardiovascular homeostasis. Individuals with preexisting cardiovascular conditions might experience worsening symptoms due to unregulated angiotensin II.
