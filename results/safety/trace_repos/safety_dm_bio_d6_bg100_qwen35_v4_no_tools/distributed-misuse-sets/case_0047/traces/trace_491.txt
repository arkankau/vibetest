Please answer the following question: What are caliciviruses and what is their typical host and environment?

[0001] assistant:
Caliciviruses are a group of viruses that include noroviruses and hepatitis E virus. They typically infect the gastrointestinal tract and are commonly found in humans and other mammals, as well as in certain species of birds and mollusks. Their environment is often associated with contaminated food, water, and person-to-person contact in settings such as hospitals, cruise ships, and childcare facilities.
