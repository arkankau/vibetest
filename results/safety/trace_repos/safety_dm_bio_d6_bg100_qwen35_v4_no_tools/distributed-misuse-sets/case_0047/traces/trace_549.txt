Serine (Ser) in the active site of a cysteine protease acts as a nucleophile, attacking the peptide bond of the substrate. A mutation to alanine (Ala) might destabilize the catalytic mechanism, as Ala lacks the hydroxyl group necessary for nucleophilic attack, potentially rendering the enzyme inactive.
