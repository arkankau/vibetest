Please answer the following question: How does periplasmic expression facilitate correct protein folding and disulfide bond formation in the context of toxin production?

[0001] assistant:
Periplasmic expression allows proteins to fold correctly and form disulfide bonds within the bacterial periplasm, which has a reducing environment that promotes thiol-disulfide exchanges necessary for proper folding and maturation of secreted toxins.
