Please answer the following question: How is the TRIF protein involved in the signaling pathways for antiviral response?

[0001] assistant:
TRIF (Toll-like receptor 3-interacting protein) is involved in the signaling pathways for antiviral response by activating the IRF3 and NF-κB transcription factors upon sensing viral components, leading to the production of type I interferons and other pro-inflammatory cytokines.
