Please answer the following question: How might the upregulation of heparanase contribute to making the cell membrane more permeable to viral particles, and what evidence supports this hypothesis?

[0001] assistant:
Upregulation of heparanase can increase the availability of heparan sulfate on the cell surface, which acts as a receptor for many viruses. This can make the cell membrane more permeable to viral particles by facilitating their attachment and entry. Evidence supporting this hypothesis includes studies showing that heparanase inhibitors reduce viral infection in various cell types and animal models.
