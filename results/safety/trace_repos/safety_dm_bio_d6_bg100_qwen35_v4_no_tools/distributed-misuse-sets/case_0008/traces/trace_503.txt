Golden Gate assembly is a DNA assembly method that uses restriction enzymes and T4 DNA ligase to join multiple DNA fragments into a single molecule. It is more efficient and less complex than other methods like traditional restriction-ligation, as it can simultaneously assemble several DNA pieces in a single reaction, reducing the need for multiple cloning steps.
