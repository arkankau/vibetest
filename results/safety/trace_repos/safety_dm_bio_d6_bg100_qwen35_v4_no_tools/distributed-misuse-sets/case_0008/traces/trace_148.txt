Yes, specific measures are used to mitigate sampling bias in selecting dominant influenza strains for the vaccine. The World Health Organization (WHO) and other global health organizations gather data from surveillance systems worldwide to predict the strains. This collaborative approach helps ensure a more representative selection of strains likely to circulate in the upcoming season.
