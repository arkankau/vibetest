Please answer the following question: What are the known interactions between PB1 protein and other molecules within the influenza virus?

[0001] assistant:
The PB1 protein of the influenza virus interacts with PB1-F2 to regulate viral RNA synthesis and virulence. It also interacts with the nucleoprotein (NP) and other viral proteins to facilitate viral replication and transcription.
