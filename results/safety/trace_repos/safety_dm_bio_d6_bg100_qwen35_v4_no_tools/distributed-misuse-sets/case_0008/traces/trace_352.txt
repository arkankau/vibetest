Please answer the following question: Does RNR play a role in the integration of viral DNA into the host genome, and if not, what is the reason for this?

[0001] assistant:
RNR (ribonucleotide reductase) does not play a direct role in the integration of viral DNA into the host genome. The enzyme RNR is involved in the synthesis of deoxyribonucleotides, which are necessary for DNA replication. Viral integration, on the other hand, is typically mediated by viral enzymes like integrase in retroviruses.
