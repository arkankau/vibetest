Please answer the following question: What is the primary function of the STING protein in the context of viral infections, particularly in response to CDNs?

[0001] assistant:
The primary function of the STING protein in the context of viral infections is to detect cyclic GMP-AMP (cGAMP), a second messenger produced in response to cytosolic double-stranded DNAs (CDNs), and to initiate an intracellular signaling cascade that leads to the activation of interferon and inflammatory responses.
