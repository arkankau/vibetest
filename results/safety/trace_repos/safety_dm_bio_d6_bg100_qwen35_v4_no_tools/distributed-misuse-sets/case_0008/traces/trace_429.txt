Please answer the following question: In large-scale bacterial fermentation, how does phosphate-limited media influence the total biomass yield and the resulting levels of nucleic acids and phosphorous compounds released into the waste stream?

[0001] assistant:
In phosphate-limited media, the total biomass yield is typically reduced due to the essential role of phosphorus in nucleic acid synthesis. This limitation leads to lower levels of nucleic acids and phosphorous compounds in the biomass. Consequently, the levels of these compounds released into the waste stream are also lower.
