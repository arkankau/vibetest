Please answer the following question: In what way does the viral nucleoprotein (N) serve as a scaffold for viral RNA synthesis during the recovery of infectious virus from cDNA in segmented negative-strand RNA viruses?

[0001] assistant:
The viral nucleoprotein (N) serves as a scaffold by binding to the viral RNA segments, facilitating their organization and protection during replication. This organization is crucial for the synthesis of new viral RNA molecules from cDNA intermediates.
