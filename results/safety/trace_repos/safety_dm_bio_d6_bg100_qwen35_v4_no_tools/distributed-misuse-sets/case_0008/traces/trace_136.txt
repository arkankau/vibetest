Please answer the following question: What is the primary function of the human 2,6-sialyltransferase enzyme and how does it affect the sialic acid composition of cells?

[0001] assistant:
The primary function of the human 2,6-sialyltransferase enzyme is to transfer sialic acid to the alpha-2,6-linked sialyl acceptors, primarily on glycoproteins and glycolipids. This affects the sialic acid composition by increasing the levels of alpha-2,6-linked sialic acid, which is crucial for various cellular processes including immune evasion and viral binding.
