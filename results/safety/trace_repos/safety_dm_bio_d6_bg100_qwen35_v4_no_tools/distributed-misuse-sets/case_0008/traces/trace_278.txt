Please answer the following question: What are the sources and symptoms of saxitoxin poisoning, and can it cause slurred speech without involving alcohol?

[0001] assistant:
Saxitoxin poisoning typically comes from consuming contaminated shellfish that have eaten toxic algae. Symptoms include gastrointestinal distress, numbness starting from the lips, and can progress to paralysis of the respiratory system. Slurred speech can occur as a symptom, but it is not directly linked to alcohol consumption.
