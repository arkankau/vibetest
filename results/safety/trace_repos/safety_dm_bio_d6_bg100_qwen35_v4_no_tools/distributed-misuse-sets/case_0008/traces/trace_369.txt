Please answer the following question: What is CD46 and how does it function in cellular processes, particularly in relation to viral infections?

[0001] assistant:
CD46 is a cell surface protein that functions as a receptor and cofactor for several viruses, including some strains of human papillomavirus (HPV) and cytomegalovirus (CMV). It primarily acts as a decoy receptor, helping to limit viral infections by facilitating viral entry into cells without leading to productive infection, thus providing a protective function for the host.
