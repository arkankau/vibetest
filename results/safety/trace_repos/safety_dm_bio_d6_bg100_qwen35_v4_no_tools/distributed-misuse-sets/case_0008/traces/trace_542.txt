Please answer the following question: Does Interleukin-10 (IL-10) play a significant role in immune control during KSHV infection and is it produced by CD4+ T cells?

[0001] assistant:
Interleukin-10 (IL-10) plays a significant role in immune control during KSHV infection by modulating immune responses. However, IL-10 is primarily produced by CD14+ monocytes/macrophages, regulatory T cells, and B cells, rather than CD4+ T cells.
