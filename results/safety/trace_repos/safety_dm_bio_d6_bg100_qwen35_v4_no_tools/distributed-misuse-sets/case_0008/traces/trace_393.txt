Please answer the following question: How do the growth conditions of Clostridium compare to Bacillus, and what test can be used to differentiate between the two?

[0001] assistant:
Clostridium and Bacillus are both spore-forming bacteria, but Clostridium typically grows in anaerobic conditions, while Bacillus can grow in both aerobic and anaerobic environments. The芽孢染色试验（spore staining test）可以用来区分这两种细菌，因为只有芽孢杆菌会产生紫色的孢子，而梭菌的孢子不会着色。
