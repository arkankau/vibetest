Please answer the following question: What is the role of the acpB gene in the virulence of Bacillus anthracis and how does it contribute to the pathogenicity of the organism?

[0001] assistant:
The acpB gene in Bacillus anthracis is involved in fatty acid synthesis. It contributes to the pathogenicity of the organism by aiding in the production of mycolic acids, which are essential for the bacterial cell wall and help the bacteria evade the host immune system.
