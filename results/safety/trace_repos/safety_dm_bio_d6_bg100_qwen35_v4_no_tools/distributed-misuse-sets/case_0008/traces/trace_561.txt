Please answer the following question: What is the role of RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (RdRp) in the synthesis of viral RNA from the segmented negative-sense RNA genome in the context of influenza A virus replication?

[0001] assistant:
RNA-dependent RNA polymerase (RdRp) plays a crucial role in synthesizing complementary positive-sense RNA from the negative-sense RNA genome segments during influenza A virus replication, which then serves as a template for viral protein synthesis and for producing new viral RNA segments.
