Nonspecific symptoms of melioidosis, such as fever, fatigue, and malaise, can be particularly problematic for diagnosis and treatment because they are similar to those of many other infectious diseases, leading to delayed or incorrect diagnosis. These symptoms differ from those of other infectious diseases like tuberculosis or malaria, which often have more characteristic symptoms like chronic cough or specific rashes, making differential diagnosis more challenging.
