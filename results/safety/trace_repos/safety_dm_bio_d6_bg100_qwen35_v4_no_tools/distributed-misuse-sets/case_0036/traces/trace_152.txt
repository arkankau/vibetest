Please answer the following question: Does the viral immediate-early protein ICP47 inhibit the transporter associated with antigen processing (TAP)?

[0001] assistant:
No, ICP47 from herpes simplex virus (HSV) inhibits the major histocompatibility complex (MHC) class I pathway by degrading the TAP proteins indirectly through the proteasome, not directly by inhibiting the TAP transporter itself.
