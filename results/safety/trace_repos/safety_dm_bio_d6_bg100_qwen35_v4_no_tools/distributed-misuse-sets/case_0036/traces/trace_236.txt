Caliciviruses can be cultivated using both cell cultures and helper virus-free reverse genetics systems. Cultivation in cell cultures typically requires specific cell lines, while reverse genetics systems allow for the manipulation of viral RNA and can be used to rescue infectious virus without a helper virus, though these systems are more complex and require specialized equipment and expertise.
