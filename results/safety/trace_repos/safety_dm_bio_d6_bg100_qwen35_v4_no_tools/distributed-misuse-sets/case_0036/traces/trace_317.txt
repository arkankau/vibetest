Please answer the following question: Can measles virus spread to other organs and tissues by directly infecting organ-specific cells through airborne transmission, and if so, is this the primary mechanism?

[0001] assistant:
Measles virus can spread to other organs and tissues by infecting organ-specific cells through airborne transmission. However, this is not the primary mechanism; the primary route of infection is via the respiratory system, with subsequent viremia allowing spread to other organs.
