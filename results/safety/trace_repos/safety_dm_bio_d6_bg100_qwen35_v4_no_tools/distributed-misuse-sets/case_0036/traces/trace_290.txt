Please answer the following question: What historical precedents can be found in the past where avian influenza viruses have acquired mammalian transmissibility, and what were the outcomes?

[0001] assistant:
The H1N1 virus that caused the 2009 influenza pandemic is a notable historical precedent where an avian influenza virus acquired the ability to transmit among humans. Another example is the H7N9 virus, which emerged in China in 2013 and has shown some capability of human-to-human transmission, though limited. In both cases, the outcomes included significant public health responses and efforts to control the spread of the virus.
