Please answer the following question: What is the standard protocol for adding protease inhibitors to prevent protein digestion in protein purification from cell cultures?

[0001] assistant:
Protease inhibitors are typically added to cell cultures before harvesting to prevent protein digestion. Common inhibitors include aprotinin, leupeptin, and pepstatin, which are usually added to the culture medium or the lysis buffer during the purification process.
