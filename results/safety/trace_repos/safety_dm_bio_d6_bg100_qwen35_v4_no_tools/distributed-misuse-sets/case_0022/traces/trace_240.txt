The immunomodulatory capacity of oncolytic viruses (OVs) can lead to variable patient responses because different individuals may mount diverse immune reactions. This variability complicates the characterization of the immune checkpoint inhibitors (ICIs) and overall immune response dynamics in clinical settings.
