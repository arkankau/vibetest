Please answer the following question: What is the function of the NF-κB pathway in the host antiviral response and how might ICP34.5 activate it to counteract host defenses?

[0001] assistant:
The NF-κB pathway is crucial for the host antiviral response, as it promotes the production of antiviral cytokines and type I interferons. ICP34.5, a protein encoded by some herpesviruses, can activate the NF-κB pathway to counteract host defenses by inhibiting the host's antiviral response and promoting viral replication.
