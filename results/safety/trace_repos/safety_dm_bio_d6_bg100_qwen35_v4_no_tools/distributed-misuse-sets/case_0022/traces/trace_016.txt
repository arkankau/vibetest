Please answer the following question: What are the mechanisms by which cellular proteases can degrade the lambda Red recombinase enzymes, and how does the controlled expression of these genes prevent such degradation?

[0001] assistant:
Cellular proteases can degrade lambda Red recombinase enzymes through proteolytic pathways, likely by targeting these enzymes for degradation via the ubiquitin-proteasome system or autophagy. Controlled expression of protease inhibitor genes or the lambda Red recombinase itself can prevent such degradation by maintaining stable enzyme levels or protecting the enzymes from proteolytic cleavage.
