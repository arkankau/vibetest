Please answer the following question: What is reverse genetics and how does it involve the use of cDNA clones?

[0001] assistant:
Reverse genetics involves using known sequences to generate a functional copy of a gene, which is then introduced into a host organism to study its effects. It involves cDNA clones by first synthesizing a DNA fragment complementary to a known RNA sequence, allowing for the introduction and expression of the gene in a new context to analyze its function.
