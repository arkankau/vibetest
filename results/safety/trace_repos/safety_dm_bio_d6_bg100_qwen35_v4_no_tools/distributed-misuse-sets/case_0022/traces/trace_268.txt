Please answer the following question: What are the oxygen levels at the top and bottom of Thioglycolate broth, and how do they affect cell and bacterial survival?

[0001] assistant:
Oxygen levels at the top of a Thioglycolate broth are higher, while they decrease towards the bottom. Higher oxygen levels support aerobic respiration, whereas the lower, anaerobic conditions in the bottom favor anaerobic bacteria. This gradient allows for the cultivation of both aerobic and anaerobic microorganisms.
