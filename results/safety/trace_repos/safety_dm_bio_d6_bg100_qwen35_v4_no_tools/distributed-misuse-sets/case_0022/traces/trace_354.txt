Please answer the following question: What are synonymous mutations and how do they affect the viral gene without changing the amino acid sequence of the viral protein?

[0001] assistant:
Synonymous mutations, also known as "silent mutations," are changes in the DNA sequence that do not alter the amino acid sequence of the viral protein because they result in the same codon being used, which codes for the same amino acid. These mutations affect the gene by changing the DNA sequence without impacting the protein's function or the viral infection process.
