Please answer the following question: How does anthrax typically manifest in a patient and what are its primary symptoms?

[0001] assistant:
Anthrax typically manifests in three main forms: cutaneous, inhalation, and gastrointestinal. The primary symptoms are skin ulcers and swelling at the site of infection in cutaneous anthrax; fever, respiratory distress, and low blood pressure in inhalation anthrax; and nausea, vomiting, and abdominal pain in gastrointestinal anthrax.
