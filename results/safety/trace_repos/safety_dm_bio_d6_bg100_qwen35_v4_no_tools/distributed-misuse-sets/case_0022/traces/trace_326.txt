Infecting multiple cell types helps a virus evade the host's immune system by spreading throughout the body in various niches, making it harder for the immune system to target and eliminate all infected cells. This diversification reduces the likelihood that the immune system can effectively clear the infection, allowing the virus to persist chronically.
