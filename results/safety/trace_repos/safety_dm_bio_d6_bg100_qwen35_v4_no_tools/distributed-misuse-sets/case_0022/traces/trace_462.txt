Please answer the following question: Does gB (glycoprotein B) have any known functions related to immune evasion or MHC class I downregulation?

[0001] assistant:
gB (glycoprotein B) is known to be involved in immune evasion by facilitating the entry of herpesviruses into cells, which can indirectly aid in evading the immune response. It also plays a role in MHC class I downregulation by triggering endocytosis of MHC class I molecules, thus reducing their presence on the cell surface and helping the virus evade immune detection.
