Please answer the following question: Can reassortment be controlled or manipulated in reverse genetics studies on influenza A viruses, and if so, how?

[0001] assistant:
Reassortment in influenza A viruses can be controlled or manipulated in reverse genetics studies by co-transfecting cell cultures with plasmids containing different viral RNA segments. This allows researchers to study specific combinations of viral genes and their effects on virus behavior.
