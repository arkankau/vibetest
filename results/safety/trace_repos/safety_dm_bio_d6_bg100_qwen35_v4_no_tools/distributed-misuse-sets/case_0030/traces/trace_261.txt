Please answer the following question: Does VZV establish latency in sensory ganglion neurons after primary infection, distinguishing it from other herpesviruses?

[0001] assistant:
Yes, VZV (Varicella-Zoster Virus) establishes latency in sensory ganglion neurons after primary infection, distinguishing it from some other herpesviruses like HSV-1 and HSV-2, which also establish latency but primarily in trigeminal and sacral ganglia, respectively.
