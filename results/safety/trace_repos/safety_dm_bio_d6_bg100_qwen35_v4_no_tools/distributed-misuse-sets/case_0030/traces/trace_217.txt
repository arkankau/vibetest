Please answer the following question: What is the Psi packaging signal and how is it used in the construction of third-generation adenovirus vectors?

[0001] assistant:
The Psi packaging signal is a specific RNA sequence in adenovirus DNA that is essential for the assembly of viral particles. In the construction of third-generation adenovirus vectors, this signal is used to ensure that only the desired genetic material is packaged into the viral vector, enhancing the specificity and efficiency of gene delivery.
