Please answer the following question: How stable are enterovirus/rhinovirus in the environment, and what factors contribute to their ability to persist outside the host?

[0001] assistant:
Enterovirus and rhinovirus are generally stable in the environment for several days to weeks, depending on factors such as temperature, humidity, and surface type. Higher humidity and cooler temperatures tend to enhance their stability. Factors like pH, UV light, and chemical disinfectants can also significantly affect their persistence outside the host.
