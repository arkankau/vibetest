Please answer the following question: What specific mechanisms do B cells use to produce antibodies against Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) and what role do these antibodies play in controlling virus replication?

[0001] assistant:
B cells produce antibodies against EBV by first recognizing Epstein-Barr virus antigens presented on antigen-presenting cells via B cell receptors. These antibodies then neutralize the virus, prevent it from infecting new cells, and mark it for destruction by other immune cells, thereby playing a crucial role in controlling virus replication.
