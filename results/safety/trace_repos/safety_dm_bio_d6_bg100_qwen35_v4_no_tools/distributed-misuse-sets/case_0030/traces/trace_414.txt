Please answer the following question: Which of the eight influenza A virus genome segments are most commonly included in the primer-probe sets of commercially available RT-PCR diagnostic kits used in hospital labs?

[0001] assistant:
The eight influenza A virus genome segments are not all equally targeted in RT-PCR diagnostic kits. Typically, primers and probes target the matrix (M) gene and the nucleoprotein (NP) gene, which are more consistently expressed across different influenza A subtypes.
