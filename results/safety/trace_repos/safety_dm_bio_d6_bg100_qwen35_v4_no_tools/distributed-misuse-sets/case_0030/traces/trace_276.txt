Please answer the following question: What is the mechanism by which the VP16 protein activates viral gene expression in the host cell?

[0001] assistant:
The VP16 protein activates viral gene expression by enhancing the binding of transcription factors to the promoter region of viral genes, thereby increasing transcription. It does this by interacting with host cell transcriptional machinery components like CBP/p300, recruiting them to the viral promoter.
