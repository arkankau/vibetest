Please answer the following question: Explain the role of the F protein in the measles virus and how its ablation or overexpression might affect the virus's ability to replicate in interferon-deficient cells.

[0001] assistant:
The F protein, also known as fusion protein, mediates the fusion of the viral envelope with the host cell membrane, facilitating entry of the virus into the cell. Ablation or overexpression of the F protein can affect the virus's ability to replicate in interferon-deficient cells, as it plays a crucial role in viral entry, which is independent of interferon.
