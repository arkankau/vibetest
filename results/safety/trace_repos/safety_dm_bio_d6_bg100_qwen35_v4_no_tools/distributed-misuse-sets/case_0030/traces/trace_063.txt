Please answer the following question: In the context of the H1N1 Spanish influenza of 1918, how does the reassortment of RNA genomes between avian and human influenza viruses contribute to the generation of new influenza strains with pandemic potential?

[0001] assistant:
Reassortment of RNA genomes between avian and human influenza viruses allows for the exchange of genetic material, creating new viral strains that can infect humans and potentially spread rapidly. This process can introduce new surface proteins that human immune systems have not encountered before, facilitating the emergence of highly infectious and deadly strains with pandemic potential.
