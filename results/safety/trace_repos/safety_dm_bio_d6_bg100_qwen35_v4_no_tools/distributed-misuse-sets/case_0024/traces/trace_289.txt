Cellular gene expression analysis techniques such as RNA sequencing (RNA-Seq) and quantitative PCR (qPCR) can be employed to study the IFN-mediated antiviral response in human epithelial lung cells infected with the new strain and its nearest relative. Comparing the gene expression profiles can provide insights into differences in the virulence effects of the two strains by identifying upregulated or downregulated genes involved in the immune response.
