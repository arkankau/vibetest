Please answer the following question: How does the EBV BamHI C promoter (CP) function in the context of EBV-infected cells and is it commonly used in gene therapy?

[0001] assistant:
The EBV BamHI C promoter (CP) drives the expression of early and late EBV genes in infected cells. It is commonly used in gene therapy vectors due to its strong and reliable transcriptional activity.
