Please answer the following question: How does the acpA gene function and what role does it play in the virulence of Bacillus anthracis?

[0001] assistant:
The acpA gene in Bacillus anthracis encodes an acyl carrier protein, which is crucial for fatty acid biosynthesis. This gene contributes to the bacterium's virulence by enabling the production of essential lipids necessary for the formation of the protective capsule and the development of toxin complexes.
