Please answer the following question: Can shellfish accumulate paralytic shellfish poisons from their diet, and if so, which organisms in their food chain are responsible for producing these toxins?

[0001] assistant:
Yes, shellfish can accumulate paralytic shellfish poisons from their diet. These toxins are primarily produced by certain species of marine algae, particularly dinoflagellates like Alexandrium and Saxitoxin-producing species.
