Please answer the following question: What role does including an antibiotic resistance gene play in the selection and maintenance of the vector in mammalian cells during gene therapy?

[0001] assistant:
Including an antibiotic resistance gene in the vector allows for the selection of cells that have been successfully transfected with the vector, as these cells can survive in the presence of the antibiotic. This facilitates the maintenance and propagation of the vector in mammalian cells.
