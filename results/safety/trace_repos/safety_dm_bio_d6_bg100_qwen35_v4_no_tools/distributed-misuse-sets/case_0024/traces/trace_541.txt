Please answer the following question: How does the N protein stabilize the viral genome, and what are the implications for viral assembly and infectivity?

[0001] assistant:
The N protein, or nucleoprotein, binds to the viral RNA genome, stabilizing it by wrapping around the RNA to form a helical structure. This stabilization is crucial for viral assembly as it helps package the RNA into new virions. In terms of infectivity, the N protein's protective role ensures the integrity of the genome during replication and transmission, thereby enhancing viral infectivity.
