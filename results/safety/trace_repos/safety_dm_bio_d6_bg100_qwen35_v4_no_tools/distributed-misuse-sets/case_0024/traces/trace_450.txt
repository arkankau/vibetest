Please answer the following question: Describe the ligation of DNA fragments to form plasmids and its relevance in the reverse genetics technique for positive-stranded RNA viruses.

[0001] assistant:
In the ligation of DNA fragments for plasmids, cut DNA fragments are inserted into a plasmid vector and joined using DNA ligase to create recombinant plasmids. This technique is crucial in reverse genetics for positive-stranded RNA viruses as it allows the creation of cDNA copies of viral genomes, enabling the study of gene function and virus-host interactions.
