Please answer the following question: How can changes in the viral haemagglutinin (HA) protein enhance the transmission of influenza viruses, making it a critical factor in Gain of Function studies?

[0001] assistant:
Changes in the viral haemagglutinin (HA) protein can enhance the transmission of influenza viruses by altering their ability to bind to and enter host cells more efficiently, potentially increasing their infectivity and transmissibility. This makes HA a critical factor in Gain of Function studies, which aim to understand how viruses might evolve to become more dangerous.
