Please answer the following question: How do Macrophages recognize and process Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) infected cells for presentation to T cells?

[0001] assistant:
Macrophages recognize EBV-infected cells through pattern recognition receptors that detect viral components. They then phagocytose the infected cells, process viral antigens, and present them on MHC molecules to T cells, activating an immune response.
