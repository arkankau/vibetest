Please answer the following question: What is the primary function of the CMV immediate early promoter and where is it commonly used in gene therapy?

[0001] assistant:
The primary function of the CMV immediate early promoter is to drive the initial and high-level expression of genes it controls, making it very effective for rapid gene expression. It is commonly used in gene therapy to ensure early and robust expression of therapeutic genes in various cell types.
