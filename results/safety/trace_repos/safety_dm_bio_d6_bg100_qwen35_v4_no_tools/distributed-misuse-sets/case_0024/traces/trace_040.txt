Please answer the following question: What advantages do in silico modeling and computational simulations have over other experimental assays like live animal imaging, and in what scenarios might they be preferred?

[0001] assistant:
In silico modeling and computational simulations allow for rapid, cost-effective, and ethically neutral testing of hypotheses, and can explore a larger parameter space than live animal imaging. They are particularly preferred in early drug discovery, toxicity testing, and understanding complex biological systems where ethical concerns or practical limitations make live animal imaging impractical.
