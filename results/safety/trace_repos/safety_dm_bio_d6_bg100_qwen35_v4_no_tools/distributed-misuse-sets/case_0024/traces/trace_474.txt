Please answer the following question: How does persistent NLRP3 inflammasome activation contribute to the development of a cytokine storm during COVID-19?

[0001] assistant:
Persistent NLRP3 inflammasome activation during COVID-19 leads to the excessive production of cytokines like IL-1β and IL-18, which can trigger a cytokine storm. This storm further exacerbates inflammation and can cause multi-organ failure.
