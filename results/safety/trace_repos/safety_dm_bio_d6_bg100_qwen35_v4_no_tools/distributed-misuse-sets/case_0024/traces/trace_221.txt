Please answer the following question: What is the role of the cya gene in the virulence mechanism of Bacillus anthracis and how does it function?

[0001] assistant:
The cya gene in Bacillus anthracis encodes adenylate cyclase, a toxin that increases intracellular cyclic AMP levels, leading to cellular changes that contribute to virulence. This enzyme functions by catalyzing the production of cyclic AMP from ATP, which disrupts normal cellular processes and facilitates bacterial infection.
