Please answer the following question: What is the origin and genetic background of CHO cells, and do they have any inherent advantages in supporting adenoviral replication and packaging for vaccine production?

[0001] assistant:
CHO cells (Chinese Hamster Ovary cells) originate from a Chinese hamster ovary and were first isolated in the 1950s. They are widely used in biopharmaceuticals due to their ability to secrete high-quality proteins. CHO cells do not inherently support adenoviral replication and packaging; however, they can be engineered to produce adenoviral vectors for vaccine production by introducing necessary genes.
