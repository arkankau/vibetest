Please answer the following question: Can HDACi cause an increase in the expression of pro-apoptotic genes in EBV-associated malignancies?

[0001] assistant:
HDACi (histone deacetylase inhibitors) can indeed cause an increase in the expression of pro-apoptotic genes in EBV-associated malignancies by reactivating latent viral genes and inducing cellular stress responses that lead to apoptosis.
