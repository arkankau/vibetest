Please answer the following question: How does the requirement for DNA intermediates in the replication cycle of negative-strand RNA viruses impact their rescue from cloned DNA?

[0001] assistant:
Negative-strand RNA viruses require DNA intermediates during their replication cycle, which is why they need to be rescued from cloned DNA. This process often involves reverse transcription, converting the viral RNA into DNA, which can then be integrated into the host genome or used to produce new viral RNA and proteins.
