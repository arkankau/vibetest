Please answer the following question: What are the properties of the surface protein Lpp-OmpA and how well does it facilitate the display of a protein on the outer surface of B. anthracis spores?

[0001] assistant:
Lpp-OmpA is a surface protein in Bacillus anthracis that facilitates the display of heterologous proteins on the bacterial surface. It is known to be effective for this purpose, allowing for the successful expression and presentation of foreign proteins on the outer surface of B. anthracis spores.
