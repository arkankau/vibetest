The immune responses generated against the membrane (M), envelope (E), and nucleocapsid (N) proteins of SARS-CoV-2 differ in their ability to neutralize the virus. Neutralizing antibodies against the spike protein's receptor-binding domain are most effective, but some responses to the M and N proteins can also contribute to viral neutralization. However, the N protein, being highly conserved, may generate more broadly protective antibodies, while antibodies against the M and spike proteins may be more specific to the virus.
