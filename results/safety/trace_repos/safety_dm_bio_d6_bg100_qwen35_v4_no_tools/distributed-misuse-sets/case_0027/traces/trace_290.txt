Please answer the following question: Describe the role of ribosomal RNAs (rRNAs) in protein synthesis and their relevance to viral latency in herpesvirus research.

[0001] assistant:
Ribosomal RNAs (rRNAs) form the structural and catalytic core of ribosomes, which are responsible for decoding mRNA to synthesize proteins. In the context of herpesvirus research, rRNAs are less directly involved in viral latency but are crucial for the host cell's protein synthesis machinery, which herpesviruses often hijack during latent infection.
