Please answer the following question: Is the mechanism by which Botulism and Nerve Gases cause respiratory paralysis the same?

[0001] assistant:
I don't know. While both botulism and nerve gases can cause respiratory paralysis, they do so through different mechanisms. Botulism toxin inhibits neurotransmitter release at the neuromuscular junction, whereas nerve gases typically cause direct toxicity to nerve cells.
