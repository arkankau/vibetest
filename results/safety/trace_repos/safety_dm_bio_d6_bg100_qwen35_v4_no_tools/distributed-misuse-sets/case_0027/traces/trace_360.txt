Please answer the following question: What safety protocols should be followed when working with highly pathogenic influenza viruses like the 1918 strain?

[0001] assistant:
When working with highly pathogenic influenza viruses like the 1918 strain, strict biosafety level 4 (BSL-4) protocols should be followed, including the use of full-body protective suits, negative pressure laboratories, and rigorous decontamination procedures.
