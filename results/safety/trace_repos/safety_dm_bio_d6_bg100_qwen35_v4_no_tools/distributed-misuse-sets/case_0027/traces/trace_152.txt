Please answer the following question: How does polymerase cycling assembly and yeast homologous recombination aid in the synthesis and assembly of large viral genomes?

[0001] assistant:
Polymerase cycling assembly (PCA) and yeast homologous recombination are both powerful techniques used to synthesize and assemble large viral genomes. PCA allows for the stepwise construction of DNA fragments, which can then be assembled into larger constructs, while yeast homologous recombination enables the precise integration of DNA sequences into yeast vectors, facilitating the generation of complex viral genomes.
