Please answer the following question: Are there any recognized associations between the cytochrome P450 (CYP) 3A family and immune responses in general?

[0001] assistant:
Cytochrome P450 3A (CYP3A) enzymes can influence drug metabolism, and certain drugs that interact with CYP3A can affect immune responses by altering pharmacokinetics or signaling pathways. However, there is no direct evidence of CYP3A enzymes themselves having a recognized role in immune responses.
