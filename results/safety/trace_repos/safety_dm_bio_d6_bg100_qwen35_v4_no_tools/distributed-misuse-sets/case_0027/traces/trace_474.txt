Please answer the following question: Can DNA-launched infectious systems directly manipulate the viral RNA genome, and if so, how does this compare to RNA-launched systems?

[0001] assistant:
DNA-launched infectious systems can directly integrate into and manipulate the host genome, including the viral RNA genome, through mechanisms like gene editing. In contrast, RNA-launched systems, such as RNA viruses, typically replicate via RNA intermediates and do not directly integrate into the host genome, though they can affect gene expression and RNA metabolism.
