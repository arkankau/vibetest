The selection of strains for the annual influenza vaccine is based on predictions of the dominant virus strains expected to circulate during the upcoming season, made by the World Health Organization and other global health bodies. These predictions aim to ensure the vaccine provides the best possible protection against the most prevalent influenza strains.
