Please answer the following question: In what scenarios might the use of live attenuated VZV vaccines be contraindicated due to the risk of reversion to a virulent form?

[0001] assistant:
Live attenuated VZV vaccines should not be used in immunocompromised individuals, pregnant women, or those with active tuberculosis, as there is a risk of the virus reverting to a virulent form.
