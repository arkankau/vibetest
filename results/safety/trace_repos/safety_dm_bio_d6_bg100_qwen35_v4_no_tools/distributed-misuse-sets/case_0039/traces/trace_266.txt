Please answer the following question: Is the BAC system used in herpesvirus research to enable the expression of viral proteins for structural analysis in bacterial systems?

[0001] assistant:
The BAC system is not typically used for expressing viral proteins in bacterial systems for structural analysis in herpesvirus research. Instead, BAC (Bacterial Artificial Chromosome) systems are used to maintain and manipulate large segments of viral DNA in bacteria, often for the purpose of producing infectious viral particles or for genetic analysis, not specifically for protein expression in bacteria.
