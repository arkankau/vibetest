Please answer the following question: What is the evidence linking SARS-CoV2 to bats as a possible host?

[0001] assistant:
SARS-CoV-2 shares a high genetic similarity with bat coronaviruses, and intermediate hosts like pangolins have also been identified, suggesting bats could be the original reservoir. However, the exact transmission path from bats to humans is not fully understood.
