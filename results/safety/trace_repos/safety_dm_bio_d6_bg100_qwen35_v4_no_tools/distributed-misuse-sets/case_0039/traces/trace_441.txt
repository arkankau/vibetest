Saxitoxin poisoning typically causes symptoms within 30 minutes to 3 hours after ingestion, primarily affecting the gastrointestinal and neuromuscular systems. Alcohol poisoning symptoms usually develop more gradually, often taking several hours after heavy alcohol consumption, and can include vomiting, confusion, and respiratory depression.
