Please answer the following question: What is the role of the latency-associated transcript (LAT) in preventing apoptosis of infected neurons in Herpes Simplex Virus (HSV)?

[0001] assistant:
The latency-associated transcript (LAT) in Herpes Simplex Virus (HSV) plays a crucial role in preventing apoptosis of infected neurons by producing viral microRNAs that modulate gene expression and protect neurons from viral-induced cell death.
