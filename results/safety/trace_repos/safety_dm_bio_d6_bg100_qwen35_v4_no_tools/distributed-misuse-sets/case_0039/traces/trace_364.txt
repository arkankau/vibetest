Please answer the following question: How does the phosphorylation of nucleoside analogs by thymidine kinase (TK) affect the viral DNA polymerase activity in herpes simplex virus (HSV)?

Phosphorylation of nucleoside analogs by thymidine kinase (TK) in herpes simplex virus (HSV) leads to the formation of triphosphate prodrugs, which are then incorporated into the viral DNA, thereby inhibiting viral DNA polymerase activity and hindering viral replication.
