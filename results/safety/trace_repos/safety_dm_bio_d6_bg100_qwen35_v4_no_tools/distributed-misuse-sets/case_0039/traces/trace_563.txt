Please answer the following question: Is the T3SS1 gene cluster in Vibrio parahaemolyticus linked with virulent strains that cause acute gastroenteritis, and if so, what specific properties does it have?

[0001] assistant:
The T3SS1 gene cluster in Vibrio parahaemolyticus is indeed linked with virulent strains that cause acute gastroenteritis. This cluster encodes for the type III secretion system, which is crucial for injecting effector proteins into host cells, thus facilitating bacterial invasion and pathogenesis.
