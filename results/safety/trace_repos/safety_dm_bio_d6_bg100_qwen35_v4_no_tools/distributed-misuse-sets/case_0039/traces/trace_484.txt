Please answer the following question: Determine the role of 5' and 3' terminal sequences in preventing the host cell's antiviral response, and how their maintenance is essential for successful reverse genetics experiments.

[0001] assistant:
The 5' and 3' terminal sequences of viral RNA genomes often contain elements that prevent the host cell's antiviral response, such as the interferon system, by inhibiting RNA interference or by promoting RNA stability. Maintaining these sequences is crucial for reverse genetics experiments as they ensure the viral RNA is recognized and replicated by the host cell machinery, allowing for the generation and propagation of infectious viral particles.
