Please answer the following question: What is the incubation period for Marburg virus (Marburg marburgvirus), and does it allow for significant person-to-person spread?

[0001] assistant:
The incubation period for Marburg virus is typically 5 to 10 days, after which symptoms may develop. Yes, it does allow for significant person-to-person spread, primarily through direct contact with the blood, secretions, organs, or other bodily fluids of infected people, as well as indirect contact with environments contaminated with these fluids.
