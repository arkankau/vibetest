The 5' and 3' terminal sequences are crucial for reverse genetics in negative-sense RNA viruses as they provide essential signals for RNA synthesis and genome replication. These sequences ensure that the synthetic RNA can be correctly transcribed and translated in host cells, facilitating virus reconstitution from cloned cDNA.
