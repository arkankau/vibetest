Please answer the following question: How does iatrogenic botulism typically occur, and is it related to the botulism neurotoxin (Botox) used in medical treatments?

[0001] assistant:
Iatrogenic botulism typically occurs when a patient receives a contaminated dose of a botulinum toxin, usually through contaminated medical equipment or solutions. It is not related to Botox, which is a purified form of botulinum toxin type A used in medical treatments for specific therapeutic and cosmetic purposes and is not harmful when used correctly.
