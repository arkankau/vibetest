Please answer the following question: Does rolling circle amplification have the capability to synthesize viral RNA genomes, and if so, what are its typical applications?

[0001] assistant:
Rolling circle amplification (RCA) is not typically used for synthesizing viral RNA genomes. RCA is a technique used to amplify circular DNA molecules. Its applications include DNA sequencing, genotyping, and the creation of DNA probes. Synthesizing viral RNA genomes is more commonly achieved through reverse transcription or RNA synthesis techniques.
