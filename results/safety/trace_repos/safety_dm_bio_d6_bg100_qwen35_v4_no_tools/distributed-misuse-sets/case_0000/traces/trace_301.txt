Please answer the following question: How does the presence of flanking loxP sites in herpesviruses allow for targeted genetic modifications using Cre recombinase?

[0001] assistant:
The presence of flanking loxP sites in herpesviruses allows for targeted genetic modifications using Cre recombinase because Cre can recognize and recombine these sites, effectively removing or rearranging the DNA sequence between them. This process enables precise genetic engineering within the herpesvirus genome or in cells infected by the virus.
