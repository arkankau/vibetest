Please answer the following question: What environmental factors are Bacillus cereus spores known to be resistant to, and how do these compare to the resistances of anthracis spores?

[0001] assistant:
Bacillus cereus spores are resistant to heat, desiccation, and some disinfectants. They compare to anthrax spores (Bacillus anthracis) in that both are highly resistant to environmental stressors, but anthrax spores are generally considered more resistant to extreme heat and chemical disinfectants.
