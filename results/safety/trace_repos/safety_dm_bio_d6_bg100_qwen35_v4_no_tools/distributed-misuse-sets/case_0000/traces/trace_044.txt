Please answer the following question: What is the relationship between syncytium and the lysis of infected cells releasing viral progeny in herpesvirus infections?

[0001] assistant:
In herpesvirus infections, syncytium formation is often a result of viral infection, where multiple cells fuse together. This process can lead to the lysis of the infected syncytial cell, releasing viral progeny to infect neighboring cells.
