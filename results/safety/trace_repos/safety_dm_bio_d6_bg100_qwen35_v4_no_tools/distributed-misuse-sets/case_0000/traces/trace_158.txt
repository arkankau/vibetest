Please answer the following question: Describe the formation of a circular episome that is maintained in the nucleus as a means of establishing herpesvirus latency in host cells.

[0001] assistant:
Herpesviruses establish latency by integrating their DNA into the host cell's genome as a circular episome, which is maintained in the nuclear environment without undergoing replication or transcription. This allows the virus to persist in a dormant state, evading the immune system and potentially reactivating later to replicate and spread.
