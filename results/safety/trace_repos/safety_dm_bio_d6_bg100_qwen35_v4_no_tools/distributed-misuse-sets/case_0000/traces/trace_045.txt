Please answer the following question: What is the importance of having a plasmid encoding the viral RNA-dependent RNA polymerase for the recovery of negative-sense RNA viruses during reverse genetics studies?

[0001] assistant:
Having a plasmid encoding the viral RNA-dependent RNA polymerase is crucial for the recovery of negative-sense RNA viruses during reverse genetics studies because it allows for the in vitro transcription of viral RNA from cloned cDNA, which can then be used to infect cells and generate infectious virus particles.
