Please answer the following question: Explain the mechanism by which messenger RNAs (mRNAs) are involved in gene expression and discuss their potential connection to viral latency in herpesvirus research.

[0001] assistant:
mRNAs are involved in gene expression by carrying genetic information from DNA in the nucleus to the ribosomes in the cytoplasm, where they are translated into proteins. In herpesvirus research, mRNAs can be crucial for maintaining viral latency, as the virus can produce minimal mRNAs that keep essential proteins for reactivation without fully activating the immune response.
