Please answer the following question: What is the role of the Hemagglutinin (HA) gene in the H1N1 virus and how does it contribute to transmissibility between guinea pigs?

[0001] assistant:
The Hemagglutinin (HA) gene in the H1N1 virus encodes a protein that allows the virus to attach to and enter host cells. This protein plays a crucial role in the virus's transmissibility between guinea pigs by facilitating the binding to and penetration of host respiratory cells, thereby enhancing viral spread.
