Please answer the following question: What are the specific locations of the main virulence determinants of Bacillus anthracis within the pXO1 and pXO2 plasmids?

[0001] assistant:
The main virulence determinants of Bacillus anthracis are located on the pXO1 plasmid, including the genes for the protective antigen (PA), edema factor (EF), and lethal factor (LF). The pXO2 plasmid contains the capsule genes (cap) that contribute to virulence.
