Please answer the following question: What evidence supports the hypothesis that EBNA1 is primarily responsible for maintaining the Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) genome as an episome through its interaction with oriP?

[0001] assistant:
EBNA1's interaction with the origin of plasmid replication (oriP) is crucial for maintaining the EBV genome as an episome. This is supported by experimental data showing that EBNA1 binds to and remodels oriP, facilitating viral DNA replication and stability within the host cell.
