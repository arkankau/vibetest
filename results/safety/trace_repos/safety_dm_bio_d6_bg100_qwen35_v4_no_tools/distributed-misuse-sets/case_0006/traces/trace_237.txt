Please answer the following question: Which nucleotides are used in the process of producing viral progeny and why?

[0001] assistant:
The nucleotides used in the process of producing viral progeny are typically those provided by the host cell, as viruses often lack the necessary enzymes to synthesize their own nucleotides. These nucleotides are essential for the synthesis of viral RNA or DNA required for replication and assembly of new virions.
