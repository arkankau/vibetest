Please answer the following question: What is the primary function of Natural Killer (NK) cells in the immune system and how do they interact with Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) infected cells?

[0001] assistant:
Natural Killer (NK) cells primarily function to recognize and eliminate virus-infected or tumor cells. They interact with EBV-infected cells by detecting altered patterns of surface molecules, which triggers NK cell cytotoxicity, leading to the destruction of the infected cells.
