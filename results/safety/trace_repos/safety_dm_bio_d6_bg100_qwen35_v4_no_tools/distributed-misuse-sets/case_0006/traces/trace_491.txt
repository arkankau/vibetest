Please answer the following question: Is Cytomegalovirus (CMV) known to be associated with Kaposi's sarcoma, especially in immunocompromised individuals?

[0001] assistant:
Cytomegalovirus (CMV) is not directly associated with Kaposi's sarcoma. However, CMV infection can be more severe in immunocompromised individuals, including those with HIV/AIDS, which increases their risk of developing Kaposi's sarcoma.
