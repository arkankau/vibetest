Please answer the following question: What is the function of the HSV protein ICP4 in the replication of viral DNA, if any?

[0001] assistant:
ICP4, or Immediate Early Protein 4, is a transcription factor in herpes simplex virus (HSV) that plays a crucial role in the viral life cycle by promoting the transcription of early and late viral genes, thereby facilitating the replication of viral DNA and protein synthesis.
