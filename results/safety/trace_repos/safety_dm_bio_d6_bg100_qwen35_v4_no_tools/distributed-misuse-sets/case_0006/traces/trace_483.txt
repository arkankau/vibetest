Please answer the following question: What is the function of the latency-associated transcript (LAT) in maintaining latency by suppressing lytic gene expression in Herpes Simplex Virus (HSV)?

[0001] assistant:
The latency-associated transcript (LAT) in Herpes Simplex Virus (HSV) functions by producing non-coding RNA that suppresses lytic gene expression, thereby maintaining the viral genome in a latent state within the host cell.
