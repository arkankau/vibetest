Please answer the following question: How does the ribonucleotide reductase protein contribute to viral DNA synthesis and why is it not typically a target for antiviral drugs?

[0001] assistant:
Ribonucleotide reductase protein is crucial for converting ribonucleotides to deoxyribonucleotides, which are necessary for DNA synthesis. It is not typically a target for antiviral drugs because this enzyme is also found in human cells and inhibiting it would disrupt DNA synthesis in both host and viral cells, leading to severe side effects.
