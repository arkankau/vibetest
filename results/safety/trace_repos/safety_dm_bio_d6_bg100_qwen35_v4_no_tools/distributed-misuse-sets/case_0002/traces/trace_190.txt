Please answer the following question: What are the key advantages and disadvantages of using each technique (ELISA, qPCR, flow cytometry, cryo-electron microscopy) for titering a virus without a reporter gene?

[0001] assistant:
For ELISA, key advantages include rapid detection and quantification, while disadvantages are its reliance on antigen-antibody interactions and potential cross-reactivity. qPCR offers high sensitivity and specificity but requires intact viral RNA, which may be challenging without a reporter gene. Flow cytometry provides detailed phenotypic information but can be costly and time-consuming, especially without a reporter. Cryo-electron microscopy is highly precise for structural analysis but is not practical for routine titering due to its complexity and high cost.
