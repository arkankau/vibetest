Please answer the following question: What is the function of the single-guide RNA (sgRNA) in the CRISPR-Cas9 system when targeting RNA viruses for genetic modifications?

[0001] assistant:
The single-guide RNA (sgRNA) in the CRISPR-Cas9 system guides the Cas9 enzyme to the specific viral RNA sequence for cleavage, enabling genetic modifications.
