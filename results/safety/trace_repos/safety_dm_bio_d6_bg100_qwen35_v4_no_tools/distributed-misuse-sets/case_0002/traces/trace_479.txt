Please answer the following question: In routine influenza A surveillance protocols, which viral gene segment is most frequently targeted by commercially available RT-PCR primer sets and why?

[0001] assistant:
In routine influenza A surveillance protocols, the HA (hemagglutinin) and NA (neuraminidase) gene segments are most frequently targeted by commercially available RT-PCR primer sets because they are highly variable and can help identify new strains and subtypes.
