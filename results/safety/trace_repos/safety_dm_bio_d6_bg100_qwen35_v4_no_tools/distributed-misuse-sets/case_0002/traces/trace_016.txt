Silent (synonymous) nucleotide substitutions within the primer-binding region of an RNA virus typically do not affect the melting temperature or the overall amplification efficiency in a one-step RT-PCR reaction, as these changes do not alter the amino acid sequence of the encoded protein. However, if the substitutions significantly alter the primer sequence, they could potentially reduce binding affinity and thus affect amplification efficiency.
