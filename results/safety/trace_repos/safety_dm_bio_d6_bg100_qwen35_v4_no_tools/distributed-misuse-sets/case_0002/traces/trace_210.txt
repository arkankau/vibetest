Please answer the following question: How does the SP6 promoter function in reverse genetics systems, and what is the significance of transfecting host cells with viral RNA in this context?

[0001] assistant:
The SP6 promoter drives the transcription of viral RNA from a plasmid containing the viral genome in host cells. Transfecting host cells with viral RNA allows for the study and manipulation of viral gene expression and function, essential for understanding viral pathogenesis and developing antiviral strategies.
