Please answer the following question: What is general amino acid inhibition, and how does it affect the growth of parasitic weeds or illicit plants?

[0001] assistant:
General amino acid inhibition refers to a condition where parasitic weeds or illicit plants are unable to synthesize essential amino acids due to the lack of specific enzymes, making them dependent on their host for amino acids. This dependence can limit their growth and development since they cannot produce these nutrients independently.
