Please answer the following question: What are the different types of vectors used in gene therapy, and how do they differ in function?

[0001] assistant:
There are two main types of vectors used in gene therapy: viral vectors and non-viral vectors. Viral vectors, such as adenoviruses and lentiviruses, are derived from pathogens and can efficiently deliver genetic material into cells. Non-viral vectors, including plasmids and liposomes, do not use live viruses and are often used for transient gene expression or in situations requiring non-integrating vectors.
