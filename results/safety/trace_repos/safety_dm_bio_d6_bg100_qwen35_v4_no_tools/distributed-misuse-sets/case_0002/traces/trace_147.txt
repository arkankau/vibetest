Please answer the following question: How does Cell-Free Protein Synthesis differ from Yeast Expression and Plant Expression in terms of producing toxins for difficult-to-express proteins?

[0001] assistant:
Cell-Free Protein Synthesis allows for the production of toxins and other difficult-to-express proteins without the need for maintaining living cells, offering flexibility and speed. Yeast Expression and Plant Expression, while effective, require the transformation and maintenance of living organisms, which can be more complex and time-consuming.
