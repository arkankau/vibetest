Please answer the following question: What is the definition of reverse genetics and how is it applied in the study of viruses, particularly influenza?

[0001] assistant:
Reverse genetics is a technique that allows the generation of infectious clones from a viral genome, enabling the study of gene function and virus replication. In the context of influenza, it is used to understand viral genetics and pathogenesis by creating specific viral strains for experimental studies.
