Please answer the following question: What is the GD-Pangolin-CoV virus, and how is it related to pangolins?

[0001] assistant:
The GD-Pangolin-CoV virus is a coronavirus found in pangolins and is closely related to SARS-CoV-2. It shares genetic similarities with SARS-CoV-2, suggesting pangolins may serve as intermediate hosts between bats and humans in the transmission of similar coronaviruses.
