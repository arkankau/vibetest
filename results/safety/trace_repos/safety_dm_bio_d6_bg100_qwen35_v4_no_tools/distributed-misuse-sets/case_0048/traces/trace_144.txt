Please answer the following question: What specific effect does targeting and degrading host cell antiviral proteins have on preventing interferon signaling and how does this help herpesviruses evade the host's innate immune system?

[0001] assistant:
Targeting and degrading host cell antiviral proteins, such as PKR and IFN-regulatory factors, prevents interferon signaling by blocking the activation of key interferon-stimulated genes. This helps herpesviruses evade the host's innate immune system, allowing them to replicate and spread more effectively.
