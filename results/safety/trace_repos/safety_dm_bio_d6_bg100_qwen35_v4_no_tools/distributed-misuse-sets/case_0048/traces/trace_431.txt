Insect cell expression systems can avoid some limitations of microbial systems by providing a eukaryotic environment that supports post-translational modifications like glycosylation, which is essential for the proper folding and function of certain animal venom toxins. Additionally, insect cells can express a wider range of proteins, including those with complex structures that are difficult for microbes to produce.
