Please answer the following question: What is the genetic profile of the PB2 gene found in H3N2 influenza viruses and how does it compare to the PB2 gene of the H5HA-mutant/pdm09 influenza virus?

[0001] assistant:
The PB2 gene of H3N2 influenza viruses typically contains the "PAI" mutation (a specific amino acid change at position 627, from proline to alanine), which is associated with increased virulence. In contrast, the PB2 gene of the H5N1-mutant/pdm09 (pandemic 2009) influenza virus often lacks this mutation, instead possessing a different set of mutations that confer high pathogenicity in humans.
