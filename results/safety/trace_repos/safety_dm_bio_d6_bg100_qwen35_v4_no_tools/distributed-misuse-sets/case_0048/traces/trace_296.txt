Please answer the following question: How do virus neutralization assays and antibody titration help in determining the ability of the virus to cause severe disease in hosts, and what specific measurements are involved?

[0001] assistant:
Virus neutralization assays and antibody titration help determine the ability of a virus to cause severe disease by measuring the virus's infectivity and the immune response against it. Neutralization assays measure the concentration of virus that is neutralized by antibodies, indicating the virus's susceptibility to immune response. Antibody titration measures the concentration of antibodies in the host's serum, indicating the strength of the immune response. Specific measurements include the dilution of virus or antibodies at which 50% of the virus is neutralized (the 50% neutralization titer) and the antibody concentration required to achieve a certain level of protection.
