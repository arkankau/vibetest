Please answer the following question: Why are receptors in pigs' respiratory tract important for their function as 'mixing vessels' for IAVs?

[0001] assistant:
Receptors in pigs' respiratory tract are important for their function as 'mixing vessels' for Influenza A Viruses (IAVs) because pigs can be infected with both avian and swine influenza viruses, allowing these receptors to facilitate the interaction and potential reassortment between different viral strains.
