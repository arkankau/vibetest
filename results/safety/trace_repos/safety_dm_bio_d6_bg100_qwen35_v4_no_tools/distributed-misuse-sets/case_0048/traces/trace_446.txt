Please answer the following question: What is the role of innate immune response in protecting against viral infections and how might pre-exposure to H1N1 virus affect it?

[0001] assistant:
The innate immune response provides an immediate defense against viral infections by producing cytokines, activating phagocytic cells, and generating interferons. Pre-exposure to H1N1 might prime the immune system, potentially enhancing the innate and adaptive responses to a subsequent H1N1 infection, though the exact nature of this effect can vary.
