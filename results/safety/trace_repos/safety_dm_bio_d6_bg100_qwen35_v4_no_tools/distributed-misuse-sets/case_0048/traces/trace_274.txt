Please answer the following question: What is CD4 and what role does it play in the immune response, particularly in relation to Measles virus entry into cells?

[0001] assistant:
CD4 is a protein found on the surface of T-helper cells that acts as a co-receptor for the measles virus, facilitating its entry into cells. This interaction is crucial for the virus to infect immune cells, particularly T-helper cells, which can impair the immune response and contribute to the severity of measles.
