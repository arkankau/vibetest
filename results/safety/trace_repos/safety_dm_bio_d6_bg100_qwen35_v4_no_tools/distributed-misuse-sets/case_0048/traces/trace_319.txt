Please answer the following question: What are the limitations or challenges associated with using yeast shuttle vectors in the generation of infectious cDNA clones of viruses?

[0001] assistant:
Yeast shuttle vectors can be limited by their inability to support the replication of certain viral elements, such as long terminal repeats (LTRs) found in retroviruses. Additionally, they may not efficiently maintain large insert sizes, which can be problematic for generating full-length cDNA clones of some viruses.
