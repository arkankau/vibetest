Please answer the following question: What are the benefits of using sucrose gradients over other sugar gradients (galactose, glucose, fructose) in the initial purification steps of scHPXV?

[0001] assistant:
Sucrose gradients have been used in the initial purification steps of scHPXV (seminovirus pseudotype of human papillomavirus) because sucrose can provide a more gentle and consistent density for virus particles compared to other sugars. Sucrose gradients are also easier to manipulate and can support a wider range of sample concentrations, offering better resolution and protection of virus particles during the purification process.
