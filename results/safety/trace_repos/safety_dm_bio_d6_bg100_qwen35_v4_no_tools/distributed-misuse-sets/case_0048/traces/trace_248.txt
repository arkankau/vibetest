Please answer the following question: Does US3 promote the degradation of host antiviral proteins, and if so, how does this mechanism help in immune evasion?

[0001] assistant:
US3, an enzyme from the herpes simplex virus 1, promotes the degradation of host antiviral proteins such as interferon-induced protein with tetratricopeptide repeats 3 (IFIT3). This mechanism helps in immune evasion by reducing the host's antiviral response, allowing the virus to replicate more efficiently.
