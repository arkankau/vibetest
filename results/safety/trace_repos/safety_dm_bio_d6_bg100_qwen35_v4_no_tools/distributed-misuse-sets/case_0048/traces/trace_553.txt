Please answer the following question: What would be the effect of randomly integrating an F plasmid into the genome of organism A and then mating it with organism B?

[0001] assistant:
Random integration of an F plasmid into the genome of organism A would make it F+ if the plasmid is expressed, allowing it to transfer genetic material to organism B during mating. Organism B could then become F+ if the plasmid is successfully transferred and expressed.
