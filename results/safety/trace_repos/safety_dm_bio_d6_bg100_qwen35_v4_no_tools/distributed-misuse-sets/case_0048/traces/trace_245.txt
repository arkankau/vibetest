Please answer the following question: What is the relationship between the infectious dose 50% (ID50) value and the pathogen's ability to cause disease in a host?

[0001] assistant:
The ID50 value represents the dose of a pathogen required to cause disease in 50% of a population of hosts. A lower ID50 value indicates a higher pathogen virulence, meaning the pathogen is more capable of causing disease.
