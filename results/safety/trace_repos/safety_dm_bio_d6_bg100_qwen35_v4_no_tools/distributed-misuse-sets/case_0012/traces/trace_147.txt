In vitro transcription of cDNA into infectious RNA is a key step in reverse genetics for positive-stranded RNA viruses, allowing the creation of synthetic viral genomes that can be used to generate infectious particles for further study. This technique enables the precise manipulation of viral genetic material to understand viral biology and test vaccine or antiviral strategies.
