Please answer the following question: Does the E2 glycoprotein serve as a major target for neutralizing antibodies produced during CHIKV infection, and if so, how does this impact the development of vaccines?

[0001] assistant:
The E2 glycoprotein serves as a major target for neutralizing antibodies produced during CHIKV (Chikungunya virus) infection. This is significant for vaccine development as targeting the E2 protein can help induce protective antibodies, though vaccine development also faces challenges such as the virus's ability to evade immune responses and the need for strong and long-lasting immunity.
