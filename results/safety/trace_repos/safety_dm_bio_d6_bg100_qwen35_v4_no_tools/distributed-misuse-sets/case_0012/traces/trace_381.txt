Please answer the following question: How does lysosomal degradation of MHC class I molecules affect their presentation on the cell surface and prevent recognition by CD8+ T cells?

[0001] assistant:
Lysosomal degradation of MHC class I molecules reduces their cell surface expression, thereby diminishing their availability for recognition by CD8+ T cells. This process effectively downregulates MHC class I molecules, making it harder for T cells to detect and respond to infected or abnormal cells.
