Please answer the following question: Is the viral nucleoprotein (NP) involved in the transcription of viral RNA segments into mRNA during influenza A virus replication?

[0001] assistant:
I don't know. The viral nucleoprotein (NP) is primarily involved in the packaging of viral RNA into the virion and stabilizing the RNA genome, but it is not directly involved in the transcription of viral RNA segments into mRNA. Transcription in influenza A viruses is catalyzed by the viral RNA polymerase.
