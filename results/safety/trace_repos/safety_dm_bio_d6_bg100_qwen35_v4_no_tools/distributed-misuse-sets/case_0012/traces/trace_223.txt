Please answer the following question: What is the definition and purpose of a synthetic defective interfering (DI) RNA in the context of virology?

[0001] assistant:
A synthetic defective interfering (DI) RNA is a truncated version of a viral genome that cannot produce a functional virus but can interfere with the replication of full-length viral genomes. Its purpose in virology is to serve as a potential antiviral agent by reducing viral load in infected cells without the risk of toxicity or the emergence of resistant viral strains.
