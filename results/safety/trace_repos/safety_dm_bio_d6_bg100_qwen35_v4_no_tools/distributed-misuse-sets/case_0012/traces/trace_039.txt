Please answer the following question: Which specific chemical parameters (e.g., COD, BOD₅, TN, TP, UV254, anionic surfactants, or conductivity) are routinely screened by municipal wastewater treatment plants to detect unusual industrial discharges, and what are common alert thresholds for each?

[0001] assistant:
Municipal wastewater treatment plants routinely screen for Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD), Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD₅), Total Nitrogen (TN), Total Phosphorus (TP), UV254 absorbance, anionic surfactants, and conductivity to detect unusual industrial discharges. Common alert thresholds include COD > 500 mg/L, BOD₅ > 20 mg/L, TN > 20 mg/L, TP > 1 mg/L, UV254 > 0.2 cm⁻¹, high levels of anionic surfactants (typically > 1 mg/L), and significant changes in conductivity.
