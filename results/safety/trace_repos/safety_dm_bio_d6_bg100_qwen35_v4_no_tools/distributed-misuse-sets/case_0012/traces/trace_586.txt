Please answer the following question: What is the advantage of using an internal ribosome entry site (IRES) in a bicistronic expression vector for gene expression in the viral genome?

[0001] assistant:
The advantage of using an IRES in a bicistronic expression vector is that it allows for the internal initiation of translation, enabling simultaneous expression of two proteins from a single transcript without relying on the cap-dependent translation pathway.
