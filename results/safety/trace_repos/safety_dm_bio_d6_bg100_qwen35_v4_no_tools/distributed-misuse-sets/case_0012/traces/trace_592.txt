Please answer the following question: How does ICP34.5 influence the replication and spread of the herpes simplex virus within the nervous tissue?

[0001] assistant:
ICP34.5 is an early/late viral gene product of herpes simplex virus that encodes two non-structural proteins, UL34 and UL35. These proteins inhibit the host cell's antiviral responses and promote the replication and spread of the virus within the nervous tissue by enhancing viral DNA synthesis and modulating the host's immune response.
