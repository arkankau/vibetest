Please answer the following question: How can swapping the E1 and E2 genes between different CHIKV strains help in understanding the role of these proteins in viral infectivity and immune evasion?

[0001] assistant:
Swapping the E1 and E2 genes between different CHIKV strains allows for the study of how these envelope proteins contribute to viral infectivity and immune evasion by comparing the traits of the modified viruses with those of the original strains. This can reveal which proteins are crucial for specific viral functions and how they interact with the host immune system.
