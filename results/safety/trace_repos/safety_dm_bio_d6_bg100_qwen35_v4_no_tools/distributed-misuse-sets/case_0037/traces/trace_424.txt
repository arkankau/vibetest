Please answer the following question: What is the known mechanism by which the PB1-F2 protein might influence viral replication or host cell interaction in the context of influenza A virus infection?

[0001] assistant:
The PB1-F2 protein of the influenza A virus is believed to influence viral replication and host cell interaction by inducing cell apoptosis, which can release progeny viruses, and by affecting cellular metabolic processes and viral gene expression.
