Please answer the following question: What is the mechanism by which a self-inactivating (SIN) feature in lentiviral vectors reduces the risk of generating replication-competent recombinants?

[0001] assistant:
A self-inactivating (SIN) feature in lentiviral vectors reduces the risk of generating replication-competent recombinants by deleting or modifying the long-terminal repeat (LTR) regions, which are necessary for viral replication. This deletion or modification prevents the viral genome from being transcriptionally active in the presence of an infectious wild-type viral genome, thus minimizing the chance of recombination and replication.
