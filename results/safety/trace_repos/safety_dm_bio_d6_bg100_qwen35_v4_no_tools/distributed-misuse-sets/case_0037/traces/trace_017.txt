Please answer the following question: What is the function of immediate-early (IE) genes during the initial stages of herpesvirus infection and how do they contribute to the establishment of latency?

[0001] assistant:
Immediate-early (IE) genes are among the first to be expressed upon herpesvirus infection. They contribute to the establishment of latency by promoting viral gene expression, enhancing viral DNA replication, and modulating host cell processes, which help the virus to persist within the host.
