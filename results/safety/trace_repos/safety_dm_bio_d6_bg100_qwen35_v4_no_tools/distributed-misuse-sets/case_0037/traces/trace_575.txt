[0001] assistant:
The envelope protein of SARS-CoV-2, also known as the Spike (S) protein, has a unique structure with multiple domains that allow it to interact with host cells and evade the immune system. This structure, combined with its high immunogenicity, makes it a key target for both natural infection and vaccine-induced immunity.
