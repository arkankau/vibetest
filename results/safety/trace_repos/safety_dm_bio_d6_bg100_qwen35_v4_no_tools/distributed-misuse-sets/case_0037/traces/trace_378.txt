Please answer the following question: What are the potential solutions or workarounds for the instability of large cDNA clones in bacterial plasmid vectors in the context of developing reverse genetic systems for viruses with large RNA genomes, such as coronaviruses?

[0001] assistant:
Large cDNA clones can be unstable in bacterial plasmid vectors due to their size. One solution is to use smaller, split cDNA clones and recombine them in eukaryotic cells. Another approach is to use specialized vectors like cosmids or bacterial artificial chromosomes (BACs) that can accommodate larger DNA inserts.
