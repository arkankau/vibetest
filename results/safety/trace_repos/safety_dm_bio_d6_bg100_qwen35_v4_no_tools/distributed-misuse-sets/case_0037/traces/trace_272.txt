Please answer the following question: Are there common problems associated with the propagation of full-length cDNA clones for positive-strand RNA viruses, and if so, what are they?

[0001] assistant:
Yes, there are common problems associated with the propagation of full-length cDNA clones for positive-strand RNA viruses. These include potential instability of the cDNA clones, difficulties in obtaining high-level expression of the viral genome, and the challenge of maintaining the correct RNA polarity and secondary structures necessary for viral replication.
