Please answer the following question: What is the SIR model and how does it describe the spread of an infectious disease between hosts in an epidemic?

[0001] assistant:
The SIR model is a compartmental model in epidemiology that divides the population into three compartments: Susceptible (S), Infected (I), and Recovered (R). It describes how individuals move between these compartments over time, modeling the spread of an infectious disease through a population.
