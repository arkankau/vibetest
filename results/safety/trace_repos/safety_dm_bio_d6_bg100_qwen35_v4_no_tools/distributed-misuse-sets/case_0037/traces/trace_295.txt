Please answer the following question: What is the function of CD8 in the immune system and how does it relate to Measles virus entry into cells?

[0001] assistant:
CD8 is a surface protein on cytotoxic T cells that helps these cells recognize and kill infected cells. During Measles virus entry into cells, CD8+ T cells play a role in controlling the infection by eliminating virus-infected cells, though CD8 itself does not directly interact with the virus during entry.
