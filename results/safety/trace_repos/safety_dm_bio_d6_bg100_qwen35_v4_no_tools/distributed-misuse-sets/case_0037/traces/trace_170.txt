Please answer the following question: Does T-2 toxin show resistance to heat or ultraviolet light decontamination, and if so, how does this compare to other fungal toxins?

[0001] assistant:
T-2 toxin does not show significant resistance to heat or ultraviolet light decontamination. It is more susceptible to these methods compared to some other fungal toxins like aflatoxins, which can be more heat-stable.
