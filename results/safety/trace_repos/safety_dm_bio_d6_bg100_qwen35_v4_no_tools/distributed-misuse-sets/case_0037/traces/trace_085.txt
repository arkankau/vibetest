Please answer the following question: Describe the role of the hypervariable region in the viral spike protein and its influence on infectivity.

[0001] assistant:
The hypervariable region of the viral spike protein plays a crucial role in recognizing and binding to host cell receptors, which is essential for viral entry and infectivity. Its high variability allows the virus to evade the host immune system and adapt to new hosts or environments.
