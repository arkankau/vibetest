Please answer the following question: Explain the method of random transposon mutagenesis and how it is used in reverse genetics for influenza viruses.

[0001] assistant:
Random transposon mutagenesis involves inserting transposable elements into the genome at random locations, which can disrupt gene function. In reverse genetics for influenza viruses, this method is used to generate mutant viruses with specific gene deletions or insertions, allowing for the study of gene function and the effects of genetic modifications on virus behavior.
