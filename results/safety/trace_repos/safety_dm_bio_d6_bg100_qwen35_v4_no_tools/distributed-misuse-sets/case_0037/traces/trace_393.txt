Please answer the following question: How does reassortment occur in influenza A viruses and what is its biological significance?

[0001] assistant:
Reassortment in influenza A viruses occurs when two different strains infect the same cell and exchange genetic segments, leading to a new viral genome. This process is significant because it can generate novel virus strains with potentially new antigenic properties, contributing to the emergence of pandemics.
