I don't know the specific genes contained within the UL and US segments of all viruses, as these can vary greatly depending on the virus type. However, typically, UL segments (Unique Long) in herpesviruses contain genes important for viral replication and pathogenesis, while US segments (Unique Short) often contain genes for early viral proteins involved in transcription and regulation.
