Please answer the following question: What is the significance of viral haemagglutinin (HA) in inducing host immune responses, and how might this be relevant to Gain of Function research?

[0001] assistant:
Viral haemagglutinin (HA) is a surface protein of influenza viruses that is crucial for host cell binding and infection. Its induction of robust immune responses, particularly antibody production, is significant for vaccine development. In the context of Gain of Function (GOF) research, manipulating HA might enhance viral virulence or transmissibility, raising concerns about potential biosafety risks.
