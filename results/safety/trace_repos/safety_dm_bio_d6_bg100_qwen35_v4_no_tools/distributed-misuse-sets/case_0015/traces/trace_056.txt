Please answer the following question: What are the key genetic changes that enable avian influenza virus transmission between ferrets, and how might these changes also be relevant to mammalian adaptation in wild strains?

Key genetic changes that enable avian influenza virus transmission between ferrets include mutations in the hemagglutinin (HA) protein, particularly in the receptor binding domain to enhance binding to mammalian cell receptors, and changes in the polymerase genes to improve replication efficiency in mammalian cells. These changes are also relevant to mammalian adaptation in wild strains, as they facilitate the virus's adaptation to new hosts, potentially increasing the risk of interspecies transmission and pandemic potential.
