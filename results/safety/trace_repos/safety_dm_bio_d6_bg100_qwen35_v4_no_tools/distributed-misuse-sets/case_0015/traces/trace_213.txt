Please answer the following question: What are the common methods used to purify recombinant proteins from inclusion bodies, and what challenges might arise with these methods in the context of toxins?

[0001] assistant:
Common methods for purifying recombinant proteins from inclusion bodies include solubilization with detergents, denaturation and renaturation, and refolding protocols. Challenges in the context of toxins can include incomplete refolding leading to toxic protein aggregates, and the potential for toxic intermediates during the purification process.
