Please answer the following question: How do complement proteins C3b and C4b function in the complement system, and could they be used to evade neutralization by complement?

[0001] assistant:
C3b and C4b function as opsonins and aid in immune complex clearance by marking pathogens for phagocytosis. They can also be used by pathogens to evade complement neutralization through a process called complement regulatory protein (CRP) binding, which helps shield the pathogen from further complement activation.
