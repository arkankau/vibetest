Please answer the following question: How does the latency-associated transcript (LAT) contribute to the switch from lytic to latent infection in Herpes Simplex Virus (HSV)?

[0001] assistant:
The latency-associated transcript (LAT) contributes to the switch from lytic to latent infection in Herpes Simplex Virus (HSV) by serving as a template for non-coding RNA that supports viral latency. This RNA helps maintain the viral genome in a latent state by regulating viral gene expression and protecting the virus from immune responses.
