Please answer the following question: Does the cell line HEK293 support the culture of influenza viruses?

[0001] assistant:
The HEK293 cell line, which is derived from human embryonic kidney cells, does not natively support the culture of influenza viruses. These viruses typically require specific mammalian cell lines that provide the necessary factors for viral replication, such as MDCK (Madine-Darby Canine Kidney) or Vero cells.
