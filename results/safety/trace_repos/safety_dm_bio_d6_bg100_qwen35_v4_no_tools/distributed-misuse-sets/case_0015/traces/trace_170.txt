TRP1 marker in yeast allows for the selection of transformed cells because the TRP1 gene is required for tryptophan biosynthesis. When grown in media lacking tryptophan, only yeast cells that have been transformed with a plasmid carrying the TRP1 gene can grow, as they can produce their own tryptophan.
