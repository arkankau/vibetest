Please answer the following question: How does the 'CD150-blind' rMV affect the development of clinical signs and viremia in infected animals?

[0001] assistant:
The 'CD150-blind' rMV does not infect cells that express CD150 (also known as SLAM or CD86), which affects its ability to cause clinical signs and viremia, as it cannot productively infect a subset of cells that are likely important for disease pathogenesis.
