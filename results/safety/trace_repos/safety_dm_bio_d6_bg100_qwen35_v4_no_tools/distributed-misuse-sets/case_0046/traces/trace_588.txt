Please answer the following question: What is the genetic profile of the PB2 gene found in H1N1 influenza viruses and how does it compare to the PB2 gene of the H5HA-mutant/pdm09 influenza virus?

[0001] assistant:
The PB2 gene in H1N1 influenza viruses typically contains the signature mutation S274N, which is associated with increased viral replication and pathogenicity. In comparison, the PB2 gene of the H5N1 virus (pdm09) often includes not only the S274N mutation but also additional mutations like E627K and D701N, which enhance the virus's ability to replicate in mammals and potentially increase its virulence.
