Key steps include designing appropriate templates, selecting appropriate RNA polymerases, optimizing transcription conditions, and ensuring efficient RNA purification. Considerations involve template fidelity, RNA yield, and potential for RNA degradation. Compared to cell-based methods, in-vitro transcription offers higher yield and control but may be less efficient for complex viral genomes.
