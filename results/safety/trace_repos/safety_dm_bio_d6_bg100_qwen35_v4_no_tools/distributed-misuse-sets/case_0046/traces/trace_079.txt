Please answer the following question: What changes in receptor-binding specificity are observed due to HA mutations in the H5HA-mutant/pdm09 influenza virus compared to the wild-type virus?

[0001] assistant:
The H5HA-mutant/pdm09 influenza virus exhibits changes in receptor-binding specificity, allowing it to bind more efficiently to avian-like receptors rather than the human-like receptors preferred by the wild-type virus. This shift enhances its potential for transmission among birds and contributes to its pandemic potential.
