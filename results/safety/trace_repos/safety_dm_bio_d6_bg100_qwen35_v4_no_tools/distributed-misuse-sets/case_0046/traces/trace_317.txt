Please answer the following question: What is the process of amplification of viral genomes using PCR and how does it differ from de novo synthesis?

[0001] assistant:
PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction) amplifies viral genomes by creating multiple copies of a specific DNA sequence using primers and DNA polymerase. It does not synthesize de novo genetic material but rather makes many copies of an existing viral genome. De novo synthesis, on the other hand, involves creating new DNA or RNA from scratch without a template.
