Please answer the following question: What are the limitations of insertional mutagenesis when using exogenous DNA in genetic studies?

[0001] assistant:
Insertional mutagenesis using exogenous DNA can lead to gene disruption, unwanted gene activation, and unpredictable gene expression levels. Additionally, the integration of the exogenous DNA can sometimes be random, affecting non-target genes and potentially causing unintended phenotypic changes.
