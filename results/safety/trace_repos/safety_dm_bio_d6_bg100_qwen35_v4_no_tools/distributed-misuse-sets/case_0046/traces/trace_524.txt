A decreased virulence of a zoonotic pathogen due to altered ecology could reduce its ability to cause severe human disease by weakening its mechanisms for tissue invasion or toxin production. This typically does not lead to increased disease potential but rather to less severe infections, as the pathogen becomes less capable of causing severe illness.
