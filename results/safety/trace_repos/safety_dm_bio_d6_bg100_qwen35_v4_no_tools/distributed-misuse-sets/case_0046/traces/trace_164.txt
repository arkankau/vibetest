Please answer the following question: How does the soil and water in jungles contribute to the presence of the bacteria that causes Melioidosis, and is this more common than in other environments?

[0001] assistant:
Melioidosis bacteria thrive in warm, humid soil and water, particularly in tropical and subtropical regions like jungles. The bacteria, *Burkholderia pseudomallei*, can survive in these environments for extended periods. While common in such environments, it is not necessarily more prevalent than in other similar conditions; its occurrence depends more on human or animal exposure to contaminated soil or water.
