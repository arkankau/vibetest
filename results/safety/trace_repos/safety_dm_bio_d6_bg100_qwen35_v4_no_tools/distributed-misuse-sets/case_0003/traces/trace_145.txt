Please answer the following question: How does a reverse genetics approach allow researchers to directly study the function of a viral gene?

[0001] assistant:
A reverse genetics approach allows researchers to directly study the function of a viral gene by introducing or expressing a known sequence of a viral gene into a host cell, bypassing the need to first isolate the virus. This enables the observation of the gene's effects in a controlled environment, facilitating the understanding of its role in the viral life cycle.
