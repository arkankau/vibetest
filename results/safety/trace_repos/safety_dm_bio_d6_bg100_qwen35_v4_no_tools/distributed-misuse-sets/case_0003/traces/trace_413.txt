Please answer the following question: What is the role of the National Science and Engineering Board for Biosecurity in regulating dangerous pathogens in the United States?

[0001] assistant:
The National Science and Engineering Board for Biosecurity provides oversight and guidance on the safe handling and use of select agents and toxins to protect public health and safety. It does not have direct regulatory authority but advises the Select Agent Program administered by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention and the Department of Agriculture.
