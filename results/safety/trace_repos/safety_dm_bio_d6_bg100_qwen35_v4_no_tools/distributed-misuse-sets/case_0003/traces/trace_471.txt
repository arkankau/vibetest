Please answer the following question: What is the function of Tumor necrosis factor-alpha (TNF-α) in the immune response to KSHV and is it predominantly produced by CD4+ T cells?

[0001] assistant:
Tumor necrosis factor-alpha (TNF-α) plays a role in the immune response to KSHV by promoting inflammation and activating other immune cells. While it can be produced by various cell types, it is not predominantly produced by CD4+ T cells; macrophages and other immune cells also contribute significantly to TNF-α production.
