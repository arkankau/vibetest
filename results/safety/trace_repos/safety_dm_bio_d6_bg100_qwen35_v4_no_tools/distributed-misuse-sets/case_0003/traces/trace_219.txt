Please answer the following question: In synthetic biology, how does a riboswitch regulate downstream gene expression, and would its presence influence the horizontal spread frequency of the DNA element carrying it?

[0001] assistant:
A riboswitch regulates downstream gene expression by binding specific molecules, which alters the RNA structure and either activates or represses gene transcription. Its presence would not directly influence the horizontal spread frequency of the DNA element carrying it, as riboswitch function is localized to gene regulation rather than DNA transfer mechanisms.
