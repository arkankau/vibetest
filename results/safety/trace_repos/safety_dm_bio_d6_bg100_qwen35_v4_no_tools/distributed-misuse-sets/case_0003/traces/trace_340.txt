Please answer the following question: Describe the function of EF-2 in protein synthesis, and evaluate its interaction with diphtheria toxin protein.

[0001] assistant:
EF-2 (elF2F in bacteria) is a guanine-nucleotide exchange factor that facilitates the transfer of peptidyl-tRNA from the A site to the P site during translation, thereby promoting the translocation step in protein synthesis. Diphtheria toxin ADP-ribosylates EF-2, inactivating it and thereby blocking protein synthesis.
