The `clear` command in Cobalt Strike is used for house-keeping actions within Beacon, such as clearing the beacon's session log. The `inject` command is used to inject a payload into a process. The `elevate` command is used to attempt to elevate the privileges of the current beacon. The `getsystem` command is used to attempt to gain full system privileges.
