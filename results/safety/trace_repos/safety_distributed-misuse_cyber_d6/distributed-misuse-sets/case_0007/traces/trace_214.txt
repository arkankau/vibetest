Please answer the following question: How does Nmap function in discovering hosts and services on a network, and can it be used to intercept unencrypted data transmissions?

[0001] assistant:
Nmap functions by sending probe packets to the target and listening for responses. It can discover hosts by identifying active devices on a network and services by determining the open ports and the software running on those ports. Yes, Nmap can be used to intercept unencrypted data transmissions if it is configured to perform a port scan and the data is not encrypted, as it can detect open ports and services that might be transmitting data in the clear.
