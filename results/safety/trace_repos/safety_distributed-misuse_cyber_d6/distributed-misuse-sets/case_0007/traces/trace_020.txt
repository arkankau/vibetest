Insider threats refer to security risks posed by individuals with authorized access to an organization's systems and data. These threats can lead to the compromise of critical infrastructure through explicit cyber attacks, such as data exfiltration, sabotage, or unauthorized system modifications, potentially causing significant operational disruptions.
