Please answer the following question: How does Control Flow Integrity (CFI) work to prevent the exploitation of return-oriented-programming (ROP) techniques?

[0001] assistant:
Control Flow Integrity (CFI) works by ensuring that control transfers only occur through allowed call and jump targets, thus preventing the execution of arbitrary code that ROP techniques might otherwise introduce. It maintains a whitelist of valid control flow targets, and any attempt to transfer control to an invalid target is detected and blocked.
