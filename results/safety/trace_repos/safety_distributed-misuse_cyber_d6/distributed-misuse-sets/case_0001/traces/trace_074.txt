[0001] assistant:
The Teardrop attack is a form of denial-of-service attack that exploits the way Windows 95 and 98 handle fragmented IP packets. It is not effective for guessing hashed passwords because it does not involve any form of password cracking or exploiting vulnerabilities in hashing algorithms; instead, it focuses on disrupting network services by causing buffer overflows in the victim's system.
