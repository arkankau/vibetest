[0001] assistant:
HTTPS encryption secures communication by encrypting data between a user's browser and a website's server, making it difficult for attackers to intercept or read the data. However, HTTPS alone does not directly hide the location of a cyber attack; it primarily secures the data being transmitted. To hide the location, additional techniques like IP obfuscation or using proxy servers might be employed.
