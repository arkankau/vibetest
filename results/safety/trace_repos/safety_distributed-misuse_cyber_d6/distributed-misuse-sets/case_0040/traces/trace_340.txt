Deep-packet inspection of Modbus TCP/IP traffic at the Ethernet frame level involves analyzing the TCP/IP headers and the Modbus application layer data to identify and interpret Modbus messages. Common evasion techniques security researchers study include modifying the Modbus function codes, inserting dummy data, and using encrypted or compressed payloads to hide the Modbus traffic.
