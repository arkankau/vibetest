Please answer the following question: What are the known propagation methods of Stuxnet, and how does the LNK vulnerability fit into its overall propagation strategy?

[0001] assistant:
Stuxnet primarily propagated through USB drives and affected systems when they were booted or when files were copied to them. The LNK vulnerability allowed Stuxnet to spread automatically when a user viewed an infected thumbnail preview in Windows Explorer, fitting into its strategy by enabling silent and automated infection without user interaction.
