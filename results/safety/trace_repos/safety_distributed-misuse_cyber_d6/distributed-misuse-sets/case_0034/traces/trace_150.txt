Please answer the following question: How can malware maintain control over a system after a reboot if it is not stored in persistent storage?

[0001] assistant:
Malware can maintain control after a reboot by injecting code into the boot process, such as modifying the Master Boot Record (MBR) or the BIOS/UEFI firmware, or by using a rootkit that hides its presence in the system's boot sequence.
