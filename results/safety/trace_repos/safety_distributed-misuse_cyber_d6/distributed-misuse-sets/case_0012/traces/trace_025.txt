Please answer the following question: What is a Virtual Private Network (VPN) and how does it encrypt internet connections to protect data during transmission?

[0001] assistant:
A Virtual Private Network (VPN) is a service that creates a secure, encrypted connection over a less secure network, like the internet. It encrypts internet connections by using encryption protocols to scramble data, making it unreadable to anyone who intercepts the transmission.
