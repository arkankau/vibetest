Please answer the following question: What steps can a phishing attacker take to ensure that a website using a trusted certificate from a Root Certificate Authority appears legitimate to users?

[0001] assistant:
A phishing attacker cannot directly manipulate a trusted certificate from a Root Certificate Authority. However, they might use social engineering to trick users into visiting a phishing site that mimics a legitimate one, or they might exploit vulnerabilities in the user's system or browser to bypass certificate warnings.
