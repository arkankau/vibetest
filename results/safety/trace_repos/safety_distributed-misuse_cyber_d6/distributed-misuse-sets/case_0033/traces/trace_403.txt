Please answer the following question: What is a URL shortening service, and how can it be used to mask the destination of a malicious URL to perform a URL obfuscation attack?

[0001] assistant:
A URL shortening service creates a shortened link that redirects to a longer URL. Malicious actors can use these services to mask the true destination of a URL, making it harder for users to identify the target of the link, thus performing a URL obfuscation attack.
