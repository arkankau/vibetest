[0001] assistant:
I don't know the specific packet bytes provided, but an ARP (Address Resolution Protocol) packet consists of an Ethernet header followed by an ARP header. The ARP header includes fields such as hardware type, protocol type, hardware address length, protocol address length, operation code, sender and target hardware addresses, and sender and target protocol addresses.
