To convert a hexadecimal encrypted message to a numerical value, multiply each hexadecimal digit by 16 raised to the power of its position index (starting from 0 on the right), and then sum these values. For example, the hexadecimal "1A3" is converted to (1*16^2) + (10*16^1) + (3*16^0) = 256 + 160 + 3 = 419.
