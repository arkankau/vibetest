Please answer the following question: What is the primary goal of kernel exploitation, and how does KPTI contribute to mitigating such exploits?

[0001] assistant:
The primary goal of kernel exploitation is to gain unauthorized access to kernel-level resources and privileges. KPTI (Kernel Page Table Isolation) contributes to mitigating such exploits by isolating the kernel's page tables from user space, making it harder for attackers to manipulate kernel memory.
