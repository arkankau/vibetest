Please answer the following question: What is a botnet and how can it be used to launch a DDoS attack on a website?

[0001] assistant:
A botnet is a network of compromised devices that can be controlled remotely to perform tasks, often maliciously. It can be used to launch a DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service) attack by simultaneously flooding a website with traffic from these devices, overwhelming its servers and making it inaccessible to legitimate users.
