Please answer the following question: What is the purpose of the 'adc' instructions in the function and how do they affect the values in the registers?

[0001] assistant:
The 'adc' instruction stands for "add with carry" and is used to add two values along with a carry-in from a previous operation. It affects the values in the registers by adding the values in the specified registers or between a register and an immediate value, and also takes into account any carry from a previous operation, storing the result in one of the registers.
