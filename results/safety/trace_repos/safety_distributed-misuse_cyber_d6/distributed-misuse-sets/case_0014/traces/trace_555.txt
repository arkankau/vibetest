Please answer the following question: What is a ROP Chain and how does pivoting the stack onto a precompiled ROP Chain in the binary affect the decompilation process?

[0001] assistant:
A ROP (Return-Oriented Programming) Chain is a technique used to execute arbitrary code by chaining together small sequences of instructions ("gadgets") found within the program's own code or libraries. Pivoting the stack onto a precompiled ROP Chain in the binary can make decompilation more challenging because the original control flow and function calls are obfuscated, and the decompiled code may appear as a series of unrelated gadgets rather than a coherent sequence of high-level instructions.
