[0001] assistant:
The 'lsof' command lists files that are currently being used by processes, including network ports and sockets, which can help identify open network connections and running services on an IoT device. This information can highlight potential attack surfaces by showing which ports are open and which services are active, allowing for targeted security assessments and hardening measures.
