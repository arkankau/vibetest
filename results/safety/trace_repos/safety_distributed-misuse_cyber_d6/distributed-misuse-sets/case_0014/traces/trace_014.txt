Please answer the following question: What is the role of a Root Certificate Authority in the context of digital certificates and how does it affect user trust?

[0001] assistant:
A Root Certificate Authority (CA) issues digital certificates that verify the ownership of a public key by the named subject of the certificate. This establishes trust in the certificate chain, ensuring that users can rely on the authenticity of websites and other entities they communicate with securely.
