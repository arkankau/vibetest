Please answer the following question: What is a phishing kit and how can it be used to clone a website for a phishing attack?

[0001] assistant:
A phishing kit is a collection of tools and resources, often delivered via a website, designed to create convincing copies of legitimate websites to trick users into divulging personal information. It can be used to clone a website by replicating its design and functionality, making the phishing site appear legitimate to users who are then directed to enter their credentials or other sensitive information.
