Beacon Object Files (BOFs) are small, modular scripts used in Metasploit's post-exploitation framework, specifically within Meterpreter sessions. They enable Meterpreter to perform various tasks, such as maintaining persistence, exfiltrating data, and evading detection, by providing a flexible and extensible scripting interface.
