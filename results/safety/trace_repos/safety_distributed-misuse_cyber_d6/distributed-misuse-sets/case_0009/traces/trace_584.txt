Please answer the following question: What is the potential risk of using double MD5 hashing for password storage, and is it considered secure?

[0001] assistant:
Double MD5 hashing is not considered secure for password storage. The MD5 algorithm is vulnerable to collision attacks, and applying it twice does not significantly enhance security. Modern best practices recommend using more secure hashing algorithms like bcrypt, scrypt, or Argon2.
