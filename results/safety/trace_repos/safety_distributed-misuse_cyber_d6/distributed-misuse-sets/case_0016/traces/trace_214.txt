Please answer the following question: What is the exact effect of `-Wl,--omagic` on page alignment, text-segment writability, and the ELF program header layout in a bare-metal firmware image, and why might a secure-boot routine flag an image built with this flag as non-conforming?

[0001] assistant:
The `-Wl,--omagic` flag in the linker script creates a bare minimum executable with no initial stack or heap, and the text segment is not writable. This can lead to non-aligned pages and a simplified ELF program header. Secure-boot routines might flag such images as non-conforming because they require a more robust environment with proper alignment and security features, which `-Wl,--omagic` omits.
