Please answer the following question: For a custom ExtensionObject that carries a vendor-defined TypeId already registered on the server, does the OPC UA binary encoding include any mandatory payload-length or padding-verification step that a DPI device could use to detect non-conforming data sizes?

[0001] assistant:
The OPC UA binary encoding for an ExtensionObject includes a mandatory payload length field, which a DPI device can use to detect non-conforming data sizes. However, padding verification is not a standard part of the encoding process.
