Please answer the following question: How does the runasadmin command differ from other similar commands in Cobalt Strike?

[0001] assistant:
The runasadmin command in Cobalt Strike is specifically designed to escalate privileges to Administrator on a Windows machine, unlike other similar commands which may not always achieve this level of elevation. It simplifies the process of gaining higher privileges by directly attempting to run a command with administrative rights.
