Please answer the following question: Does differential privacy rely on the assumption that any piece of data could potentially be used to identify individuals, and how does this compare to de-identification methods?

[0001] assistant:
Differential privacy assumes that any single piece of data could potentially be used to identify an individual, thus requiring strong protections. In contrast, de-identification methods aim to remove or anonymize direct identifiers but may still leave data vulnerable to re-identification attacks.
