Please answer the following question: How does an IPS log information about DOS attacks, and what actions does it take based on this logging?

[0001] assistant:
An IPS logs information about DOS attacks by monitoring network traffic for patterns indicative of such attacks, such as a sudden increase in traffic volume or a high number of connection requests. Based on this logging, the IPS can take actions like blocking the source IP, throttling traffic, or alerting administrators to take further action.
