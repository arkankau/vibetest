Please answer the following question: Where are payload modules stored in the Metasploit framework, and how do stages relate to this storage?

[0001] assistant:
Payload modules in the Metasploit framework are stored within the "payloads" directory. Stages, which are parts of the payload that are sent to the target in multiple packets, are related to this storage as they are referenced and utilized by the exploit modules when delivering the payload.
